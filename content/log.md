## [2026-05-01 to 2026-05-07] compressed | Bunker Ancestors — Millbrook to Matlock and Maulden

Research spanning six generations of the Bunker ancestral line — from Thomas Bunker (1704, Westoning/Millbrook) through to Frederick Bunker's marriage to Eliza Geary (1892, Ampthill). The Millbrook, Bedfordshire home is established across three generations; the 1852 dispersal to Bedford and the separate Derbyshire branches (Caleb and siblings, 1870s) are fully traced. The Tall-Ward family of Maulden — Caleb's first wife's line — is fully documented, and the Maulden nexus that brought Eliza Geary into Frederick's life is established. Caleb's second family (Barnsley, Derbyshire) is documented through to Ernest's 1967 death, along with the inter-branch contact Roland maintained.

Key findings:
- **Deep ancestry chain:** William Bunker (1663) + Sarah Greene → Thomas (1704) + Mary Reade (Toddington) → Thomas (1738) + Martha Sinfield (Millbrook 1761) → James Thomas (1764) + Lydia Brown → James (1807) + Elizabeth Watts (Millbrook 1830). James Thomas Bunker's family-of-origin (previously unknown) confirmed. 1806 mass christening: seven children in a single Millbrook ceremony (births 1790–1804) — the diagnostic marker of Nonconformist Wesleyan practice. An 8th child, Martha (b. 21 Dec 1801), newly identified; fate unknown
- **Elizabeth Watts (1810–1868):** Lace maker, Cranfield-born. James confirmed as agricultural labourer (1851). - **Caleb (1845–1918) + Rebecca Tall:** Married Christmas Day 1868, Howard Chapel Bedford. Rebecca died 7 July 1870; her father Edward Tall died nine days later, 16 July — double Maulden bereavement left infant Frederick without his maternal generation. Caleb moved to Matlock ~1871, remarried Margaret Barnsley (October 1871, Matlock)
- **Caleb's Barnsley second family:** Six children confirmed (all previously unknown) — Annie (1872), Herbert T.B. (1875, New Tupton), Maggie (1876), Agnes (1878), May (1880), Ernest (1882).
