## Parents

**[[james-bunker-1749|James Bunker]]** (1749–1818) was the eleventh child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and Mary Reade, christened 21 February 1749 at Millbrook. He married **[[elizabeth-pedder-1752|Elizabeth Pedder]]** (1752–1830) on 2 March 1772 at Millbrook. James died 10 July 1818; Elizabeth outlived him by twelve years, dying in 1830.

## Family life

James and Elizabeth were a Millbrook family, marrying there and raising their children there. James died in 1818 aged ~69. Elizabeth's survival to 1830 gave her 58 years of marriage and a long widowhood.

## Wider family

The Pedder surname appears in another Millbrook Bunker line: James Bunker (1777), son of [[edward-bunker-1742|Edward Bunker (1742)]], had a first wife named Sarah Pedder. Whether the two Pedder women were related is not established.
