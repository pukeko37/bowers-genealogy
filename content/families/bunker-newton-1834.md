## Parents

**[[job-bunker-1804|Job Bunker]]** (1804–unknown) was the eldest son of [[joel-bunker-1789|Joel Bunker]], born before his parents' marriage and christened at Eversholt in 1812. He married **[[sarah-newton-1814|Sarah Newton]]** (1814–unknown) on **13 October 1834** at Eversholt — when he was about 30 and she about 20. Both were in Eversholt in the 1841 and 1851 censuses.

Three died young (Jonas at 16, William at 3, Eliza as infant). The name Robert **Joel** Bunker is a deliberate memorial to the grandfather.

## Family life

Job was an Eversholt man his whole life — born near Ampthill (the next town), christened at Eversholt, married there, living there across two censuses. His family reflects the settled Eversholt community that his father Joel had been part of since at least 1812.
