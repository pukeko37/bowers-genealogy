## Parents

[[thomas-norman-1793|Thomas Norman]] (27 May 1793–19 December 1855) was born in Over, Cambridgeshire — the third child of [[robert-norman-1769|Robert Norman]] and [[frances-green-1769|Frances Green]]. [[elizabeth-fear-1794|Elizabeth Fear]] (1794–29 July 1832) was born in the Swavesey area, daughter of Richard Fear (1755–1840) and Sarah Woodcock (1762–1843).

They married in August 1815 at Swavesey — sources give both 13 and 20 August, most likely representing banns and ceremony. Thomas was 22, Elizabeth was about 21.

## Children

Five children are documented:

1. **Thomas Norman** (christened 15 October 1815, Willingham) — born just two months after the marriage; died in childhood or infancy; absent from all later records
2. **Robert Norman** (~1821–?) — in 1841 at home in Swavesey aged 20; in 1851 as head of his own household at Swavesey with wife Mary Ann (born Coton) and young children Jane, Elizabeth, Rebekah; Thomas senior lodging with him
3. **Sarah Norman** (~1822–?) — in 1841 at home in Swavesey aged 15; no further record held
4. **[[thomas-norman-1827|Thomas Norman]]** (1827–1905) — born Swavesey; agricultural labourer; married [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann Tabram]] 1850; seven children; see [[norman-tabram-1850]]
5. **Ann Norman** (1832–1843) — born and died in Swavesey; the youngest child, born the same year Elizabeth died; survived eleven years

## Family Life

Thomas and Elizabeth's marriage began in Swavesey but the family initially lived near Willingham — their first child was christened there in October 1815, just weeks after the wedding. Willingham is about four miles from Swavesey. They appear to have returned to Swavesey before 1821, when Robert was born and recorded there.

By 1841 Thomas was at Boxworth End, Swavesey: four children at home, his wife nine years dead. He raised four children — Robert, Sarah, Thomas, and Ann — as a widower. The youngest, Ann, was less than a year old when Elizabeth died; she lived until 1843, dying at about eleven. Thomas never remarried.

In 1851 Thomas was living as a lodger at his son Robert's Swavesey household, a widower aged 57, still working as an agricultural labourer. He died four years later, on 19 December 1855, in Swavesey, aged 62.

## Elizabeth's death in 1832

Elizabeth died on 29 July 1832 — the same year as her youngest child Ann's birth. The pairing of Ann's birth year (1832) with Elizabeth's death date (29 July 1832) strongly suggests Elizabeth died in childbirth or from puerperal complications following Ann's birth. This left Thomas with four surviving children: Robert (~11), Sarah (~10), Thomas (~5), and infant Ann. The same pattern would recur in the next generation: Thomas (1827) later told his own children that his mother had died when he was about five.

## The two Thomas Normans

Thomas and Elizabeth named their first son Thomas — born October 1815, christened at Willingham. This child died young. They then named their fourth surviving child also Thomas — born 1827 in Swavesey. The pattern of repeating a dead child's name in the next living child was common in 19th-century England. The Thomas Norman who married Ann Tabram and became the grandfather of [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman (1859)]] is this second Thomas.

## Wider Family

Thomas Norman (1793) was one of seven siblings from the [[norman-green-1788|Norman-Green family]]. His siblings Elizabeth (1795–1877), James (1801–1872), Luke (1803–1878), and Richard (1805–1890) are known but not yet researched. They may represent Norman cousins for Thomas Norman (1827)'s children.

Elizabeth Fear's family also remains only partially known. Her parents Richard Fear (1755–1840) and Sarah Woodcock lived to old age — Richard to 85, Sarah to 81 — in Swavesey. Elizabeth's surviving sister Mary Fear (1802–1866) would have been known to Elizabeth's children growing up.

## Open Questions

- Who was the first child Thomas Norman (1815)? His death date and burial place are unknown.
- What became of Robert Norman (~1821)? He appears in 1851 as head of household at Swavesey; later records not yet held.
- What became of Sarah Norman (~1822)?
- Did Thomas Norman (1793) have any contact with the extended Fear or Norman sibling families after Elizabeth's death?
