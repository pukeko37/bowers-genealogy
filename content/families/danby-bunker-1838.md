## Parents

**[[stephen-danby-1812|Stephen Danby]]** (abt 1812–1867), excise officer, married **[[eliza-bunker-1815|Eliza Bunker]]** (1815–1885) on **11 March 1838** at Eversholt, Bedfordshire. Stephen was from Hockliffe (Bucks Gazette). Eliza was the eldest daughter of Joel Bunker, publican of the Green Man Inn, Eversholt. Stephen died in 1867; Eliza died in Derby in 1885.

## Family life

The excise service was a mobile career. In 1841 the family were at Thame, Oxfordshire; by 1851 in Staffordshire; from 1861 onwards in Derbyshire (St Werburgh's parish, then St Peter's). Stephen died in 1867; Eliza remained in Derby until her death in 1885 aged 70.

This is a collateral branch — the Danby family two generations removed from the main Bunker research lines. Their Derbyshire connection is incidental to, but distinct from, the Derbyshire migration of the Millbrook Edward-line Bunkers in the 1880s.
