## Parents

[[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham Batchelor]] (1825–1892), born Whipsnade, son of [[thomas-bachelor-1781|Thomas Bachelor]] and Alice Woodcroft. [[hannah-maria-heels-1814|Hannah Maria Heels]] (1814–1886), born Lockinge, Berkshire. Marriage date and place not yet confirmed by clippings; they are both in Whipsnade from 1841 onward. Hannah was previously married to [[samuel-heels-1797|Samuel Heels]] (the Chequers tenant), who died in 1845.

## The Chequers Inn

The licensee sequence at the Chequers Inn is now resolved. [[samuel-heels-1797|Samuel Heels]] was the documented tenant at the 1843 pub auction, paying £16/year to owner Edward Burr (Dunstable Brewery). Samuel died in 1845, leaving Hannah a widow on Whipsnade Green with a two-year-old son (Thomas Heels, 1843). She subsequently married Abraham Batchelor, who took on the licence she brought with her.

**Confirmed licensee sequence:**
- **Samuel Heels** — documented tenant 1843; died 1845
- **Hannah (Heels) + Abraham Batchelor** — from ~1845 until 1867
- **[[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]] + [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]]** — from December 1867

**How the transition worked:** Hannah was Samuel's second wife (~1842 marriage). She was in Whipsnade from 1841. When Samuel died two years after the pub auction, Hannah held the family's residential and commercial position on Whipsnade Green. Her marriage to Abraham Batchelor was a natural continuation of that tenure — a family licence transfer, not a change of proprietor.

**Residence timeline:**
- 1841–1861: Whipsnade (By Whipsnade Green; Frog Corner)
- 1871–1881: Studham, Bedfordshire
- 1891: Abraham back in Whipsnade (Hannah had died 1886)
- 1892: Abraham died (death registered in the Luton district)

## Connection to the Batchelar Family

Abraham is related to [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]], GBP Sr's second wife. The exact relationship — through the two Batchelar/Bachelor families of Flamstead and Dagnall — is the outstanding open question. If confirmed, it means GBP Sr's connection to the Chequers predates both his marriage to Eliza and his marriage to Ruth Cooke: he knew the pub through the Batchelar family, married Eliza (a Batchelar), then took the licence that her cousin Abraham had held.
