## Parents

**George Maynard** and **Sarah Rumball** married in **Luton, Bedfordshire, on 14 February 1794**. The family were Nonconformists — specifically Baptist — as the children's births are recorded in the Baptist register (RG4-8). No person pages have been created for George and Sarah; their dates, occupations, and parentage are unestablished.

George Maynard appears as a named party in Susan's marriage records in 1849 and again in 1868 — confirming his paternity across thirty or more years of documentation, and suggesting he lived to a considerable age.

## Children

Three children are documented:

1. **Mary Maynard** (1795–1884) — the eldest; lived to 89
2. **Keziah Maynard** (1800) — documented by birth only; no further records found
3. **[[susan-maynard-1802|Susan Maynard]]** (1 March 1802 – unknown) — the youngest; born in Luton; moved to Whipsnade as an adult and had three marriages there
