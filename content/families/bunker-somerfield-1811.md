## Parents

**[[james-bunker-1777|James Bunker]]** (1777–1864) married **[[mary-somerfield-1793|Mary Somerfield]]** (abt 1793–unknown) on **25 June 1811** at Millbrook — his second marriage, following his first wife Sarah Pedder. Mary was about 18 at the time; James was 34.

## Children

Five children, all born in Millbrook:

| # | Name | Born | Notes |
|---|------|------|-------|
| 1 | [[naomi-bunker-1813\|Naomi Bunker]] | 1813 | Likely twin with Sally; pre-marital son Richard (1837); married Robert Babbington 1845 |
| 2 | Sally Bunker | 1813 | Likely twin with Naomi; not yet traced beyond birth |
| 3 | Rebecca Bunker | 1815 | Not yet traced |
| 4 | James Bunker | 1816 | Not yet traced |
| 5 | Mary Bunker | 1818 | Not yet traced |

Naomi and Sally were both christened in 1813 — almost certainly twins.

## Notes

The Millbrook page previously stated James (1777) had "a daughter Naomi and possibly others" by Mary Somerfield. This is now confirmed as five children. Mary Somerfield's fate after the birth of Mary (1818) is not documented; James subsequently married Catherine Blunt (his third wife), who was with him in the 1841 census.
