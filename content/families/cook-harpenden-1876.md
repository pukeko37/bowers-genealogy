## Parents

[[james-cook-1843|James Cook]] (probably born 1843, Kensworth/Whipsnade) and Mary (maiden name unknown) lived in Harpenden, Hertfordshire. Their marriage date and place are not yet documented. James is the James Cook (FamilySearch 9KCT-R5M) who was a son of [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] and Elizabeth Frost — confirmed by the FamilySearch record for Thomas Edward Cook. ## Family Life

The family was based in Harpenden, Hertfordshire — a market town approximately five miles from Whipsnade and the ancestral Cook heartland of Kensworth. Thomas Edward Cook's burial at Kensworth echoes his grandfather David Cook's marriage there in 1842: the family's roots in that parish span at least three generations.
