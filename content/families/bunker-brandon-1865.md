## Parents

[[george-bunker-1845|George Bunker (1845)]] and [[harriet-brandon-1846|Harriet Brandon (1846)]] married in **December 1865 at Lambeth**, London. George had left Millbrook sometime before the marriage; Harriet's origins are unresearched.

## Family Life

George and Harriet's family is entirely a London family — all children were likely born in Lambeth or Tottenham. The census record shows them moving from Lambeth (1871) to Tottenham (1881), where they remained through at least 1901. None of the eight children have been further researched.

## Wider Family

George is the youngest child of [[john-bunker-1803|John Bunker (1803–1889)]] and Maria West. His father died in 1889 in Millbrook; his brothers [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph]] and [[samuel-bunker-1842|Samuel]] were lifelong Millbrook men. George represents the furthest geographical dispersal of John (1803)'s children. See [[bunker-west-1824]].
