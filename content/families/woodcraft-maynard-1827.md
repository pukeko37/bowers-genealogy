## Parents

**William Woodcraft** (1777–1835) was a Whipsnade man, probably native to the village or its immediate surroundings. His age at marriage — about 50 — and the apparent presence of adult or near-adult children in Susan's 1841 household suggest he had a prior family, though no earlier marriage has been found. The Woodcroft/Woodcraft name appears in Whipsnade records across several generations: **Alice Woodcroft** (1791–?) of the same parish married Thomas Bachelor in 1816 at Whipsnade and settled at Frog Corner. Whether she and William were siblings or otherwise related has not been established, but the overlap of surname, village, and hamlet is suggestive.

**Susan Maynard** (1802–?) was born on 1 March 1802 in Luton, Bedfordshire, to George Maynard and Sarah Rumball (married Luton, 14 February 1794). The family were Nonconformists; her birth is recorded in the Baptist register (RG4-8). She had at least two known siblings: Mary Maynard (1795–1884) and Keziah Maynard (1800). Susan appears to have come to Whipsnade as an adult, whether for work or through family connection; her reason for settling there is not documented.

William and Susan married on **20 May 1827 at Whipsnade**. William was about 50; Susan was 25.

## Children

Five children were christened at Whipsnade, all from this marriage:

1. **Ann Woodcraft** — christened 6 January 1828
2. **George Woodcraft** — christened 6 December 1829
3. **James Woodcraft** — christened 9 September 1832
4. **Amos Woodcraft** — christened 3 August 1834
5. **Charles Woodcraft** (1836–1876) — christened 14 February 1836

William Woodcraft is dated 1777–1835 in FamilySearch. If that death year is correct, Charles was a posthumous child — christened in February 1836, some months after his father's death. No person pages have been created for the children; they are documented by christening only.

## The 1841 Household

The 1841 census places Susan — recorded as "Susan Woodcroft," age 40 — at **Frog Corner, Whipsnade**. The same hamlet was home to the Batchelor family (see [[bachelor-woodcroft-1816]]). Her recorded household:

- Thomas Woodcroft, 25
- David Woodcroft, 20
- Charlotte Woodcroft, 15
- George Woodcraft, 11
- James Woodcraft, 9
- Amos Woodcraft, 6
- Charles Woodcraft, 5

Thomas, David, and Charlotte are too old to be Susan's children by the 1827 marriage. They may be William's children from an earlier relationship, which would also explain his age at marriage. Their ultimate fates are not known. The presence of Alice Woodcroft (1791) as a near-contemporary in the same village raises the possibility that Thomas, David, and Charlotte were from the same extended Woodcroft household — cousins or the children of a sibling — but this is unconfirmed.

## After William's Death

Susan was widowed by 1836 at the latest. She remained at Frog Corner through 1841 with four of her children and possibly William's earlier children. She remarried twice:

- **Edmund Durrant** (1795–before 1868), married 9 June 1849 at Whipsnade; agricultural labourer. The 1851 census records her as "Susanna Durrant," occupation **"Labs Wife And School Mistress"** — she was running a small school in the village alongside her domestic role, a notably independent position. In the same household in 1851 were Thomas Woodcraft (35) and Charlotte Woodcraft (27), still present a decade after the 1841 census.

- **[[thomas-batchelar-1803|Thomas Batchelar]]** (1803–1872), married 11 January 1868 at Whipsnade. His first wife [[susan-matthews-1800|Susan Matthews]] had died in 1857. Susan Maynard was his second wife; she was known as "Susan Durent" (her Durrant married name) at the time of the marriage. See [[batchelar-matthews-1826]] for Thomas's first family.

Susan's own fate after Thomas's death in December 1872 is not established.
