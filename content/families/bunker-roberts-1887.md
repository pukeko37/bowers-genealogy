## Parents

[[albert-bunker-1865|Albert Bunker (1865)]] married **Emma Roberts** (born 1867) on 11 April 1887 at Moggerhanger, Bedfordshire — the same parish where his elder brother Thomas had married Charlotte Roberts in December 1885. Charlotte and Emma Roberts were almost certainly sisters.

Of eleven children, five died in infancy or early childhood. Arthur James (1895–1916) was killed in the First World War.

## Family life

Albert and Emma settled in the South Normanton area of Derbyshire, where Albert had moved by 1891. He was still in Nottinghamshire in 1911.

In the 1921 census, Albert's mother [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]] — by then widowed and approximately 79 years old — was living with Albert and Emma. She remained close to the family through the end of her life.
