## Parents

Charles Stewart Main (1884–1963) and [[elizabeth-ann-norman-1888|Elizabeth Ann Norman]] (1888–1953) married in 1916.

## Children

1. [[george-stewart-main-1919|George Stewart Main]] (1919–?) — known in the family as George Main

## Family Life

The family lived at 143 De Beauvoir Road, in the Dalston area. In 1921 the household included Elizabeth's mother [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen]], sister [[lillian-frances-norman-1893|Lillian]], and brother [[charles-norman-jr-1895|Charles Jr]] with his wife. By 1939, Charles Stewart and Elizabeth were still at 143 De Beauvoir Road — just 22 houses from Ellen at number 121.

Elizabeth died in 1953 in Lambeth, aged 65. Charles Stewart died in 1963.

## George Stewart Main

Their son [[george-stewart-main-1919|George Main]] deserves special note. Born in Dalston in 1919, he served as Rifleman 689895 through six years of overseas Army postings in WWII before resuming his pre-war employment at the **New Zealand High Commission** in London. His NZ government career almost certainly helped facilitate [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy]] and [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland's]] 1968 emigration to Auckland.

George retired to Birchington-on-Sea in Kent — commuting to London until retirement, then settling permanently. He devoted 45 years to caring for three relatives in Kent:

- Aunt [[alice-norman-1890|Alice]] (widowed 1955, died 1980)
- Aunt [[norah-winifred-norman-1902|Norah]] (widowed 1978, died 1996)
- Cousin Gwendoline May Norman (died 2000)

In retirement he became a songwriter — "Birchington-on-Sea" and "Thanet Forever" — and made television appearances. See [[george-stewart-main-1919]].
