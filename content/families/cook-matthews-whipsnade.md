## Parents

[[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] (1816–1900) was born in Kensworth, Bedfordshire, the brother of [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] (GBP Sr's third wife). This was David's **second marriage** — he had first married Elizabeth Frost (1818–1843), who died in 1843 leaving two or three young children; see [[cook-frost-1842]].

[[charlotte-matthews-1823|Charlotte Matthews]] (1823–1910) was born in Whipsnade, the sister of [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] (GBP Sr's first wife). They married in 1856 in Whipsnade.

## Family Life

David and Charlotte lived at By Whipsnade Green, Whipsnade — the same hamlet recorded for Charlotte's parents Thomas and Mary Wilson, and for GBP Sr himself at various points. Both were lifelong Whipsnade residents until late in life. Charlotte's occupation in 1851 was Straw Plaiter. David died in 1900; Charlotte followed in 1910. Both deaths were registered in the Luton district.

## Wider Family

This family sits at the intersection of the Matthews and Cook families:

- Charlotte's sister [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne]] married [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]] in 1835
- David's sister [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth]] married GBP Sr in 1867 (his third wife)
- GBP Sr therefore had his first and third wives from these two interlinked Whipsnade families

David's son [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook]] (from his first marriage to Elizabeth Frost) held the Chequers Inn licence 1904–1908 after Ruth's death — making Charles both David's son and Ruth's nephew.
