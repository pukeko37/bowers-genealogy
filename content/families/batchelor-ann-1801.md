## Parents

**David Batchelor** (dates unknown) married **Ann** (surname unknown) on 3 November 1801 at St Luke Old Street Church, Finsbury, London. Nothing is known of David's origins; his birth year, birthplace, and parentage have not been established from available sources.

The London marriage is notable for a family whose subsequent life unfolded entirely in the Chilterns countryside. Whether David and Ann were Londoners temporarily settled in Middlesex, or Chilterns people who happened to marry in the capital, is unknown.

## Children

Four children are recorded:

1. William Batchelor — christened 7 March 1802, **Little Gaddesden, Hertfordshire**
2. **[[thomas-batchelar-1803|Thomas Batchelar]]** — born 9 November 1803, christened 22 June 1804, **Ivinghoe, Buckinghamshire** *(Eliza Batchelor's father)*
3. Elizabeth Batchelar — baptised October 1805, **Edlesborough, Buckinghamshire**
4. Susannah Batchelar — baptised 27 September 1807, **Edlesborough, Buckinghamshire**

The spread of christening parishes — Little Gaddesden (Herts), Ivinghoe (Bucks), and Edlesborough (Bucks) — maps onto a cluster of Chilterns border parishes within a few miles of each other.

## Family Life

Beyond the bare christening records, nothing is documented about this family's daily life. David Batchelor does not appear in any identified census record, suggesting he may have died before 1841 or has not yet been found.

Ann's maiden name is not given in any available source. The mother is named "Ann" or "Anne" consistently across the children's christening records.
