## Parents

[[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]] (1845–1918) and [[rebecca-tall-1844|Rebecca Tall]] (1844–1870) married on Christmas Day, 25 December 1868, at Howard Chapel, Mill Street, Bedford — a Nonconformist chapel. Caleb was born in Millbrook, Bedfordshire; Rebecca in the same county, raised in Maulden.

## Children

1. [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick William Tall Bunker]] (1869–1932) — born 15 November 1869, Ampthill registration district, Bedfordshire; birth announced *Bedfordshire Mercury* 29 November 1869. Actual village unconfirmed — Maulden (the Tall family home, within the Ampthill district) is the most likely location.

## Family Life

This family lasted less than two years. Rebecca died on 7 July 1870, aged about 26, when Frederick was approximately one year old. Her father [[edward-tall-1806|Edward Tall]] died nine days later. Caleb, left with an infant son and no mother-in-law generation to call on, left Frederick with his maternal grandmother [[sarah-ward-1806|Sarah Ward]] in Maulden and moved to Matlock, Derbyshire.

Frederick was named **William Tall** in the middle — the "Tall" honouring his mother's family, a gesture that preserved Rebecca's name in her son even though she did not survive to raise him.

Caleb's subsequent life in Derby suggests he did not return to Bedfordshire. He and Frederick maintained some contact — Frederick was in Matlock with his father in 1881 — but they lived separately for most of Frederick's upbringing and adult life. Caleb died in 1918 in Burton upon Trent, long after Frederick had settled in Luton.

## Wider Family

- **Caleb's parents:** [[james-bunker-1807|James Bunker]] and Elizabeth Watts, married Millbrook 1830. See [[bunker-watts-1830]]. Caleb was the eighth of nine children.
- **Caleb's grandparents:** [[james-thomas-bunker-1764|James Thomas Bunker]] and [[lydia-brown-1769|Lydia Brown]], married St Paul's Walden 1788. See [[bunker-brown-1788]].
- **Rebecca's parents:** [[tall-ward-maulden|Edward Tall and Sarah Ward]], Maulden, Bedfordshire. Sarah raised Frederick after the deaths of 1870.
- **Frederick's family:** Married Eliza Geary in 1892. See [[bunker-geary-1892]].
