## Parents

[[phoebe-alice-bunker-1843|Phoebe Alice Bunker]] (1843–1923) and [[samuel-fensome-1842|Samuel Fensome]] (abt 1841–1920) married on **16 August 1863** at Saint Paul, Bedford — their home parish. Phoebe had been living at Dane Street, St Paul's with her widowed mother; Samuel was a Ravensden man who had moved to Bedford by the time of the marriage. A marriage notice was published in Bedford on 26 July 1863, three weeks before the wedding.

That same year, Phoebe's sister [[lydia-bunker-1833|Lydia Bunker]] also married in Bedford — Samuel Plackett Lane on 21 September 1863. Two Bunker sisters, two Samuels, eight weeks apart.

Samuel was born in **Ravensden, Bedfordshire**, christened 31 May 1841. His parents were Thomas Fensom (1813–1884) and Sarah Barrick (1821–?), married in Ravensden 1839. He had three known siblings. He died in **Mansfield, Nottinghamshire** in 1920, about ten miles from their 1911 home at Hucknall Torkard. Phoebe outlived him by three years, dying in Yorkshire in 1923.

Children's birthplaces span the migration: Herbert Charles (1866), Edgar (1868), Alice Jane (1872), and Reginald Edwin (1873) were all born in Bedford. Sidney Thomas (1877) was born in Pilsley — establishing that the family migrated **between 1873 and 1877**. Percy Louis (1879), Maud M (1882), and Florence Sarah (1884) were also born in Pilsley. No death dates are recorded for Alice Jane, Reginald Edwin, Maud, or Florence in the FamilySearch record.

## Family Life

The family's geographic arc mirrors the broader Bunker Derbyshire migration. In 1871 they were still in Bedford; their fourth child Reginald Edwin was born there in 1873. Sidney Thomas, born in Pilsley in 1877, is the first child born in Derbyshire — placing the migration **between 1873 and 1877**. They remained in Pilsley through 1901, two full decades in the same village.

The timing is striking: George Bunker's son Albert and Caleb Bunker's son Herbert T.B. were both born in New Tupton around 1875 — the same window in which the Fensomes were making their move from Bedford. Pilsley is the adjacent village to New Tupton. The Fensome and Bunker families were therefore settling within a mile of each other at almost exactly the same moment in the mid-1870s.

By 1911 Samuel and Phoebe had moved to Hucknall Torkard, Nottinghamshire, just across the county boundary from Pilsley. Samuel died there in Mansfield in 1920.

Samuel Fensome's occupation is unrecorded. The Pilsley area was coal-mining country; it is possible he worked in the collieries, but this is speculation pending further research.
