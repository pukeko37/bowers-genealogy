## Parents

[[samuel-capel-1750|Samuel Capel]] (1750–1808) and [[sarah-knight-1755|Sarah Knight]] (1755–1839), married 15 November 1772 at Cranfield, Bedfordshire. Both were born in Cranfield; the family was thoroughly rooted in that parish.

10. Mary Keppel (1795–Deceased)

**Note on the three "Sarah" entries (1791, 1792, 1794):** These likely represent the same child recorded under different spelling variants, or one died young and the name was reused — a common practice. The surviving Sarah who married John Watts in 1809 is the 1791 christening (M93Y-6MZ in FamilySearch).

**Note on naming convention:** John Knight Keppel (1789) embeds his mother's maiden name "Knight" as a middle name — the same practice seen in Herbert Thomas **Barnsley** Bunker and Alfred **Eusden** Norman elsewhere in this research.

## Family Life

Samuel died in 1808. Sarah Knight survived him by over thirty years, dying about 1839 in Millbrook — by which time her daughter Sarah Capel was also living in Millbrook. The family's spelling instability (Capel, Cappoul, Keppel, Kepple) suggests a surname that was phonetically familiar but not standardised in local records.

## Significance

Samuel Capel and Sarah Knight are Caleb Bunker's great-great-grandparents on the maternal Watts line: Samuel & Sarah → [[sarah-capel-1791|Sarah Capel]] → [[elizabeth-watts-1810|Elizabeth Watts]] → [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]]. The Cranfield Capel/Knight family is now the oldest documented generation of Caleb's maternal ancestry.
