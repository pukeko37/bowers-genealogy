## Parents

[[herbert-thomas-barnsley-bunker-1875|Herbert Thomas Barnsley Bunker]] (1875–abt Mar 1917) and [[martha-emma-smith-1877|Martha Emma Smith]] (1877–1940) married in 1901 at Kings Norton, Worcestershire. Herbert was working in railroad employment and the family followed him to his postings — Sheffield (1901), Raunds, Northamptonshire (1911), and finally the Sambourne/Alcester area of Warwickshire.

## Children

Three children, all born during Herbert's railway years:

1. [[sybil-may-bunker-1901|Sybil May Bunker]] (1901–1973)
2. [[herbert-lawrence-bunker-1905|Herbert Lawrence Bunker]] (1905–?)
3. [[grace-bunker-1909|Grace Bunker]] (1909–1974)

The 1911 census records all three children but notes "poor name spelling" in the entries.

## Family Life

Herbert's railway career shaped the family's geography entirely — they lived wherever he was stationed, in railway houses provided as part of the stationmaster's role. The family's moves from Worcestershire to Yorkshire to Northamptonshire to Warwickshire trace his postings.

[[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland Bunker]] — Herbert's half-nephew — used to stay with this family during school holidays as a boy, likely at Raunds, Northamptonshire or an earlier station.

Herbert died in about March 1917, probably at Coughton Court — a Red Cross convalescent hospital near Alcester — leaving Martha a widow with children aged sixteen, twelve, and eight. She lived another 23 years, dying in 1940.
