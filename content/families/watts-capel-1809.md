## Parents

[[john-watts-1790|John Watts]] (1790–1814) and [[sarah-capel-1791|Sarah Capel]] (1791–1851+), married 27 July 1809 at Cranfield, Bedfordshire.

## Children

1. John Watts (1809–Deceased)
2. [[elizabeth-watts-1810|Elizabeth Watts]] (1810–1868) — married James Bunker 1830
3. Joseph Watts (1811–1891)
4. James Watts (1813–Deceased)

## Family Life

John and Sarah married at Cranfield (Sarah's home parish) and had four children in five years. John Watts died in 1814, buried at Lidlington — aged about 24. Their youngest child James was barely a year old. Elizabeth was four.

Sarah was widowed at roughly 23 with four children under five. She remained in the Millbrook area, later living at "Village Hollow," Millbrook. She remarried Edward Roffe in 1824 (ten years after John's death) and John Thompson in 1839. See [[sarah-capel-1791]] for the full account of her three marriages.

## Significance

This family connects [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]]'s ancestry to the Cranfield/Lidlington cluster of central Bedfordshire — the same area as the [[bunker-watts-1830|Bunker-Watts]] Millbrook family. Through [[elizabeth-watts-1810|Elizabeth Watts]], Caleb descends from the Watts and Capel families; through John Watts from the Knights and Gurneys. These were established Bedfordshire families going back to at least the mid-eighteenth century.
