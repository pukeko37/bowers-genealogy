## Parents

[[alfred-dumpleton|Alfred Dumpleton]] (1848–1917, Chalgrave → Kensworth) and [[emma-hucklesby|Emma Hucklesby]] (born Sundon, Bedfordshire) married on **22 February 1870** at Sundon. Alfred had been living at Chalgrave through 1861 and was recorded at Sundon by 1871 — he moved there before or for the marriage. By 1881 the family had settled at Kensworth, where they remained through the 1901 census. Alfred died in 1917, probably at Kensworth.

## Children

Alfred and Emma had five children: four sons (names not yet established) and one daughter:

1. **[[alice-dumpleton-1883|Alice Mabel Dumpleton]]** (1883 – 13 Nov 1940) — married [[thomas-cook-1882|Thomas Edward Cook]] 1906; buried Kensworth

The four sons are unidentified by name. **Arthur Thomas Dumpleton (1879–1951)**, buried at Kensworth, is a strong candidate for one of them — his birth year places him ahead of Alice, consistent with the family sequence. The other Kensworth Dumpletons — Lizzie (1876), Sydney George (1893), Lucy (1895), Arthur Maurice (1900), Mary Jane (1902) — are from different branches: Lizzie is confirmed not to be Alfred and Emma's child (their children were four boys and Alice only), and the later group (1893–1902) are more likely grandchildren.

## Family Life

Alfred and Emma's census trail traces a short migration from the chalk-ridge village of Chalgrave south-east to Sundon (where Emma's family was based), and then west to Kensworth, where they put down roots. The move to Kensworth set up the next generation: Alice married [[thomas-cook-1882|Thomas Edward Cook]] in 1906, continuing the Cook family's long association with Kensworth that runs back to David Cook and Elizabeth Frost's marriage there in 1842.
