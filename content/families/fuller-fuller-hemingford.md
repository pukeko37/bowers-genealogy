## Parents

**Thomas Fuller** and **Rachael Fuller** (maiden name unknown) were a family of **Hemingford Abbots, Huntingdonshire** — a small parish on the River Great Ouse, immediately adjacent to Fenstanton. Their daughter [[adeliza-fuller-1862|Adeliza Fuller]]'s baptism record confirms this parentage. No person pages have been created for Thomas and Rachael; their dates, occupations, and backgrounds are unestablished.

## Children

Three children are documented, all from the 1871 census of the Hemingford Abbots area:

1. **Albert R. Fuller** (birth year unknown)
2. **[[adeliza-fuller-1862|Adeliza Fuller]]** (1862–1938) — married [[john-tabram-1861|John Tabram (1861)]] in December 1879
3. **Cuthbert T. Fuller** (1871) — the youngest child visible in the 1871 census

## Significance

The proximity of Hemingford Abbots to Fenstanton — the two parishes border each other — is what brought [[adeliza-fuller-1862|Adeliza]] into John Tabram's life. The Tabram family of Fenstanton, through John's aunt [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann Tabram]], connects back to the Norman family of Swavesey, and through them to [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] of London.

Adeliza's maiden name was given to her daughter Ethel Fuller Tabram as a middle name — the standard Victorian practice of carrying the mother's family name forward into the next generation.
