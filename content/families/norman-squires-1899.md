## Parents

[[robert-henry-norman-1868|Robert Henry Norman]] (1868–1954), youngest child of [[thomas-norman-1827|Thomas Norman]] and [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann Tabram]] in the [[norman-tabram-1850|Norman-Tabram family]], married [[ada-squires-1864|Ada Squires]] (1864–1923) in 1899 at St Ives, Huntingdonshire. Ada was four years older than Robert and came from Fendrayton, Cambridgeshire — a village about 8 miles from Swavesey. They married relatively late: Robert was 31, Ada 35.

## Children

1. **Elsie Norman** (1900–1987)
2. **Annie Norman** (1902–1985)

Both daughters were long-lived. The family remained in Swavesey throughout Robert's life.

## Family Life

Robert and Ada spent their married life in Swavesey. By 1911 they were living at **Middle Watch** — a hamlet within Swavesey parish. Robert's occupation across all censuses was agricultural labourer. The two daughters, Elsie and Annie, were 11 and 9 in 1911.

Ada died in 1923 aged 59. Robert, the youngest of his generation, outlived her by 31 years, finally dying in 1954 aged about 86. He was the last survivor of Thomas and Ann's seven children, and among the last of his generation of the wider Norman family.

## Wider Family

Robert was the youngest of the Norman-Tabram family. His sister [[mary-ann-norman-1861|Mary Ann]] also lived in Swavesey and in 1911 her address was in the same area. His brother [[charles-norman-1859|Charles]] had gone to London and founded the urban branch of the family that eventually produced [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy Norman]] and the Bunker/Bowers family.
