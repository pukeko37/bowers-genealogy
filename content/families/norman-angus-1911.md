## Parents

[[thomas-george-norman-1886|Thomas George Norman]] (1886–1949) and [[mary-elizabeth-angus-1885|Mary Elizabeth Angus]] (1885–1969) married 19 August 1911 at DeBeauvoir Town, Middlesex.

## Children

1. [[gwendoline-may-norman-1915|Gwendoline May Norman]] (1915–2000) — never married; administered her father's estate with her mother; lived in Kingsland baker's shop for 40+ years; moved to Kent; died 2000

## Family Life

Thomas had varied occupations — drape and fancy lace goods manufacturer (1921), cushion case manufacturer (1939), and during WW2 he ran a baker's shop in Dalston/Kingsland. The family lived at 106 Hertford Road, Hackney (1921) and 6 Englefield Road, Kingsland (1939).

During WW2, their house at Englefield Road was bombed. The family moved into Thomas's baker's shop to live. Thomas died in 1949. Daughter Gwendoline, who never married, continued living in the converted baker's shop for over 40 years after the bombing. In 1984, Andrew Bowers and David Bowers visited her there.

Gwendoline eventually moved to Kent, where she was cared for by her cousin George Stewart Main until her death in 2000.
