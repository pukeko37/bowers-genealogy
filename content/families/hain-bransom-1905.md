## Parents

[[james-hain-1877|James Hain]] (1877–1932) and [[margaret-anne-bransom-1880|Margaret Anne Bransom]] (1880–1964) married in December 1905 at Whipsnade. James was the farmer at Dell Farm — one of the two farms reached by roads across the Whipsnade village green — son of the previous farmer [[james-edward-hain-1832|James Edward Hain]] (1832–1911). Margaret Anne was the fifth child of [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]], and thus the granddaughter of [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] through her mother's side.

The marriage joined two families who had lived within a few hundred yards of each other for generations: the Hains working Dell Farm while the Bransoms and Cooks lived By Whipsnade Green.

## Family Life

The 1911 census shows the Dell Farm household: James (33, Farmer), his widowed father James Edward (private means), his sister Margaret D Hain, and Margaret Anne's younger sister [[minnie-rose-bransom-1884|Minnie Rose Bransom]] (27, Housekeeper) — who had come to help manage the farmhouse. The baby Dora (born 1910) was visiting the maternal grandparents Edwin and Mary Bransom at By Whipsnade Green on census day.

James Edward Hain died later in 1911 — he had been the farmer before his son, and is buried at St Mary Magdalene, Whipsnade. Dell Farm was part of the Pedley Settled Estates, auctioned November 1915. James purchased the farm at that auction — becoming owner outright — and a 1926 valuation records him as a "Good Farmer," the only personal character note in the documentary record. See [[dell-farm]] for the full farm history.

## Wider Family

Margaret Anne's sister [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]] had married into the Perry family; her sister [[clara-lucy-bransom-1877|Clara Lucy Bransom]] married Cyril John Baldock in 1912; and her sister [[minnie-rose-bransom-1884|Minnie Rose Bransom]] served as housekeeper at Dell Farm. The Bransom family's connection to the Whipsnade Cooks runs through Margaret Anne's mother [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]], who was [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]]'s daughter — placing the Hain family at one remove from the wider Birchmore–Cook web that underpins so much of the Whipsnade story.

## Open Questions

- Margaret D Hain (James Jr's sister): a "Margaret Hain" with no dates is recorded in St Mary Magdalene churchyard — almost certainly her
- James Hain (1877–1932): died London, Jun 1932 quarter (GRO: London C, vol. 1c, p. 2), aged 54; not buried at Whipsnade — circumstances of his presence in London unknown
