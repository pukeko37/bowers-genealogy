## Parents

[[richard-norman-1723|Richard Norman]] (abt 1723–1783) and **Mary Hall** (1730–1782) married on 1 October 1749 at Over, Cambridgeshire — Richard's adopted parish and place of occupation (weaver). Richard had been widowed in 1765 after his first marriage to [[mary-essex-1722|Mary Essex]]; he and Mary Hall married fourteen years after his first wedding.

Mary Hall (1730–1782) has not yet been given her own person page. Her parentage is unknown from current sources.

## Children

- [[robert-norman-1769|Robert Norman]] (1769–1842) — the only child identified from this marriage in current sources

Robert was born when Richard was approximately 46 and Mary around 39. He was about fourteen when his father died in 1783, and about thirteen when his mother died in 1782 — left an orphan in his early teens.

## Family Life

Richard Norman was a weaver in Over, Cambridgeshire. The family were settled at Over; Robert was born, lived, and died there. The fenland village of Over, on the edge of the South Cambridgeshire fens, was the family's world across multiple generations.

Robert's relatively late birth (1769) to a father born in 1723 suggests he may have had older half-siblings from the first marriage — William Norman (1739) and Richard Norman (1745) — who would have been adults by the time Robert was born.
