## Parents

[[joseph-bunker-1798|Joseph Bunker]] (abt 1797–Jan 1868) married **Mary Brown** (born ~1800, Westoning, Bedfordshire) on 11 May 1821 at Steppingley. Mary's birthplace in Westoning — a village 3 miles from Steppingley — is confirmed by the 1851 census.

## In the censuses

**1841** (Steppingley, Village, p.2): Joseph (40), Catherine (10), Susan (9). [Mary and older/younger children presumed present but not separately linked in FamilySearch.]

**1851** (Steppingley): Joseph (~53, Ag Lab, born Steppingley), Mary (wife, 49, born Westoning), Thomas (son, 10, born Steppingley), Amelia (daughter, 13, born Steppingley). The older children — Arthur (~28), Caroline (~25), William (~22) — had left home.

**1861** (Steppingley, Church Street): Joseph, Thomas (son, 20, born Steppingley), Eliza Scott (7, born Steppingley — relationship not stated; possibly a granddaughter or servant). By 1861 even Thomas's younger siblings were gone.

## Family life

Joseph and Mary raised their large family in Steppingley over four decades. He is a lifelong Steppingley man — christened there, married there, raising children there, and appearing in three consecutive censuses there. He died in January 1868 in Ampthill (the registration district for Steppingley), aged approximately 70.

The two Williams (a first son christened 25 Dec 1829 who married in 1850, and a second son born ~1837) suggest the family may have used the same name twice — either because they wanted to honour a name that was important to them, or because the FamilySearch tree contains a merge error. Without primary baptism records for the 1837 William, this cannot be resolved.

## Significance for RB-K

The systematic search of the Steppingley Bunker families for the parent of **RB-K** (Ruth Bunker born ~1841, Steppingley, four censuses 1841–1871) rules out this family. The search now rests on Joseph's brother **William Bunker (1799)**, son of Thomas (1767) — FamilySearch L2TY-2KZ — who remains entirely unresearched.
