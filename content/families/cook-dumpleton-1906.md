## Parents

[[thomas-cook-1882|Thomas Edward Cook]] (9 Apr 1882 – 3 Sep 1955) and [[alice-dumpleton-1883|Alice Mabel Dumpleton]] (1883 – 13 Nov 1940) married in 1906. Thomas was born at Harpenden, Hertfordshire; Alice's origins are not documented but the Dumpleton family appears in the Studham/Kensworth churchyard records across multiple generations, placing her family in the same immediate area where they settled.

## Family Life

Thomas and Alice lived and died in Kensworth, Bedfordshire. Alice predeceased Thomas by fifteen years, dying in November 1940. Thomas lived until September 1955. Both are buried in the Kensworth churchyard.

Their settlement at Kensworth connects to the deeper roots of Thomas's probable family line: his grandfather [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] had married Elizabeth Frost at Kensworth in 1842, and the Cook family's association with the Kensworth/Whipsnade area runs back further still through [[daniel-cook-1790|Daniel Cook]] and [[mary-birchmore-1785|Mary Birchmore]].
