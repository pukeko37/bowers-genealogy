## Parents

[[thomas-bunker-1779|Thomas Bunker]] (1779–1849) and **Mary Burton** (born 1785) — marriage date not established; probably around 1810–1815, given that their known son Samuel was born ~1818 in Steppingley.

Thomas was the son of [[james-bunker-1749|James Bunker (1749)]] and Elizabeth Pedder, and grandson of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and Mary Reade. The family's roots were in Millbrook, but by Samuel's birth (~1818) Thomas had moved to Steppingley, likely drawn by the established Bunker presence there from the William (1740) line.
