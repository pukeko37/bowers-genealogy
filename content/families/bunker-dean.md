## Parents

[[samuel-bunker-1842|Samuel Bunker (1842–1913)]] and [[harriet-dean-1844|Harriet Dean (1844–1931)]] married on **25 December 1865** at Millbrook — Christmas Day, when Samuel was 23 and Harriet was 21. They lived their married life in Millbrook on Millbrook Road. Samuel died in 1913; Harriet outlived him by 18 years, dying in 1931.

## Samuel as clerk and sexton

Samuel's obituary (*Bedfordshire Times*, 20 June 1913) records that "the deceased had been for about 20 years clerk and sexton at the parish church, until the present Rector came to Millbrook. His father also [served in the same capacity]." He was buried in the churchyard where he had worked.

Harriet is therefore **Mrs Bunker of the 1919 Huett effigy story**. Samuel had encountered the buried Tudor effigies while digging in the churchyard — kept a stone head — and after his death Harriet preserved it and remembered the location. In 1919, aged approximately 75, she led excavators to the spot.
