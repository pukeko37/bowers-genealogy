## Parents

[[mark-gillet-1823|Mark Gillet]] (1823–1895) and [[elizabeth-west-bunker-1824|Elizabeth West Bunker]] (1824–1878) married on **14 April 1845 at Marston Moretaine**, Bedfordshire. Elizabeth was the eldest child of [[john-bunker-1803|John Bunker (1803)]] and Maria West. Mark's origins are not yet researched.

## Family Life

After the Marston Moretaine marriage in April 1845, the couple appear in the 1851 census at Marston Moretaine, having begun their family there. By 1861 they had moved to Maulden, Bedfordshire, where they remain in the 1871 census. Elizabeth died in March 1878 in Bedfordshire — at Maulden or nearby. Mark survived her by seventeen years.

Elizabeth's settlement pattern is telling: she moved only a few miles from Millbrook, staying within the tight cluster of villages — Marston Moretaine, Maulden, Millbrook — that formed the Bunker family's geographic world. She could have visited her father [[john-bunker-1803|John (1803–1889)]] in Millbrook with relative ease throughout her life.
