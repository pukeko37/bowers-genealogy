## Parents

[[thomas-bunker-1738|Thomas Bunker]] (christened 4 Jun 1738 Millbrook; buried 14 Dec 1825 Toddington) and [[martha-sinfield|Martha Sinfield]] (dates unknown) married on 6 May 1761 at Millbrook, Bedfordshire. The marriage record spells Martha's surname "Sinfeild or Sinfild."

Thomas was the son of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker]] (1704–1784) and [[mary-reade-1702|Mary Reade]] (1702–1775). He was 23 years old at marriage. Martha's origins are unknown.

## Children

Only two children are documented in FamilySearch:

1. **Edward Bunker** — christened 24 January 1762, Millbrook (father "Thomas Bunker", mother "Martha")
2. **[[james-thomas-bunker-1764|James Thomas Bunker]]** — born 17 April 1764; christened 7 April 1765, Millbrook (father "Thomas Bunker", mother "Martha")

James Thomas is the direct ancestor of Roland Bunker. Whether Edward or any other children had descendants is not yet researched.

## Family Life

Thomas and Martha settled in Millbrook, continuing the family's three-generation presence in the village. Thomas was christened in Millbrook in 1738 and his own children were christened there in 1762 and 1765. He outlived both his wife (date unknown) and his son James Thomas (died 1824), reaching age 87 before his burial at Toddington in 1825.

The burial at Toddington — not Millbrook — is notable. His parents had married at Toddington in 1728, so the family had longstanding connections there.
