## Parents

[[george-birchmore-1786|George Birchmore]] (1786–1838) and Ann Norton (1797–1866), married 6 April 1815 at Flamstead, Hertfordshire — two years after George's illegitimate son [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]] was born with Elizabeth Plummer.

## Children

1. George Burchmore (1817–22 Aug 1866) — exact death date confirmed by St Leonard churchyard listing
2. William Burchmore (1819–1916)
3. John Norton Burchmore (1821–1873)
4. Anne Burchmore (1832–Deceased)

## Family Life

George and Ann lived at Roe End Farm, Flamstead. George died there in 1838; Ann survived him by 28 years, dying in 1866 — the same year as Elizabeth Plummer. The family remained in Flamstead; William Burchmore (1819–1916) lived to 97.

The four children of this marriage are [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]]'s half-siblings through their shared father. No documented contact between GBP Sr and his Flamstead half-siblings has been found. GBP Sr grew up in the Whipsnade/Studham area of Bedfordshire, a county away.

## Open Questions

- Did any of the four Flamstead Birchmores know of or have contact with GBP Sr?
- William Burchmore (1819–1916) lived to 97 — any records of his life in Flamstead? Curiously, he does not appear in the St Leonard Find a Grave listing, suggesting he may have been buried elsewhere.
- The St Leonard listing also contains Charlotte Burchmore (1805–1885) and Ann Burchmore (1808–1853). Both predate the 1815 Norton marriage and so cannot be children of this family. Their parentage is unclear — possibly children of William Burchmore (1791–1856), a probable sibling of George (1786). See [[birchmore-redding-1784]].
