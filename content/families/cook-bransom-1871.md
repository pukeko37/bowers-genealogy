## Parents

[[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] (1850–1928) and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]] (1848–1937) married in 1871. Both were lifelong Whipsnade residents. Mary was the middle illegitimate child of [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] and thus the sister of [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]].

Edwin was born to [[jeremiah-bransom-1808|Jeremiah Bransom]] and [[sarah-watts-1816|Sarah Watts]] of Whipsnade and Edlesborough — see [[bransom-watts-1833]]. His sister [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois Brandsom]] (1843–1924) married [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook (1843–1908)]] — see [[cook-brandsom-1868]] — so the Bransom family was doubly connected to the Whipsnade Cook families, through two separate marriages.

Edwin appears in the Whipsnade census in every recorded year from 1851 through 1911. Mary similarly never left the village — she is recorded "By Whipsnade Green" through 1911, then moved to Luton where she died in 1937 aged approximately 89.

## Children

All seven children were christened at the Wesleyan Methodist chapel in Dunstable:

1. [[albert-bransom-1871|Albert Bransom]] (1871–1940) — christened 8 October 1871
2. [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]] (1873–1963) — born 20 September, christened 5 November 1873
3. [[ebenezer-bransom-1875|Ebenezer Bransom]] (1875–1965) — born 31 August, christened 25 October 1875
4. [[clara-lucy-bransom-1877|Clara Lucy Bransom]] (1877–1957) — born 14 September, christened 2 December 1877
5. [[margaret-anne-bransom-1880|Margaret Anne Bransom]] (1880–1964) — married [[james-hain-1877|James Hain]], farmer at Dell Farm, Whipsnade, December 1905; two daughters
6. [[walter-william-bransom-1881|Walter William Bransom]] (1881–1963) — born 26 November 1881, christened 5 February 1882
7. [[minnie-rose-bransom-1884|Minnie Rose Bransom]] (1884–1960) — born 24 January, christened 13 April 1884

## Family Life

The family lived in Whipsnade throughout. By 1901 Edwin and Mary were alone, all children having left home. The 1911 census shows them with their daughter Florence (by then "Florence Perry", age 37) and Florence's daughter Florence Hilda Perry (age 8), and a young Dora Minne Hain (age 0). Edwin died in 1928; Mary outlived him by nearly a decade, dying in 1937 (death registered in the Luton district).

## Significance

Florence Bransom (Mary and Edwin's daughter) was one of the two witnesses at the 1894 marriage of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] and [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] at Whipsnade — Lucy's niece, then aged about 20. The other witness, [[walter-cook-1869|Walter Cook]], was the son of Edwin's sister [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois Brandsom]] — so Florence and Walter were first cousins.
