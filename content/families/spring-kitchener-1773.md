## Parents

[[william-spring-1753|William Spring]] (born 1753, Shillington, Bedfordshire) and [[sarah-kitchener-1752|Sarah Kitchener]] (born 3 December 1752, Shillington, Bedfordshire) married on 11 November 1773 at Shillington — neighbours of the same parish, born just one year apart. They are Rhoda Spring Bottoms's paternal grandparents and Eliza Geary's great-great-great-grandparents.

Sarah Kitchener was the daughter of **William Kitchener** (1721) and **Elizabeth Gudgeon** (1727–1764) of Shillington, who married in 1746. Elizabeth Gudgeon died in 1764, when Sarah was about twelve. The Kitcheners were a Shillington family; this is the current ancestral limit on this line.

William Spring (1753) has no parents recorded in the FamilySearch tree. He is the earliest established Spring ancestor.

## Family Life

The Spring family was firmly rooted in Shillington, a village in the north of Bedfordshire (near Hitchin, Hertfordshire). The family's second generation (William Spring 1779) moved south to the Barton-le-Clay/Shillington area and eventually settled near Bedford. The Kitchener ancestry from Shillington represents Bedfordshire rural stock across at least two generations.
