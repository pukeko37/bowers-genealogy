## Parents

[[james-bunker-1873|James Bunker (1873–1945)]] married **Julia Carter** (born 1871) on 17 January 1893 at South Normanton, Derbyshire. James was nineteen. He had already made his way north from Millbrook to the coalfields, presumably in the late 1880s, and was sufficiently settled to marry locally.

Albert Edward Bunker (1897–1916) was killed in the First World War. Alice May Bunker (1902–1996) lived to ninety-four.

## Family life

James and Julia lived in South Normanton and the surrounding Nottinghamshire coalfield. James's recorded occupation in 1895 was Collier — the only member of the Bunker siblings explicitly documented as a miner. His brothers in the same area almost certainly worked in the same industry.

By 1911 James was in Nottinghamshire; by 1939 he had settled at Blackwell, Derbyshire. He was buried in Derbyshire on 15 March 1945, aged approximately seventy-one — by far the longest-lived of the Bunker brothers.
