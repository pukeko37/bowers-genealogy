## Parents

[[william-thody-1774|William Thody]] (abt 1774 – 1858) married **Alice Gardner** on **27 January 1804** at **St Clement's, Hastings** — the garrison church for the regiment then billeted in Hastings. The register describes William as a bachelor. Both parties signed with marks.

Nothing further is known about Alice Gardner — her origins, parentage, and age at marriage are unrecorded.

## Children

One child is documented:

- **Elizabeth Thody** — baptised at St Clement's, Hastings, in October 1804, approximately nine months after the marriage

No person page has been created for Elizabeth Thody. Her subsequent fate is unknown.

## The End of this Family

Alice presumably died before 1809. By that year the regiment was deep in the Peninsular War in Spain and Portugal, and William had married a second time — a widow named Elizabeth (formerly Meyers) at Badajoz. There is no death record for Alice; her death, wherever it occurred, has not been found. If she followed the regiment to the Continent (as soldiers' wives sometimes did), she may have died abroad. If she remained in England, she died without leaving a trace in available records.

The daughter Elizabeth Thody, born in 1804, would have been left behind in any case — infants and young children of soldiers rarely accompanied campaigns. Her fate is entirely unknown.
