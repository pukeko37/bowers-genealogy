## Parents

[[peter-matthews-1665|Peter Matthews (1665–1730)]] was born at Bedford, Bedfordshire, and died at Dunstable in 1730. He married **[[mary-jenkins-1665|Mary Jenkins]]** (1665–1748) at an unknown date and place. Mary outlived him by eighteen years.

Their own parentage is undocumented — this is the earliest known generation on the Peter Matthews line.

## Children

Seven children, all christened at Dunstable, Bedfordshire:

1. **[[peter-matthews-1702|Peter Matthews]]** (~1702–1748) — continued the line; married Mary Busby 1727
2. **Mary Matthews** (christened 9 Feb 1703, Dunstable)
3. **Martha Matthews** (christened 17 Nov 1704, Dunstable)
4. **Edward Matthews** (~1709)
5. **Rebecca Matthews** (~1712)
6. **Rosanna Matthews** (~1715)
7. **Michael Matthews** (~1718)

## Family Life

The family was rooted at Dunstable, Bedfordshire in the late 17th and early 18th century. Their grandson [[peter-matthews-1734|Peter Matthews (1734)]] eventually settled at Kensworth, a few miles north, where the family remained for several generations before [[peter-matthews-1770|Peter Matthews (1770)]] crossed into Whipsnade.
