## Parents

[[thomas-batchelar-1803|Thomas Batchelar]] (1803–1872), born Dagnall, Buckinghamshire, was the son of [[david-batchelor-unknown|David Batchelor]] and Ann. He married [[susan-matthews-1800|Susan Matthews]] (1800–1857) on 22 May 1826 at **Shenley, Hertfordshire** — a parish a few miles from Dagnall, Thomas's Buckinghamshire home ground. Susan was the daughter of [[peter-matthews-1770|Peter Matthews]] and [[mary-walker-1766|Mary Walker]] of Whipsnade.

Thomas was an agricultural labourer — described as "Ag Lab" in the 1851 and 1861 censuses, and by the time of his probate as a **Shepherd**, the more specific pastoral role that would have been common on the Whipsnade chalk downland. Susan, in the 1851 census, is listed as **Grocer** — an unusual occupation for a married woman and an indication of independent enterprise within the household.

## Children

Four children, all christened at Whipsnade:

1. **Emma Batchelor** (christened 8 April 1827) — married Edward Potton at Whipsnade, 19 March 1857
2. **[[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]]** (christened 6 December 1829; died 1867) — married [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]] 1859; mother of [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen]] and [[elizabeth-birchmore-1862|Elizabeth Birchmore]]
3. **Ann Batchelar** (christened 22 December 1833) — married George Gadsden at Whipsnade, 1 November 1858
4. **Elizabeth Batchelar** (christened 24 July 1842; died 1916) — married John Room at Ivinghoe, 9 April 1864

## Family Life

The family lived in Whipsnade throughout. Census records place them at **Beech Bucket Wood** (1841), in the hamlet area at the edge of the parish, and later **By Whipsnade Green** (1861). Thomas appears in every census from 1841 to 1871.

**1841 census:** Thomas (35), Susan (40), Emma (14), Eliza (11), Ann (7). Elizabeth had not yet been born.

**1851 census:** Thomas (48), Susan (50, Grocer), Eliza (21), Ann (17), Elizabeth (8). Emma had left the household.

**1861 census:** Thomas (60) at By Whipsnade Green — his household now included his son-in-law [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]] (45), daughter Eliza Birchmore (31), and grandchildren George Birchmore Jr (11) and Ellen Birchmore (1). The young family had moved in after George and Eliza's 1859 marriage.

Susan died on 20 November 1857, aged 57. The 1858 and 1859 marriages of Ann and Eliza both required Thomas as a named father in the register — he was an active presence at both ceremonies.

Thomas subsequently remarried: on 11 January 1868 at Whipsnade he wed **[[susan-maynard-1802|Susan Maynard]]** (aged 67). Her maiden name was Maynard (father George Maynard, mother Sarah Rumball, both from Luton); "Durent" was the surname from her second marriage, to Edmund Durrant of Whipsnade. She had in fact been married twice before Thomas: first to **William Woodcraft** at Whipsnade in 1827, widowed by 1835; then to **Edmund Durrant** at Whipsnade in 1849, widowed before 1868. In the 1851 census she is recorded as a **School Mistress** in Whipsnade — an unusual independent occupation for a woman in a village of this size. The 1871 census shows Thomas (69) and Susan (71, birthplace Luton, Bedfordshire) still in Whipsnade. Thomas died 22 December 1872 at Whipsnade; his estate (under £200) passed by will to executor Henry Perkins, Whipsnade farmer — the tenant of [[dell-farm|Dell Farm]] from 1851 to 1871, a near-neighbour on the village green.
