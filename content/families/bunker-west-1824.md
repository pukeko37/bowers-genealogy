## Parents

[[john-bunker-1803|John Bunker (1803–1889)]] was the son of [[james-bunker-1777|James Bunker (1777)]] by his first wife Sarah Pedder — the eldest documented surviving son of that line. He married **Maria West** (born 1806) on 15 August 1824 at Ampthill. Maria was from the Ampthill district; her family has not been traced.

John spent his life in Millbrook. He appears in the 1861 census with his own household near his elderly father James (who died 1864). He died in 1889, aged approximately 86.

## In the censuses

**1841** (HO107/6, Village Millbrook): John (35), Maria (35), Sarah (15), John (11), James (8), Hannah (5), Joseph (1 — the future [[joseph-bunker-1840]]), Eliza Blunt (20, not a family member — possibly servant; Blunt surname also connected to Catherine Blunt, James 1777's third wife).

**1851** (HO107/1754/425, Millbrook): John (47, Ag Lab, Millbrook-born), Maria (48, Ampthill-born), John son (21), Joseph son (11), Samuel son (8, Scholar), George son (6). A lodger John Gillet (5, Marston) also present.

**1861** (RG09/1001/48, Millbrook Road): Still at Millbrook Road; Samuel (18, Agricultural Labourer) in household.

## Family life

John and Maria's household was the continuation of the Bunker presence in Millbrook beyond James (1777)'s generation. Where James lived into extreme old age (died 1864 at ~87), John followed the same pattern, dying in 1889 at ~86. The Millbrook Bunkers were extraordinarily long-lived on the male line through these two generations.

The family is distinguished by its rootedness. While the cousins from James Bunker (1807)'s line — George, David, Charles, Caleb, and the others — left Millbrook by 1852, John's son Joseph (1840) never left. He was in the village at every census from 1841 to 1911.
