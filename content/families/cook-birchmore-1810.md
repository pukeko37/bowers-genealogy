## Parents

[[daniel-cook-1790|Daniel Cook]] (abt 1790–1857) was born and raised in Whipsnade, an agricultural labourer. [[mary-birchmore-1785|Mary Birchmore]] (1785–?) was also from Whipsnade, daughter of William Birchmore (1751) and Mary Simonds. They married on 12 October 1810 and lived their lives in Whipsnade.

## Children

Seven children are recorded:

1. [[sarah-cook-1811|Sarah Cook]] (1811)
2. [[james-cook-1813|James Cook]] (1813)
3. [[george-cook-1815|George Cook]] (1815)
4. [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] (1816–1900) — first married Elizabeth Frost (1842); then married [[charlotte-matthews-1823|Charlotte Matthews]] (1856); see [[cook-frost-1842]] and [[cook-matthews-whipsnade]]; father of [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook (1843–1908)]], who held the Chequers Inn licence 1904–1908
5. [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] (1820–1904) — married [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]] 1867; held Chequers Inn licence until death; see [[birchmore-plummer-cooke-1867]]
6. [[jesse-cook-1821|Jesse Cook]] (1821) — married Phoebe Holland 1843; Kensworth
7. [[lois-cook-1827|Lois Cook]] (1827–1903) — married Henry Harrowell 1849; School Mistress 1851

## Family Life

Daniel died in 1857 in Flamstead, Hertfordshire. Mary's death date is unknown. Ruth, born 1820, had three illegitimate children before her 1867 marriage to GBP Sr; she was evidently in Whipsnade through all of this — every census 1851–1901 places her there.

## The Birchmore Connection

Mary Birchmore came from the Birchmore family of Whipsnade, which had roots in the village going back at least to her father William Birchmore (1751). George Birchmore Plummer Sr's father was named George Birchmore — a man of a generation that could have been among William Birchmore's children. If so, GBP Sr and Ruth Cooke were second cousins through the Birchmore line before their 1867 marriage. This would make Ruth's marriage to GBP Sr a union of two lines that were both rooted in the Whipsnade Birchmore family. Not confirmed, but consistent with the tight community networks visible throughout this research.
