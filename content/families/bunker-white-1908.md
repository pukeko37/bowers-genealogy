## Parents

[[annie-may-bunker-1888|Annie May Bunker (1888–1929)]] married **Albert White** (born 1884) in July 1908 at Ampthill. Annie May was about twenty; Albert would have been about twenty-four.

## Children

No children are documented.

## Family life

By 1911 Annie May and Albert were living at Marston Moretaine, Bedfordshire — about two miles north-east of Millbrook, staying close to the family's home area despite Annie May's earlier time in Luton.

Annie May died in 1929 at Ampthill, aged approximately forty or forty-one. Albert White survived her, dying in 1946. The couple had no recorded children.
