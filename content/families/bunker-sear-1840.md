## Parents

[[samuel-bunker-1818|Samuel Bunker]] (~1818, Steppingley) and **Hannah Sear** (~1820, Woburn/Water End, Bedfordshire) married in **January 1840** at an unestablished location. Hannah's maiden name Sear is given by the user; her birthplace in the census varies between Woburn (1851) and Water End (1861), both of which are in the same area of south Bedfordshire.

Their first child [[ruth-bunker-1840|Ruth]] was born 1 August 1840 — seven months after the marriage. Pre-marital conception.

## The Westoning births

The first six children were born in Westoning, although the family lived in Steppingley (confirmed by the 1841 census address of "Cutlers, Steppingley"). Hannah likely returned to her home area around Woburn/Water End for each confinement. After ~1851, the younger children (Susan, Mary, Sarah, Edward 2nd) were born in Steppingley — perhaps Hannah stopped making the journey as the family grew.

## Family life

Samuel was an agricultural labourer throughout, appearing at Steppingley in the 1841, 1851, 1861, and 1871 censuses. The household addresses are Cutlers (1841), and Peaks End (1861). By 1881 Samuel (62) had moved with Ruth and the younger Edward to **Little Munden, Hertfordshire**, a migration not yet explained.

Ruth worked in the straw trade — Straw Plaiter in 1861 and 1871, Hat Sewer (Straw) in 1881. This was the dominant cottage industry for Bedfordshire women in this period.

Elizabeth Dolton (age 0, Steppingley, 1861) appears in the household but is not a Bunker. Her relationship is unexplained — possibly a grandchild, a servant's infant, or a lodger's child.
