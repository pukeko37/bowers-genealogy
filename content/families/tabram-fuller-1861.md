## Parents

**[[john-tabram-1861|John Tabram]]** (1861–1939) was born in Fenstanton, Huntingdonshire, the son of [[john-tabram-1836|John Tabram (1836)]] and Elizabeth Lewis. He was baptised in a bulk baptism in April 1861 along with his older siblings. He spent his entire life in or close to Fenstanton, following the same pattern of unbroken parish attachment as his father. His aunt [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann Tabram]] had married [[thomas-norman-1827|Thomas Norman]] of Swavesey in 1850, making [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] John's first cousin.

**[[adeliza-fuller-1862|Adeliza Fuller]]** (1862–1938) was born in **Hemingford Abbots, Huntingdonshire** — a small parish immediately adjacent to Fenstanton, which explains how she and John came to meet. Her parents were **Thomas Fuller** and **Rachael Fuller**, confirmed by her baptism record; her siblings were Albert R. Fuller and Cuthbert T. Fuller (1871 census). See [[fuller-fuller-hemingford]] for her family of origin. "Adeliza" is a medieval name (a variant of Adelaide) that enjoyed a Victorian revival. Her maiden surname Fuller was preserved in her daughter Ethel's middle name — a common practice for carrying forward the mother's family name.

They married in **December 1879**, registered in the St Ives registration district, Huntingdonshire. The marriage almost certainly took place in Fenstanton — St Ives was the district registry office for the area, not the marriage venue. John was 18; Adeliza was 17. Their first child was born in 1881.

## Children

Three children, all born in Fenstanton:

1. **Ellen Rachel Tabram** (1881–1882) — died in infancy
2. **Ethel Fuller Tabram** (1883–)  — married a Lomas; middle name "Fuller" from Adeliza's maiden name
3. **Mabel Sarah Tabram** (1885–)

No person pages have been created for the children.

## Family Life

The family lived in Fenstanton throughout John and Adeliza's working lives — they appear there in every census from 1881 to 1911. At some point after 1911 they moved to **1 Southoe, Buckden**, a village about ten miles south-west, likely to be near their daughter Ethel Lomas in her later years.

Both died at Buckden in close succession: Adeliza on 8 February 1938 (probate to Ethel Lomas, effects £609 15s. 8d.) and John on 7 February 1939 — almost exactly one year later, at the same address (probate to Ethel Fuller Lomas, effects £506 12s. 4d.).

## Connection to the Norman Family

John's aunt [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann Tabram]] (his father's sister) married [[thomas-norman-1827|Thomas Norman]] of Swavasey in 1850. Their son was [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] — making Charles John's first cousin. This is the link that connects the Tabram family of Fenstanton to the Norman family of Swavesey and eventually London.
