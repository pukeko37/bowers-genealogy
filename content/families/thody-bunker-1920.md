## Parents

[[harry-thody-1894|Harry Thody]] (1894–1986) and [[beulah-bunker-1896|Beulah Rebecca Lucy Bunker]] (1896–1948) married on **2 August 1920** — Beulah's 24th birthday — at the Parish Church, **Little Gaddesden, Hertfordshire**, by licence. The marriage certificate gives Harry's address as Little Gaddesden and Beulah's as 50 Hampton Road, Luton. Harry's father (William Allan Thody, Policeman) was already deceased. Beulah's father Frederick William Tall Bunker was listed as a Bricklayer. The witnesses were **[[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|R. H. Bunker]]** (Roland, Beulah's brother) and **[[norah-winifred-norman-1902|N. W. Norman]]** — [[norah-winifred-norman-1902|Norah Winifred Norman]], the youngest sister of [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy Norman]], then 18 years old. The rector was Thomas Syondze.

The two witnesses together represent the two families about to be joined. That Ivy's youngest sister stood at this wedding in August 1920 — nearly a year before Roland and Ivy married — shows the Norman and Thody families were already closely bound before the second wedding made it official.

Harry had returned from WW1 — he volunteered in September 1914 aged 20, served with the Bedfordshire Regiment and Royal Army Service Corps driving supply wagons to the front through the Somme, Arras, Passchendaele, Lens, and Cambrai, was wounded, participated in the Advance into Germany, and demobilised in February 1919. Beulah was the daughter of [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick Bunker]] and [[eliza-geary-1859|Eliza Geary]].

Less than a year after this wedding, Beulah's brother [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland]] married [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy Norman]]. The connection between Harry and the Norman family clearly preceded that marriage.

## Family Life

Harry and Beulah lived in [[luton|Luton]]. Before the partnership, Harry was trading independently from **35 Dunstable Road, Luton** — confirmed by a photograph of the van (c. 1928–29, dated by the children present): *H. THODY, 35 Dunstable Rd, Luton; Upholstery, Bedding, Curtains, Loose Covers; REMOVALS; phone 1342*. After Roland left the Navy in 1930, Harry and Roland started **Thody Bros. Ltd** — incorporated as a limited company. Harry ran the removals and upholstery side; Roland ran the furniture shop at the corner of Dunstable Road and Oak Road, Luton. A photograph of one of the new pantechnicon vans — published by **H G Creasey & Sons** of Knebworth, Hertfordshire as an example of their coachbuilding work — shows the livery: *THODY BROS. Ltd REMOVALS / REMOVALS & STORAGE / THODY BROS. Ltd. HOUSE FURNISHERS, Dunstable Road Luton*. The Kensworth telephone number visible in the photograph is Creasey's own contact number, not Thody Bros's. The storage offering was backed by a lockup yard and storage building at the far end of Oak Road, near Luton Town FC's Kenilworth Road ground. The business was styled "Bros" though they were brothers-in-law, not brothers. Harry was still its company director in 1968; Thody Bros was wound up in 1970, at which point the yard was sold to Luton Town FC, who converted it into a gymnasium. It has since been demolished as the stadium expanded.

By 1939, Harry and Beulah were living at **"Carnoy," 9 Oakley Road, Luton** — the house named after Carnoy on the Somme, where Harry had served in the Great War. Beulah's mother [[eliza-geary-1859|Eliza Geary]] (then 80) shared the household with them, along with their three children (redacted in the 1939 register as they were still living). Harry's occupation is recorded as "Furniture Dealer & Manufacturer"; Beulah's as "Unpaid Domestic Duties."

Beulah died suddenly on **29 June 1948**, aged 51, from an **asthma attack**. [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland]] and [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy]] were on holiday in Killarney, Ireland; they had to cut the trip short and return to Luton on receiving the news. Beulah was buried at Luton General Cemetery on 3 July 1948.

Harry later married **Nellie Walker** (née Haze, born ~1906, died 1989) on 6 July 1968 at St James' Church, Silsoe, Bedfordshire. No children from this second marriage. Harry lived to 92, dying on 29 September 1986 at Silsoe.

## Wider Family

- **Harry's parents:** [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] (1850–1911) & [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] (1856–1930). See [[thody-cook-1894]].
- **Beulah's parents:** [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick Bunker]] & [[eliza-geary-1859|Eliza Geary]]. See [[bunker-geary-1892]].
- **The Thody-Norman-Bunker connection:** Lucy Cook was step-sister to Ellen Birchmore (Ivy's mother). Lucy's son Harry befriended Beulah's brother Roland. Harry married Beulah, Roland married Ivy. Two families joined through a step-sisterhood that reached back to George Birchmore Plummer's third marriage in 1867.
