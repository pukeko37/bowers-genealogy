## Parents

[[robert-norman-1769|Robert Norman]] (February 1769–17 June 1842) was born in Over, Cambridgeshire and spent his whole life there. [[frances-green-1769|Frances Green]] (28 May 1769–1810) was born in Swavesey and died in Over. Both were born in the same year, in adjacent villages.

They married on 16 November 1788 at Swavesey, Cambridgeshire — Robert was 19, Frances was also 19. Their first child Alice (b. ~1787) appears to have been born before the marriage, suggesting either a pre-marital conception or a slight imprecision in the birth record.

## Children

Seven children are documented:

1. **Alice Norman** (~1787–?) — born before the parents' marriage; possibly the Alice Norman (F, ~50) in Robert's 1841 household at Church End, Over
2. **Mary Norman** (1790–?) — no further record held
3. **[[thomas-norman-1793|Thomas Norman]]** (27 May 1793–19 December 1855) — agricultural labourer, Swavesey; married [[elizabeth-fear-1794|Elizabeth Fear]] 1815; see [[norman-fear-1815]]
4. **Elizabeth Norman** (1795–1877) — no further record held
5. **James Norman** (1801–1872) — no further record held
6. **Luke Norman** (1803–1878) — no further record held
7. **Richard Norman** (1805–1890) — no further record held

Only Thomas is currently linked to subsequent wiki pages. The other six siblings represent potential Norman branches in the Over/Swavesey area that have not yet been researched.

## Family Life

Both Robert and Frances were native to the Over/Swavesey cluster of villages — a tightly connected agricultural community on the flat fen-edge of South Cambridgeshire. Marriage between families from adjacent villages was typical; Frances's parents were from Swavesey, Robert's from Over, and the two parishes are separated by only a few miles.

Robert was a farmer's labourer or agricultural worker, as his son and grandson would be. The family remained in Over through Robert's entire life.

Frances died in 1810, aged about 41 — Richard, the youngest, was about five. Robert did not remarry and lived another thirty-two years as a widower, dying at about 73. The 1841 census shows him at Church End, Over, with what is likely his eldest daughter Alice, then in her mid-fifties.

## Frances's name

Frances's surname is recorded inconsistently: "Marks" or "Mann" at birth (from her father Francis Mann/Marks), and "Green or Mann" at marriage — apparently borrowing her mother's maiden name Green. The name complexity may reflect the family's own usage. FamilySearch catalogues her as "Frances Green."

## Wider family

Frances's parents were Francis Mann (1746–1819) and Elizabeth Green (1745–1824). Robert's parents are not yet identified — his family of origin represents an open research question and the current root of the Norman line in the wiki.
