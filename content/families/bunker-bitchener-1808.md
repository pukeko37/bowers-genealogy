## Parents

[[william-bunker-1776|William Bunker (1776–1855)]] married **Hannah Bitchener** (1782–Deceased, FamilySearch KH6V-LJ2) on 4 May 1808 at Steppingley — about a year after his first wife Elisabeth Burridge died. Hannah's origins and death date are not documented.

**Judith (1807):** FamilySearch lists her under Hannah's relationship, but she was born a year before William and Hannah married. She may be the posthumous child of first wife Elisabeth Burridge (who died 1807), mis-attributed by FamilySearch to the second marriage. Alternatively, she was conceived before the formal 1808 marriage. Without the baptism transcript, this cannot be resolved.

**Implied children ~1808–1810:** William's sources page includes attached records dated 1808 and 1810 that were not fully transcribed in the clipping. These are additional parish register entries that presumably record baptisms or burials of children not in the print view — consistent with the family's established pattern of infant deaths (both children from the first marriage died young).

**Philadelphia:** The name Philadelphia is unusual and deliberately chosen — possibly honouring a place, a Quaker connection, or a family association not yet identified.

**1846 marriage source:** A record dated 1846 (England Marriages) is attached to William — almost certainly the marriage of **Judith Bunker (~1807)**, who would be approximately 39 at the time.

## Family life

William and Hannah raised their surviving children in Steppingley over a long marriage. William appeared in the 1841 and 1851 censuses at Steppingley; by 1861 he had moved to Dunstable Street, Ampthill. No Ruth appears among the documented children.
