## Parents

[[peter-matthews-1734|Peter Matthews (1734–1812)]] was the son of [[peter-matthews-1702|Peter Matthews (1702)]] and [[mary-busby-1709|Mary Busby]], christened at Dunstable but born at Kensworth. He married **Susannah Durrant** (1739–1821) on **12 October 1761 at Flamstead, Hertfordshire** — a significant detail: Flamstead is the same village associated with the Birchmore and Batchelor families who later became intertwined with the Matthews descendants. No source records how Peter (1734) and Susannah Durrant came to marry there; Flamstead may have been her home parish.

Susannah Durrant's own origins have not been researched beyond these facts.

## Children

Three children confirmed, all christened at Kensworth, Hertfordshire:

1. **Susannah Matthews** — christened 30 November 1763, Kensworth
2. **Mary Matthews** — christened 8 February 1767, Kensworth
3. **[[peter-matthews-1770|Peter Matthews]]** — christened 7 January 1770, Kensworth

A possible further child: "Susannah Matthews" baptised 1 September 1776 at Upper Gravenhurst, Bedfordshire, with parents Peter Matthews and Susannah Matthews. If genuine, this would be a second Susannah (perhaps the 1763 daughter had died and the name was reused). Upper Gravenhurst is ~10 miles from Kensworth. The source is plausible but uncertain; treat as unconfirmed.

## Family Life

Peter (1734) settled at Kensworth, where he died in 1812 aged about 78. The family had migrated from the Dunstable parish of his father and grandfather to the Kensworth/Flamstead orbit — a move of only a few miles, but a parish boundary crossing that would shape the next generation. His son [[peter-matthews-1770|Peter Matthews (1770)]] took the family one step further, crossing into Bedfordshire to marry [[mary-walker-1766|Mary Walker]] at Whipsnade in 1798.

## Open Questions

- Where was Susannah Durrant from? Flamstead is the most likely answer, but not confirmed.
- What became of Susannah Matthews (1763) and Mary Matthews (1767)?
- Is the 1776 Upper Gravenhurst baptism a genuine second Susannah, or a misattachment?
