## Parents

[[john-james-mason-1878|John James Mason]] (Jack, 1878–1962) and [[mabel-annie-norman-1883|Mabel Annie Norman]] (1883–1969) married in 1912 in Hackney. He was 34, she was 29. This was the first of the double Mason-Norman marriages — John's brother Isaac married Mabel's sister Alice six years later.

John was one of three orphaned Mason brothers who had grown up at 27 Hosier Lane, running their parents' milk shop after both parents died. See [[mason-james-1874]].

## Children

1. John Isaac Mason (1913–1984)
2. Catherine Ellen Mason (1916–1970)

## Family Life

John and Mabel ran a newsagent and stationer's shop at 150 Acre Lane, Clapham. Mabel's sister [[nellie-norman-1885|Nellie]] lived with them and worked as a shop assistant. In 1939, the household also included John's elder brother Richard William Mason (1875–1948) and his wife Martha.

In 1944–45, the shop at 150 Acre Lane was damaged by V-weapons (V1 flying bombs). John, Mabel, and Nellie fled to 32 Park Hill, Harpenden, taking refuge with [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy]] and [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland]]. After the war, they rebuilt and reopened the shop in a changing Brixton area.

John died in 1962, aged 84. Mabel died in 1969 in Camberwell, aged 86.
