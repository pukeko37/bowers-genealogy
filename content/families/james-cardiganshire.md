## Overview

This page records the family of origin of [[catherine-james-1852|Catherine James]] (1852–1900), who was born in **Llanfihangel Geneur Glynn**, Cardiganshire, Wales, and later emigrated to London where she ran a dairy at Hosier Lane, Smithfield. The parents and wider family have not been identified in the current sources; no Welsh research has been undertaken.

## Known Members

- **Thomas James** (~1835, groom, single) — Catherine's probable brother. In 1861 he was head of the household in Rhydypenne, Cynnull-mawr parish, Cardiganshire, with Catherine (age 9, scholar) in his household. He is listed as single and employed as a groom.
- **[[catherine-james-1852|Catherine James]]** (1852–1900) — married [[richard-mason-1850|Richard Mason]] in Aberystwyth on 5 May 1874; emigrated to London; ran a dairy at 27 Hosier Lane; died 1900; buried Abney Park Cemetery.
