## Parents

[[joseph-batchelor-1755|Joseph Batchelor]] was christened 11 January 1755 in Flamstead, Hertfordshire. His father was James Batchelor (1725), who had married Mary Field on 31 May 1748 in Leighton Buzzard — placing the Batchelor family at the Hertfordshire/Bedfordshire border from at least that date. Joseph married **Sarah Bingham** (born 1758) in Flamstead. The details of their marriage date and Sarah's origins are not documented beyond the FamilySearch print view.

## Children

Ten children, all born in Flamstead:

1. Elizabeth (1774)
2. Hannah (1776)
3. Lydia (1777)
4. James (1780)
5. [[thomas-bachelor-1781|Thomas Bachelor]] (1781) — settled Whipsnade; patriarch of the Whipsnade Bachelor family
6. Mary (1781) — same birth year as Thomas; possibly a recording error for one of them
7. Fanny (1783)
8. Ann (1785)
9. William (1787)
10. Samuel (1789–1872)

## Family Life

The family was rooted in Flamstead throughout Joseph's lifetime. Joseph's son [[thomas-bachelor-1781|Thomas Bachelor]] (1781) moved to Whipsnade in the early nineteenth century, settling at Frog Corner and marrying Alice Woodcroft there in 1816. Thomas became the patriarch of the Whipsnade Bachelor family — his son [[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham Batchelor]] was the probable Chequers Inn licensee from around 1845 until GBP Sr took over in 1867.

Joseph himself died at an unknown date; his death is not documented in the current sources.
