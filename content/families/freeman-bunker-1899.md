## Parents

**Edward Albert Freeman Sr.** (born 1877) married [[mary-eliza-bunker-1879|Mary Eliza Bunker (1879)]] at Ampthill in 1899. Mary Eliza was about twenty; the first child was born the same year.

## Children

Four children:

| # | Name | Born | Died |
|---|------|------|------|
| 1 | Dorothy Mary Freeman | 1899 | Deceased |
| 2 | John Henry Freeman | 1900 | Deceased |
| 3 | Nellie Freeman | 1903 | Deceased |
| 4 | Edward Albert Freeman Jr. | 1909 | 1909 |

Edward Albert Freeman Jr. (1909–1909) died in infancy.

## Family life

The family settled in Luton, where Mary Eliza is recorded in the 1901, 1911, and 1939 censuses — a forty-year presence in the town. Nothing further is known of Edward Albert Freeman Sr.
