## Parents

[[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] (1850–1911) and [[mathilde-parent-1853|Mathilde Elizabeth Parent]] (1853–1892) married 14 May 1873 in Chicago, Illinois. The ceremony was performed by Edward P. Godwin, Pastor of the First Congregational Church. William gave his name as William A. Thodey; Mathilde was called "Tillie E. Parent" in the certificate.

William had enlisted in the US Army in Detroit in May 1871 and been posted to Newport Barracks, Kentucky. He deserted on 3 July 1873 — seven weeks after the wedding. He appears never to have returned to the Army.

## Children

1. [[george-allen-thody-1874|George Allen Thody]] (1874–1958) — born in Canada (Sandwich, Ontario); known as "Allen" in the 1891 census
2. [[louis-albert-thody-1876|Louis Albert Thody]] (1876–1975) — born 8 December 1876, Cotton End, Bedfordshire; christened 13 February 1877 at Wesleyan Methodist, Toddington; later emigrated to Utah and California
3. [[clarence-james-thody-1884|Clarence James Thody]] (1884–1918) — Captain, Manchester Regiment 8th Bn.; born Houghton Regis, Bedfordshire; killed in action 30 August 1918, Riencourt-lès-Bapaume, France; buried Manchester Cemetery Plot A.11; former Master of Ardwick Green Industrial School, Manchester
4. [[lena-florence-thody-1889|Lena Florence Thody]] (1889–1927) — born Totternhoe, Bedfordshire; appears in 1901 census at Studham aged 11

## Family Life

The family led a transatlantic existence in its early years. After marrying in Chicago, William and Tillie spent time in Canada — the couple's eldest son George Allen was born in Sandwich, Ontario. By the end of 1876 the family was back in Bedfordshire, where Louis Albert was born at Cotton End and christened at Toddington.

At some point around 1879–1881, Tillie returned to Canada to care for her father, taking young Louis Albert with her. George Allen, then about six, stayed in Bedfordshire with William. The 1881 census shows William lodging at Sharnbrook as a Police Constable, listing himself as "widower" — a census error or informal shorthand for his separated state. During this period William fathered an illegitimate child, [[mary-ann-nash-1880|Mary Ann Nash]] (1880, Kentish Town, London), with [[matilda-nash-1858|Matilda Nash]].

Tillie returned from Canada sometime before 1884 (when Clarence was born at Houghton Regis), and the family was reunited. The 1891 census shows the complete household at Totternhoe: Tillie back as wife, George Allen and Albert (Louis) as teenage printers' apprentices, Clarence and Florence as young children, and "Minnie Thody" (Mary Ann Nash) listed as "niece [illegitimate daughter]."

Tillie died in 1892 at the age of approximately 39. William married [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] in February 1894. Clarence (16 in 1901) and Florence Lena (11 in 1901) remained with William and Lucy at Studham, while the older boys — George Allen and Louis Albert — had by then left home.

## Significance

This family is Harry Thody's half-sibling group. Clarence Archibald and Florence Lena were part of William and Lucy's Studham household when Harry was a small child. They are present as step-siblings in Harry's earliest years.

Louis Albert Thody (1876–1975) had an extraordinary lifespan of nearly a century, converted to the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in Utah in 1904, and married in Los Angeles in 1938.
