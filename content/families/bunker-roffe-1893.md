## Parents

[[john-bunker-1871|John Bunker (1871–1900)]] married **Ellen Mary Roffe** (born 1873) on 16 October 1893 at Millbrook. John was the only one of the elder Bunker brothers who remained in Millbrook through 1891 and married there.

Ellen Mary Roffe's family connection to the earlier Millbrook Rofes (who married into the Bunker line in 1764 via [[roffe-bunker-1764]]) is unestablished.

Two children:

Bertha Florence (1893–1983) lived to ninety. Albert Edward (1894–1917) was killed in the First World War.

## Family life

John and Ellen Mary lived in Millbrook. John died in 1900 at Ampthill — the registration district town — aged approximately 28 or 29. Ellen Mary was left a widow with two young children, the younger just six years old.
