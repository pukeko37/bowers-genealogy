## Parents

[[thomas-bachelor-1781|Thomas Bachelor]] (abt 1781–?) was born in Flamstead, Hertfordshire and settled in Whipsnade. His father was [[joseph-batchelor-1755|Joseph Batchelor]] (1755–?), also of Flamstead; the family had roots in the Flamstead/Leighton Buzzard area from at least 1748. Alice Woodcroft (1791–?) was born in Whipsnade or the surrounding area. The Find a Grave listing for St Mary the Virgin, **Eaton Bray** (4 miles from Whipsnade) confirms a Woodcraft family there, represented by WWI veterans Harry Woodcraft (1890–1917) and Ernest Woodcraft — likely 2–3 generations after Alice. Eaton Bray is the leading candidate for Alice's family of origin; the Eaton Bray parish registers would be the next source to check. See [[st-mary-virgin-eaton-bray-findagrave]]. They married on 2 August 1816 in Whipsnade. The family lived at Frog Corner, a hamlet within Whipsnade parish.

## Children

Five children are recorded:

1. George Batchelor (1811–1880) — born before the 1816 marriage; parentage unclear
2. David Bachelor (1817)
3. Sarah Batchelor (1819)
4. [[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham Batchelor]] (1825–1892) — held the Chequers Inn licence; married Hannah Maria Heels
5. Rebecca Batchelor (1829)

Alice Woodcroft has no page yet; no further clippings have been found for her.

## Connection to the Chequers

[[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham]] is the key figure connecting this family to the Chequers Inn narrative. Through his marriage to [[hannah-maria-heels-1814|Hannah Maria Heels]] (related to Samuel Heeles, the 1843 tenant), the Chequers licence likely passed to Abraham. This family therefore bridged the period between the Heeles tenancy (1843) and GBP Sr and Ruth Cooke's tenure (from 1867). See [[batchelor-heels-whipsnade]] and [[birchmore-plummer-cooke-1867]].
