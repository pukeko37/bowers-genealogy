## Parents

[[richard-bunker-1859|Richard Bunker (1859)]] married **Emma Page** (born 1861) on 27 March 1882 at Millbrook. Their first child, Harold William, had already been born in 1881. The "Page" middle name openly acknowledged Emma's maiden name — an informal recognition of the pre-marital birth that was common practice when couples intended to marry.

Little else is known of Emma Page's background.

## Family life

Richard and Emma were in Millbrook at the time of their marriage. By 1891 Richard had moved the family to the South Normanton area — following his brothers Thomas, Albert, George, and James north to the Nottinghamshire and Derbyshire coalfields. A decade later, however, he had returned to Bedfordshire: the 1901 and 1911 censuses find him at Flitwick, about eight miles from his Millbrook birthplace. The reason for the return is unknown.

The First World War claimed two of Richard's sons: Harold William Page Bunker (1881–1917) and Joseph Bunker (1894–1917).
