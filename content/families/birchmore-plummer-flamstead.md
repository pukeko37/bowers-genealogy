## Family Life

George Birchmore (1786) was a farmer at Roe End Farm, Flamstead, Hertfordshire. He had a son with Elizabeth Plummer in 1813, two years before marrying Ann Norton in 1815. Whether George and Elizabeth had an ongoing relationship, or whether the birth was a single encounter, is not documented. No marriage between them is recorded in FamilySearch.

[[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]] took his mother's surname, "Plummer" — the standard outcome for an illegitimate child. Elizabeth Plummer raised GBP Sr alone; she outlived George by 28 years, dying in 1866 at an unknown location. GBP Sr himself was born in the Studham, Bedfordshire area (per his 1861 census birthplace) — which may indicate Elizabeth was from that area, or that she was there when GBP Sr was born.

## Wider Family

George (1786) subsequently married [[birchmore-norton-1815|Ann Norton]] in 1815, with whom he had four legitimate children (George, William, John Norton, Anne) — GBP Sr's half-siblings. No contact between GBP Sr and his half-siblings is documented. GBP Sr grew up in Bedfordshire while the legitimate Birchmore family remained in Flamstead, Hertfordshire.
