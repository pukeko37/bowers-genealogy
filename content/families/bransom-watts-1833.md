## Parents

[[jeremiah-bransom-1808|Jeremiah Bransom]] (born 1808) and [[sarah-watts-1816|Sarah Watts]] (born 1816, Edlesborough, Buckinghamshire) married on 7 November 1833 at Edlesborough, Bucks. Jeremiah was an Agricultural Labourer; the family settled in Whipsnade, Bedfordshire, where they remained for decades.

## Children

Six children are recorded, though the name spellings vary considerably across records (Bransom, Brandsom, Brandsome):

1. Frederic Bransom (1836)
2. Betsy Brandsom (1838)
3. Elizabeth Bramson (1838) — possibly a twin with Betsy, or a record duplication
4. Alfred Bransome (1840)
5. [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois Brandsom]] (1843–1924) — married [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook]] 1868
6. [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] (1850–1928) — married [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]] 1871

## Family Life

The 1851 census shows Jeremiah as head of household with Sarah and several children. By 1881 Sarah (listed as "Sarah Brandsom, 68, from Edlesborough") was living with Lois and Charles Cook's household at Whipsnade, and Lois's sister Betsy was also there — suggesting Jeremiah had died or the family had dispersed.

## Wider Significance

Through two marriages into the Cook families of Whipsnade, the Bransom family became deeply intertwined with [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]]'s branch of the Cooks:
- Lois married [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook (1843–1908)]] — a man from a separate Cook family in the same village
- Edwin married [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]] — Ruth Cooke's daughter

The witnesses at the 1894 wedding of [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] and [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] — [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]] and [[walter-cook-1869|Walter Cook]] — were grandchildren of this family on both sides: Florence through Edwin, Walter through Lois.
