## Parents

[[george-bunker-1868|George Bunker (1868–1916)]] married his second wife, **Annie Maria Elston** (born 1886), in Nottinghamshire in 1908 — six years after the death of his first wife Catherine Ward.

## Children

One child:

| # | Name | Born | Died |
|---|------|------|------|
| 1 | Frank Bunker | 1909 | 1993 |

Frank Bunker (1909–1993) was George's youngest child and the only one born of the second marriage.

## Family life

George died on 7 June 1916 at Grimsby, Lincolnshire, leaving Annie Maria a widow and Frank aged about seven. George was buried at South Normanton, Derbyshire. Annie Maria's fate after 1916 is unresearched.
