## Parents

[[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]] (1813–1884) and [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] (1815–1858) married 1 November 1835 at [[whipsnade|Whipsnade]], Bedfordshire. This was George's first marriage.

Anne was the daughter of [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews]] (1767–1862) and [[mary-wilson-1779|Mary Wilson]] (b. 1779), married 3 June 1801. The family had at least nine children; her known siblings were [[able-matthews-1813|Able Matthews]] (c.1813), [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis Matthews]] (1820–1903), and [[charlotte-matthews-1823|Charlotte Matthews]] (1823–1910). The family lived at Houchen End / By Whipsnade Green — the same hamlet where GBP Sr also had an address. See [[matthews-wilson-1801]].

## Children

1. [[george-birchmore-plummer-jr-1850|George Birchmore Plummer Jr]] (b. 1850) — continued using the Birchmore name. Had four children with Elizabeth Sarah Morse (no marriage recorded).

## The Mormon Emigration

In the 1840s, Anne's family converted to Mormonism (LDS). At least two siblings emigrated to Utah: [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis]] sailed from Liverpool on the "Ellen Marie" in 1853 with 332 LDS converts; [[able-matthews-1813|Able]] followed in 1855. Anne's sister [[charlotte-matthews-1823|Charlotte]] stayed in Whipsnade throughout her life; her father [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas]] remained in Whipsnade until his death in 1862. **Anne chose her husband George over her emigrating family** — she stayed in England and never saw her Utah siblings again.

The family story later became that there was a "Quaker background" — possibly a sanitising of the more controversial Mormon connection over the generations.

[[anne-matthews-1815|Anne]] died in 1858 aged 43, her death registered in the Luton district. George married Eliza Batchelor the following year. See [[birchmore-plummer-batchelor-1859]].

## The Cook Connection

Anne's sister [[charlotte-matthews-1823|Charlotte Matthews]] married [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] (1816–1900) of Kensworth — the brother of [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]]. When GBP Sr married Ruth Cooke in 1867 (his third marriage), he was marrying the sister of his first wife's sister's husband. The Matthews and Cook families were already bound together at Whipsnade before GBP Sr's marriages linked them twice. See [[cook-matthews-whipsnade]] and [[birchmore-plummer-cooke-1867]].
