## Parents

[[arthur-perry-1866|Arthur Perry]] (born c.1866, Harlington, Bedfordshire) and [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]] (1873–1963, born Whipsnade) married in 1898 at Luton, Bedfordshire. Florence was the second child of [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]] of Whipsnade — see [[cook-bransom-1871]].

Arthur was a Railway Porter employed by the Great Northern Railway Company, based in Dunstable throughout the known record.

## Children

Three children are recorded:

1. [[percy-arthur-perry-1900|Percy Arthur Perry]] (1900, Dunstable) — registered Luton district; present in Arthur's Dunstable household 1901 and 1911; married Dorothy Annie Purser in December 1927, Luton; had military service in 1918
2. [[florence-hilda-perry-1903|Florence Hilda Perry]] (1903, Whipsnade) — registered Luton district; residing at Whipsnade with her mother and maternal grandparents in 1911
3. [[elsie-perry-1907|Elsie Perry]] (1907, Dunstable) — registered Luton district; present in Arthur's Dunstable household in 1911 (age 4)

## Family Life

The family lived in Dunstable after the 1898 marriage. By 1901 they were established there, with Arthur as head of household and one-year-old Percy.

The 1911 census presents an unusual picture: the household had split across two locations. Arthur remained in Dunstable with Percy (11) and Elsie (4), and his sister-in-law [[clara-lucy-bransom-1877|Clara Bransom]] (Florence's younger sister) was also living with him. Florence, meanwhile, was back at her parents' home in Whipsnade with daughter Florence Hilda (8). The reasons for this arrangement are unknown — it may reflect a temporary domestic arrangement (Florence caring for her parents, or Clara's work situation), or something more permanent. No evidence of formal separation has been found.

Florence died in June 1963 in Luton, aged approximately 89. Arthur's death date and place have not been established.

## Wider Family

Florence's family of origin is the [[cook-bransom-1871|Bransom-Cook household]] of Whipsnade. Her sister [[clara-lucy-bransom-1877|Clara Lucy]] lived with Arthur in 1911, and married Cyril John Baldock in 1912. Another sister [[margaret-anne-bransom-1880|Margaret Anne]] married James Hain of Dell Farm, Whipsnade in 1905.
