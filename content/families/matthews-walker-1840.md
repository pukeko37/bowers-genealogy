## Parents

**[[able-matthews-1813|Able Matthews]]** (born 19 April 1813, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire) was the fifth child of [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews]] and [[mary-wilson-1779|Mary Wilson]]. He was a Whipsnade agricultural labourer like his father, part of the extended LDS-converting Matthews family.

**[[mary-walker-1817|Mary Ann Walker]]** (born ~1817, Aylesbury area, Buckinghamshire; full name confirmed by son William's Geni record) married Able on **22 November 1840** at Dunstable, Bedfordshire, with James Walker as witness. How the couple met is not recorded — Dunstable was a natural gathering point for the Whipsnade area, a few miles to the north-west.

## Early Marriage Years — Houchen End

In the year following the marriage, Able and Mary were living with Able's sister [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] and brother-in-law [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer Sr.]] at Houchen End, Whipsnade. The 1841 census records the four adults and a newborn Sarah Matthews (F, 0) in the Birchmore household. This kind of shared household was common in the early years of marriage when resources were tight — the Whipsnade chalk-downland labourers had few means. GBP Sr. and Anne had been married since 1835; in 1841 they were letting Able and Mary share their home.

## Whipsnade Household — 1851

By 1851 Able and Mary had their own household in Whipsnade. Three children are recorded:

| Child | Age (1851) | Birth year | Birthplace |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sarah Matthews | 10 | ~1841 | Whipsnade, Bedfordshire |
| Elizabeth Matthews | 4 | ~1847 | Whipsnade, Bedfordshire |
| William Matthews | 3 | ~1848 | Whipsnade, Bedfordshire |

The six-year gap between Sarah and Elizabeth is notable — perhaps a child or two were born and died in between, or the couple were separated during some of those years. Able does not appear in his parents' 1841 household, and his own 1841 household shows only one infant; he may have been working away from Whipsnade intermittently.

## LDS Conversion and Emigration Planning

The family converted to the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in the 1840s, as part of the wider Matthews family movement toward Mormonism. Able was baptised LDS at some point in this period. The emigration to Utah was not immediate — it took several years after baptism to make the crossing.

## Emigration — 1855 and Beyond

The family emigrated from Liverpool in 1855. Saints by Sea confirms they sailed **Liverpool to Philadelphia**, departing **27 February 1855** and arriving **20 April 1855** (FamilySearch sources also cite the ship *Siddons*; Saints by Sea confirms the voyage but does not name the ship in the passenger record). Six people sailed:

| Passenger | Age | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Abel Matthews | 41 | Head; FamilySearch ID KWJZ-9HJ |
| Mary A. W. Matthews | 37 | Wife — [[mary-walker-1817|Mary Ann Walker]]; alive at departure |
| Sarah Matthews | 14 | ~1841; fate after St. Louis untraced |
| Elizabeth Matthews | 8 | ~1847; fate after St. Louis untraced |
| William Matthews | 6 | ~1848 — [[william-abel-matthews-1848|William Abel Matthews Sr.]] |
| **Anna Matthews** | **11 months*** | Born **23 February 1853**; not in 1851 census; almost certainly the "Ann" on the manifest — see note below |

**[[mary-walker-1817|Mary Ann Walker]] was alive when they sailed.** She died in **St. Louis, Missouri** at some point between April 1855 and June 1861 — a window of up to six years in St. Louis. The cause is unrecorded.

**\*Age discrepancy for Anna:** The BMR records her as "11 months" but Anna was born 23 February 1853 and the ship departed 27 February 1855 — making her 2 years and 4 days old, not 11 months. The clerk's age entry appears to be a transcription error. The identification is nonetheless secure: the name matches (Ann/Anna), she is the only known child of that name in the family, and she is absent from all later records — consistent with dying in St. Louis before Able reached Utah.

Able made the overland crossing in 1861 with the **David H. Cannon Company**, departing 1 June and arriving Deseret 16 August 1861. He was a widower by then and settled in **Lehi, Utah County**, where he later remarried Jennette (from Scotland).

## Children

Four children are now confirmed — three from the 1851 English census plus one new infant on the 1855 ship manifest:

- **Sarah Matthews** (~1841) — fate untraced after St. Louis
- **Elizabeth Matthews** (~1847) — fate untraced
- **[[william-abel-matthews-1848|William Abel Matthews Sr.]]** (born 18 March 1848, Whipsnade) — emigrated with father; settled Lehi then Virgin, Utah; married Rebecca Vince; died 16 August 1917
- **Anna Matthews** (born 23 February 1853) — born after 1851 census; sailed with the family February 1855; almost certainly the "Ann (11 months)" on the Saints by Sea manifest (the BMR age appears to be a clerk error — she was actually ~24 months at departure); fate unknown, likely died in St. Louis
