## Parents

[[ernest-caleb-bunker-1882|Ernest Caleb Bunker]] (1882–1967) and [[helena-annie-melrose-1883|Helena Annie Melrose]] (1883–1960) married on 1 September 1913 at the Wesleyan Chapel, Gloucester Road, Horfield, Bristol. Ernest was already living in Bristol by the time of the marriage. Both were 30 and 29 respectively.

## Children

1. [[kenneth-ernest-bunker-1917|Kenneth Ernest Bunker]] (1917–1985) — born during WWI while Ernest was on active service
2. [[herbert-arthur-bunker-1922|Herbert Arthur Bunker]] (1922–2007) — named for his uncle [[herbert-thomas-barnsley-bunker-1875|Herbert Thomas Barnsley Bunker]], who died in 1917

## Family Life

Ernest enlisted in 1914, serving in France, Belgium, and Germany through 1920 — the full span of WWI and the occupation period. His elder son Kenneth was born in 1917 during his father's wartime absence. Ernest returned from service and the family continued in Bristol, where they were visited by [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland Bunker]] and his son [[alan-geoffrey-bowers-1928|Alan]] in the 1930s.

At some point after the 1930s the family moved from Bristol to Watford, Hertfordshire. Helena died in 1960; Ernest died in Watford in April 1967.
