## Overview

This page records the family of origin of [[richard-mason-1850|Richard Mason]] (1850–1890), who was born in Aberystwyth, Cardiganshire, Wales, and later ran a dairy at Hosier Lane, Smithfield. The family is known from the 1851 and 1861 Welsh census records but has not been further researched.

## Parents

- **Morgan Mason** (born ~1822, Llanbadarn Fawr, Cardiganshire) — horse-related occupations: ostler in 1851, postillion in 1861. Llanbadarn Fawr is the parish immediately adjacent to Aberystwyth.
- **Catherine Mason** (born ~1826, Trerddol, Cardiganshire) — Trerddol (also written Tre'r-ddôl) is a village in north Cardiganshire, some distance from Aberystwyth, suggesting the parents came from different parts of the county before settling in Aberystwyth.

## Children

All born in Aberystwyth:

1. **Lewis Mason** (~1849)
2. **[[richard-mason-1850|Richard Mason]]** (1850–1890) — Cowkeeper, 27 Hosier Lane, Smithfield; buried Abney Park Cemetery
3. **Anne Mason** (~1854)

By 1861 the family lived on **Thespian Street, Aberystwyth**, where Richard (aged ~11) was recorded as a Scholar.
