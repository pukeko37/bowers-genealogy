## Parents

[[william-thody-1774|William Thody]] (abt 1774 – 7 August 1858) married **Elizabeth Dance** — she is called "Elizabeth" in the marriage register but **"Jane"** in every subsequent document — in **February 1845** at **Holy Trinity Brompton**, London. Both were widowed; both signed with marks. The register gives William's father as Samuel Thoady and Elizabeth/Jane's father as William Smith.

William was about 71 at this marriage; Jane was apparently around 55–60, based on her 1851 census age of 60.

Nothing more is known about Jane's origins beyond her father's name. The discrepancy between "Elizabeth" in the church register and "Jane" in all later records is unexplained — it may be a clerical error at the marriage, or she may have used her middle name in daily life.

## Children

No children are recorded from this marriage. Both parties came to it already widowed and past childbearing age.

## Family Life

The 1851 census records William (77) and Jane (60) at Chelsea — living among the community of Chelsea Pensioners where William had settled after his military discharge in 1816. Jane's father **James Smith** (listed as such in the 1851 census, though the marriage register says "William Smith") was also in the household, aged 78.

They were living at **15 Paradise Walk, Chelsea**, by the time of William's death. He died there on **7 August 1858**, cause recorded as "Natural exhaustion." Jane was present at the death. He was buried at Victoria Park Cemetery, Hackney, on 13 August 1858. The death register gives his age as 73, but the 1851 census implies a birth year of about 1774, making him closer to 84.

Jane's fate after William's death is not documented. No death record has been found for her.

## Open Questions

- Why does the marriage register say "Elizabeth" when all later records say "Jane"?
- Who was Jane's first husband? The marriage register records her as a widow but gives no name.
- When and where did Jane die after William's death in 1858?
- Was "William Smith" or "James Smith" her father's name? The marriage register and 1851 census disagree.
