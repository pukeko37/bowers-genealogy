## Parents

[[thomas-matthews-1797|Thomas Matthews]] (christened 12 Aug 1798, Kensworth) was the eldest child of [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews Sr.]] and [[mary-wilson-1779|Mary Wilson]], and spent his whole life as an agricultural labourer in Kensworth. He married Elizabeth Hopkins on **1 November 1840** at Kensworth, listed in the marriage record as "Widowed" — he had a previous wife named Mary (surname unknown) who bore him at least three children before dying between 1831 and 1840.

Elizabeth Hopkins (b. ~1800–1801, Kensworth, Hertfordshire) was likely a Kensworth woman. Her mother Elizabeth Hopkins (65 in 1841) was living with the family in the 1841 census, probably widowed. Elizabeth was 40 in 1841 — slightly older than Thomas.

## Children

Thomas had children from both marriages, all raised in Kensworth:

**From first wife Mary:**
- **Mary Matthews** (christened 2 Sep 1827, Kensworth) — married Daniel Evans, 27 Jan 1849, Kensworth.
- **Sarah Matthews** (christened 24 Jul 1831, Kensworth) — present in 1841 household (age 10); not found in 1851.
- **Charles Matthews** (b. ~1832, Kensworth) — present in 1841 (age 9) and 1851 (age 19). Fate thereafter unknown.

**From Elizabeth Hopkins (confirmed):**
- **Naomi Matthews** (b. ~1835, Kensworth) — married Charles Desborough, 24 Sep 1864, Luton.

## Family Life

Thomas Matthews (the younger) was the non-emigrant counterpart to his emigrant siblings. While [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis]] and [[william-matthews-1806|William]] sailed to Utah in 1853, and [[able-matthews-1813|Able]] followed in 1855–1861, Thomas remained in Kensworth working the land. He died in 1862, the same year as his father.

The family lived at the **North Side Kensworth Road** (noted in the 1861 census) — in the same Hertfordshire village where both Thomas and his father were born. His children lived out their lives in the same area: Mary married locally, Naomi married in Luton.

The 1861 census household includes **John Evans** (11, Kensworth), the likely grandson of Thomas through his daughter Mary who married Daniel Evans in 1849. A "David Matthews" (8, Kensworth) also appears — possibly another grandchild or a nephew.
