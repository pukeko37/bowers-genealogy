## Parents

[[george-birchmore-1762|George Birchmore]] (1762–1843) and Susannah Redding (1763–Deceased) married 28 October 1784 at Flamstead, Hertfordshire. George farmed at Row End Farm, Flamstead for his entire life.

## Children

1. Sarah Burchmore (1782–Deceased) — born before the marriage; a pre-marital child
2. [[george-birchmore-1786|George Birchmore]] (1786–1838) — GBP Sr's father
3. William Burchmore (1791–17 Jan 1856) — probable third child; buried St Leonard, Flamstead; newly identified from the churchyard listing. No FamilySearch record found to date; relationship inferred from birth year falling within the marriage period.

## Family Life

The Flamstead Birchmores were an established Hertfordshire yeoman farming family. George (1762) lived at Row End Farm his entire life and died there in 1843 aged 80 — a long and settled life. His son [[george-birchmore-1786|George (1786)]] also farmed at Roe End Farm and died there in 1838.

George (1762)'s parents were Thomas Birchmore (1729–1806) and Sarah Andrew (1735–1816) of Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire — placing the Birchmore family in this corner of Hertfordshire for at least four generations before [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]] was born.

## Wider Family

The Flamstead Birchmore family is distinct from the Whipsnade Birchmore family (William Birchmore, 1751) who appear in the research through [[mary-birchmore-1785|Mary Birchmore]] (Ruth Cooke's mother). Both families carry the name Birchmore but no documented connection between the two lines has been found.
