## Parents

**[[william-roffe|William Roffe]]** (dates unknown) married **[[mary-bunker-1737|Mary Bunker]]** (abt 1737–1801) on 4 May 1764 at Millbrook. Nothing is documented about William Roffe beyond his marriage and the children's records. Mary was buried at Steppingley on 24 April 1801.

## Family life

The Roffe family's connection to Steppingley (where Mary was buried in 1801) parallels that of her brother [[william-bunker-1740|William Bunker (1740)]], who settled and died at Steppingley. Two of Mary's children shared a name (Edward) — the second Edward (1769) presumably named after the infant who died in 1767.

## Wider family

Mary was the fifth child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and Mary Reade.
