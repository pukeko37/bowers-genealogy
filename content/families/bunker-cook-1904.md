## Parents

[[mabel-bunker-1883|Mabel Bunker (1883)]] married **Mark Edward Cook** (born 1883, Wotton, Bedfordshire) on 21 May 1904 at Millbrook. Both were twenty-one. The ceremony was at Millbrook — Mabel's home parish — suggesting family was present.

Mark Edward Cook died in 1955.

## Family life

[[grace-bunker-1902|Grace Bunker]] was born in Luton in Q3 1902, before the marriage. She was registered under the Bunker surname, indicating Mabel was unmarried. After the 1904 marriage, Grace was left with her Millbrook grandparents, [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann]] — and appears there in the 1911 census aged eight. By 1921 Grace was back with Mabel, listed in the census as *Grace Bunker Cook*, a surname that encodes her unusual history: born Bunker, acknowledged into the Cook family, but never formally taking Cook alone.

Mark Cook's occupation in 1911 was **stone mason's labourer**. In 1921 the family was at **15 Ashton Street, High Town, Luton**, where six of their children were living under the same roof. Mark's surname Cook and the George Cook who appears as a witness in the 1904 marriage register are probably father and son (George being Mark's father or brother).

Mabel's date of death is unestablished. Mark died in 1955.
