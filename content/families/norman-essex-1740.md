> **This family is likely misattributed.** FamilySearch assigns a marriage between Richard Norman (P68D-T5X, our Richard) and Mary Essex to 1740, while also recording his marriage to Mary Hall in 1749 — a date at which Mary Essex was still alive (she died 1765). Simultaneously marrying two women is implausible. A second FamilySearch profile, L565-ZVH (Richard Norman, 1718–1753), is also attached to Mary Essex's same 1740 Cottenham marriage, with a different set of children. The Essex marriage almost certainly belongs to L565-ZVH alone. This page is retained as a holding record pending primary source verification. Do not rely on it for narrative purposes.

## The Attribution Problem

[[mary-essex-1722|Mary Essex]] (1722–1765) married a Richard Norman on 27 April 1740 at Cottenham, Cambridgeshire. This is documented in the Cambridgeshire Bishop's Transcripts and England Marriages. The marriage itself is not in doubt. What is in doubt is *which* Richard Norman.

FamilySearch has attached this marriage to two separate profiles:

- **P68D-T5X** (our Richard Norman, abt 1723–1783): attributed children from this marriage are William Norman (1739) and Richard Norman (1745).
- **L565-ZVH** (Richard Norman, 1718–1753): attributed children are Robert (1741), William (1744), Alice (1745), John (1752), Thomas (1753), Richard (1753–54).

These are irreconcilable. One attribution is wrong. The case for L565-ZVH being the correct husband of Mary Essex is stronger:
- L565-ZVH dies in 1753, leaving Mary a widow, consistent with her dying alone in Cottenham in 1765.
- If P68D-T5X married Mary Essex in 1740 and she survived to 1765, his 1749 marriage to Mary Hall would be bigamy.
- L565-ZVH's children span 1741–1754 — a plausible family for someone who died in 1753. P68D-T5X's Essex children (1739, 1745) are fewer and less coherent.

## Open Questions

- Which Richard Norman actually married Mary Essex in 1740? Primary source verification of the Cottenham parish register entry would identify the father's given name precisely and potentially resolve this.
- Are the children listed under P68D-T5X (William 1739, Richard 1745) real people attached to the wrong Richard Norman, or artefacts of the profile contamination?
