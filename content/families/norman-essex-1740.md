> **Resolved: this family does not belong to [[richard-norman-1723|our Richard Norman]] (P68D-T5X).** The Essex marriage was previously misattributed to P68D-T5X by FamilySearch and has since been corrected and removed. The marriage between Mary Essex and a Richard Norman at Cottenham in 1740 is real, but the husband is almost certainly FamilySearch profile L565-ZVH (Richard Norman, 1718–1753) — a separate individual. This page is retained as a reference record only.

## What is Known

[[mary-essex-1722|Mary Essex]] (1722–1765) married a Richard Norman on 27 April 1740 at Cottenham, Cambridgeshire. This is attested in the Cambridgeshire Bishop's Transcripts and England Marriages. The marriage itself is not in doubt.

The husband is **not** our [[richard-norman-1723|Richard Norman]] (1723–1783). His corrected FamilySearch profile (P68D-T5X) now shows only his marriage to Mary Hall (1749, Over) and one child, Robert Norman (1769).

The probable husband is FamilySearch profile **L565-ZVH** (Richard Norman, 1718–1753), whose children from the Essex marriage are listed as: Robert (1741), William (1744), Alice (1745), John (1752), Thomas (1753), Richard (1753–54). L565-ZVH dies in 1753, leaving Mary a widow for the final twelve years of her life before her death at Cottenham in 1765. No person page for L565-ZVH has been created as he falls outside the direct line.
