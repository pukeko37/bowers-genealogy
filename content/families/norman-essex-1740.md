## Parents

[[richard-norman-1723|Richard Norman]] (abt 1723–1783) and [[mary-essex-1722|Mary Essex]] (1722–1765) married on 27 April 1740 at Cottenham, Cambridgeshire — the parish where both were born. Richard was around 17 and Mary 18.

## Children

- William Norman (1739–1822)
- Richard Norman (1745–Deceased)

No further detail on these children is currently held in the wiki. William (1739) was born before the 1740 marriage date in the records — possibly a date discrepancy in the source, or the marriage was registered late. Person pages not yet created.

## Family Life

Richard and Mary were both Cottenham-born, and they married in their home parish. Richard subsequently moved to Over, Cambridgeshire — possibly for his weaving trade — where he later married again. Whether Mary moved with him to Over or remained in Cottenham is not established; she died in Cottenham in 1765.

Mary died twenty-five years before Richard. He had already remarried — Mary Hall (1749) — and it was through that second marriage that his son [[robert-norman-1769|Robert Norman]] was born in 1769.
