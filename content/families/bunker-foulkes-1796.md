## Parents

[[thomas-bunker-1767|Thomas Bunker]] (abt 1767–1843) married **Susannah Foulkes** (1765–1843) on 21 November 1796 at Steppingley. Thomas was the eldest son of [[william-bunker-1740|William Bunker (1740)]] and Ann Peet. Susannah's origins are not documented. Remarkably, both died in the same year, 1843.

Thomas's 1841 census address is **How End, Houghton Conquest** — the neighbouring parish rather than Steppingley itself — suggesting the family had shifted slightly from the ancestral Steppingley ground by that date.

## Family life

The family were rooted in Steppingley, carrying forward the presence established by Thomas's father William (1740) who had settled there after marrying Ann Peet in 1768. The community in which Thomas and Susannah raised their children was the same one Thomas had grown up in.

## Joseph Bunker (1798–1868) — now documented

Joseph married Mary Brown (born ~1800, Westoning) on 11 May 1821 at Steppingley. Eight children: Arthur (1823), Caroline (1826), William (1829), Catherine (~1831), Susan (1832), William 2nd (~1837), Amelia (~1838), Thomas (~1840–41). **No Ruth.** The 1841 and 1851 censuses confirm no Ruth in his household.
