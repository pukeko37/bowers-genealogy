## Parents

[[george-bunker-1868|George Bunker (1868–1916)]] married his first wife, **Catherine Ward** (born 1875), on 25 January 1896 at Mansfield, Nottinghamshire. George was then in his late twenties; Catherine was about twenty.

## Children

Three children:

| # | Name | Born | Died |
|---|------|------|------|
| 1 | Annie Bunker | 1896 | Deceased |
| 2 | Thomas Bunker | 1899 | Deceased |
| 3 | Dorothy Bunker | 1902 | Deceased |

Catherine Ward died in 1902 — possibly in connection with Dorothy's birth. The three children were still living at the time FamilySearch records were compiled.

## Family life

George and Catherine were in the Mansfield/Nottinghamshire area throughout their marriage. After Catherine's death, George continued living in Nottinghamshire and remarried in 1908 (see [[bunker-elston-1908]]). He died in 1916 at Grimsby, Lincolnshire — away from his Nottinghamshire home — and was buried at South Normanton, Derbyshire.
