## Parents

**[[john-bunker-1746|John Bunker]]** (1746–1796) was the tenth child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and Mary Reade, christened at Millbrook. He married **[[elizabeth-cartwright-1737|Elizabeth Cartwright]]** (1737–1785) on 25 August 1768 at Millbrook. Elizabeth was nine years older than John and predeceased him by eleven years, dying in 1785. John died in 1796, buried at **Millbrook** St Michael & All Angels (XAUTO/FHS/BEDFORDSHIRE/BUR/00269498) — confirmed by the parish burial register. FamilySearch (K6SV-9TQ) gives the burial location as "Ampthill," which is a common area-level conflation for Millbrook; the primary source supersedes it.
