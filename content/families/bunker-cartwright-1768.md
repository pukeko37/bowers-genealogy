## Parents

**[[john-bunker-1746|John Bunker]]** (1746–1796) was the tenth child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and Mary Reade, christened at Millbrook. He married **[[elizabeth-cartwright-1737|Elizabeth Cartwright]]** (1737–1785) on 25 August 1768 at Millbrook. Elizabeth was nine years older than John and predeceased him by eleven years, dying in 1785. John died in 1796, buried at Ampthill rather than Millbrook, suggesting he may have moved away in his final years.
