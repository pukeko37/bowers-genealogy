## Parents

**[[william-bunker-1740|William Bunker]]** (1740–1816) was the seventh child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and Mary Reade, christened at Millbrook. He married **[[ann-peet-1746|Ann Peet]]** (1746–1827) on 11 October 1768 at Steppingley, the village where he settled and spent the rest of his life. He died there on 6 June 1816; Ann outlived him by eleven years.

## Family life

William moved from Millbrook to Steppingley, perhaps in connection with Ann Peet's family there. His sister [[mary-bunker-1737|Mary Bunker (1737)]] was also buried at Steppingley in 1801, suggesting a family presence in that village.
