## Parents

[[william-bunker-1776|William Bunker (1776–1855)]] married **Elisabeth Burridge** (1777–1807) on 19 February 1798 at Steppingley. Elisabeth died in 1807 after nine years of marriage, possibly in childbirth. William subsequently remarried; see [[bunker-bitchener-1808]].

## Children

Both children died in infancy.

| # | Name | Baptised | Buried | FamilySearch ID |
|---|------|----------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Ann Bunker | 18 Nov 1798, Steppingley | 25 Jun 1800, Steppingley | MM17-BVZ |
| 2 | Mille Bunker | 7 Jun 1801, Steppingley | 8 Jan 1802, Steppingley | MM17-N4G |

"Mille" is likely a variant of Milly or Millicent. Both children died before their second birthday. The first marriage produced no surviving children.

## Open questions

- Cause of Elisabeth's death in 1807 — the FamilySearch source dated 1807 is attached to William but its content was not fully captured.
- Whether Judith Bunker (1807), listed under the second marriage in FamilySearch, was actually the posthumous child of Elisabeth rather than a pre-marital child of Hannah Bitchener. If Elisabeth died in childbirth in 1807, Judith could be hers.
