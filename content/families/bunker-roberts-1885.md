## Parents

[[thomas-bunker-1862|Thomas Bunker (1862–1902)]] married **Charlotte Roberts** (born 1864) on Christmas Day, 25 December 1885, at Moggerhanger, Bedfordshire. His younger brother Albert would marry Emma Roberts at the same parish less than two years later; Charlotte and Emma were very likely sisters from the same Moggerhanger Roberts family.

## Children

No children of Thomas and Charlotte are documented.

## Family life

Thomas and Charlotte were in Millbrook or the surrounding area around the time of their marriage. By 1891 Thomas had moved to Nottinghamshire, and by 1901 he was at South Normanton, Derbyshire — the same migration his brothers were making. Thomas died in South Normanton in early 1902 and was buried there on 17 April. He was approximately forty years old.

What became of Charlotte Roberts after Thomas's death is not known.
