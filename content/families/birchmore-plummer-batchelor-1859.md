## Parents

[[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]] (1813–1884) and [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]] (1829–1867) married 18 June 1859 at Old Church, [[st-pancras|Saint Pancras]], London. This was George's second marriage; his first wife [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] (1815–1858) had died the year before.

George's name history is notable — born illegitimately to George Birchmore and Elizabeth Plummer, he used the surnames Birchmore and Plummer interchangeably across his life. In this marriage record he appears as "George Plummer Birchmore." See [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813]] for the full name history.

## Children

1. [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen Birchmore]] (1860–1940) — christened Ellen Plummer
2. [[elizabeth-birchmore-1862|Elizabeth Birchmore]] (1862–1954) — domestic servant in London; died aged 92 at Alice Norman's home in Balham
3. Thomas Birchmore (1865–1865) — died in infancy

The children used the surname Birchmore, though Ellen was originally christened Plummer. The surname settled into Birchmore by the time Elizabeth was christened in 1863.

## Family Life

The census and source records now give a clear picture of this family.

Both George and Eliza were Whipsnade residents their entire lives — George in every census from 1841 to 1881, Eliza in every census from 1841 to 1861. Their marriage at St Pancras on 18 June 1859 was a deliberate excursion to London, not evidence of London residence. Eliza's father [[thomas-batchelar-1803|Thomas Batchelar]] (born Dagnall, Buckinghamshire) was present at the ceremony. Why two Whipsnade residents chose St Pancras rather than their local parish church is not documented — George had a prior connection to St Pancras (his son GBP Jr was born there in 1850), and St Pancras Old Church had a long history as a venue for those wishing to marry outside their home parish.

**After the marriage, George and Eliza moved into Thomas Batchelar's house.** The 1861 census places the whole family together at **"By Whipsnade Green"** — Thomas as head, Eliza listed as his daughter, George as "son" (i.e., son-in-law), and the children. George was employed as an **Agricultural Labourer**. Also in the household was [[george-birchmore-plummer-jr-1850|GBP Jr]] (age 11), George's son from his first marriage — George brought the boy from the first marriage into the new household.

George's address at a later point was **Houchen End**, a small hamlet also within Whipsnade parish.

Eliza died in 1867 in Whipsnade, when Ellen was about seven and Elizabeth about five. George married [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] later that same year, on 26 December 1867 at Whipsnade. No children from this third marriage are recorded.

Ellen grew up in Whipsnade alongside her step-sister [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] (Ruth's daughter), and went on to work as a cook at Hall Farm before marrying [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] in 1883, forming the [[norman-birchmore-1883|Norman-Birchmore family]].

## Wider Family

- **Eliza's father:** [[thomas-batchelar-1803|Thomas Batchelar]] (1803–1872), born Dagnall, Buckinghamshire; shepherd and labourer in Whipsnade from at least 1841. His father was [[david-batchelor-unknown|David Batchelor]] (dates unknown), confirmed by the Ivinghoe christening record and Thomas's own 1868 marriage record.
- **Eliza's mother:** [[susan-matthews-1800|Susan Matthews]] (1800–1857), daughter of [[peter-matthews-1770|Peter Matthews]] (1770, Kensworth, Herts) and [[mary-walker-1766|Mary Walker]] (1766, Kensworth, Herts). Peter and Mary were lifelong Whipsnade residents. Susan worked as a Grocer in 1851, unusual for a married woman of her time. The Matthews family connection between Susan and [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] (GBP Sr's first wife, also from Whipsnade) is probable but not yet confirmed — both women likely draw from the same Matthews community rooted in Whipsnade across this period.
- **The Chequers connection:** [[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham Batchelor]] held the Chequers Inn licence before GBP Sr and Ruth Cooke — giving GBP Sr a route to knowing the Batchelar family through the pub. Abraham's line traces to [[thomas-bachelor-1781|Thomas Bachelor]] (1781, Flamstead), who is likely connected to but distinct from Eliza's father Thomas Batchelar (1803, Dagnall) via [[david-batchelor-unknown|David Batchelor]].
- **George's parents:** George Birchmore (1786–1838) & Elizabeth Plummer (1790–1866) — unmarried
- **George's first family:** George & Anne Matthews (1815–1858), married 1835 Whipsnade. One son: George Birchmore Plummer Jr (b. 1850)
- **George's third marriage:** Ruth Cooke (1820–1904), married 1867 Whipsnade. No children. Ruth may be the connection to Lucy Thody, Ellen's step-sister mentioned in Ivy's narrative.
- **Ellen's family:** [[norman-birchmore-1883]]
