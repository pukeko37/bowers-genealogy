## Parents

[[job-bunker-1780|Job Bunker (1780)]] was the second son of [[joseph-bunker-1754|Joseph Bunker (1754)]] and [[martha-garratt-1756|Martha Garratt]]. He was christened at Millbrook on 14 May 1780. He married **[[rebecca-atterbury-1785|Rebecca Atterbury]]** (born 1785); the marriage date and place are not yet documented, but their first child was born in 1807, placing the marriage around 1806 at the latest.

Rebecca's origins are unknown. The Atterbury surname has not been traced in any other Millbrook Bunker connection.

## Family Life

This is a sizeable Millbrook family entirely unknown to the wiki before this ingest. Job and Rebecca raised eight children in the village across the early decades of the nineteenth century, placing them in Millbrook at the same time as the James Thomas Bunker (1764) line. Their children's birth years run parallel to the children of [[james-bunker-1807|James Bunker (1807)]] — Job's first-cousin-once-removed.

None of Job and Rebecca's children have yet been traced beyond their FamilySearch entries. They do not appear in the 1841 census records identified for Millbrook. Whether any remained in the village or dispersed like the James (1807) children is unknown.

Joel Bunker (1808–1875) is the only child with a death year confirmed. His survival to 1875 suggests at least some of the family reached adulthood and may be traceable through census or marriage records.

## Burial register notes

Job's daughter Anne (1819–1819) may be a candidate for burial register entry #1 (Amelia 1819) if "Amelia" is a transcript error for "Anne." However, the names are sufficiently different that a clerical error seems unlikely. The Amelia entry may be a child from a different branch.
