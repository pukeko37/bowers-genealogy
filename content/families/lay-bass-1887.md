## Parents

**James Lay** (abt 1862/63–1931) was born in Lavendon, Buckinghamshire, the son of a Lavendon family. He worked as a stone mason throughout his adult life — recorded variously as "stone mason" (1891), "stone waller mason" (1901), and "jobbing stone mason" (1911). He died in 1931.

**[[sarah-bass-1859|Sarah Bass]]** (1859–1951) was born in Maulden, Bedfordshire, the eldest daughter of [[daniel-bass-1829|Daniel Bass]] and [[mary-ward-tall-1827|Mary Ward Tall]]. She grew up in Maulden, worked as a dressmaker in her early twenties, and married James Lay in **April 1887 at Ampthill, Bedfordshire**. The marriage brought her to Lavendon — across the county border — where she remained for decades.

## Children

Four children, all born in Lavendon, Buckinghamshire:

1. **Herman Vivian Lay** (1888–1946) — eldest son; name sometimes spelled "Hermon Vivian"
2. **Beatrice Eva Lay** (1890–date unknown) — eldest daughter; listed as "Deceased" on FamilySearch without a death year
3. **Gertrude Florence Lay** (1892–1954)
4. **Oliver Cecil Lay** (1894–1979)

## Family Life

The Lays settled in Lavendon and built their lives there. By 1891 Sarah was working as a **general shop keeper** alongside James's stone masonry — the household already showing the pattern of dual income that would continue. By 1911 she was "assistant in shop," and the household still included Gertrude Florence (18) and Oliver Cecil (17).

Sarah's mother [[mary-ward-tall-1827|Mary Ward Tall]] — widowed and elderly — moved to Lavendon around 1901 to live with the family, appearing in the 1901 census as "mother-in-law" in the household. She died there in April 1907 aged ~79.

James died in 1931. Sarah lived on until **March 1951**, dying in Luton, Bedfordshire at nearly 92.

## Wider Family

Sarah's family of origin is the [[bass-tall-1855|Bass-Tall family]] of Maulden. Her parents were Daniel Bass (died 1868) and Mary Ward Tall; her siblings were Florence Bass (1862) and Edward Ernest Bass (1864–1936). Through her mother, she is connected to the Tall family of Maulden — making her aunt by marriage to [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]] and cousin to [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick Bunker]].
