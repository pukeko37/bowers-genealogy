## Parents

[[samuel-heels-1797|Samuel Heels]] (born ~1797–1801, Bedfordshire) married **Anne** (born 1803) before 1824, near Totternhoe, Bedfordshire. Anne died in 1840, leaving Samuel a widower with at least four children. Two years later he married [[hannah-maria-heels-1814|Hannah Maria Heels]] (1814–1886), with whom he had one further child. Samuel died in 1845.

Anne's surname is not recorded in the current sources.

## Children

1. **Mary Ann Heels** (1825)
2. **Rebecka Heels** (1826–1911)
3. **Elizabeth Heels** (1834)
4. **Emma Heels** (1838–1848) — died aged 10, two years after her mother

## Family Life

Samuel and Anne were associated with the Totternhoe and Whipsnade area of south Bedfordshire. By 1843 Samuel was the sitting tenant of the Chequers Inn at Whipsnade Green, paying £16 per year to the owner Edward Burr (Dunstable Brewery). Anne had died three years before this point; Samuel was running the pub as a widower.

His second wife Hannah Maria Heels — who shared the surname though apparently from a separate branch — brought further family connections to the Whipsnade community. After Samuel's death in 1845, Hannah married [[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham Batchelor]], probably the route through which the Chequers licence passed to the Batchelor family.

The fates of Anne and Samuel's four daughters after 1840 are mostly untraced. Rebecka Heels (1826–1911) lived to 85 and is the best-documented of the four.
