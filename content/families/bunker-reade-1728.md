## Parents

[[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker]] (1704–1784) was born in Westoning, Bedfordshire, the son of William Bunker (1663–1716) and Sarah Greene (1663–1737). He married [[mary-reade-1702|Mary Reade]] (1702–1775) on 23 January 1728 at Toddington, Bedfordshire. Mary was two years his senior.

By the time their sixth child Thomas was christened at Millbrook in 1738, the family had settled in Millbrook, where Thomas Bunker lived until his death in 1784. Mary died in 1775, nine years before her husband.

## Children

Twelve children in total, spanning from 1728 to 1754. Three died in infancy: two named William (1728 and 1731) and a Mary (1729).

Thomas Bunker (1738, #6) is the direct ancestor of Roland Bunker. He married Martha Sinfield in 1761 and fathered James Thomas Bunker (1764).

## Family Life

The family's move from Westoning to Millbrook is visible in the records: Thomas was christened at Westoning (1704), he married at Toddington (1728), but by 1738 his children were being christened at Millbrook. Millbrook became the Bunker family's ancestral home for the next three generations. Thomas and Mary raised a large household and saw ten of their twelve children survive to adulthood.
