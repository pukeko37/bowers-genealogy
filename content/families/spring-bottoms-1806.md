## Parents

[[william-spring-1779|William Spring]] (christened 29 August 1779, Shillington, Bedfordshire) and [[sarah-bottoms-1786|Sarah Bottoms]] (1786–1829). The nature of their relationship is uncertain. Their two children — [[rhoda-spring-bottoms-1806|Rhoda]] (born November 1806, Wootton, Bedfordshire) and Phoebe (born 1807) — were born before William's marriage to Mary (about 1808). The christening record for Rhoda uses the form "Rhoda or Phoebe Spring or Bottoms," which suggests she was recorded under both her parents' surnames — a practice sometimes used in parish registers for children of informal or uncertain unions.

Whether William and Sarah were married, briefly co-habitating, or in some other arrangement is not established. Sarah died in 1829, long after Rhoda had married and started her own family. William Spring lived on into old age, residing in Barton-le-Clay, Bedfordshire with his second family by Mary.

## Children

1. **[[rhoda-spring-bottoms-1806|Rhoda Spring Bottoms]]** (Nov 1806 – 4 Jan 1884) — christened Wootton, Bedfordshire; married Joseph Geary 1824; Eliza Geary's grandmother. See [[geary-spring-1824]].
2. **Phoebe Spring** (1807–Deceased) — born one year after Rhoda; no further research.
