## Parents

[[alan-geoffrey-bowers-1928|Dr Alan Geoffrey Bowers]] (1928–2012) and [[jacqueline-reynolds-1930|Jacqueline Lucy Reynolds]] (1930–2014) married in 1954 at Newcastle-under-Lyme, Staffordshire. They met at Liverpool University.

Alan was the younger son of [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland]] and [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy]], born in Portsmouth as Alan Geoffrey Bunker. His father changed the family name to Bowers in 1936 when Alan was eight.

Jacqueline studied at Liverpool University and then the London School of Economics (post-graduate certificate in Child Care, 1951–1952). During her time at LSE she lived with Ivy and Roland at 32 Park Hill, [[harpenden|Harpenden]] — getting to know her future in-laws a year before the wedding.

## Children

1. Andrew Nicholas Bowers (b. 1959, New Zealand)
2. Richard Ian Bowers (b. 1962, New Zealand)
3. Philippa Jane Bowers (b. 1964, New Zealand)

## Family Life

After their wedding, Alan trained at Worcester Royal Infirmary (1954–1956) while Jacqueline worked as a social worker in Worcester.

In 1957, they emigrated to [[auckland|Auckland]], New Zealand. There were no jobs for newly qualified doctors in post-war Britain — Alan's entire Liverpool medical school class had to emigrate. The passage was arranged through Jacqueline's relative Laurie Copeland, a captain with the Port Line (England–NZ trade route). Alan worked as ship's doctor, which paid for one fare.

All three children were born in New Zealand. In 1968, eleven years after Alan and Jacqueline's emigration, Ivy (68) and Roland (70) followed them to Auckland to spend their retirement nearby. Roland left behind their elder son [[norman-kenneth-bowers-1925|Norman]], who stayed in the UK with the Royal Navy.

Alan died in 2012. Jacqueline died in 2014.
