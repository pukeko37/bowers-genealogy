## Parents

**[[daniel-brightman-1718|Daniel Brightman]]** (abt 1718–1787) married **[[anne-bunker-1732|Anne Bunker]]** (1732–1800) on 3 September 1753 at Millbrook — the year Anne turned 21, and the year before their first child was born. Daniel was about fourteen years her senior; his origins are not documented. He died in 1787, leaving Anne to raise the younger children alone. She died at Ampthill on 20 April 1800, aged about 68.

## Children

Twelve children between 1754 and 1778, matching the total of Anne's own mother Mary Reade. Of the twelve, at least five died before reaching adulthood.

## Family life

This family represents Anne Bunker's branch — a Brightman family rooted in the Millbrook / Ampthill area in the second half of the eighteenth century. Anne's mother Mary Reade had twelve children; Anne matched that total exactly. The pattern of infant and early deaths also mirrors the wider experience: four of the twelve did not reach adulthood, and one (Josias) died at seven.

Anne was widowed in 1787 when the youngest child Ruth was only nine years old. She lived another thirteen years as a widow, dying in 1800. Her daughter Anne Brightman died the same year.

## Wider family

Anne was the fourth child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and Mary Reade. The Brightman family's connections beyond this household are not yet researched.
