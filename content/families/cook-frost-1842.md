## Parents

[[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] (1816–1900) married Elizabeth Frost (1818–1843) on 8 May 1842 at Kensworth, Hertfordshire. Elizabeth died in 1843 — the same year as her son Charles's birth — likely from complications of childbirth. David subsequently married [[charlotte-matthews-1823|Charlotte Matthews]] in 1856; see [[cook-matthews-whipsnade]].

Elizabeth Frost's origins are not documented beyond her birth year (1818). No page has been created for her.

## Children

Three children are listed in the David Cook FamilySearch Family Tree 1 print view (LRWN-XV2):

1. **Mary Ann Cook** (FamilySearch G4LP-4D7, 1839–Deceased) — listed as born 1839, three years before the 1842 marriage. She may have been born out of wedlock before her parents' formal marriage, or the marriage date in FamilySearch may contain an error.
2. **[[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook]]** (FamilySearch G4LP-JD7, 1843–1908) — the Chequers Inn licensee 1904–1908; married [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois Brandsom]] 1868; eight children.
3. **[[james-cook-1843|James Cook]]** (FamilySearch 9KCT-R5M, 1843–Deceased) — listed with the same birth year as Charles; possibly a twin, or a short birth interval has been lost. Confirmed as the James Cook of Harpenden, Hertfordshire, who had children 1876–1882 — FamilySearch links his son [[thomas-cook-1882|Thomas Edward Cook]] (1882–1955) to David Cook and Elizabeth Frost. Thomas is buried at Kensworth, the same parish where David and Elizabeth married. See [[cook-harpenden-1876]].

## Family Life

After Elizabeth's death, David was left with at least two young children in 1843. The 1851 census may show one or more of these children in the household of their uncle George Cook (1815) — suggesting David may have had family support in raising the orphaned children. David himself is recorded in Charlotte Matthews's household by the mid-1850s, suggesting he moved to Whipsnade and remarried by 1856.

## Significance

This family page resolves a key error from an earlier research session, which had attributed [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook (1843–1908)]] to George Cook (1815) based on an 1851 census record. The David Cook Family Tree 1 print view is authoritative: Charles was David's son, making him [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]]'s nephew through her brother David — the same familial relationship but through a different sibling than previously believed.
