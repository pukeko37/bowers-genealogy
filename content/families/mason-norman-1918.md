## Parents

[[isaac-mason-1882|Isaac Mason]] (Ike, 1882–1955) and [[alice-norman-1890|Alice Norman]] (1890–1980) married in 1918. He was 36, she was 28. This was the second of the double Mason-Norman marriages — Isaac's brother John had married Alice's sister Mabel in 1912.

Isaac was born in Clerkenwell and grew up at St Sepulchre Newgate — directly adjacent to Smithfield and Hosier Lane, where the Norman family lived. He was the youngest of the orphaned Mason brothers. See [[mason-james-1874]].

## Family Life

Isaac ran a newsagent at 4 Yukon Road, Balham. Alice and Isaac took in her aunt Elizabeth Birchmore (1862–1954) — [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen's]] sister, who had never married. Elizabeth lived with them for over 33 years, from around 1921 until her death in 1954 at age 92. In the 1939 Register, the household at Yukon Road was Alice, Isaac, and Elizabeth (then 77).

Ivy's narrative remembers Nellie working in the family shop "alongside Ike."

Isaac died in 1955. Alice was widowed and eventually moved to Kent, where she was cared for by her nephew George Stewart Main (son of sister Elizabeth Ann). Alice died in 1980 in Kent, aged 90.

## Significance

The double Mason-Norman marriage — brothers marrying sisters — created a tight mutual support network. The Masons provided employment (Nellie worked in John's shop), housing (Elizabeth Birchmore lived with Isaac and Alice for decades), and stability for a family that had lost its patriarch in 1907.
