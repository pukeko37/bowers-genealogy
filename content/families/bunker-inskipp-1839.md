## Parents

**[[joel-bunker-1789|Joel Bunker]]** (c.1782–1861) married **[[elizabeth-inskipp-1792|Elizabeth Inskipp]]** (1792–unknown) on **16 April 1839** at Flitton with Silsoe, Bedfordshire. This was Joel's second marriage, three years after the death of his first wife [[martha-taylor-1786|Martha Taylor]] in October 1836. At the time of the marriage Joel was running the Green Man Inn at Eversholt; he left Eversholt in 1846 and the couple eventually appear in the 1861 census at Fairlight, Sussex.

No children from this marriage are recorded in FamilySearch.
