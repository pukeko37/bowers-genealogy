## Parents

**[[william-thody-1774|William Thody]]** (abt 1774 – 7 August 1858) was a soldier of the 40th Regiment of Foot, born in Cardington, Bedfordshire, son of Samuel Thody and Elizabeth Pearsey. By the time of this marriage he had already buried his first wife, Alice Gardner, whom he had married in Hastings in January 1804 while stationed there.

**Elizabeth** (maiden name unknown; formerly wife of James Meyers) was a widow with a son, William Myers, born at Elvas in Portugal. She married William Thody at **Badajoz, Spain**, around 1809 — during the Peninsular War, while the 40th Regiment was on campaign. She herself was in Cardington, Bedfordshire, by December 1814, petitioning the parish for support for her son William Myers while William was "serving in America" (the War of 1812). Nothing further is known of Elizabeth's life or death.

## Children

- **[[allen-thody-1813|Allen Thody]]** (5 October 1813 – 22 March 1898), born in Spain during the Peninsular War. He returned to Cardington — his father's birthplace — and spent his entire life there as a farm labourer.

William Myers (Elizabeth's son from her first marriage to James Meyers) is a stepchild in this household, not Allen's blood sibling. His subsequent fate is unknown.

## Family life

This family existed in the most unusual of circumstances — born of a wartime marriage in occupied Spain, scattered almost immediately by continued campaigning. Elizabeth and the children were in Cardington in December 1814; William was in North America. He returned in time to fight at Waterloo in June 1815, was wounded, and was discharged from the army in 1816 with a Chelsea pension. The family never apparently reunited in a single household: Elizabeth remained in Bedfordshire (at Cotton End, Cardington parish), while William settled in Chelsea.

Allen Thody grew up in Cardington. He could not write — like his father, he signed his mark on his children's documents. He married Elizabeth Saunders in 1834 and remained in the same Cardington parishes his entire life: Cotton End, Eastcotts. His father William died in Chelsea in 1858, when Allen was 44.
