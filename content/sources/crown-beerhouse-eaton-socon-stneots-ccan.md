## Source

St Neots Community and Local History Archive (CCAN): "Crown Beerhouse, Great North Road, Eaton Socon — 1920s"
URL: https://st-neots.ccan.co.uk/content/catalogue_item/crown-beerhouse-great-north-road-eaton-socon-1920s

The page includes a 1920s photograph of the Crown Beerhouse and keeper records. Direct access was blocked (403) during research; details were recovered from search engine snippets.

## Key Facts

- **Premises:** Crown Beerhouse, Great North Road, Eaton Socon
- **Keeper 1922–1931:** Albert Norman
- **Owner:** Mrs Archbald
- **Current status:** The Crown Inn, 1 Great North Road, Eaton Socon, PE19 8EN — still operating as a pub (Chef & Brewer chain)

## Interpretation

**Albert Norman** is almost certainly **Albert Newman Norman (1888–1970)**, sixth child of [[tom-norman-1856|Tom Norman]] and [[emily-thulborn-1856|Emily Thulborn]], listed in the 1911 census and in FamilySearch records.

Tom Norman's 1911 census occupation at Eaton Socon was "Farm Labourer and Beerhouse Keeper." Given that his son Albert was the Crown Beerhouse keeper from 1922, the most likely reading is that **Tom was the Crown Beerhouse keeper in 1911**, and Albert took over from him — either while Tom was still alive (Tom died 1925, Albert started 1922) or shortly after.

## Confidence

**Probable, not confirmed.** The 1911 census image (RG14/173/21, schedule 11) has not been examined and would show the actual address. The keeper list before Albert has not been obtained — the CCAN archive or Sue Jarrett's booklet ("The Inns and Outs of Eaton Socon Parish", 80pp, covers beerhouses extensively) would confirm whether Tom held the Crown before Albert.

## Further Research

- **1911 census image:** RG14/173/21, schedule 11 — would confirm street address
- **Sue Jarrett, "The Inns and Outs of Eaton Socon Parish"** (available from diandsaulbooks.co.uk) — lists full keeper sequences for all Eaton Socon licensed premises including the Crown Beerhouse
- **Huntingdonshire licensing records** at county archives — annual renewals would name the keeper
