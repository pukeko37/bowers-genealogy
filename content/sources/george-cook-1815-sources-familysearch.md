## Notes

Only two sources documented — both birth records. No marriage, death, or census records appear in this FamilySearch entry. George is one of the less-documented siblings of Ruth Cooke and David Cook. He should not be confused with his nephew [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook]] (1843–1908, the Chequers Inn licensee), who is the son of George's brother David, not of George himself.

Note the discrepancy in birth location: George was born in Luton (the market town), while his siblings are christened at Kensworth. The Baptist register in Luton likely served a wider area including families living nearby — this is consistent with Daniel Cook's family living in Whipsnade/Kensworth.
