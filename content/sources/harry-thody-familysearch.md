## Key Facts

- **Birth registration:** 1894, Luton, Bedfordshire (Luton district covered Markyate area)
- **Born:** 26 August 1894, Markyate, Hertfordshire (confirmed by WWI service record and 1939 National Register)

### 1901 Census — Studham, Bedfordshire
Household: William Allen Thody (50, Police Constable, born Cotton End, Bedfordshire); Lucy Thody (44, born Whipsnade); children: Clarence Archibald Thody (16, born Dunstable), Florence Lena Thody (11, born Totternhoe), Harry Thody (6, born Markyate). William and Lucy married 6 February 1894 in Whipsnade; Harry was their only child together. Clarence and Florence are William's children from his first marriage (to Mathilde Elizabeth Parent).

### 1911 Census — Flamstead, Hertfordshire
William had died between 1901 and 1911. Household: Lucy Thody (54, widowed, head, born Whipsnade); Harry Thody (16, son, born Markyate, apprentice upholsterer); Lena Florence Thody (21, step-daughter — i.e., William's daughter from first marriage). Also in household: James Ernest Read (26, born Owston, Leicestershire) and Arthur Edward Harold Read (19, born Owston, Leicestershire) — likely lodgers.

### WWI Service Records

**Bedfordshire Regiment, No. 19831** (service record):
Harry volunteered September 1914, age 20. Birthplace recorded as Hertfordshire; residence Dunstable, Bedfordshire.

**Royal Army Service Corps — published account:**
*"THODY, H., Private, Royal Army Service Corps. He volunteered in September 1914 and in the following year was drafted to France, where he served on the Somme, at Arms [Arras], Passchendaele, Lens and Cambrai, engaged in Transporting supplies to the front lines, and was wounded. He also served throughout the Retreat and Advance of 1918, and was demobilised in February 1919. He holds the 1914-15 Star, and the General Service and Victory Medals. Studham, near Dunstable. 693/A"*

### Marriage Certificate — 2 August 1920
Certified copy from General Register Office. No. 253. Parish Church, Little Gaddesden, Hertfordshire. By licence.

- **Harry Thody:** age 25, Bachelor, Upholsterer, residing Little Gaddesden; father William Allan Thody, Deceased
- **Beulah Rebecca Lucy Bunker:** age 24, Spinster, residing 50 Hampton Road, Luton; father Frederick William Tall Bunker, Bricklayer

Married by Rector Thomas Syondze. Witnesses: **R. H. Bunker** ([[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland Bunker]], Beulah's brother) and **N. W. Norman** ([[norah-winifred-norman-1902|Norah Winifred Norman]], [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy Norman]]'s youngest sister, aged 18).

Note: 2 August was Beulah's 24th birthday. The two witnesses represent the two families about to be joined: Roland for the Bunkers, Norah for the Normans — confirming that Harry was already close to the Norman family nearly a year before Roland and Ivy married.

### 1939 National Register — 9 Oakley Road, Luton
Entry indexed as "Marcy Thody" (transcription error — this is Harry). Born 26 Aug 1894; married; occupation "Furniture Dealer M?" (partially obscured — "Furniture Dealer & Manufacturer"). Household also includes Beulah, Eliza Bunker (aged 80), and three redacted "UNKNOWN" entries (the living children).

### Beulah's Probate — 26 August 1948
Harry is listed alongside John Quint Clayton and Joseph Taylor in the probate record for Beulah Rebecca Lucy Thody (died 29 June 1948). Probate granted London 26 August 1948.

### 1968 Marriage Certificate — 6 July 1968
Certified copy from General Register Office. No. 9. Parish Church, St James, Silsoe, County of Bedford. By licence.

- **Harry Thody:** age 73, Widower, Company Director, residing 15 Bedford Avenue, Silsoe; father William Alan Thody (deceased), Policeman
- **Nellie Haze:** age 62, Widow, residing 2 Highfield Road, Luton; father Albert Edward Walker (deceased), Farmer

Married by Vicar B. L. Hixan. Witnesses: George William Baxter, Derek C. Holden.

Note: Harry's occupation as "Company Director" at age 73 — this was Thody Bros, the furniture firm he had run with Roland Bunker since ~1930. The company was wound up in 1970.
