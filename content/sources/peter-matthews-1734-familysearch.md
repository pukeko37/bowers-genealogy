## Key Facts Established

**Birth:** 28 January 1734, Kensworth, Bedfordshire. **Christening:** 28 June 1734, Dunstable, Bedfordshire — a five-month gap suggesting the family was between parishes or the birth date is derived rather than recorded.

**Death:** 6 January 1812, Kensworth, Bedfordshire.

**Parents:** [[peter-matthews-1702|Peter Matthews (1702)]] and Mary Busby — confirmed by the christening record, which names the father as "Peter Matthews" and the mother as "Mary."

**Marriage:** 12 October 1761 at Flamstead, Hertfordshire, to **Susannah Durrant** (also spelled "Durrent" in the register). Three marriage record sources all confirm the Flamstead venue and date.

**Children confirmed by christening records, all at Kensworth:**
- Susannah Matthews — christened 30 November 1763, Kensworth
- Mary Matthews — christened 8 February 1767, Kensworth
- Peter Matthews — christened 7 January 1770, Kensworth

One additional record (1 Sep 1776, Upper Gravenhurst, Bedfordshire) names a "Susannah Matthews" baptised with parents Peter Matthews and Susannah Matthews. This may be a second Susannah (perhaps the 1763 daughter had died and the name was reused), or a misattachment in FamilySearch. Upper Gravenhurst is ~10 miles from Kensworth; treat with caution.

## Notes

The shift from Dunstable christenings (Peter 1702's family) to Kensworth christenings (Peter 1734's family) marks a geographic transition in this line. By 1761 Peter (1734) was marrying at Flamstead, Hertfordshire — just over the county border — and by 1763 all his children's christenings were at Kensworth. The family had fully settled into the Kensworth/Flamstead orbit.
