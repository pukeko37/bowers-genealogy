### Marriage — 21 February 1833, Fenstanton

Two Huntingdonshire Parish Register sources and one England Marriages index all document the same event. One source gives 17 Feb, two give 21 Feb; the Fenstanton-specific source (place original: "Fen Stanton, Huntingdon") gives 21 February as the more precisely localised record. Thomas was **Single** (first marriage); Mary Ann James was a widow.

### Alfred Eusden christening — 25 December 1834, Fenstanton

Two sources (England Births and Christenings; Huntingdonshire Parish Registers) record the baptism of [[alfred-eusden-1834|Alfred Eusden]] on Christmas Day 1834 at Fenstanton. Father: Thomas Eusden. Mother: Mary Ann. Alfred was christened approximately nine months before his father's burial.
