FamilySearch print view for Rebecca Tall, Frederick's mother.

**Vitals:** Born 1844, Bedfordshire. Died 7 July 1870, buried 7 July 1870, Ampthill, Bedfordshire.

**Residence:** 1851 Maulden, Bedfordshire (census).

**Marriage:** Caleb Bunker, 25 December 1868, Howard Chapel, Mill Street, Bedford, Bedfordshire.

**Children:** Frederick William Tall Bunker (1869–1932).

Rebecca married Caleb on Christmas Day 1868 and died less than two years later, when her son Frederick was approximately one year old. She was buried in Ampthill — the same area where Frederick was born and registered. Her father [[edward-tall-1806|Edward Tall]] died just nine days after her, on 16 July 1870.
