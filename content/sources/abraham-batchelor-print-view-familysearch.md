## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1825, Whipsnade (christened 20 March 1825, Whipsnade)
- **Died:** 1892, Luton, Bedfordshire
- **Residences:** 1841, 1851, 1861 Whipsnade; 1871, 1881 Studham; 1891 Whipsnade; Frog Corner; By Whipsnade Green

## Notes

Abraham's residence pattern is highly significant: in Whipsnade throughout 1841–1861, then Studham 1871–1881, returning to Whipsnade by 1891. The move away from Whipsnade aligns with GBP Sr and Ruth Cooke taking the Chequers licence in December 1867 — Abraham almost certainly held the licence until around that point and vacated when GBP Sr took over. His addresses "Frog Corner" and "By Whipsnade Green" place him in the same Whipsnade Green hamlet as Thomas Bachelor (his father), the Matthews and Cook families, and Ruth Cooke.

Relationship to [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]] is not yet confirmed by clippings. His father Thomas Bachelor (1781, Flamstead) and Eliza's probable father Thomas Batchelar (1803, Dagnall) are from neighbouring villages — the exact family connection between the two Thomas Batchelar/Bachelor lines is the outstanding research question.
