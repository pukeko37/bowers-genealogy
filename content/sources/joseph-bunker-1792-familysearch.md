FamilySearch person MM18-MKN. Print view and 14-source sources page.

**Vitals:** Born 3 December 1792. FamilySearch gives birthplace as "Saint Pauls Walden, Hertfordshire" — the village where his parents married in 1788. Likely a data anomaly; his siblings were all born in Millbrook. Christened 7 April 1806, Millbrook. Died 21 October 1853; GRO death registration vol 3B, page 187, Bedford district. Find a Grave gives burial at **Stevington Baptist Chapel Chapelyard** — confirming Baptist affiliation in Stevington; the "Millbrook" death location on the FamilySearch print view is likely a tree error.

**Residences:** 1841 at Silver Street, Stevington. 1851 at Stevington as Agricultural Labourer, aged 58 (birthplace given as "Poleswarden, Hertfordshire" — corrupted transcription, probably "Polsden" or similar error for Millbrook/Bedfordshire).

**Marriage:** Mary Markham (1793–1866), 11 October 1814, Stevington.

**Children** (all christened Stevington):
- Ann Bunker (1816–1843)
- John Bunker (1817–1887)
- William Bunker (1821–1821) — died in infancy
- Ebenezer Bunker (1822–1902) — emigrated to Australia with cousin Ephraim Bunker (son of Caleb); married in Australia (to be confirmed)
- James Bunker (1824–1904) — married Stevington 25 Dec 1850 (wife Sophia Pantar)
- Samuel Bunker (1827–1829) — died in infancy

**Note:** Marriage at Stevington one year before Caleb's marriage there (1815). The two eldest brothers appear to have moved to Stevington together c.1814. Joseph was buried at Stevington Baptist Chapel, confirming Baptist affiliation — distinct from the Wesleyan Methodist connection in the main Bunker line.
