## Overview

Saints by Sea passenger records for Able Matthews and family on the **Liverpool to Philadelphia** voyage, **27 February 1855 – 20 April 1855**. Source: LDS British Mission Registers (BMR), p. 111.

Abel (KWJZ-9HJ) is confirmed as [[able-matthews-1813|Able Matthews]] by FamilySearch ID. Origin given as "Bedford" rather than "Whipsnade" — consistent with Able having moved toward the Dunstable/Bedford area by this time.

## Mary Ann Walker was alive at departure

**Mary A. W. Matthews (37, Wife)** — the initials "A. W." match "Ann Walker." She is [[mary-walker-1817|Mary Ann Walker]], Able's wife, confirming she was alive when the family sailed in February 1855. She died in **St. Louis, Missouri** sometime between April 1855 and June 1861 (when Able joined the David H. Cannon Company overland crossing as a widower). Her death in St. Louis occurred after arrival in America, not during the sea crossing.

## Children — matching known records

- **Sarah (14):** matches Sarah Matthews (~1841) in the 1851 census (age 10). Fate after St. Louis untraced.
- **Elizabeth (8):** matches Elizabeth Matthews (~1847) in the 1851 census (age 4). Fate untraced.
- **William (6):** matches William Abel Matthews Sr. (b. 18 March 1848, Whipsnade), KWJH-VZZ / MGP6-STH — later settled Virgin, Utah; died 1917.
- **Ann/Anna (recorded as 11 months):** Able Matthews had a daughter **Anna** born **23 February 1853** — not in the 1851 census (taken March 1851, before her birth). She is almost certainly this passenger. The BMR records her age as "11 months" but she was actually ~24 months at departure (2 years and 4 days). Clerk error in age, secure identification on name and family context. Fate unknown; she does not appear in any later Utah records, and likely died in St. Louis.

## Note on ship name

The FamilySearch sources previously cited the ship "Siddons" for Able's 1855 voyage. Saints by Sea confirms the voyage dates (Liverpool to Philadelphia, 27 Feb – 20 Apr 1855) but does not name the ship in the individual passenger records. "Siddons" may be correct from another source; this record neither confirms nor contradicts it.
