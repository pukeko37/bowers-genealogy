## Data Quality Note

The JSON contains a `pregnancy_status` field for the 1859 Birchmore–Batchelor marriage: *"Pregnant at wedding (Ellen born 9 months later)"*. This is an error in the source data. Ellen being born nine months after the wedding provides no evidence of pre-marital pregnancy — she could have been conceived at or after the wedding. This claim has been removed from all wiki pages derived from this source. Do not use `pregnancy_status` from this JSON as a reliable fact.

## Summary

Comprehensive compiled research file covering the extended family of Ivy Helen Norman (1899–1994). Structured JSON with detailed records for the Birchmore/Plummer, Norman, Mason, Bunker/Bowers, and Thody families. Spans 1786–2014. Contains biographical narratives, addresses, occupations, census references, wartime experiences, emigration details, and analysis of recurring patterns.

## Key Content Not in Other Sources

- **Bunker/Bowers family**: Ron's parents Frederick Bunker & Eliza Geary, grandparents Caleb Bunker & Rebecca Tall, sister Beulah
- **1936 name change mystery**: Roland changed family name from Bunker to Bowers, never explained
- **Mason family**: Welsh immigrants Richard & Catherine Mason, their orphaned sons, double marriage into Norman family
- **Norman children details**: Occupations, wartime addresses, and life stories for all 9 siblings
- **Alan & Jacqueline**: Medical career, emigration to NZ in 1957, children Andrew (1959), Richard (1962), Philippa (1964)
- **Recurring patterns**: Illegitimacy, early parental death, widowhood survival, never-married women, family businesses as mutual support
- **The Mormon connection**: Anne Matthews's family emigrated to Utah in 1853 as Mormon pioneers; Anne chose to stay with husband George
- **George Birchmore Plummer**: Held license for The Chequers pub, Whipsnade
- **Care networks**: George Stewart Main cared for three relatives in Kent for 45 years
