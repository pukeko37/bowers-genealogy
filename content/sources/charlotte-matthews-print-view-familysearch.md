- **Alternate names:** Charlotte Matthews Cook, Charlotte Mathews, Charlottie Matthews

## Notes

Charlotte remained in Whipsnade her entire life while siblings Able and Daniel Willis emigrated to Utah. She married [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]], making her the sister-in-law of [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] (GBP Sr's third wife). The "Charlotte Matthews Cook" alternate surname in FamilySearch is a data entry convention, not a birth name — Cook was her married name. She died in Luton in 1910, 52 years after her sister Anne.
