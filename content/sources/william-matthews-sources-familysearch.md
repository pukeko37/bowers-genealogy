FamilySearch person page KWJH-VZZ for William Matthews (b. 1806). Five sources attached.

**Christening (20 Apr 1806, Kensworth, Hertfordshire):** "England, Births and Christenings, 1538-1975". Father: Thomas Mathews; Mother: Mary. Christened at Kensworth, Hertford, England.

**1851 census (Whipsnade, Bedfordshire):** William Mathews, male, age 45 (b. ~1806, Kensworth, Hertfordshire), unmarried, Agricultural Labourer, son in household of Thomas Mathews (head, 84) and Mary Mathews (70). Piece/Folio 1757/219, HO107.

**LDS membership record:** Baptised LDS 5 April 1848 at Bedfordshire, England. (Two duplicate membership records confirm this date.)

**Utah, Mormon Pioneer Overland Travel Database:** William Matthews (b. 1 Jan 1806) departed 13 Jun 1853 with the **Cyrus H. Wheelock Company**; arrived Utah 16 Oct 1853. FamilySearch IDs of company members in record: KWJH-VZZ, LDTY-LGW, 21RR-99L, K11V-CLM, KLLJ-2Q7, K89X-SGS, KHG9-WQ1.

**Key correction:** William Matthews was previously documented as a non-emigrant ("stayed in England"). This source confirms he emigrated to Utah in 1853 on the same company as his brother [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis]].
