FamilySearch sources page for Emma Geary (1856–1874), aggregating 7 attached sources including her christening, census returns 1861 and 1871, death registration, and Find a Grave burial record.

**Key facts confirmed:**

- Christened 20 Jan 1856, Haynes, Bedfordshire. Parents: John Geary and Lucy.
- Birth registration 1856, Ampthill, Bedfordshire (Volume 3B, p. 369).
- 1861 census: Scholar, age 5, Church End, Haynes.
- 1871 census: Listed twice — once at parental home as Platter (age 15, Haynes), and once as Servant (age 15) in the household of Matilda Pratt, St Peter, Bedford. The double appearance is likely a census indexing error; the servant entry may be a misidentification.
- Death registration: 1874, Bedford, Bedfordshire (Volume 3B, p. 186). Age 18.
- Burial: 31 July 1874, Foster Hill Road Cemetery, Bedford (Find a Grave).

Emma died unmarried, aged 18, at Bedford — the only sibling after young childhood deaths to die in early adulthood. She was the third of the siblings to die at Bedford's Foster Hill Road Cemetery (the others being Phoebe in 1910 and Lydia Rose in 1932).
