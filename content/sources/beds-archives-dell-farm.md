Bedford Borough Council community history page on Dell Farm, Whipsnade, drawing on Bedfordshire Archives holdings including the Pedley Settled Estates auction particulars (ref Z214/3, annotated with buyers' names and prices paid).

## Key Facts Extracted

**Ownership sequence:**
- John Sutton (died 1795) → Isaac Dell → John Pedley (1827) → Arthur Macnamara (by 1842) → sold at auction 1915 → James Hain (purchased 1915)
- Post-1967: Luton County Borough; now Dell Farm Outdoor Education Centre

**Tenancy sequence:**
- Thomas Fawcett (1841–42)
- Henry Perkins (1851–1871) — 125–142 acres; confirmed across three censuses
- William Gladman (1882) — 141 acres; building repairs £866/14/8 in this period
- James Hain & James Purton Turvey jointly (1905) — 132 acres at £132/7/-
- James Hain sole tenant (by 1911)

**1915 auction:** Pedley Settled Estates, 2,050 acres across five parishes + Hertfordshire. Auctioneer Joseph Stower, Chancery Lane. Dell Farm lot: 125 acres, 3 roods, 39 perches. Farmhouse described as "superior residence" — 5 bedrooms, dining and drawing rooms. James Hain purchased at auction.

**1926 valuation:** James Hain described as "Good Farmer." Homestead described as "badly arranged."

## Cross-Reference
