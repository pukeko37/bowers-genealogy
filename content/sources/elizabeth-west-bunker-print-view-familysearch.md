## Significance

Elizabeth West Bunker took her mother Maria West's surname as a middle name — a naming practice also seen in her brother [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph (1840)]] who is surnamed Bunker but this is a family pattern. She was the eldest child of John (1803) and Maria West. Her marriage at Marston Moretaine (April 1845), very shortly after the birth of her first child John (1846), means she may have left Millbrook in late 1844 or early 1845. She then settled at Maulden — just 3 miles from Millbrook — and remained in the area for the rest of her life.

Her 10 Gillet children are not part of the Bunker tree by surname but are Bunker-descended. None are further researched.
