Find a Grave listing of all memorials recorded at St Mary the Virgin Churchyard, Studham, Bedfordshire (cemetery ID 2399576). Approximately 430 memorials across 22 pages. Clipped 2026-06-06.

## Relevance to this wiki

**William Allan Thody (1851 – 1 Feb 1911)** — confirmed buried at Studham. The FAG year of birth (1851) is a minor variant on our established date of 11 November 1850; this is the expected kind of rounding in memorial transcriptions. Burial at Studham was already documented in our wiki from the funeral card and parish burial register, so this is corroboration only.

**Lucy Thody (1857 – 25 Jan 1930)** — confirmed buried at Studham alongside William. The gravestone date is **25 January 1930**, which conflicts with our existing FamilySearch-sourced date of **25 February 1930**. The inscribed date on a gravestone is typically more reliable for the day and month. The birth year 1857 (FAG) vs. 1856 (our wiki) is the same minor rounding seen with William. See [[lucy-cook-1856]] for the updated note.

**Captain Clarence James Thody (16 Oct 1884 – 30 Aug 1918)** — appears in the Studham churchyard record despite being killed in France and buried at Manchester Cemetery, Riencourt-lès-Bapaume (CWGC Plot A.11). This is almost certainly a **memorial inscription** added to a family grave or church wall, not an interment. Clarence grew up in Studham in his father's household; it is natural that the family would have commemorated him at their home parish church.

**Noel Jesse Turvey (25 Dec 1896 – 1 Nov 1978)** and **Millie Turvey (14 Jun 1898 – 9 Sep 1973)** — buried at Studham rather than Whipsnade. These are likely a branch of the Whipsnade Turvey family who settled across the parish boundary. James Purton Turvey (the Dell Farm co-tenant who died 1910) was at Whipsnade; Noel and Millie may be his children or those of a sibling, born in the mid-1890s and raising their family at Studham. No pages have been created for them — noted here as a research lead.

**Alfred Brown Bramson (1873 – 20 Dec 1950)** — buried at Studham under the spelling "Bramson" (vs. "Bransom" in the Whipsnade family). An unnamed "??? Bramson" with unknown dates is also listed, suggesting at least two. Whether this is the same family under a variant spelling or a distinct family sharing a similar surname is unresolved.

## Names not connected to this wiki

The churchyard contains a large number of families with no established connection to the research scope: Allen, Bailey, Barnard, Belfield, Bishop, Butler, Elms, Foskett, Gladman, Hawes, Hoar, Hull, Rance, Tarsey, Thorne, Waters, and many more. These are not currently relevant but may provide parish context if the research expands.
