Ten FamilySearch print views for the surviving children of [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker (1840–1914)]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox (1842–after 1921)]]. Each print view supplies vitals, residence history, marriage(s) and children, and the parents' and siblings' list. The sibling list is identical across all ten — confirming the family structure.

## Key facts extracted

**Richard Bunker (1859–Deceased)**
- Born 19 Dec 1859, Millbrook; christened 4 Mar 1860; registered Ampthill 1860
- Residences: Millbrook 1861/1871; South Normanton, Derbyshire and Nottinghamshire 1891 (two records — may reflect an ambiguous census entry); Flitwick, Bedfordshire 1901/1911
- Married Emma Page, 27 Mar 1882, Millbrook. Harold William *Page* Bunker born 1881 — pre-marital child, middle name acknowledging mother's maiden name.
- Ten children: Harold William Page (1881–1917), Elizabeth Fanny (1883–1885), Lilly (1885–1964), Bertha Florence (1889–1961), Henry George (1890–1982), Richard (1891–1966), Joseph (1894–1917), John (1897–1970), Ethel May (1899–1992), Thomas (1901–1998). Two sons (Harold, Joseph) died in WWI.

**Thomas Bunker (1862–1902)**
- Born 1862, Millbrook; christened 20 Apr 1862
- Residences: Millbrook 1871/1881; Nottinghamshire 1891; South Normanton, Derbyshire 1901
- Married Charlotte Roberts, 25 Dec 1885, Moggerhanger, Bedfordshire. No children documented.
- Buried 17 Apr 1902, South Normanton — died aged about 40.

**Albert Bunker (1865–Deceased)**
- Born 1865, Millbrook; christened 4 Jun 1865; registered Ampthill 1865

- Married Emma Roberts, 11 Apr 1887, Moggerhanger, Bedfordshire. Eleven children with high infant mortality: Annie (1888–1888), Alice Maud (1889–1893), Ethel May (1891–1892), Harold Edward (1893–1969), Arthur James (1895–1916, WWI), Frank (1897–1897), Nellie (1898–?), Elsie (1900–1982), Hilda (1902–1959), Thomas John (1903–1903), Walter (1905–?).

**George Bunker (1868–1916)**

- Residences: Millbrook 1871/1881; Nottinghamshire 1911
- Died 7 June 1916, Grimsby, Lincolnshire; buried South Normanton, Derbyshire
- First wife: Catherine Ward (1875–1902), married 25 Jan 1896, Mansfield, Nottinghamshire. Children: Annie (1896–?), Thomas (1899–?), Dorothy (1902–?). Catherine died 1902, possibly in childbirth.
- Second wife: Annie Maria Elston (1886–?), married 1908, Nottinghamshire. Child: Frank (1909–1993).

## Notable patterns

- **Migration to the South Normanton coalfields**: Thomas, Albert, George, and James all appear in Nottinghamshire by 1891, and South Normanton, Derbyshire by 1901. This is a wholesale migration of the elder brothers, probably beginning in the late 1880s following James's lead (he married there in January 1893, at age 19 — he must have moved as a teenager). The driver was almost certainly coal mining employment. See [[south-normanton]].
- **Roberts sisters at Moggerhanger**: Thomas married Charlotte Roberts (1885) and Albert married Emma Roberts (1887), both at Moggerhanger, Bedfordshire. Likely sisters from the same Roberts family.
- **WWI deaths among grandchildren**: Albert Edward Bunker (James's son, 1897–1916), Arthur James Bunker (Albert's son, 1895–1916), Harold William Page Bunker (Richard's son, 1881–1917), Joseph Bunker (Richard's son, 1894–1917), Albert Edward Bunker (John's son, 1894–1917).
- **Christening date anomaly**: George (1868), John (1871), and James (1873) all show christening date 19 October 1873 in FamilySearch. Plausible for James; almost certainly an error or a batch christening for George and John.
