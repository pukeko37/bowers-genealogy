**Key facts:**

- **Residence**: 150 Acre Lane, Brixton, London SW2 — the same address as his brother John's shop. Richard was living there as a resident, not just a visitor. "Brixton" and "Clapham North" are both used for this address; it sits on the boundary.
- **Died in hospital**: Olympic Hospital, London SE24 (Herne Hill / Tulse Hill area), not at 150 Acre Lane. He was taken to hospital before death.
- **Administrator**: [[john-james-mason-1878|John James Mason]], described as **"subpostmasters assistant"** — this is John's occupation at the time. It reveals that the 150 Acre Lane business functioned as a **sub-post office**, a common adjunct to newsagent/stationer shops. John was the sub-postmaster or his assistant.
- **Effects**: £221 8s. 3d. — a modest estate. Richard owned little; he was a lodger at his brother's address. Compare: Isaac Mason (1955) £7102; John James Mason (1962) £344.
