FamilySearch print view and sources page (15 sources) for Joseph Sinfield (M984-6XV).

## Vitals

**Christened:** 28 January 1844, All Saints' Church, Kempston, Bedfordshire. Birth registered Bedford 1844 (GRO vol 6, page 34). Census records consistently give birthplace as "Bedford" — Kempston was a separate parish immediately adjoining Bedford.

**Died:** 12 April 1914, Luton, Bedfordshire, age 70 (GRO vol 3B, page 429). Obituary in *Luton Bedfordshire Advertiser and Luton Times*, 17 April 1914 (text not retrieved — British Newspaper Archive).

## Parents

**William Sinfield** (born ~1808–1811, Kempston, Bedfordshire) — Labourer throughout census records.
**Sarah Sinfield** (born ~1812, "Wyberston Eaton"/Eynesbury area, Huntingdonshire) — from the Eaton Socon / St Neots border area.

The Sinfield family rooted in Kempston from at least the early 19th century. Kempston is approximately 8-10 miles from Millbrook — the same village where Thomas Bunker (1738) married a **Martha Sinfield** in 1761. Whether William Sinfield's Kempston family connects to those Millbrook Sinfields is unconfirmed.

## Children's later lives

**Annie Maria Sinfield** (born ~1864, Bedford) — by 1911 married to **Alfred John Hyde** (born ~1859, City of London), occupation **Blocker, Straw Hats industry**. Joseph is living with them in Luton in 1911 as "father-in-law." The straw hat industry runs through the entire family: Martha and Comfort Ann as sewers, and now Annie's husband as a blocker (who shapes hat bodies on wooden moulds).

**Elizabeth Julia Sinfield** (born ~1867, Bedford) — married **George Linger** on 24 December 1886 in Luton. Their son **Arthur Linger** (born ~1888) appears in the Sinfield household in 1891, aged 3.
