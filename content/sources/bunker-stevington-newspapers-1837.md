## Ephraim Bunker — stealing peas, 1837

*Bedfordshire Mercury, 5 August 1837.*

"Ephraim Bunker, and Charles and Robert Simpson were charged with stealing a quantity of peas at [a farm]. William Cole, the witness, said his father was a farmer at [Stevington]. On Saturday the 15th ult. between eight [o'clock...]"

Three boys charged with stealing peas from a farm near Stevington. Ephraim Bunker is one of the three; Charles and Robert Simpson are the others.

**Identification: [[caleb-bunker-1790|Caleb Bunker]]'s son Ephraim (born 1821).** In August 1837 he was 16 years old, living with his father at Stevington (Caleb's household confirmed at Stevington West End, 1841 census). The age fits perfectly for "a boy." This is the same Ephraim who later emigrated to Australia and died at Crookwell, New South Wales, on 5 July 1887.

The snippet is too brief to know the outcome of the case. The co-defendants (Simpson) are not Bunkers.
