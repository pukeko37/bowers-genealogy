## Key Facts

- **Born:** 11 October 1856, Swavesey, Cambridgeshire
- **Alternate name:** Thomas Norman (birth name)
- **Died:** 1925, Huntingdonshire, England
- **Birth registration:** St Ives, Swavesey (same registration district as siblings)
- **Residences:** Swavesey 1861, 1871; Alconbury Weston 1891, 1901; Eaton Socon/Nottinghamshire 1911
- **1911 census:** Eaton, Nottinghamshire (possible misattribution — Eaton Socon is on the Huntingdonshire/Bedfordshire border, not Nottinghamshire)

## Notes

Tom Norman (baptised Thomas) was the third of Thomas Norman's children, three years older than Charles. He was the only sibling who moved out of Swavesey parish, settling in Alconbury Weston by 1891 — his father appears to have followed him there by 1901. Tom died in Huntingdonshire 1925. His birth registration at St Ives confirms the family's connection to that registration district, the same for siblings Charles, Alfred Eusden, Robert Henry, and Mary Ann.
