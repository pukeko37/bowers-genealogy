## Data quality issues

The print view birth years are unreliable: three children are shown as born "1831" (William, Catherine, Susan) when the christening record for William gives 25 Dec 1829 and the 1841 census confirms Susan was age 9 (born ~1832). The duplication of two "William Bunker" children may reflect the family actually naming two sons William, or a FamilySearch merge error. The christening record gives 25 Dec 1829 for one William — the "1837" William may be a distinct second son or a data artefact.
