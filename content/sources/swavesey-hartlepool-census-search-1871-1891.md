FamilySearch filtered search: birthplace = Swavesey, Cambridgeshire; residence = Hartlepool, Durham; collections restricted to 1871, 1881, and 1891 censuses. Returned 56 records.

The search confirms that George Norman (1851) and Richard Norman (1853) appear in the 1881 Stranton results — consistent with all existing wiki records.

The wider results reveal a substantial and concentrated Swavesey migration to Stranton township, West Hartlepool. Analysis of the full dataset is in [[swavesey-stranton-diaspora]].

**Caveats:** The search was restricted to exact birthplace = "Swavesey." Census enumerators recorded birthplaces inconsistently; many Swavesey migrants may have given "Cambridgeshire" or approximate spellings (the results include "Swavesay", "Swansey", "Swansly?", "Swai?Sey", "Swanesey" as variants that were matched). The true number of Swavesey-born residents in the Hartlepool area is likely higher than 56.
