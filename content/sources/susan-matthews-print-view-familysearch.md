## Key Facts

- **Born:** 17 September 1800, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire
- **Christened:** 26 October 1800, Whipsnade
- **Died:** 20 November 1857, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire
- **Residences:** 1841, 1851 Whipsnade; 1871 entry belongs to Thomas's second wife (Susan Durent, née Maynard), not to Susan Matthews
- **Alternate names:** Susan, Susan Mathews
- **Parents:** Peter Matthews (father), Mary Walker (mother)
- **Husband:** Thomas Batchelar (married 22 May 1826, Shenley, Hertfordshire)
- **Children:** Emma, Eliza, Ann, Elizabeth

## Correction note (2026-05-20)

This source was initially filed under [[matthews-wilson-1801]], incorrectly attributing Susan to Thomas Matthews (1767) and Mary Wilson. The May 2026 ingest of the full FamilySearch sources list for this record (KZB3-93C) and for Thomas Batchelar establishes that Susan's father was Peter Matthews (1770) and her mother was Mary Walker — confirmed by the explicit parent listing in the christening record and the sources page. She married Thomas Batchelar and was Eliza Batchelor's mother. See [[susan-matthews-familysearch]] for the fuller source summary.
