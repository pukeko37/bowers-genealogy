The 1911 census household at St John at Hackney, Kingsland (RG14/477, schedule 236). Eleven people in one dwelling: ten Normans and one visitor.

**Thomas George Norman** (24) is listed as head of household — not Ellen. He was a warehouseman in the drapery and fancy lines trade, the principal working male in the household.

**Ellen Norman** (51) is listed as housekeeper, widowed. She managed the home.

**Occupations of the working children:**
- Mabel, Nellie, and Elizabeth — all machinists in the lace goods trade, consistent with the Kingsland/Hackney textile district
- Alice — clerk in the boot trade
- Lily Frances — clerk in an engineer's chemicals firm
- Charles Jr — clerk at the Port of London Authority

Ivy (11) and Norah (8) were still at school.

**The 11th person** was **Mary Elizabeth Angus** (25, Little Thurlow, Suffolk), listed as a visitor, fur liner in the furs trade. By 1939 she was at 6 Englefield Road, Kingsland — Thomas's address — and the index records their marriage in 1911 at DeBeauvoir Town. She was Thomas's future wife, visiting on census night before their marriage later that year.

**Birthplace pattern confirms the family narrative:**
- Mabel (1883), Nellie (1885), Thomas (1886), Elizabeth (1888) — born Islington; family was in the Islington/Hackney orbit before the Hosier Lane move
- Lily (1893), Charles (1895), Ivy (1899), Norah (1902) — born City of London; the Hosier Lane period
- Alice listed as Islington here (other records: Hackney, 1890) — minor discrepancy

**No Mason brothers were present.** All three (Richard William, John James, Isaac) were enumerated separately at Lambeth, Norwood in 1911.
