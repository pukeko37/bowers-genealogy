FamilySearch sources page for Richard Norman (P68D-T5X), with 16 attached sources. **This profile is significantly contaminated** — it has accumulated records from at least two, possibly three, different Richard Normans. Individual attachments should not be accepted without independent verification.

**What appears reliable:**

**Baptism (26 Nov 1723, Cottenham):** Confirmed by two independent entries in the Cambridgeshire Bishop's Transcripts and one in England Births and Christenings. Parents: William Norman and Alice (also given as Alice Norman / Alice Briggs). This is the solid foundation of the profile.

**Marriage to Mary Hall (1 Oct 1749, Over):** Not directly evidenced in the 16 sources listed here, but present in the print view and in Robert Norman's own FamilySearch profile. Accepted as credible.

**What is disputed or wrong:**

**Marriage to Mary Essex (27 Apr 1740, Cottenham):** The records confirm a marriage between *a* Richard Norman and Mary Essex at Cottenham in 1740 — but this marriage is also attached to a second FamilySearch profile, L565-ZVH (Richard Norman, 1718–1753), with a different and larger set of children. The Essex marriage cannot belong to both. Since Mary Essex survived to 1765 and our Richard married Mary Hall in 1749, accepting the Essex marriage for P68D-T5X creates a bigamy impossibility. The Essex marriage almost certainly belongs to L565-ZVH alone.

**"Robert Norman" (christened 11 Feb 1740, Cottenham):** Listed in the sources as a child of Richard Norman and Mary Essex. This is a *different* Robert Norman — born at Cottenham in 1740, entirely distinct from our [[robert-norman-1769|Robert Norman]] (born Over, 1769). Probably a child of L565-ZVH.

**The 1810 burial of "Robt Norman":** Listed as evidence confirming our Richard as father of Robert Norman. But our Robert Norman died in June 1842, not 1810. This burial record belongs to a different person and is spurious as parentage evidence for the 1769 Robert.

**Duplicate Richard Norman sons (1745 and 1746):** Two christening records for a "Richard Norman" son at Cottenham — one dated 11 Feb 1745, one dated 11 Feb 1746 — are almost certainly the same record appearing in two different source databases with a one-year transcription error.

**Daughter Mary Norman (born 31 Mar 1834):** Attached as Richard's daughter, fifty-one years after his death in 1783. An obvious error confirming the profile has been merged with descendants of the same name.

**"Richd Norman Mail" records (1768):** A baptism record at Cottenham lists "Richd Norman Mail" as father. "Mail" is an unusual surname element and may reflect a clerical misreading or a different family entirely. Attachment to P68D-T5X is unverified.
