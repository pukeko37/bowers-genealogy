Notice published in *The London Gazette*, Issue 24898, page 5592, dated 5 November 1880.

**The Bankruptcy Act, 1869. In the County Court of Derbyshire, holden at Derby.**

In the Matter of Proceedings for Liquidation by Arrangement or Composition with Creditors, instituted by **Caleb Bunker**, of Matlock Bath, in the county of Derby Boot and Shoe Maker, and Toy and Smallware Dealer, trading as **Thomas Barnsley**.

Notice is hereby given that a Second General Meeting of the creditors of the above-named person has been summoned to be held at the offices of Mr. James Potter, All Saints-chambers, No. 22, Irongate, in Derby, on the 15th day of November, 1880, at three o'clock in the afternoon precisely. Dated 4th day of November, 1880.

**Solicitor:** James Potter, All Saints-chambers, No. 22, Irongate, Derby.

- Dated 4 November 1880 (two days before first meeting on 2 November; notices run ahead of events)
- Same solicitor: James Potter, All Saints-chambers, No. 22, Irongate, Derby
