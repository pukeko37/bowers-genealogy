FamilySearch sources page (L227-TG7) and print view for Ebenezer Bunker, son of [[joseph-bunker-1792|Joseph Bunker (1792)]] and Mary Markham. 9 sources attached.

## Key facts established

**Birth:** 1822, Stevington, Bedfordshire. Christened **26 September 1824** at Stevington — same mass christening as his cousin Ephraim (Caleb's son).

**1841 census:** In father Joseph's household at Silver Street, Stevington. Age given as 15 (birth estimated 1826 by the census — the standard rounding artefact for pre-1837 births in the 1841 census).

**Marriage (England):** Married **Sarah Cocking** on **28 November 1844** at **Stevington, Bedfordshire** (GRO vol. 6 p. 89, Bedford district, Q4 1844). Witness: Samuel Cocking (her brother or father). This is before emigration — he took his wife with him to Australia. The previously recorded claim "Ebenezer married in Australia" was incorrect.

**Emigration:** Ebenezer and Sarah sailed to NSW as **assisted immigrants** on the same vessel as cousin Ephraim — the ***Elizabeth***, arriving **31 March 1845**. The NSW Assisted Immigrants Inwards register (FamilySearch ark:/61903/1:1:CJR5-76T2, p. 431) records Ebenezer age 23, wife Sarah age 20, both **engaged by Robert Coopershaw, "Rocky Point," Cooks River** — an area south of Sydney. Ephraim's separate entry in the same scheme records him alone. They departed England shortly after Ebenezer's November 1844 marriage.

John Edward was christened 15 September 1850 at St Peter's, Campbelltown, NSW (Australia Births & Baptisms). His mother is listed as "Sarah Hockings" — a variant spelling of Cocking.

**1861 census (uncertain):** A FamilySearch source attaches an 1861 England and Wales census record from Jersey to Ebenezer: male, age 44 (estimated birth year 1817), birthplace "Steventon, Bedfordshire", occupation "Pensioner Chelsea", marital status married. The age is 5–6 years older than expected (Ebenezer was ~38–39 in 1861). The Jersey location is anomalous given his first child was born in NSW in 1850. This record may be misattributed; it is noted here as uncertain.

**1891 census:** Camden, New South Wales.

**Death:** 21 June 1902, Campbelltown, NSW. Age 80. Buried **St Peters Anglican Church Cemetery, Campbelltown**. Cemetery inscription record confirms "Sarah Bunker" (his wife) on the same record — she predeceased him, dying 1899.

## Settlement

Ebenezer settled in the **Campbelltown / Camden** district of New South Wales, south-west of Sydney. This is the same area where cousin Ephraim first settled (Ephraim married at Camden in 1852) before moving to Crookwell. Their settling in adjacent areas confirms the cousins remained in contact after emigrating.

## Settlement

Both Ebenezer and Ephraim were initially engaged to Robert Coopershaw at **Rocky Point, Cooks River** (present-day southern Sydney suburbs). How and when they moved to the Camden / Campbelltown district — where Ebenezer's first child was christened in 1850 — is not yet documented.

## Note on the emigration narrative

The previous wiki text stated "Ebenezer married in Australia" and "whether they sailed on the same vessel remains unknown." Both were incorrect. Ebenezer married at Stevington on 28 November 1844 and emigrated with his wife on the same ship as Ephraim. See [[ephraim-bunker-1821-familysearch]].
