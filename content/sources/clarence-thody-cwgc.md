Commonwealth War Graves Commission (CWGC) casualty record #206581 for Captain Clarence James Thody, clipped 2026-05-10 from cwgc.org.

**Key facts:**
- Rank: **Captain**
- Regiment: **Manchester Regiment, 8th Battalion**
- Burial: **Manchester Cemetery, Riencourt-lès-Bapaume**, France — Plot **A. 11**
- Country of Service: United Kingdom
- Additional Info: "Son of W. A. Thody, of Dunstable, Beds. Master of Ardwick Green Industrial School (Manchester)."

**Significance:**
- Confirms WWI death definitively — he was a commissioned officer, not merely of military age
- His rank of Captain suggests he was commissioned from the outset in 1914 (as a New Army officer) or rose quickly through the ranks — possibly aided by his background running an institution requiring administrative authority
- His peacetime career as **Master of Ardwick Green Industrial School** in Manchester was a demanding institutional role, managing a reform/industrial school for destitute or delinquent boys
- The CWGC entry lists his father as "W. A. Thody, of Dunstable" — confirming the family connection to [[william-allen-thody-1850]] and placing William in Dunstable in the period when CWGC records were compiled (likely just after the war)
- The Manchester Cemetery at Riencourt-lès-Bapaume was named for the regiment that captured the village during the Second Battle of Bapaume (31 Aug – 3 Sep 1918), part of the Hundred Days Offensive; Clarence fell on 30 August 1918, the day before the formal battle designation began
