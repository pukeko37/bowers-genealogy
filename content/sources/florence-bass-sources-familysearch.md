FamilySearch sources page (6 sources) for Florence Bass (GFZV-PHS), born 1862 Maulden, death date unknown.

**1871 census:** Florence Bass, age 9, scholar, born Maulden. At Duck End, Maulden with mother Mary Bass (43, widowed, staymaker), sister Sarah (11), brother Edward (7).

**1881 census:** Florence Bass, age 17, single, **domestic servant**, Islington, London — in the household of Thomas Brown (43, commercial clerk) and Hebe W Brown (44). Birthplace recorded as "Moulden" (transcription of Maulden). She had left Maulden for London service by this date.

**Marriage registration 1889:** Florence Bass married in Ampthill registration district, 1889. Volume 3B, p. 637. The FamilySearch source entry is oddly indexed as "Entry for Florence Bass and Mary Ellen Botsford" — this appears to be a tree attachment error; the 1891 census confirms her husband was Sidney Shotbolt, not Botsford.

**1891 census:** Florence Shotbolt (née Bass), age 29, married, Ampthill, Bedfordshire. Husband Sydney Shotbolt (26, shoemaker, born Maulden). Son Frank Shotbolt listed as **0 days old** — born on or immediately before census day. Florence's birthplace confirmed as Maulden.

**1901 census:** Florence Shotbolt, age 39, married, Ampthill. Husband Sidney Shotbolt (36, boot & shoe maker, Maulden). Children: Frank (10), Reginald (7), Eva (8), all born Ampthill. Lodger Mary Macfarlane (34, Scotland) also present.

**1911 census:** Florence Shotbolt, age 49, married, Ampthill. Husband Sydney Shotbolt (46, bootmaker dealer, Maulden). Family of 5. Years married: 21 (implying ~1890; slight discrepancy with 1889 registration). Children at home: Frank (20), Eva (18), Reginald (17), all Ampthill.
