## Notes

**Duplicate Sarah Beals:** FamilySearch shows two Sarah Beals born 1776 to the same parents (KZLF-BJX, died 1866, and L5V4-XRC, our line). This is almost certainly either a FamilySearch duplicate entry, or — more likely — a first Sarah died in infancy before 1781 (the baptism year of our Sarah suggests she was not baptised at birth), with the name reused for a second daughter. The two entries may refer to the same person.

**Lidlington origins:** Sarah came from Lidlington, not Millbrook. The two villages are about 3 miles apart. How she came to marry a Millbrook Bunker in 1808 is not recorded.

**William Beal (1747–1828):** Sarah's father outlived her mother by over forty years and was alive when Sarah married William Bunker in 1808. The Beal/Beale family's presence in the Millbrook area — Solomon Beale the Methodist lay preacher was a Millbrook Beale — may connect to this same family, but the relationship has not been traced.
