FamilySearch sources page (22 sources attached). Contains the most detailed documentary record of William Allen Thody's life from birth to burial.

## Birth Certificate (1850)

Certified copy from General Register Office (Application PAS 9267/8/4/75). Registration District Bedford, Sub-district of Bedford & Cardington. No. 491, 11 November 1850, Cotton End. Father: Allen Thody, Labourer. Mother: Elizabeth Thody formerly Saunders. William could not write — signed with his mark. Registered 12 November 1850.

## 1851 Census

William (age 0) with parents Allan & Elizabeth Thodey at Cotton End, Bedfordshire. Father listed as born "Spain British Subject" (a British national born abroad in Spain). Siblings in household: Ann (16), Samuel (13), George (6), Elizabeth (3).

## 1861 Census

William Thodey (age 10), Scholar, at Eastcotts, Bedfordshire, with sister Elizabeth Thodey (age 13). Father no longer in household at this address.

## US Army Enlistment (11 May 1871)

Enlisted at Detroit, Michigan. Declaration: "I William Thodey, born in Bedford in the State of England, by occupation a Teamster... Twenty one years and Six months of age, brown eyes, brown hair, Dark complexion, five feet eight inches high." Enlisted for five years. Posted to Newport Barracks, Kentucky. A note attached to the record states he deserted 3 July 1873 — two months after his marriage. He may never have returned to the Army after the wedding.

## 1873 Chicago Marriage Certificate

"It is hereby certified that on this 14 day of May A.D. 1873 the undersigned a Minister of the Gospel did join in Marriage Mr. William A. Thodey and Miss Tillie E. Parent… Edward P. Godwin, Pastor First Cong. [Congregational] Church."

## Louis Albert Thody Christening (13 February 1877)

Christened at Wesleyan Methodist chapel, Toddington, Bedfordshire. Birth date: 8 December 1876. Father: William Allen Thody. Mother: Matilda [Parent]. Confirms the family was back in England by late 1876.

## 1881 Census (two sources)

William Allen Thody, 30, Police Constable, Widower, Lodger at 155 Up Street, Sharnbrook, Bedfordshire. Also in the household: George A. Thody, Lodger, age 6, born Canada. A note explains: "William's wife, Matilda (Tillie) went back to Canada to care for her father, taking their son, Louis Albert with her." The "widower" designation is a census error or shorthand — Tillie was still alive.

## 1891 Census (two sources)

Household at Totternhoe, Bedfordshire: William Thody (Head, Married, 40, Police Constable, born Cardington); Tillie Thody (Wife, 38, born Sandwich, Canada Ont.); Allen Thody (Son, 16, Printers Apprentice, born Sandwich, Canada Ont. — this is George Allen); Albert Thody (Son, 14, Printers Apprentice, born Cardington); Minnie Thody (Niece [illegitimate daughter], 10, Scholar, born Kentish Town, London); Clarence Thody (Son, 6, Scholar, born Houghton Regis); Florence Thody (Daughter, 1, born Totternhoe). Tillie had returned from Canada before Clarence's birth (~1884).

## 1894 Whipsnade Marriage Certificate

"Page 35 1894 Marriage solemnized at the Parish Church in the Parish of Whipsnade… No. 70. Married 6th February 1894 William Allen Thody, 43 years, Widower, Police Officer, residing Markyate, father Allen Thody, Labourer and Lucy Cooke, 37 years, Spinster, residing Whipsnade, no father named." Married by William T. Holden, Rector. Witnesses: [[walter-cook-1869|Walter Cook]] (son of [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook (1843–1908)]] and [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois Brandsom]]; Lois was Edwin Bransom's sister, so Walter and Florence are first cousins), Florance Bransome ([[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]], daughter of [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]] and [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] — Lucy's niece).

## 1901 Census

Household at Studham, Bedfordshire: William Allen Thody (Head, Married, 50, Police Constable, born Cotton End); Lucy Thody (Wife, 44, born Whipsnade); Florence Lena Thody (Daughter, 11, born Totternhoe); Clarence Archibald Thody (Son, 16, born Dunstable); Harry Thody (Son, 6, born Markyate). Two of William's children from his first marriage — Clarence and Florence — remained in the household under Lucy's care.

## Funeral Card (1911)

"In Loving Memory of William Allen Thody, Ex-P.C. [Police Constable] Beds. [Bedfordshire] Constabulary, Born at Cotton End, Bedford, Nov. 11th, 1850, Died at Studham, February 1st, 1911, Aged 60 Years. Interred in Studham Churchyard." Submitted by Patricia B. Rust.

## Burial Record (1911)

Studham Parish Burial Register, page 7, entry #52. Buried 4 February 1911, age 60, by J. E. Brown.
