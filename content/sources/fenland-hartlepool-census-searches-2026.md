Combined with the earlier Swavesey search (56 records), these five searches confirm a multi-village fenland diaspora concentrated in Stranton. Analysis in [[swavesey-stranton-diaspora]].

Key finding: the Randall family was a major **Fen Drayton** presence in Stranton (four separate Randall households). Richard Norman married Ann Elizabeth Randall "from Fenstanton"; the Randalls were primarily Fen Drayton people, and the two parishes are adjacent. See [[norman-randall-1875]].
