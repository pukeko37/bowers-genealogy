FamilySearch search results for surname "Matthews" born at Kensworth, Hertfordshire. Four pages of results (70 records) covering christenings, census, and LDS membership records. Clipped 2026-06-06 from five separate result pages (Search Results.md through Search Results 4.md).

## Confirmation of existing wiki data

- **Thomas Matthews the younger**: christening 12 August 1798, parents "Thomas Matthews, Mary" ✓. Present in 1851 and 1861 censuses at Kensworth ✓.
- **Peter Matthews (1770)**: christening 7 January 1770, parents "Peter Matthews, Susannah" — consistent with our existing data (Peter Matthews 1734–1812 + Susannah Durrant). Sibling Susanna (christened 30 Nov 1763) also confirmed.
- **William Matthews (LDS)**: two LDS membership records — baptism 5 April 1848; birth 1 January 1806, Kensworth ✓.
- **Sarah Matthews (1803)**: christened 30 January 1803, parents Thomas Matthews, Mary — a sibling of Thomas the younger, previously noted in our sources. Confirmed.

## Charles Matthews (1832) — open question resolved

Thomas the younger's son Charles (b. ~1832, Kensworth) remained in England. He married **Mary** (born ~1838–1841, Kensworth) and settled at **Caddington**, Bedfordshire, where the family appears in the 1871 and 1881 censuses. Known children: Henry (~1864), Elizabeth (~1865), Herbert (~1870), Mary (~1870), William. Charles did not emigrate with the LDS group. See [[charles-matthews-1832]].

## David Matthews (1853) — the 1861 mystery resolved

The "David Matthews (8)" in Thomas the younger's 1861 household is identified as David Matthews, born 1853 at Kensworth. He appears independently in the 1871 census at Kensworth, and in 1881 at Kensworth with wife **Letitia** (born 1855, also Kensworth), whom he married in 1876. Children: **Louisa** (b. 1877) and **Edwin** (b. 1881). By 1901 and 1911 the family had moved to **Luton**. David's son Edwin married Annie C. and had at least three children: Leslie, Gladys, Constance. See [[david-matthews-1853]].

David's parentage in the 1861 census is unlisted — he may be a grandson of Thomas the younger (possibly via Charles, born 1832), or a grandnephew through another line. The 1861 census records him simply as a member of the household.

## Rebecca Matthews (1866, Kensworth) — RESOLVED

~~Research lead.~~ This entry was **Rebecca Grizzell**, wife of [[william-matthews-1865|William Matthews (1865)]], son of [[william-mathews-1837|William Mathews (1837)]]. She appeared in this search under her married name. Her maiden name was Grizzell; she was not born a Matthews. The 1901 Studham residence corresponds to William (1865) working as a gamekeeper there. He was back in Whipsnade by 1911. See [[william-matthews-1865-sources-familysearch]].

## Elizabeth Hopkins birth year revised

The 1861 census records Elizabeth Matthews (Thomas the younger's wife) as born **1793** at Kensworth — a decade earlier than the ~1800–1801 estimate derived from the poorly-recorded 1841 census. The 1861 figure is taken as more reliable. She was approximately 47 when she married Thomas in 1840, not ~40 as previously assumed. Her page ([[elizabeth-hopkins-abt-1801]]) has been updated.

## David Matthews confirmed at Kensworth through 1891

The 1891 census shows David Matthews and Letitia still at Kensworth with son Edwin (10). The move to Luton therefore occurred between 1891 and 1901. See [[david-matthews-1853]].

## 1841 census household — age rounding note

The 1841 census at Kensworth records "Thomas Matthews born ~1806" — this is Thomas the younger (christened 12 August 1798), whose actual age of 42–43 was rounded down to 35 by the census enumerator, giving an estimated birth of 1806. The same census lists "Elizabeth Matthews born ~1801" = Elizabeth Hopkins (married Thomas November 1840, actual birth ~1795–1800). This resolves any apparent confusion between "Thomas born 1806" and our Thomas the younger.

Also present at Kensworth in the 1841 census: **Sarah Matthews born ~1771**, age 70 — unidentified. Too old to be Thomas's child; may be a widow from the James Matthews collateral family or a relative of Elizabeth Hopkins.

## Collateral Matthews families at Kensworth

The search results reveal at least two additional Matthews families at Kensworth with no established connection to our researched line:

**"James Matthews + Sarah" family** (christenings 1793–1811): children James (1793), Richard (1802), Susanna (1801), Sarah (1803), Sarah (1805), Hannah (1811), Rebecca (1808). The 1851 census shows a James Matthews (born 1803, Kensworth) at Hemel Hempstead.

**"James Matthews + Hannah" family** (christenings 1842–1850): children Sarah Anne (1842), Emma (1844), James Alfred George (1846), Eliza Hannah (1850). Probably a son of the above James + Sarah family.

These families are not currently connected to the wiki's scope.
