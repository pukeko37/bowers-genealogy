FamilySearch person ID MM1H-NFB. Sources page for Lydia Bunker (1833–1922), listing fourteen attached records covering her full life.

**Christening:** 28 March 1834, Millbrook, Bedfordshire. Birth date 24 September 1833. Father: James Bunker; mother: Elisabeth. England, Births and Christenings, 1538-1975.

**1841 census:** Millbrook, Bedfordshire, age 8. In household with James and Elizebeth Bunker, John (9), Gorge (6), David (4), Charles (2), Marther (0), and Lydia Bunker (70, presumably grandmother/great-aunt Lydia Brown or a neighbour). HO107/6/4.

**1851 census:** Servant in **James Sheppard** household, St Paul's parish, Bedfordshire. Age 17. Occupation: Servant. James Sheppard was a "Nursery & Seeds Man Partner," aged 28, born Bedford. Martha Sheppard (23). HO107/1751/699, piece 53. — Note: Lydia was already in domestic service in St Paul's, Bedford *before* her father James died in 1852 and the family moved to Bedford. She had moved there independently.

**1861 census:** Dane Street, St Paul's parish, Bedford. Age 27. Occupation: House Servant. In family household headed by Elizabeth Bunker. Confirmed by household 1270492 (same as Caleb's 1861 census entry).

**Marriage:** Married **Samuel Plackett Lane** on 21 September 1863 at St Peter's Martin, Bedford. Age 30, single. Birth year estimated 1833. Father: James Bunker. Multiple sources (England and Wales Marriage Registration Index vol 3B p 460; England Marriages 1538–1973; Bedfordshire Parish Registers). See [[samuel-plackett-lane-sources-familysearch]].

**1871 census:** With husband Samuel at St Paul's, Bedford. Lydia Lane, age 37 (born Millbrook). Occupation: Housewife. Husband Samuel Lane (28, Boot & Shoemaker). Daughters: Edith Mercy (6) and Eliz Maryanne (2). Albert John Lane (14, Maulden) also in household. FamilySearch ark:/61903/1:1:VFF3-YYJ.

**1881 census:** **Widowed** (Samuel died 1872). Head of household. Bedford Western Ward St Paul. Age 47. Born Millbrook. Daughters: Mary A Lane (13, Bedford) and Edith Lane (18, Bedford). RG11/1622/121, page 23.

**1891 census:** Bedford Eastern Ward. Age 58. Widowed. Head of household. With Florence Locke (4, Bedfordshire) and Samuel Locke (2, Bedfordshire) — grandchildren, presumably Edith's children by Frank Locke (married 1884). RG12/1249/58, page 21.

**1901 census:** Living with daughter **Edith Locke** and son-in-law **Frank Locke** (stonemason, born Shoreditch). Frank is head of household. Lydia is mother-in-law. Bedford Eastern Ward St Paul. Also in household: Florence Locke (14), Samuel Locke (12), Hilda Locke (7), Gertrude Locke (4). Lydia's occupation: "Living on own means." RG13/162, schedule 184.

**1911 census:** **Barton Almshouse, Turvey, Bedfordshire.** Age 77. Widowed. Occupation field: "BARTON ALMSHOUSE." Head of own entry, number in family: 1. Birthplace recorded as "Born At Milbrock Turvey, Bedfordshire" — a census transcription conflating Millbrook (birth) with Turvey (current location). RG14/387. This is the Barton almshouses at Turvey, not a private address.

**Death:** 1922, Bedfordshire. Age at death: 89. Volume 3B, page 308. Death registration as Lydia Lane.

**Children of Lydia Bunker and Samuel Plackett Lane:**
1. Edith Mercy Lane (born ~1865, Bedford) — married Frank Humphries Locke 1884; grandchildren Florence, Samuel, Hilda, Gertrude
2. Mary A Lane (born ~1868, Bedford) — present 1881, no later records traced
3. Elizabeth Mary Ann Lane (born ~1869, Bedford) — married John White 1890, Holy Trinity, Bedford/Weymouth
