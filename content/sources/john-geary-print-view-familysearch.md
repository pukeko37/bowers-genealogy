FamilySearch person KCCS-42Z. John Geary, born 1825, Haynes, Bedfordshire; christened 4 December 1825. Died 6 February 1895, Ampthill. Buried at Saint Mary The Virgin, Maulden, Bedfordshire.

**Occupation:** Woodman (1871).

**Residence history:** 1841–1881 Haynes (lifelong in the village); 1891 Maulden. Named addresses: Plum's Lane; Church End.

The move to Maulden in 1891 is consistent with all three Geary records (John, Lucy, and Eliza all show Maulden in 1891). John died in February 1895 and was buried at St Mary the Virgin, Maulden — confirming the family had established genuine roots there in their final years.

Marriage: Lucy Morris, 1848, Haynes. Eleven children (see eliza-geary-print-view-familysearch for full list).

Parents: **Joseph Geary** (1804–1890) and **Rhoda Spring Bottoms** (1806–1884), married 20 April 1824, Clophill, Bedfordshire. Clophill is immediately adjacent to Maulden; the Geary family's roots were always in this tight Bedfordshire cluster.

John's siblings: Pamela (1828), Sarah (1830), Martha (1833), Mary (1835), Joseph (1838), George (1840), Walter (1843), Caleb (1847), Henry (1852–1853).
