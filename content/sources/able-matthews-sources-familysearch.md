FamilySearch sources page for Able Matthews (also spelled Abel), 34 sources. FamilySearch ID KWJZ-9HJ.

## Key Records

### Birth

LDS Early Church Information File gives birth date **19 April 1813**, Utah, United States (membership record). Utah Cemetery Abstracts give 13 April 1813 — a likely transcription discrepancy. The Mormon Pioneer Database gives 19 April 1813. Birthplace in 1851 census is **Whipsnade, Bedfordshire**.

### Marriage to Mary Walker — 1840

Two overlapping Bedfordshire parish register entries:

| Field | Entry 1 | Entry 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Record date | 22 November 1840 | 29 November 1840 |
| Event place | Dunstable, Bedfordshire | Dunstable, Bedfordshire |
| Groom | Abel/Able Mathews | Able Matthews |
| Bride | Mary Walker | Mary Walker |
| Witness | — | James Walker |

GRO index also records the marriage in quarter Oct-Nov-Dec 1840, Luton district, vol. 6, p. 179. James Walker (Able's witness on the second register entry) is likely Mary's father or brother.

### 1841 Census

Able Matthews (25, Bedfordshire) is recorded in the **George Birchmore / Ann Birchmore** household at Houchen End, Whipsnade. Also in the household: Mary Matthews (F, 20, Bedfordshire) and Sarah Matthews (F, 0, Bedfordshire). This places Able and his newlywed wife Mary (using her married name Matthews) living with his sister Anne and brother-in-law [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]] one year after marriage, with an infant daughter Sarah already born.

### 1851 Census

Abel Mathews (38, Head, Ag Lab, born Whipsnade Beds), Whipsnade, with household:

| Name | Relationship | Age | Birthplace |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mary Mathews | Wife | 34 | Aubury, Buckinghamshire |
| Sarah Mathews | Daughter | 10 | Whipsnade, Bedfordshire |
| Elizabeth Mathews | Daughter | 4 | Whipsnade, Bedfordshire |
| William Mathews | Son | 3 | Whipsnade, Bedfordshire |

Mary's birthplace "Aubury, Bucks" is likely Aylesbury, Buckinghamshire. Three children confirmed by 1851: Sarah (~1841), Elizabeth (~1847), William (~1848).

### Emigration

Utah Mormon Pioneer Overland Travel Database: **Abel Matthews**, departure 1 June 1861, arrived **16 August 1861**, **David H. Cannon Company**, Deseret. This is four years after departing England (on the ship Siddons, 1855) — the family spent the intervening years in St. Louis, Missouri, where Mary Ann Walker died.

LDS Record of Members, 1861: membership in **Lehi, Utah**.

### Naturalization

Utah County Records: naturalized **1866** and again **1878** in Utah Territory.

### 1870 Census

Able Matthews (57, England), Lehi City, Utah Territory, with Jennette Matthews (F, 52, Scotland). Jennette is his second wife, married after Mary Walker's death in St. Louis.

### 1880 Census

Able Mathews (67, Farmer, England), Lehi, Utah Territory, with Jenetta Mathews (Wife, 60, Scotland). No children in household by this date.

### Death

Utah Cemetery Abstracts: Able Mathews, died **24 March 1891**, buried Lehi City Cemetery. Confirmed by Find a Grave Index (attached to KWJZ-9HJ).

### Children's Deaths

Utah Death Certificate for William Mathews, died 16 August 1917 (aged 69), Virgin, Washington County, Utah — attached to Able's FamilySearch page, suggesting William (born ~1848 Whipsnade) emigrated to Utah with the family and settled in the Virgin area.
