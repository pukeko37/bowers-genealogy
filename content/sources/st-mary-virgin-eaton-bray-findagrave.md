Find a Grave memorial listing for St Mary the Virgin Churchyard, Eaton Bray, Bedfordshire (cemetery ID 2362501). 398 records across 20 pages — well-transcribed relative to the Cambridgeshire churchyards. Eaton Bray is a village approximately 4 miles south-west of Whipsnade, in the Vale of Aylesbury.

## Key finding: Woodcraft family confirmed at Eaton Bray

Two Woodcraft entries, both WWI-era veterans:

| Name | Dates | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Ernest Woodcraft | birth/death unknown | Veteran memorial |
| Harry Woodcraft | 1890–16 Feb 1917 | Private, died WWI |

**Alice Woodcroft** (born abt 1791) married [[thomas-bachelor-1781|Thomas Bachelor]] at Whipsnade on 2 August 1816. Her family of origin is not documented; the family page speculates she was from "Whipsnade or the surrounding area." The presence of a Woodcraft family at Eaton Bray — 4 miles from Whipsnade, in exactly the right geographical corridor — is consistent with Eaton Bray being Alice's home parish. Harry (1890) and Ernest are likely two or three generations after Alice, representing her nephews' or cousins' line.

The connection cannot be confirmed from the listing alone — parish registers for Eaton Bray would be needed to trace the Woodcraft family backwards from these WWI-era men to Alice's generation (c.1760–1800). But Eaton Bray is now the leading candidate for the Woodcraft family of origin. See [[bachelor-woodcroft-1816]].

## Other entries of note

**Cook family**: Bessie Vera Cook (1903–1995), Sgt Edward John Cook (1922–1941), Frederick Owen Cook (1906–1974), and George Cook (28 Nov 1843–2 Aug 1863). The Victorian-era George Cook (died aged 19) is the only entry in the right period to be potentially connected to the Whipsnade Cook family, but no link is established. The others are too recent.

**Wood family**: Extensive — John Wood (1761–1834), Mary Wood (1778–1856), Ann Wood, Elizabeth Wood, George Wood and others across multiple generations. The Wood and Woodcraft families are listed separately and probably represent distinct families, despite the similar names.

**Ashwell family**: Dominant in the early pages — Thomas Ashwell, Elizabeth Ashwell, Priscilla Ashwell and multiple others, mainly 19th century. Ashwell is a common Bedfordshire/Hertfordshire surname with no documented connection to our research families.
