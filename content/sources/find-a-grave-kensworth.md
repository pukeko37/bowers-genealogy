## Relevance to this wiki

**Thomas Edward Cook (9 Apr 1882 – 3 Sep 1955)** and **Alice Mabel Dumpleton (1883 – 13 Nov 1940)** — buried together at Kensworth. Thomas was born in Harpenden, Hertfordshire, to parents James Cook and Mary. His siblings were Joseph (1876), James Walter (1878), and Charles (1881). He married Alice Mabel Dumpleton in 1906; they had a son Leslie Thomas Cook (1907).

Thomas's father James Cook is the [[james-cook-1843|James Cook (1843)]] of our wiki — son of [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] and Elizabeth Frost, who married at Kensworth in 1842 — confirmed by FamilySearch. Thomas was therefore a grandnephew of [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] and a first cousin once removed of [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] (Thody).

Alice's maiden name Dumpleton connects her to a family well-represented in St Mary the Virgin Churchyard, Studham — see [[find-a-grave-st-mary-virgin-studham]].

**Edna Evelyn Maynard (10 Sep 1906 – Jan 2001)** and **John Henry Maynard (20 Feb 1941 – 28 Feb 2009)** — possibly mother and son. The Maynard name appears in our wiki through [[susan-maynard-1802]] (born Luton); Kensworth is nearby but no connection has been established.
