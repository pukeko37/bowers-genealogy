FamilySearch person ID GWYY-S9K. Sources page for Margaret Barnsley (1850–1922), Caleb Bunker's second wife.

**Birth:** Born **14 April 1850**. Christened 12 May 1850 at Church of St Michael and All Angels, Ashton-under-Lyne, Lancashire. Birth registration 1850, Ashton, Lancashire, volume XX, page 13. Father: Thomas Barnsley (born Bradwell, Derbyshire). Mother: **Eliza** (born Bradwell, Derbyshire — same birthplace as Thomas, suggesting they were from the same Derbyshire community before moving to Lancashire). Thomas Barnsley's occupation in 1851 was "Dealer in Old and New Clothes."

**Siblings (1851 census):** Joseph Barnsley (4, born Ashton) and Elizabeth Barnsley (10, born Ashton).

**1851 census:** Ashton-under-Lyne, age 0. With Thomas (34, clothes dealer, Bradwell), Eliza (30, Bradwell), Joseph (4), Elizabeth (10). HO107/2233.

**1861 census (Ancestry):** Ashton-under-Lyne, age 10. With Thos Barnsley (44), Eliza Barnsley (42). Household schedule 44.

**1871 census:** Matlock, Derbyshire. Age 20. **Shop Assistant**. Living with her parents: Thomas Barnsley (54, stationer, Bradwell) and Eliza Barnsley (52, Bradwell). Household 74, enumeration district 11f. FamilySearch ark:/61903/1:1:VB6T-5YM. — This places Margaret with her parents in Matlock in the same census year that Caleb arrived there as a widower. Caleb was a lodger at household 168 in the same enumeration district. They were almost certainly in the same small community.

**Marriage:** Oct-Dec 1871, Bakewell registration district, volume 7B, page 1283. Record lists "Margaret Barnsley / Caleb Bunker." Multiple sources confirm.

**1881 census:** Matlock, wife. Age 30, Ashton-u-Lyne. Housewife. With Caleb and six children. RG11/3450/35.

**1891 census:** Derby, St Peter's, wife. Age 40, birthplace here recorded as "Leicestershire" (error — she was born Ashton-under-Lyne, Lancashire). RG12/2727/43.

**1901 census:** Derby, wife. Age 50, Ashton Un Lyne. With Caleb and remaining children at home. Piece 3215, folio 138.

**1911 census:** **60 High Street, Derby.** Wife. Age 60, Ashton U Lyne. Married 39 years. Housewife. With Caleb (65) and daughter Annie Elizabeth Borrington (38, Matlock Bath). Several lodgers also in household. The address and lodger pattern confirm 60 High St was a boarding house.

**1921:** FindMyPast note places Margaret at **Newton, Ashton-under-Lyne, Lancashire** — she returned to her hometown after Caleb died in 1918, with William and Emma (likely relations).

**Death:** 1922, **Staffordshire**, age 72. Volume 6B, page 376. (Note: registered Staffordshire, same county as Caleb's death at Burton upon Trent. Both died in the Burton upon Trent area — possibly she was with family or receiving care there in her final years despite being at Ashton in 1921.)

**Burial:** Find a Grave — Nottingham Road Cemetery, Chaddesden, Derby. Death date: 10 June 1922. Same cemetery as Caleb (died 25 May 1918). They share a burial ground despite dying four years apart.
