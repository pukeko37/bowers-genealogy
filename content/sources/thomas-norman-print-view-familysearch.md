## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1827, Swavesey, Cambridgeshire
- **Died:** 1905, Swavesey, Cambridgeshire
- **Residences:** Swavesey 1841, 1851, 1861, 1871, 1881; Alconbury Weston 1901 (census)
- **1891:** Census places him Swavesey

## Notes

Thomas Norman lived his entire life in Swavesey, Cambridgeshire. The 1901 census shows him at Alconbury Weston, Huntingdonshire — a small move in his late 70s, possibly following his son Tom Norman who was also in Alconbury Weston from 1891. He was an agricultural labourer throughout his working life. His son Charles was the only child to leave for London; the rest of his children remained in Cambridgeshire/Huntingdonshire.
