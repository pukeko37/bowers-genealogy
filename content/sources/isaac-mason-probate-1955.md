**Key facts:**
- Death date confirmed precisely as **14 May 1955** (previous records had year only).
- Address of death: **17 Daysbrook-road, Streatham, London SW16** — not the 4 Yukon Road, Balham address previously associated with him; he had evidently moved to Streatham by his death.
- Probate granted to **Barclays Bank Limited** — he left a will with a corporate executor rather than a family member. This suggests either no surviving close family member was willing/available to act, or he specifically chose a bank executor.
- Effects of **£7102** are substantial (compare to his brother John's £344 seven years later). Isaac had evidently accumulated more wealth, possibly from the Balham newsagent business.
- See [[john-james-mason-probate-1962]]: John Mason died at **16 Daysbrook Road** — they were literally next-door neighbours at death.
