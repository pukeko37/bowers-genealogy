Screenshot of the England and Wales probate calendar entry for Thomas Barnsley, 1890. Full text:

> **BARNSLEY, Thomas.** Personal Estate £50 1s. 7d.
>
> 3 October. Administration (with the Will) of the Personal Estate of Thomas Barnsley formerly of Matlock Bath in the Parish of Matlock but late of Church-street Bradwell both in the County of Derby who died 11 August 1890 at Bradwell was granted at Derby to Joseph Barnsley of Pontefract-place Leeds in the County of York Oil and Tallow Manufacturer the Son and one of the Next of Kin.

Key facts confirmed:
- Thomas was **formerly of Matlock Bath** — the same Matlock Bath address given in the 1880 bankruptcy notices for Caleb's business trading as "Thomas Barnsley." This directly links Thomas to the shop location.
- He retired to **Church-street, Bradwell** — his birthplace village in the Hope Valley.
- Administration granted to son **Joseph Barnsley**, oil and tallow manufacturer, of Pontefract-place, Leeds.
- Estate: £50 1s. 7d. — a very modest sum, consistent with a man who had wound up his business interests some years before.
