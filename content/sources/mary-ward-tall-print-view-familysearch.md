FamilySearch person MM5L-RW1. Mary Ward Tall.

**Vitals:**
- Born: **12 September 1827**
- Christened: **2 December 1827, Ampthill, Bedford, England** — christened in Edward's home town, not in Maulden where her parents married
- Died: **April 1907, Buckinghamshire, England**

**Birth note:** Mary was born 4 months and 8 days after her parents' marriage on 4 May 1827 — she was conceived before the wedding. The Ampthill christening (Edward's town) may reflect an early connection to that parish.

**Residence:** 1851 Maulden · 1861 Maulden · 1871 Maulden · 1881 Maulden · 1891 Maulden · **1901 Lavendon, Buckinghamshire** (Snow Hill). Remained in Maulden for 74 years, then moved to Lavendon in old age, dying there in 1907.

**Marriage:** [[daniel-bass-1829|Daniel Bass]] (1829–1868), **October 1855, Maulden, Bedfordshire**. Daniel died 1868, leaving Mary a widow aged ~41, with children aged ~9, ~6, and ~4. Daniel's death year is the same year as Caleb Bunker and Rebecca Tall's wedding at Howard Chapel.

**Children:**
- Sarah Bass (1859–1951) — remarkable longevity; died aged ~92
- Florence Bass (1862–Deceased)
- Edward Ernest Bass (1864–1936)

**Sibling list:** Confirms the same six children of Edward and Sarah as the William Toll print view: Mary Ward (1827), William Toll (1830), James Toll (1832), Susan Toll (1837), Rebecca Tall (1844), Frederick Charles Tall (1847).
