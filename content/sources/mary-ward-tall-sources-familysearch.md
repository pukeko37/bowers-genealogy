FamilySearch sources page (8 sources) for Mary Ward Tall (MM5L-RW1), born 12 September 1827, died April 1907.

**Marriage 1855:** Marriage registration to Daniel Bass, Q4 1855, Ampthill registration district (Oct–Dec). Possible spouse entries also include "Danel Bass" and "Daniel Bass" — confirmed match.
