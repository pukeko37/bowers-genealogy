- 1861 census: age 3, Church End, Haynes (too young for occupation).

- Death registration: 1936, Bedfordshire (Volume 3B, p. 438). Age 78.

Elizabeth never married. She spent her adult life in Luton working in the straw trade — first as a domestic servant, then as a straw bonnet sewer, maintaining independence into old age. She lived to 78, the longest-lived of John and Lucy's children.
