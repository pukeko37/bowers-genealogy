## Key Facts Established

**Birth:** Baptised 30 October 1774, Cardington, St Mary's. Parish register: "Baptised Joseph son of John Sanders & Elizabeth his wife, Oct: 30. 1775 [the year that follows, so the baptism occurred in 1774]." He was the son of **John Sanders** and **Elizabeth** (his wife) of Cardington.

**Marriage (1799):** Married Rebecca Arnold at Cardington, 14 May 1799. See [[rebecca-arnold-familysearch]] for the full register text. Joseph signed his name; Rebecca signed by mark.

**Children with Rebecca Arnold (all christened Cardington):**
- Mary Saunders — born 28 December 1800, christened 26 May 1801
- Anne Saunders — christened 29 July 1804
- Sarah Saunders — born c. 1808, christened 25 August 1811 (delayed; Joseph was by then deceased)

**Death:** Buried 22 June 1810, Cardington. Parish register: "1810 Buried Joseph Saunders, Shoe maker June 22d." Occupation confirmed as Shoemaker.

## Notes

Joseph died in June 1810, leaving Rebecca with three children (Mary aged 9, Anne aged c. 6, Sarah aged c. 2). Thirteen months later, Rebecca had an illegitimate child — [[elizabeth-saunders-1811|Elizabeth]] — whose father is unknown. Joseph is therefore not Elizabeth's father. Elizabeth Saunders's maiden name comes from her mother Rebecca (née Arnold, widow of Joseph Sanders/Saunders).

The spelling alternates between Sanders and Saunders in the records; the Cardington parish register uses both. Elizabeth carried the name Saunders.
