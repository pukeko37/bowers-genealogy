FamilySearch print view for Caleb Bunker, Frederick's father.

**Vitals:** Born 1845, Millbrook, Bedfordshire (christened 19 October 1845 Millbrook). Birth registration: Ampthill district, 1845. Died 25 May 1918, Burton upon Trent, Staffordshire. Buried Nottingham Road Cemetery, Chaddesden, Derby.

**Residence history:** 1851 Millbrook · 1861 Bedfordshire · 1871 Matlock, Derbyshire · 1881 Matlock · 1891 Derbyshire · 1901 Derby · 1911 Derby.

**Notable:** Two obituary entries recorded — 23 May 1918 and 24 May 1918, Derby — confirming he was a Derby resident for his final decades. He died in Burton upon Trent (possibly visiting or seeking treatment) and was buried back in Derby.

Caleb moved from his native Bedfordshire to Matlock, Derbyshire by 1871 — the year after his wife Rebecca died and his son Frederick was left with Sarah Ward in Maulden. He married [[margaret-barnsley-1850|Margaret Barnsley]] in October 1871 at Matlock. His presence in Matlock in 1871 and 1881 coincides with Frederick's recorded residence there in 1881, suggesting father and son had contact despite Frederick's primary upbringing with the Tall family.
