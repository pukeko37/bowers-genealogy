## Notes on Birth Date

Find a Grave gives the birth date as **19 March 1813**. Other sources give:
- FamilySearch Mormon Pioneer Database: 19 April 1813
- FamilySearch Utah Cemetery Abstracts: 13 April 1813

The day "19" is consistent between Find a Grave and the Pioneer DB; only the month differs (March vs April). The Cemetery Abstracts give a different day entirely (13th). No original English birth or christening record has been found to settle the question. The person page uses 19 April per the Pioneer Database, but March cannot be ruled out.

Death date (24 March 1891) and burial location (Lehi City Cemetery) are consistent across all sources.
