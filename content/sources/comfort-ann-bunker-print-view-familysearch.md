**Occupation:** Sewer of straw hats

**Residences:**
- 1851–1861: Millbrook/Bedfordshire
- 1881–1911: Luton (consistent through all census years)

Ninth and youngest child of James Bunker (1807) and Elizabeth Watts. Caleb's youngest sibling. Settled in Luton where she worked as a straw hat sewer — a characteristic Luton trade. Her survival through 1911 is documented; death date not recorded in FamilySearch. Comfort and Martha both settled in Luton, making it a Bunker family hub before Caleb's son Frederick moved there.
