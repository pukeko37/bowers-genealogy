FamilySearch person ID M98H-QBS. Sources page listing ten attached records for Samuel Plackett Lane (1841–1872).

**Birth:** Birth registration 1842, Bedford, Bedfordshire. England and Wales Birth Registration Index, volume 6, page 21. Birth year in some sources estimated as 1841 (from age at marriage); the registration year of 1842 likely reflects a slight delay.

**1861 census:** Hassett Street, **St Paul's parish, Bedford**. Age 18 (birth year estimated 1843). Occupation: **Boot Maker Apprentice**. Relationship to head: **Apprentice**. Head of household: **John Howard**, Boot Maker employing 4 men, age 42, born Bedford. Also in household: Mary Ann Howard (wife, 43, Elston Beds); John Howard (21, son, Bedford); Mary Ann Howard (16, daughter); Charles Howard (5), Bertha Howard (2), Grace Howard (0). Also in household: George Holt (20, labourer, Odell). FamilySearch ark:/61903/1:1:M7TH-Q2L. Registration: RG09, piece 991, folio 122, page 7. Household 1270860.

**Significance of the 1861 census:** This is the same piece/folio range (RG09, piece 991) as the Bunker household at Dane Street (household 1270492). Both households are in St Paul's parish, Bedford, in the same census district. Caleb Bunker (age 15, shoe maker) and Samuel Lane (age 18, boot maker apprentice) were working in the same trade, in the same parish, in 1861 — almost certainly known to each other. Lane's master John Howard employed four men on Hassett Street.

**Marriage:** Married **Lydia Bunker** on 21 September 1863 at **St Peter's Martin, Bedford** (Bedfordshire parish registers, FamilySearch ark:/61903/1:1:QPW7-1786). Samuel was 22 years, single; Lydia was 30 years, single. Father of Samuel: **George Lane**. Marriage also in England and Wales Marriage Registration Index (volume 3B, page 460) and England Marriages 1538–1973. Registered as "Samuel Stockett Lane" in some records; "Samuel Plackett Lane" in others — both names are used in FamilySearch.

**1871 census:** Head of household, St Paul's parish, Bedford. Age 28, born St Paul Bedford. Married. Occupation: **Boot & Shoemaker**. Wife: Lydia Lane (37, Millbrook, Bedfordshire). Daughters: Edith Mercy Lane (6, Bedford), Eliz Maryanne Lane (2, Bedford). Also in household: Albert John Lane (14, Maulden, Bedfordshire) — relationship unclear, listed under "Other People." FamilySearch ark:/61903/1:1:VFF3-YYN. St Paul sub-district, Bedford & Cardington.

**Death:** 1872, Bedford, Bedfordshire. Age at death: 29. England and Wales Death Registration Index, volume 3B, page 225.

**Note:** The Samuel Plackett Lane–Lydia Bunker connection resolves and vastly extends the user's prior hypothesis that Samuel was "also an apprentice shoe maker." He was indeed in the boot/shoe trade in 1861, in the same parish as Caleb, and two years later married Caleb's sister Lydia. The families were directly linked through both trade and marriage by 1863.
