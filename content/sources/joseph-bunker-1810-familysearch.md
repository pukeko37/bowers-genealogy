FamilySearch sources page **M9D8-PFM** for Joseph Bunker (1810–Deceased), second child of [[joel-bunker-1789|Joel Bunker]] and [[martha-taylor-1786|Martha Taylor]]. 6 sources attached, all added April 2025 by contributor "Keelan Michael" except the marriage record (November 2014).

## Key facts

- **Born/christened:** 20 May 1810, **Alwalton, Huntingdonshire** — confirmed by three Huntingdonshire Parish Register sources and one Births/Christenings abstract, all giving the same date and place. Alwalton is near Peterborough, about 50 miles north of Millbrook and Eversholt. Joel and Martha were living in or passing through Huntingdonshire in early 1810, just 16 months after their Millbrook marriage.
- **Married:** Fanny Jakins, **7 March 1846, Eversholt, Bedfordshire**. The marriage record notes father "Joel Bunker" — Joel was still at Eversholt (he left in September 1846, just six months after this marriage).
- **Death:** "Deceased" — no date documented

## Significance: the Millbrook lead closes

Joseph (1810) was the candidate for linking the Joseph (1754) cordwainer line to the "Joseph Bunker (~1837)" in the Victorian Millbrook census. That speculation is not supported: Joseph (1810) was at Eversholt in 1846, not Millbrook, and a son born 1837 would predate his marriage by nine years with no evidence of a prior relationship. The Joseph (~1837) in the Millbrook census is already identified as the son of John (1803) from the Edward (1742) line — that identification stands.

## Movement pattern: Joel's family, 1809–1812

The birth places of Joel's first three children trace the family's movements in the early years:
- **Job (1804):** born Ampthill, Bedfordshire (pre-marital)
- **Joel and Martha marry:** January 1809, Millbrook
- **Joseph (1810):** born May 1810, Alwalton, Huntingdonshire (~50 miles north)
- **Job christened:** December 1812, Eversholt, Bedfordshire
- **Eliza (1815):** born June 1815, Eversholt

The family were not settled in Eversholt immediately after the Millbrook marriage — they were somewhere near Peterborough in 1810 before eventually settling in Eversholt by 1812.
