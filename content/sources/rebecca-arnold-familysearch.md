FamilySearch aggregated sources page for Rebecca Arnold (KJWJ-DD3), 7 sources total. Establishes the full parentage of [[elizabeth-saunders-1811|Elizabeth Saunders]].

## Key Facts Established

**Father's will (1790):** Samuel Arnold's will, proved 2 November 1790 at the Archdeaconry of Bedfordshire, identifies Rebecca Arnold as his daughter. Samuel Arnold was a **Farmer at Cotton End in the Parish of Cardington**. His will names his children as Rebecca Arnold, Samuel Arnold, Mary Arnold, and a child his wife Mary was then pregnant with. He leaves £150 specifically to Rebecca, with the remainder to be divided equally among the other children if Rebecca predeceases them. His brothers Robert Arnold and John Arnold are named as co-executors and co-guardians of all the children. The will was signed 2 May 1790, proved 2 November 1790 — indicating Samuel died between those dates.

**Marriage (1799):** "1799 No. 299 Joseph Sanders of this Parish and Rebecca Arnold of this Parish were Married in this Church by Banns this Fourteenth Day of May in the Year One Thousand seven Hundred and Ninety-nine by me F. Cumming, Vicar… Joseph Sanders, Rebecca Arnold, her mark." Rebecca signed by mark; Joseph signed his name. Witnesses: John \_\_\_\_ and Wm Brown.

**Children with Joseph Sanders (all christened Cardington):**
- Mary Saunders — born 28 December 1800, christened 26 May 1801
- Anne Saunders — christened 29 July 1804
- Sarah Saunders — born c. 1808, christened 25 August 1811 (delayed baptism of over 3 years)

**Illegitimate child (1811):** Elizabeth (our [[elizabeth-saunders-1811|Elizabeth Saunders]]) was also baptised 25 August 1811 — the same day as Sarah — recorded as "Elizabeth, illegitimate Child of Rebecca Saunders, Widow, aged nine weeks." Rebecca apparently had both children baptised together, the one long-delayed and the newborn. Elizabeth's father is unknown.

**Death (1812):** "1812 Buried Rebecca Sanders, Widow Aug ___." She was buried at Cardington in August 1812, just over a year after Elizabeth's birth. The precise day (27) comes from a transcript of the Studham parish register. She died a widow — Joseph had been dead two years — leaving Elizabeth (aged c. 14 months) an orphan.

## Notes

The Arnold family's Cotton End connection is significant. Samuel Arnold farmed at Cotton End — the exact hamlet in Cardington parish where Elizabeth Saunders and Allen Thody would later settle and raise their family. The £150 left to Rebecca represents genuine farming-family capital. The witnesses at Allen and Elizabeth's 1834 marriage were **John Arnold** and **Elizabeth Arnold** — John Arnold was named as Samuel Arnold's brother and co-executor in the 1790 will. This may be the same man (aged perhaps 60–70 by 1834) or a son of his.
