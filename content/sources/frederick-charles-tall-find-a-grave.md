Find a Grave memorial ID 245291699. Source: Foster Hill Road Cemetery burial register and Howard Chapel register, Bedford. Contributed by Annibee. Two identical clippings captured (slightly different URLs); content is the same record. The duplicate (`Frederick Charles Tall (1846-1859) - Find a Grave... 1.md`) has been noted but not separately summarised.

**Frederick Charles Tall** — son of **Edward Tall, a carpenter, and Sarah (nee Ward)**. Born 1846, Ampthill. Died 21 May 1859, aged 12–13, Bedford. Buried 25 May 1859, Foster Hill Road Cemetery, **plot D9-76**.

Address at death: **Grey Friars Walk, St Paul's, Bedford.**

Death announced in the Bedfordshire Times and Independent, Saturday 28 May 1859.

Howard Chapel congregation. Burial at Foster Hill Road (Howard Chapel burial ground closed 1855).

No stone; grave lies within what is now the Garden of Remembrance.

**Key finding — maiden name confirmed:** The record explicitly names the mother as "Sarah (nee Ward)." This definitively resolves the open question in tall-ward-maulden and sarah-ward-1806: Sarah's maiden name was **Ward**, not Tall. Edward Tall was her husband; she took the Tall surname on marriage. Rebecca Tall's surname came from her father Edward.

**Key finding — Edward's occupation:** Edward Tall is described as "a carpenter." This is the first confirmed occupation for him.

**Family picture:** Frederick Charles (1846) died aged 12, one year before Rebecca Tall (1844) would have been 15. Frederick and Rebecca were full siblings — both children of Edward and Sarah Ward. This also confirms the family was resident in Bedford by at least 1859, with Frederick living at Grey Friars Walk.
