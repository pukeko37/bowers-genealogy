FamilySearch person M6JP-5CV. Lucy Morris, born 1826, Haynes, Bedfordshire; christened 20 August 1826 Haynes. Died 1895, Ampthill, Bedfordshire — the same year as her husband John Geary.

**Residence history:** 1841–1881 Haynes; 1891 Maulden. Named addresses: Silver Street; Church End.

Marriage: John Geary, 1848, Haynes.

Parents: **Thomas Morris** (1802–1858) and **Mary Bennet** (1801–1834), married 1825, Haynes. Mary Bennet died in 1834 when Lucy was about 8. Thomas died in 1858. Lucy was thus orphaned early.

Siblings: Susanna (1829), Joseph (1831), James (1832).

Note: Beulah Bunker's middle name "Lucy" (full name Beulah Rebecca Lucy Bunker) almost certainly honours Lucy Morris — Eliza's mother and Beulah's maternal grandmother.
