FamilySearch sources page for Ephraim Bunker (M9S7-BX7), son of [[caleb-bunker-1790|Caleb Bunker (1790)]] and Catherine Bull. 11 sources attached.

## Key facts established

**Birth:** 16 August 1821, Stevington, Bedfordshire. Christened 26 September 1824 at Stevington — the same mass ceremony as his cousin Ebenezer (Joseph's son), both three years after their births.

**1841 census:** In Caleb's household at West End, Stevington. Age 20. Unmarried. Siblings present: Ruth (15) and Lydia (15).

**Emigration:** Arrived New South Wales on **31 March 1845** on the vessel ***Elizabeth*** as an assisted (bounty) immigrant. Origin listed as "Steavington, Bedfordshe." Age given as 24. Listed alone — **no wife with him on arrival.** This resolves the previously recorded claim that he married in Bedfordshire before departing; he did not.

**Marriage:** Married **Mary Ann Betts** in **1852** at **Camden, New South Wales** (St John's, Camden). He had been in Australia for seven years before marrying. Mary Ann Betts appears in all subsequent records as his wife.

**Children (from Australian records):**
- **Ephraim Bunker** (b.~1875, died 28 March 1877, Crookwell) — died in infancy
- **Catherine Bunker** — married Martin Moore, 1895, at Crookwell
- **David George Bunker** (b.~1870) — married Ada Lilian Christie, 1896, at Crookwell

**Death:** 5 July 1887, New South Wales. Buried Crookwell General Cemetery. Death registered at Crookwell (registration no. 6018). Age at death given as 67; birth year estimated as 1820 in the death record (one year early). Father named as Caleb Bunker, mother as Catherine.

## Settlement

Ephraim settled in the **Camden / Crookwell** area of New South Wales. Camden (where he married in 1852) is south-west of Sydney; Crookwell is in the Southern Tablelands, about 190 km south-west of Sydney. By the time of his children's marriages and his own death (1887–1896), all records are from Crookwell. His cousin Ebenezer settled at Campbelltown (adjacent to Camden), suggesting both initially settled in the Camden district before Ephraim moved inland to Crookwell.

## Note on the emigration narrative

The previous wiki text stated "Ephraim married before leaving Bedfordshire." This was incorrect. He arrived alone on 31 March 1845 and did not marry until 1852. The error arose from conflation with Ebenezer, who did marry at Stevington in November 1844 before departing. See [[ebenezer-bunker-1822-familysearch]].
