FamilySearch sources page for Daniel Willis Matthews Sr., 28 sources across two clippings (original and continuation). FamilySearch ID LTWY-3J3.

## Key Records

### Birth and Christening

Parish register (England, Births and Christenings, 1538–1975): **Daniel Matthews, christened 17 December 1820, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire**. Parents: Thomas Matthews and Mary.

LDS Record of Members gives birth date as **24 June 1821** (one record) — the year 1821 is likely a transcription error for 1820, since the christening is dated December 1820 and birth must precede it.

### 1841 Census

Daniel Matthews (20, Bedfordshire) in the Thomas Matthews / Mary Matthews household at **Houchen End, Whipsnade**, with Thomas (70), Mary (60), and John (30).

### Marriage to Sarah Garner — 1842

England Marriages, 1538–1973: **Daniel Matthews** married **Sarah Garner**, **9 October 1842**, Kensworth, Hertfordshire. (Kensworth, Thomas Matthews's birthplace, was just across the county border from Whipsnade.)

LDS Record of Members (multiple entries for Sarah Garner Matthews) gives a marriage date of **10 June 1847** — this appears to be the date of their LDS temple sealing or ecclesiastical recognition of the marriage, not the civil registration.

Sarah Garner: born **13 February 1822**, Caddington, Hertfordshire. Both Daniel and Sarah were LDS-baptised on **12 September 1846**.

### Eliza's Christening — 1843

England, Births and Christenings: **Eliza Matthews**, christened **9 July 1843**, Whipsnade, parents Daniel Matthews and Sarah. (LDS record of members gives Eliza's birth date as 9 February 1844, a likely discrepancy in transcription or recording; christening 9 Jul 1843 implies birth in early 1843 or late 1842.)

### 1851 Census

Daniel Mathews (30, Ag Lab, born Whipsnade Beds), Whipsnade:

| Name | Relationship | Age | Birthplace |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sarah Mathews | Wife | 29 | Waddside, Bedfordshire |
| Eliza Mathews | Daughter | 8 | Whipsnade, Bedfordshire |
| Ann Mathews | Daughter | 6 | Whipsnade, Bedfordshire |
| Mary Mathews | Daughter | 2 | Whipsnade, Bedfordshire |

"Waddside, Bedfordshire" is a census transcription of Sarah's birthplace — she is confirmed born in Caddington, Hertfordshire (adjacent parish, often grouped with Bedfordshire in postal usage). Three daughters confirmed by 1851: Eliza (~1843), Ann (~1845), Mary (~1849).

### Daughters Who Died on the Trail

Two daughters died in 1853 during the overland crossing, buried at Pioneer Children's Memorial, Salt Lake City:
- **Sarah Ann Matthews**: born 11 May 1844, Bedfordshire; died 1853, Nebraska.
- **Emma Matthews**: born 29 April 1851, Bedfordshire; died 1853 (also a trail death).

These children do not appear in the 1851 census (Sarah Ann may be "Ann" aged 6; Emma was born April 1851, possibly after census date). Both died before reaching Utah.

### Emigration

Utah Mormon Pioneer Overland Travel Database: **Daniel Willis Matthews**, departure **13 June 1853**, arrived **16 October 1853**, **Cyrus H. Wheelock Company**, Deseret. Birth date given as 24 Jun 1820; death date given as 15 Apr 1905 (the actual death date was 15 Apr 1903 — a database error).

### 1880 Census

Daniel W. Mathews (65, Farmer, England), Virgin City, Kane, Utah Territory, with second wife Mary Lea Mathews (Wife, 44, Missouri) and children:

| Name | Relationship | Age | Birthplace |

Daniel Jr. (born 24 November 1872, Virgin Washington Utah) matches the 8-year-old Daniel. The 1900 census says Daniel and Mary Lea had been married 28 years — placing the marriage at ~1872, consistent with Daniel Jr.'s birth. Maryam (24) and Olive Lucy (22) in 1880, born Utah ~1856–1858, predate this marriage and may be Daniel Sr.'s daughters by Sarah Garner who survived the trail.

### 1900 Census

Daniel W Mathews (80, born June 1820), Grafton, Washington, Utah, with Mary L Mathews (Wife, 64, Missouri, years married 28), stepsons Jacob Lee (32) and John H Lee (39) [Mary Lea's sons from her previous Lee marriage], son Daniel W. Matthews (28, Utah), daughter Charlot Mathews (23, Utah).

### Death

Daniel Willis Matthews died **15 April 1903**, Virgin, Washington County, Utah. Buried Virgin Cemetery. Death date confirmed by multiple FamilySearch sources (some erroneously give 1905 — the 1903 date is correct per the contemporary record).

### Son Daniel Willis Matthews Jr.

Utah Death Certificate (21 May 1947, Cedar City, Iron County): Daniel Willis Matthews Jr., born 24 November 1872, Virgin, Washington, Utah; mother listed as Mary L. Groves. Confirms paternity of Daniel Sr. and the second wife's maiden name: **Groves**.
