## Notes

Richard migrated to County Durham in the 1870s, married there, and raised his family in the Hartlepool area (Stranton then West Hartlepool). This is the opposite direction from his brother Charles, who went to London. The name "Charles" was used three times for sons: the first two Charleses died young (1878 in infancy, 1898 at age 18); the third Charles (1900–1987) survived to 87. Richard Randall Norman (1886–1969) carries his father's name and his mother's surname — suggesting some significance in the Randall family. Ann Elizabeth Randall survived her husband and died in 1933.
