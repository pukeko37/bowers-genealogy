FamilySearch sources list (21 sources) for Thomas Batchelar (1803–1872), FamilySearch ID LTX6-QVT. The most significant new finding is the confirmation of Thomas's father.

## Key facts extracted

**Parents confirmed:** The christening record (Ivinghoe, 22 June 1804) names his father as **David Batchelor** and his mother as **Ann**. This is further corroborated by the 1868 marriage record (Thomas, age 64, marrying Susan Durent), which lists David Batchelor as Thomas's father.

**Birth and christening:** Born 9 November 1803, Dagnall area, Buckinghamshire. Christened 22 June 1804 at Ivinghoe, Buckinghamshire.

**Marriage 1:** Thomas Batchelor married Susan Matthews on 22 May 1826 at Shenley, Hertfordshire.

**Children** (all christened Whipsnade, Bedfordshire):
- Emma Batchelor — christened 8 April 1827
- Eliza Batchelor — christened 6 December 1829
- Ann Batchelor — christened 22 December 1833
- Elizabeth Batchelor — christened 24 July 1842

**Census record (1841):** Beech Bucket Wood, Whipsnade. Thomas age 35, wife Susanna age 40, daughters Emma (14), Eliza (11), Ann (7).

**Census record (1851):** Whipsnade. Thomas age 48, birthplace "Dagnal, Buckinghamshire", occupation Ag Lab. Wife Susan age 50, daughters Eliza (21), Ann (17), Elizabeth (8).

**Census record (1861, Whipsnade):** By Whipsnade Green. Thomas age 60, birthplace "Dagnal, Buckinghamshire", Ag Lab, head. Household includes Eliza Birchmore (31), George Birchmore (45), George Birchmore (11), Ellen Birchmore (1). This is the Birchmore household described in [[birchmore-plummer-batchelor-1859]].

**Census record (1861, Ellesborough):** A second 1861 entry (RG09/1009/15/24) shows a "Thomas Batchelor" age 58, birthplace Buckinghamshire, at "Ellesborough Green," occupation "House And Land Proprietor." This conflicts with the Whipsnade entry — both cannot be the same person on the same census night. Likely a FamilySearch misidentification or different individual.

**Daughter's marriages (Thomas as father):**
- Emma Batchelar married Edward Potton, 19 March 1857, Whipsnade
- Ann Batchelar (age 24) married George Gadsden, 1 November 1858, Whipsnade
- Eliza Batchelar married George Plummer Birchmore, 18 June 1859, St Pancras, London
- Elizabeth Batchelor (age 21) married John Room, 9 April 1864, Ivinghoe, Buckinghamshire

**Marriage 2:** Thomas Batchelor (age 64, father David Batchelor) married Susan Durent (age 67) on 11 January 1868 at Whipsnade. "Other People on This Record" lists David Batchelor (Thomas's father) and **George Maynard** — Susan's father. Her maiden name was therefore **Maynard**; Durent was her first married name. She was born in Luton, Bedfordshire (confirmed by 1871 census).

**Census record (1871):** Whipsnade. Thomas age 69, birthplace "Dagnall, Buckinghamshire", Ag Lab, head. Wife Susan Batchelar age 71, birthplace "Luton, Bedfordshire." This Susan is his second wife (Susan Durent), not Susan Matthews who died in 1857.

**Death:** 22 December 1872, Luton registration district, age 70.

**Probate:** 5 March 1873, Principal Registry. Executor: Henry Perkins of Whipsnade, Farmer. Estate under £200.
