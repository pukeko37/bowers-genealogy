FamilySearch sources page for John Geary (1825–1895), aggregating 26 attached sources including christening records, census returns, and marriage records.

**Key facts confirmed:**

- Christened 4 Dec 1825 at Haynes (Hawnes), Bedfordshire. Parents: **Joseph Geary** and **Rhoda**.
- Married **Lucy Morris** 10 September 1848 at Haynes. Lucy's father Thomas Morris is named as a witness.
- 1841 census: at Plum's Lane, Haynes. Household: Joseph Gearey (35), Rhoda Gearey (35), John (15), Pamala (13), Sarah (11), Martha (8), Maray (6), Joseph (4), George (0).
- 1851 census: John is Son-in-law in Thomas Morris's household at Hawnes, aged 25, occupation Woodman, Marital Status Married. Also in household: Lucy (wife, 25) and Eliza (daughter, 1 — the first Eliza, 1849–1856).
- Thomas Morris (Lucy's father) in 1851: Head, widower, aged 49, Ag Lab, Hawnes. Also present: James Morris (19, son).
- Marriage record confirms Joseph Geary as John's father; Thomas Morris as Lucy's father.
- Children's christening records confirm the Haynes sequence: Ann (7 Dec 1851), Eliza (18 Jan 1852 — the first Eliza), Phoebe (22 Jan 1854), Emma (20 Jan 1856).

**Joseph Geary's family** (from 1841 census): John was the eldest surviving child. Siblings present in 1841: Pamela (b. ~1828), Sarah (b. ~1830), Martha (b. ~1833), Mary (b. ~1835), Joseph (b. ~1838), George (b. ~1840). Later siblings (from christening records linked on FamilySearch): Walter (1843), Caleb (1847), Henry (1852–1853). See [[geary-spring-1824]].

**Note on the "1852 Eliza" christening:** The christening record dated 18 Jan 1852 for "Eliza Geary" refers to the *first* Eliza (born 1849), who died in 1856. The late christening date (1849 birth, 1852 christening) is unusual and unexplained. Our Eliza (1859) was a separate child, named for this deceased sister.
