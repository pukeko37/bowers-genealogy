## Key Facts Established

**Birth and baptism:** Baptised 25 August 1811, St Mary's Cardington, Bedfordshire. Parish register entry: "1811 Baptised Elizabeth, illegitimate Child of Rebecca Saunders, Widow, aged nine weeks." This places her birth approximately 24 June 1811. Her mother was Rebecca Saunders, a widow at the time; her father is entirely unknown. Published transcription (The Parish Register of Cardington, 1572–1812, Bedfordshire and Luton Archives): "AU25 Eliz illeg. child of Rebecca Saunders, widow, aged nine weeks."

**Marriage:** 19 October 1834, St Mary Cardington — see [[allen-thody-familysearch]] for the marriage record text.

**Census run 1841–1891** (Cardington / Eastcotts / Cotton End, Bedfordshire throughout):
