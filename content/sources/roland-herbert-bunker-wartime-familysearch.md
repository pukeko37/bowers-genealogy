FamilySearch Memories contribution for G7ZB-V6M. AI-generated narrative covering Roland and Ivy's life from leaving the Navy (1930) through the end of WW2. Some claims require cross-checking; one confirmed factual error noted below.

**Key confirmed facts:**
- 1936: surname change from Bunker to Bowers
- De Havilland factory at Hatfield, **9 miles from Park Hill, Harpenden**; Roland commuted by bicycle
- Roland's role: toolmaking for the Mosquito project
- Thody Bros received a fuel rationing exemption as a "critical activity"
- Luftwaffe raid on Hatfield facility: **3 October 1940**
- First Mosquito prototype flew: **25 November 1940**
- Nell (Nellie) and Lil (Lillian) Norman moved into Park Hill to run the household during the war
- **Norman Bowers joined the Royal Navy in late 1944**, pursuing officer training as an Engineer
- Roland likely left de Havilland sometime between 1945 and 1950 (last Mosquito completed November 1950 at Chester)

**Discrepancy — 150 Acre Lane / V1 damage:**
The clipping states "Ivy's sister Alice and her husband Isaac Mason moved into the Bowers' home … due to damage to their house and shop at 150 Acre Lane in Brixton from a V1 flying bomb." This appears to be a conflation of two separate people: **150 Acre Lane** is associated with [[mabel-annie-norman-1883|Mabel Norman]] and her husband [[john-james-mason-1878|John James Mason (Jack)]], while [[alice-norman-1890|Alice Norman]] and [[isaac-mason-1882|Isaac Mason (Ike)]] were at **4 Yukon Road, Balham**. The existing wiki and independent source data support the Mabel/Jack identification for 150 Acre Lane. This claim in the Memory is not accepted.
