FamilySearch sources page for Thomas Norman (GM8R-L1X). Thirteen sources attached.

**Marriage, August 1815** — Two records give slightly different dates. Cambridge Parish Registers: Thomas married "Elizh Fear or Fea?r" on 13 August 1815 at Swavesey (both listed as Single). A second Cambridge Parish Registers record and a Cambridgeshire Bishop's Transcripts record give 20 August 1815 as the date, with Elizabeth Fear's full name spelled out. The two-date discrepancy likely reflects banns reading on 13 August and the ceremony on 20 August, or a transcription variant.

**Son Thomas's christening, 15 October 1815** — Willingham, Cambridgeshire. Three separate records (Cambridge Parish Registers, Cambridgeshire Bishop's Transcripts, England Births and Christenings) confirm a son Thomas Norman was christened at Willingham on 15 October 1815 — just two months after the August 1815 marriage. This son Thomas (1815) died in infancy or childhood; he does not appear in the 1841 census. The [[thomas-norman-1827|Thomas Norman (1827–1905)] who married Ann Tabram is a different, later child also named Thomas.

**Son Robert Norman's marriage, 15 October 1844** — Cambridge Parish Registers. Thomas Norman mentioned as father in the record for his son Robert Norman's marriage to Mary Anne Pratt at Swavesey, 15 October 1844. Also present: John Pratt.

**Son Thomas Norman's (1827) marriage, 1850** — Two records. Cambridgeshire Bishop's Transcripts: Thomas Norman (son) married Ann Tabran at Swavesey, 6 February 1850; Ann's father Richard Tabran also present. Cambridge Parish Registers: marriage date given as 26 February 1850, Ann Tabram, father Richard Tabram. The date discrepancy (6 vs 26 February) may represent banns vs. ceremony. FamilySearch lists the marriage as "3 February 1850."

**Census, 1841** — England and Wales, Census. Thomas Norman, M, age 45 (b. est. 1792–1796), Cambridgeshire, Boxworth End, Swavesey. Household: Robert (M, 20), Sarah (F, 15), Thomas (M, 13), Ann (F, 9). Note: wife Elizabeth had died in 1832; Thomas appears without a wife in this household.

**Census, 1851** — England and Wales, Census. Thomas Norman, lodger, M, age 57 (b. est. 1794), born Over, Cambridgeshire. Marital status: Widower. Occupation: Ag Lab. Living at the household of son Robert Norman (age 29, Married, Ag Lab, born Swavesey) and Robert's wife Mary Ann (age 27, born Coton, Cambridgeshire), with three young children: Jane (5), Elizabeth (3), Rebekah (1).

**Burial, 23 December 1855** — Cambridge Parish Registers. Thomas Norman, age 61 years (b. est. 1794), buried Swavesey, 23 December 1855.
