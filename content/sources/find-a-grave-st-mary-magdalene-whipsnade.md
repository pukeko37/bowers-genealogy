Find a Grave listing of all 243 memorials recorded at St Mary Magdalene Churchyard, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire. Clipped 2026-06-06.

## Confirmed burials relevant to our families

**Hain family (Dell Farm):**
- **James Edward Hain** — 8 Jul 1832 – 12 Sep 1911 (exact dates; confirms our record)
- **Dora Hain** — birth and death dates unknown on Find a Grave stone; almost certainly Dora Minnie Hain (1910–1996) or another Hain
- **Margaret Hain** — birth and death dates unknown; almost certainly Margaret D Hain, James Jr's sister, recorded in the 1911 census
- **James Hain (1877–1932) is NOT in this list** — buried elsewhere

**James Purton Turvey (Dell Farm co-tenant):**
- **James Purton Turvey** — 1867 – 13 Feb 1910. He died, he did not depart. This explains his absence from the 1911 census. His widow **Sarah Turvey** (1873–1956) is buried beside him — almost certainly the Sarah Bird named as his wife in the birth record of their son Arthur James (born 1898). A large Turvey family cluster is buried in the churchyard across several generations.

**Bransom family:**
- **Albert Bransom** — 1871 – 1 Apr 1940 (exact death date confirmed; matches our record)
- **Mary Bransom** — 1872 – 30 Oct 1950 (Albert's wife; not previously in the wiki)

**Perkins family (Dell Farm, 1851–1871):**
Henry Perkins the farmer is not in this list, but his family graves are:
- Anne Perkins — 1830 – 9 Oct 1867 (his wife; died same year as Eliza Batchelor)
- Henry Holloway Perkins — 1856 – 6 Sep 1864 (child, died aged 8)
- Mary Florence Perkins — 1861 – 23 Jan 1862 (infant)
- Henry Perkins — 1859 – 13 Mar 1874 (child, died aged 14)
Three of Henry and Anne's children died young and are buried here; Henry himself may be buried elsewhere or his stone is lost.

**William Thomas Holden:**
- **William Thomas Holden** — 14 Dec 1838 – 18 Dec 1919. The Rector of St Mary Magdalene who conducted the Thody–Cook wedding on 6 February 1894. Buried at his own church. His wife **Sarah Blake Holden** (1843–1905) and children **Lilian Kate Holden** (1874–1963) and **William Meynell Holden** (1878–1911) are also buried here.

**Albert Ralph Cook — birth year discrepancy:**
The list records **Albert Ralph Cook** as born **1883**, death 13 Jan 1932. Our wiki records him as born 1886 (from FamilySearch sources). The census ages (5 in 1891, 15 in 1901) are consistent with 1886, not 1883. Likely a registration vs. baptism gap or a Find a Grave transcription error. Flagged for GRO birth entry check.

## Other notable entries

The Turvey family dominates the churchyard with at least 15 burials across three generations, including several with maiden names connecting them to the Hubbocks and Cook families of the village. **Hilda Kate Cook Turvey** (1894–1987) — maiden name Cook — may be related to the Cook families in this wiki, though the connection is not established.

The Stanbridge family (H.W. Stanbridge was a named tenant on other Pedley Settled Estates lots at the 1915 auction) also has a significant cluster: Clara (Twigg) Stanbridge 1871–1963, Heath W Stanbridge 1872–1943, and several children.
