FamilySearch sources page for Francis Ernest Geary (1870–1936), aggregating 8 attached sources including birth registration, christening, census returns 1871 and 1881, burial record, and probate record.

**Key facts confirmed:**

- Born 8 February 1870. Birth registration 1870, Ampthill, Bedfordshire (Volume 3B, p. 413).
- Christened 11 May 1873, Haynes — same day as his aunt [[lydia-rose-geary-1868|Lydia Rose Geary]], both receiving late christenings. Mother: Ann Geary. No father named.
- 1871 census: "Ernest Geary," age 1, in [[john-geary-1825|John]] and [[lucy-morris-1826|Lucy Geary]]'s household at Haynes — raised as part of his grandparents' family.
- 1881 census: "Ernest Geary," age 11, at The Warren, Haynes, still in the grandparents' household. Siblings listed: Albert (14) and Lydia (12).
- Died 28 September 1936. Probate granted 20 October 1936 at **Reading, Berkshire** — confirming he ended his life in Berkshire, far from Bedfordshire.
- Wife: **Eva Maud Geary**. Also named in the probate: Jessie Willis and William John Willis (likely Eva's family or co-executors).

**Note on burial record:** A Reading Cemetery record for "Frank Ernest Geary" buried 1 October 1936 shows age 49 (born ~1887) — this is a different person, misattached to this record in FamilySearch. The reliable death date comes from the probate entry: 28 September 1936. Francis Ernest was 66 at death.
