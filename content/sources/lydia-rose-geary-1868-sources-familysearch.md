FamilySearch sources page for Lydia Rose Geary (1868–1932), aggregating 8 attached sources including birth registration, christening, census returns 1871–1911, Find a Grave burial record, and death registration.

**Key facts confirmed:**

- Birth registration 1868, Ampthill, Bedfordshire (Volume 3B, p. 372).
- Christened 11 May 1873, Haynes, Bedfordshire — a remarkably late christening, five years after birth. Parents: John Geary and Lucy.
- 1871 census: age 2, at parental home, Haynes.
- 1881 census: Lydia Geary, age 12, Scholar, at parental home, The Warren, Haynes.
- 1911 census: Lydia Geary (listed as surname only, age 37), staying as "Aunt" in the household of **Walter Berta Southgate** (age 30, cab driver, Bedford) — this is Bertie Southgate, son of her sister Phoebe (who died January 1910). Also in household: May Southgate (16) and Amy Emma Southgate (13) — Phoebe's younger daughters — and **Jaspers Fred Geary** (9, born Luton) — Lydia's son.
- Burial: Foster Hill Road Cemetery, Bedford. Find a Grave records death as December 1932.
- Death registration: 1932, Bedfordshire (Volume 3B, p. 315). Age 63.

**Key finding:** In 1911, one year after Phoebe's death, Lydia moved in with Phoebe's son Bertie to help care for Phoebe's younger children. Her own son Jasper Fred Geary (born Luton ~1902) was with her. The identity of Jasper's father and whether Lydia married are confirmed by the 1921 census (widowed) — see [[lydia-rose-geary-1868]].
