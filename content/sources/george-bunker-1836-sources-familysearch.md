FamilySearch sources page for George Bunker (FamilySearch ID: G4JZ-ZJS). Thirteen sources attached, covering his full life from 1841 through death in 1917.

## Marriage

George Bunker married **Ann Pedder** on **8 April 1858** at **St Paul, Bedford**, Bedfordshire. A notice of the marriage also appears dated 21 March 1858 (likely the banns publication). Ann Pedder was born ~1838 in **Westoning, Bedfordshire** — the same village where Thomas Bunker (1704) had been born and raised a century and a half earlier.

## Children

Five children confirmed across census records:

1. **Sarah Ann Bunker** — born 19 Oct 1860; christened 9 December 1860 at a **Wesleyan Methodist** chapel, Bedford Circuit (Dunstable area). Confirms Nonconformist practice in this branch of the family. Age 20 in 1881 (Bedford-born).
2. **Harriett E Bunker** — born ~1865, Bedford. Age 6 in 1871, 16 in 1881.
3. **Alice Bunker** — born ~1872, Bedford. Age 9 in 1881, 19 in 1891.
4. **Albert Bunker** — born ~1875, **New Tupton, Derbyshire**. Age 6 in 1881, 16 in 1891, 26 in 1901 (birthplace then recorded as "Chesterfield, Derbyshire" — New Tupton is in the Chesterfield registration district). Albert was still in the household in 1901.
5. **Amy Bunker** — born ~1878, **Derby, Derbyshire**. Age 3 in 1881, 33 in 1911 (single); married **Lewis Allsop** on **1 July 1911** in Derby.

Albert's New Tupton birthplace (c.1875) and Amy's Derby birthplace (c.1878) together bracket the family's move: they were in Bedfordshire through Alice's birth (~1872) and in New Tupton by Albert's birth (~1875). This is the precise window of the Derbyshire migration.
