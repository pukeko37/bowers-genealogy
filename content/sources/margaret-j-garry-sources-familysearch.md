FamilySearch sources page for Margaret J Garry (P8Z4-P2J), wife of George Norman. Contains 5 attached sources spanning 1879–1911.

## Sources extracted

**1879 christening record (William Norman):** Confirms mother's name as "Margaret." William Norman christened 18 Jun 1879, born 25 May 1879, at Lynne Street United Free Methodist Chapel, West Hartlepool. Father George Norman.

**1881 census:** Margaret Norman, age 27, birthplace **East H Pool** (East Hartlepool), Durham. Occupation: Grocers Porter Wife. Living at Stranton with husband George (30, Swavesey) and sons William (1) and Thomas (4).

**1891 census:** Margaret Norman, age 37, birthplace Durham. Living at Stranton with husband George (40, Cambridgeshire) and sons Thomas (14), William (11), Alfred (7) and daughter Ethel J (9).

**1901 census:** Recorded as **"May Norman"**, age 47, birthplace Hartlepool, Durham. Living at West Hartlepool with husband George (50) and children: Thomas (25), William (21), Alfred (17), George (12), Jack (10), "Prand" (daughter, 8 — transcription error for Francis), Susie (4), Kate (7), **Poll** (daughter, 15). Also lodger John Ross (44, Glossop, Derbyshire).

**1911 census:** Margaret Norman, age 56, birthplace Hartlepool, Durham. Status: **Widowed**. "Number in Family: 8." Living at West Hartlepool with children: William (31), Alfred (27), George (22), Jack (20), Francis (daughter, 19), Kate (17), Susan (14).

## Key findings

**Birthplace confirmed:** Margaret was born in Hartlepool (or East Hartlepool), Durham — a local woman when George arrived from Swavesey in 1875.

**"May" alias in 1901:** The 1901 census recorded her as "May Norman." Her FamilySearch entry is "Margaret J Garry" — the J initial is her middle initial. "May" may be her middle name or a household nickname; she returned to "Margaret" in 1911.

**"Poll" Norman (daughter, born ~1886):** A daughter recorded as "Poll" (age 15, born West Hartlepool) in the 1901 census but not present in the 1911 household nor in the FamilySearch tree. "Poll" is a traditional form of "Mary" or "Polly." If she is a legitimate additional child, the family had 11 children rather than the 10 listed in the FamilySearch tree. She was ~25 by 1911 — possibly married and gone, or deceased.

**Ethel J Norman absent post-1891:** Ethel J (born ~1882) appears in the 1891 census (age 9) but is absent from both 1901 and 1911. She would be ~19 in 1901. Possible explanations: married before 1901, moved away, or died.

**Francis confirmed:** "Prand" in 1901 is a clear transcription error for "Francis" — confirmed as Francis Norman (daughter, born ~1892/1893) in the 1911 census.

**Kate's birth year:** Age 7 in 1901 and age 17 in 1911 point to birth ~1893/1894, not 1895 as listed in the FamilySearch tree.

**1911: widowhood documented.** The 1911 census explicitly records Margaret as "Widowed" — eight years after George's death in September 1903. She remained in West Hartlepool and continued as household head with seven children still at home.
