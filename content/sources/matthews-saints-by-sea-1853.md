## Overview

## Key finding — the voyage was a sea crossing, not the Wheelock Company

The **sea crossing** (Liverpool to New Orleans) departed **17 January 1853** and arrived **6 March 1853**. The **Cyrus H. Wheelock Company** was the subsequent *overland* trek, departing Keokuk, Iowa on **13 June 1853** and arriving Salt Lake Valley **6–16 October 1853**. The "Ellen Marie" vessel name and the date of "13 June 1853" in Daniel's FamilySearch print view appear to conflate the overland departure date with the sea voyage. The Saints by Sea record, which confirms Daniel's FamilySearch ID directly, is the authoritative source for the sea crossing. The ship name for this voyage is not stated in the individual passenger records; "Ellen Marie" may refer to this same ship under a different source, or may be a FamilySearch error.

After landing at New Orleans, the group would have travelled up the Mississippi and Missouri rivers to Iowa, arriving at the outfitting point at Keokuk in time for the Wheelock Company's June departure.

## Ann Matthews (age 8) — confirmed emigrant

A notable finding: **Ann Matthews (age 8)** is listed as a passenger in Daniel's family group. She was aged 6 in the 1851 census. This confirms she emigrated with the family in 1853. Her fate after arrival — including whether she survived the overland crossing — remains unknown, as she does not appear in Daniel's later Utah censuses. She is not mentioned in the Sarah Garner Find a Grave memorial's child list, which may be incomplete.
