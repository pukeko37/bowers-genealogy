FamilySearch person P68D-T5X. Richard Norman, born before 1723; christened 26 November 1723 at Cottenham, Cambridgeshire. Died 1783 at Over, Cambridgeshire. Occupation: Weaver (1753, Over).

**Parents:** William Norman (1687–1747) and Alice Briggs (1685–1766), married 3 May 1710 at Cottenham.

**Marriage to Mary Hall:** 1 October 1749 at Over, Cambridgeshire. Children: Robert Norman (1769–1842). This is the marriage accepted as credible for our Richard Norman.

**Marriage to Mary Essex — disputed.** The print view also records a marriage to Mary Essex (1722–1765), 27 April 1740 at Cottenham, with children William Norman (1739) and Richard Norman (1745). This attribution is not accepted — see [[richard-norman-1723]] and [[norman-essex-1740]] for the full analysis. In summary: Mary Essex died in 1765 but the Hall marriage was in 1749, creating a bigamy impossibility; and a second FamilySearch profile (L565-ZVH, Richard Norman 1718–1753) is attached to the same Essex marriage with different children, making the P68D-T5X attachment almost certainly a duplication error.

**Data quality warning.** This FamilySearch profile has accumulated obviously erroneous records, including a daughter Mary Norman supposedly born on 31 March 1834 — fifty-one years after Richard's death in 1783. The profile cannot be trusted uncritically. Individual attachments require independent verification.
