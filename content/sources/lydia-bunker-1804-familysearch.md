FamilySearch person MM1H-NXC. Print view and 24-source sources page.

**Vitals:** Born 4 February 1804, Millbrook. Christened 7 April 1806, Millbrook. Died 31 March 1883, Bedford. Buried Foster Hill Road Cemetery, Bedford — the same cemetery as her sister-in-law [[elizabeth-watts-1810]] (confirmed by earlier ingest).

**Residences:** 1841 Bedfordshire (census); 1851 St Pauls, Bedford; 1861 Saint Paul, Bedford (Mill Street address).

**Marriage:** Thomas W Inskip (1800–1875), 25 March 1832, St Paul's Church, Bedford.

**Children** (seven, all registered via Baptist/Nonconformist records at Mill Street Baptist, Bedford):
- Samuel Inskip (b. 13 Feb 1833, Bedford)
- Martha Inskip (b. 28 Feb 1836, Bedford)
- James Inskip (1839–Deceased)
- Lydia Inskip (1841–Deceased)
- Mary A Inskip (1843–Deceased)
- Hannah Inskip (1846–1938)
- Thomas Inskip (1848–Deceased)

**Nonconformist affiliation:** Children registered at **Mill Street Baptist, Bedford** (Non-Conformist Record Indexes RG4-8). Thomas Inskip was Baptist, not Wesleyan Methodist. This is a distinct denomination from the Wesleyan connection associated with the main Bunker-Watts line and the 1806 mass Millbrook christening.

**Note on name:** Not to be confused with [[lydia-brown-1769]], her mother, nor with Lydia Bunker (1826), daughter of her brother Caleb (1790).
