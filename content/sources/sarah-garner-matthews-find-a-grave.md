Find a Grave memorial ID 232789207 for Sarah Garner Matthews (13 Feb 1822 – Oct 1853). Maintained by Ryan D. Curtis; originally created by John Warnke.

## Key facts

- **Death:** Crossing the Plains (near Salt Lake City, Salt Lake, Utah). Died circa October 1853.
- **Death confirmed by:** Obituary of her daughter Eliza Matthews Taylor, which states Sarah did not make it to Salt Lake City with her family.
- **Wheelock Company listing:** Sarah Matthews (31) travelled with Daniel Willis Matthews (32), Eliza Matthews (10), Sarah Ann Matthews (9), Mary Matthews (5), Emma Matthews (2) in the division under Captain Kendall.

## Sarah Garner's parents — new data

The memorial gives Sarah's parents as:
- Father: **William Garner Sr.** (1794–1862)
- Mother: **Kitty Perry** (1796–1843)

This is the first record to name Sarah's parents. Kitty Perry died 1843 — when Sarah was ~21. Her father William survived until 1862. No further records for these individuals are yet in the wiki.

## Children listed

The memorial lists four children: Eliza Ann, Sarah Ann, Mary, and Emma. This omits Ann Matthews (confirmed on the 1853 Saints by Sea passenger manifest). The memorial's child list is likely incomplete — Ann is confirmed as a passenger and may have died during or shortly after the trail crossing, or the memorial simply was not researched fully.

## Note on birth date

The memorial gives birth date as 13 February 1822. The FamilySearch record gives christening date 10 March 1822. These are consistent — birth preceding christening by ~four weeks. Both can be considered correct for different purposes.
