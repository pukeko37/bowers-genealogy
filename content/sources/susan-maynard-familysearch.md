Two clippings: FamilySearch print view and sources list (14 sources) for Susan Maynard (1802–Deceased), FamilySearch ID M9D9-RMM. Clipped 2026-05-24 following identification of Susan as Thomas Batchelar's second wife and the deduction that her maiden name was Maynard.

## Key facts extracted

**Birth:** 1 March 1802 (one source) / 11 March 1802 (second source). Birthplace: Luton, Bedfordshire. Both birth records are from the **Baptist register** (RG4-8, Non-Conformist Record Indexes) — the family were Nonconformists. Father confirmed as **George Maynard**; mother **Sarah** (Rumball — confirmed from the print view's Parents and Siblings panel).

**Parents:** George Maynard and Sarah Rumball, married 14 February 1794 at Luton, Bedfordshire. Known siblings: Mary Maynard (1795–1884) and Keziah Maynard (1800–Deceased).

**Marriage 1 — William Woodcraft (1777–1835):** Married 20 May 1827 at Whipsnade, Bedfordshire. Five children christened at Whipsnade:
- Ann Woodcraft — christened 6 January 1828
- George Woodcraft — christened 6 December 1829
- James Woodcraft — christened 9 September 1832
- Amos Woodcraft — christened 3 August 1834
- Charles Woodcraft (1836–1876) — christened 14 February 1836

William Woodcraft is dated 1777–1835 in FamilySearch, which would make Charles a posthumous child (born February 1836 if William died in 1835). William's age at marriage (about 50) and the presence of older individuals in the 1841 census household suggest he may have had a prior family.

**1841 census (as Susan Woodcroft):** Frog Corner, Whipsnade. Age 40. Household includes Thomas Woodcroft (25), David Woodcroft (20), Charlotte Woodcroft (15), George (11), James (9), Amos (6), Charles (5). Thomas, David, and Charlotte are older than Susan's known Woodcraft children — they may be William's children from a prior relationship, but this is not confirmed.

**Marriage 2 — Edmund Durrant (1795–after 1851):** Married 9 June 1849 at Whipsnade. Edmund was born in Whipsnade, occupation agricultural labourer. This is the source of the "Durent/Durrant" surname by which Susan was known at the time of her third marriage. George Maynard is confirmed as Susan's father in the 1849 marriage record ("Entry for Edmund Durrant and George Maynard, 9 June 1849").

**1851 census (as Susanna Durrant):** Whipsnade. Age 51 (consistent with 1802 birth). Occupation: **"Labs Wife And School Mistress"** — she ran a small school in the village while married to Edmund. Household includes Thomas Woodcraft (35) and Charlotte Woodcraft (27). Edmund (56, head) and Susanna (wife, 51) are the couple. Birthplace recorded as "Setan, Bedfordshire" — a transcription error, almost certainly for Luton.

Edmund Durrant's death is not recorded in the sources here, but he was gone before 1868.

**Marriage 3 — Thomas Batchelar (1803–1872):** Married 11 January 1868 at Whipsnade. George Maynard again confirmed as Susan's father in the 1868 marriage record ("Susan Durent's Parents and Siblings: George Maynard, Father").

**1871 census:** Susan Batchelar, age 71, birthplace Luton, Bedfordshire. In Thomas's household at Whipsnade.

**Death:** Not recorded; FamilySearch lists her as "Deceased." She was 71 at the 1871 census. Thomas died December 1872; her fate thereafter is unknown.
