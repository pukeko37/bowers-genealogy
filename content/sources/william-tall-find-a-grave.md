Find a Grave memorial ID 245519978. Source: Foster Hill Road Cemetery burial register and Howard Chapel register, Bedford. Contributed by Annibee.

**William Tall** — carpenter. Born ~1830 (calculated from age at death). Died aged 32, buried **14 July 1862**, Foster Hill Road Cemetery, Bedford.

Address at death: **Derby Street, St Peter's, Bedford.**

Howard Chapel congregation — same chapel as Edward Tall and Frederick Charles Tall. Burial at Foster Hill Road (Howard Chapel burial ground closed 1855).

No stone on this grave.

**Marriage:** William married **Fanny Webb**, registered Hitchin, Q1 1852. His widow Fanny later remarried **William Ansell**, registered Hitchin, Q4 1872 — ten years after William's death.

**GRO name variant:** Recorded as William TOLE in the GRO index; FreeBMD gives TOLL. Both the Foster Hill Road burial register and the Howard Chapel register have TALL. The TOLE/TOLL entries are likely transcription errors of the same person.

**Relationship to Edward Tall:** William shared the same surname, same religious congregation (Howard Chapel), and the same parish (St Peter's, Bedford) as Edward Tall. He was born ~1830, making him 24 years younger than Edward (born 1806). The most likely relationship is **son of Edward Tall** from a marriage or relationship prior to Edward's partnership with Sarah Ward — Rebecca (1844) and Frederick Charles (1846) being the children of Edward and Sarah. However, no direct documentary evidence of the father-son relationship is held in current sources. A brother relationship (if Edward had a much younger sibling) or other kin is possible but less probable given the age gap. The relationship remains unconfirmed.

William was a carpenter, the same trade as Edward. If he was Edward's son, this represents occupational continuity across the generations.
