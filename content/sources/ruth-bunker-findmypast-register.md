## Step 2: Dataset deduplication

FindMyPast consistently returns each baptism, marriage, or burial event twice: once from the generic county-level dataset (e.g. "Bedfordshire Baptisms") and once from the specific parish-level dataset (e.g. "Bedfordshire Baptisms, Millbrook, St Michael & All Angels"). These are always the same event. Below they are collapsed to a single entry; the parish-specific record is preferred where available. Three additional events appear in both a Bedfordshire county dataset and the national GRO index (England & Wales Births/Marriages/Deaths); these are also collapsed.

**Exception noted:** The 1850 marriage at Tilsworth/Woburn appears with three distinct transcript IDs — two Bedfordshire Marriages entries for Tilsworth (R\_853592576/2 and R\_843926330/2) plus one England & Wales Marriages record for Woburn. The two Bedfordshire entries may represent genuinely different events (two Ruth Bunkers married in Tilsworth in 1850) or a digitisation duplicate. Flagged as uncertain; treated provisionally as two events.

### RB-H: Ruth Bunker b. ~1830, Millbrook (child)
**Records:** #10 (Baptism 1836, Millbrook), #11 (Burial 1836, Millbrook, born 1830), #59 (*Northampton Mercury*, 14 May 1836)  
**Notes:** The newspaper report substantially confirms this individual. A coroner's inquest was held at Millbrook on 13 May 1836 (old May Day, as noted in the paper) on the body of Ruth Bunker, "aged five years," who had been left in a room with another child when her clothes accidentally caught fire. The inquest verdict is not fully legible in the clipping but the phrasing matches an accidental death finding. The age of five is consistent with the burial record's birth year of 1830 (she would have been 5 going on 6 in May 1836). The baptism and burial in the same year at the same parish almost certainly represent the same child: baptized (possibly near death or earlier that year) and buried 1836 following the fire. This is the best-evidenced death among all the Ruth Bunkers in this register aside from RB-D.

**Probable parentage: John Bunker (1803) + Maria West.** The Millbrook Bunker households with children in the early 1830s were limited: John (1803) + Maria West; William (1767) + Sarah Beal; James (1777) + Catherine Blunt (no children documented); James (1807) + Elizabeth Watts (married Nov 1830, first child 1831 — too late). Of these, John (1803) and Maria West are by far the strongest candidate. Their documented children are: Elizabeth West (1824), Sarah (1829), John (1830), James (1832), Hannah (1835), and later Joseph (1837–1839), Joseph (1840), Samuel (1843), George (1845). A child born 1830 or 1831 who died aged 5 in May 1836 would have been omitted from the 1841 census and is exactly the kind of early-death child that FamilySearch collaborative trees routinely miss. The late baptism in 1836 (she was born ~1830 but baptized aged ~6) is consistent with the family's Nonconformist late-registration practice seen across multiple generations. The "another child" left in the room with her is most plausibly James (born ~1832, aged ~3–4 in May 1836) — old enough to be called "another child," young enough to be unable to help. William (1767) + Sarah Beal cannot be ruled out, but William was ~63 in 1830 and only Samuel (~1820) is documented from that marriage. **Confirmed by baptism transcript XAUTO/FHS/BEDFORDSHIRE/BAP/00053835, which should give the father's name.**

## Wiki check: [[ruth-bunker-1855]]

The register reveals one significant discrepancy requiring investigation:

### Birth place

The wiki records Ruth's birth place as **Lavendon, Buckinghamshire**. The GRO birth record (#29) is indexed as **Bedford, Bedfordshire** — meaning the birth was registered in Bedford registration district.

These are incompatible. Lavendon is in the Newport Pagnell registration district (Buckinghamshire/Northamptonshire border), not Bedford district. If Ruth was born at Lavendon in June 1855, the birth would have been registered at Newport Pagnell, not Bedford.

Possibilities:
1. **The birth was actually in Bedford district**, not Lavendon. The wiki's claim may have originated from the 1857 Lavendon christening being mistaken for the 1855 birth place.
2. **The GRO record (#29) is a different Ruth Bunker** — another infant Ruth Bunker born in Bedford district in 1855, not our person.

The 1857 Lavendon christening (as cited in [[ruth-bunker-1855-familysearch]]) places the family in Lavendon for the christening, not necessarily the birth. Christening location ≠ birth location, especially with Nonconformist families who sometimes christened children years late. The transcript of BMD/B/1855/3/AG/000505/027 must be examined to resolve this.

### Marriage 1880

Both the GRO record (#40, Bedford district) and the parish record (#41, Stevington) are present, confirming the 27 September 1880 marriage of Ruth Bunker and George Draper Whiting at Stevington. No issues.
