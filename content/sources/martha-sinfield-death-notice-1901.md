Death notice in the *Luton Times and Advertiser*, Friday 12 July 1901, page 8 (Family Notices column).

**Text (as captured):** "July 7, at 21, Cumberland-street, Luton, Martha Sarah Sinfield, aged 60 years."

This is a deaths-column notice, not a narrative obituary — it appears in a list alongside other local deaths that week (Harriet Andrews, 76; Joseph Thomas Hawes, 18 months).

## New information

**Address at death: 21 Cumberland Street, Luton.** This is the first documentary record of where the Sinfield family were living at the time of Martha's death. Cumberland Street was a residential street in Luton; the address gives a concrete anchor for the family in the town.

**Confirms:** Date (7 July 1901), age (60), name ("Martha Sarah Sinfield"), place (Luton).
