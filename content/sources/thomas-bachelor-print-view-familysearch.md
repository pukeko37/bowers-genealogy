## Notes

Thomas was born in Flamstead, Hertfordshire — the same village where Daniel Cook (Ruth Cooke's father) died. He moved to Whipsnade and settled there, his address recorded as "Frog Corner" — a hamlet also associated with Abraham. His relationship to Thomas Batchelar (1803, Dagnall) — Eliza Batchelor's probable father — is unconfirmed but likely. Dagnall is 4 miles from Flamstead; both men were Batchelar/Bachelor from the same Chilterns area. George Batchelor (1811) being listed as a child despite predating the 1816 marriage warrants further investigation.
