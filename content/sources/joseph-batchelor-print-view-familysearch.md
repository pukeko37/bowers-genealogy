## Notes

Joseph confirms the Batchelor family's deep roots in the Flamstead area, going back at least to his own father James (1725). James Batchelor married in Leighton Buzzard, Bedfordshire — placing the family at the Hertfordshire/Bedfordshire border across multiple generations. This border location is relevant to the unresolved question of how Thomas Batchelar (1803, Dagnall, Buckinghamshire — just across the next border) relates to this family. Dagnall is approximately 5 miles from Flamstead.

Note: Joseph's son Samuel Batchelor (1789–1872) shares a name and approximate generation with Samuel Heels (1797–1845), the Chequers tenant — but they are different people.
