## Marriage confirmed

**Martha Sarah Bunker married Joseph Sinfield on 25 May 1863 at Saint Paul, Bedford.** Two sources confirm: the church marriage register ("England Marriages, 1538–1973", entry for Joseph Sinfield and Martha Sarah Bunker, 25 May 1863, Saint Paul, Bedford, pg 242) and the GRO marriage registration index (1863, Bedford, vol 3B, page 488). The "Henry Hickman" marriage record attached to her FamilySearch page at the same GRO volume/page appears to be a data collision for the same district/quarter and should not be taken as a separate marriage.
