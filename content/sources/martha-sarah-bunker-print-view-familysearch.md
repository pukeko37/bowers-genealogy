Sixth child of James Bunker (1807) and Elizabeth Watts. Settled in Luton, where she died in 1901 aged ~60. An obituary was published — suggests she was known in the community. Her presence in Luton pre-dates Frederick Bunker (Caleb's son) and Beulah's deaths there; Luton was clearly a Bunker family destination. Caleb's sister.
