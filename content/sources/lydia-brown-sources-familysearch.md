FamilySearch sources page for Lydia Brown (LYHK-W98). 10 sources attached.

## Key records

**Marriage** — 31 December 1788, Saint Paul's Walden, Hertfordshire. Married James Bunker (James Thomas Bunker).

**Mass christening — 7 April 1806, Millbrook** — Lydia appears as mother "Lydia" in six children's christening records on the same day (Caleb, Joseph, Ruth, Phoebe, Ebenezer, Lydia, Martha). Consistent with Nonconformist practice. Children's birth dates:
- Caleb Bunker: born 28 May 1790
- Ebenezer Bunker: born 6 May 1798
- Phoebe Bunker: born 10 Oct 1796
- Joseph Bunker: born 3 Dec 1792
- Lydia Bunker: born 4 Feb 1804
- Martha Bunker: born 21 Dec 1801
- Ruth Bunker: born 17 Aug 1794

**1841 census** — Millbrook, registration district Ampthill. Listed as "Lydia Bunker", age 70, in the household of son James Bunker (James Bunker 1807). Last known census record; she died the following year.

**Death registration** — 1842, Ampthill district, Bedfordshire. Volume 6, page 4. Registered under "Lydia Bunker" (married name).

## Notable details
- Birth year range estimated 1767–1771 from 1841 census (age 70); wiki carries "abt 1769" as the midpoint
- Death registered Ampthill, vol 6 p 4 — confirms she died in the Millbrook/Ampthill area
- Her maiden name "Brown" is known only from the marriage record; in all other records she appears as "Lydia" or "Lydia Bunker"
- Appears only as first name "Lydia" in children's christening records — consistent with how parish registers recorded mothers
