Bedfordshire Archives reference **P31/14/64a**, held within the Parish Records of Maulden (fonds P31), Overseers: Apprenticeship sub-fonds (P31/14, 1658–1788).

**Title:** Jn Bunker apprentice to Jos Bunker, Millbrook, Cordwainer.
**Date:** 8 January 1784.
**Persons indexed:** Bunker, Jonathan; Bunker, Joseph.

This is a parish apprenticeship indenture — a document by which the Maulden overseers of the poor bound a child or young person as an apprentice to a named master. The apprentice is **Jonathan (or John) Bunker**, a person from Maulden parish. The master is **Joseph Bunker of Millbrook, Cordwainer** (shoemaker).

## Significance

**Joseph Bunker's occupation:** This is the only record found giving an occupation for [[joseph-bunker-1754|Joseph Bunker (1754–1808)]], youngest son of Thomas Bunker (1704) and Mary Reade. In January 1784 he was approximately 30 years old and established enough in his trade to take on a parish apprentice. His trade was **cordwainer** — a maker of new shoes, distinct from a cobbler who repairs them. The shoemaking/cordwaining trade appears to run continuously in the Millbrook Bunker family: in the census era, Richard Bunker (collateral line, ~1821) and James Bunker (~1780, collateral) appear as shoemakers; so does [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker (1845)]] in his early years at Bedford.

**Maulden connection:** The apprentice Jonathan/John Bunker came from Maulden — the same village where the Tall family (connected to the Bunker line through [[rebecca-tall-1844|Rebecca Tall]]) were rooted from the 1820s onwards. In 1784 this is an earlier and independent Maulden–Millbrook link, suggesting some Bunker presence or connection in Maulden predating the Tall marriage by four decades. Jonathan/John Bunker may be a poor relation of the Millbrook Bunkers, or may share only the surname.

## Search context

This record is item 1 of 31 results from a Bedfordshire Archives catalogue search, clipped during a search for the Lady Holland / Holland estate map of the 1840/41 Millbrook sale. The Holland estate map was not identified among the results. It may be catalogued under the Bedford Estates (Russell) papers rather than parish records.
