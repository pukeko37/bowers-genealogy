## Notes

Elizabeth Birchmore was the second child of [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer Sr]] and [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]], and thus the half-sister of [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen Norman]] (née Birchmore). She followed the classic Victorian path of a rural girl entering domestic service in London: Islington by 1881, Clerkenwell by 1891 — the same neighbourhood where the Norman-Birchmore family would later live.

By 1921, Elizabeth had moved to 4 Yukon Road, Balham — the address of her niece [[alice-norman-1890|Alice Norman]] and Alice's husband [[isaac-mason-1882|Isaac Mason]]. She lived there until her death in 1954 at age 92, over 33 years in the Mason household. She was the last surviving child of GBP Sr and Eliza Batchelor.
