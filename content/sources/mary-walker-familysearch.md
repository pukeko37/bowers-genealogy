- The LDS Record of Members (filed by the LDS church retrospectively): birth 1 February 1766

The 1841 census age of 70 is consistent with birth ~1771 (christening 1770). The 1851 census age of 84 suggests birth ~1767. The FamilySearch profile displays "1766–1859." There is also a note attached giving "Mary Willis, born 24 December 1779, Stanbridge" — this appears to be erroneous data from a different individual attached by a previous contributor.

On balance, birth c.1766–1768 (before the recorded Kensworth christening, which may be for a sibling or have recording errors) seems most consistent with the census ages. The wiki uses 1766 as the birth year per the LDS record, with the caveat that the christening record at Kensworth in 1770 may record a different event.
