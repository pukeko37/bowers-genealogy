FamilySearch print view for Edward Tall, Rebecca's father and Frederick's maternal grandfather.

**Vitals:** Born 9 November 1806, Ampthill, Bedfordshire (christened/baptised same date). Died 16 July 1870, Bedford, Bedfordshire. Buried Howard Chapel, Bedford.

**Residences:** 1851 Maulden, Bedfordshire · 1861 Bedfordshire · Derby Street, Bedford.

Edward shares Maulden residences in 1851 and 1861 with [[sarah-ward-1806|Sarah Ward]], and shares the Derby Street, Bedford address — consistent with them being a married couple. Rebecca Tall (b. 1844) living in Maulden at the 1851 census is the daughter of this household.

Edward died on 16 July 1870 — just nine days after his daughter Rebecca died on 7 July 1870. Both died the same summer, leaving Frederick (age 1) with his grandmother [[sarah-ward-1806|Sarah Ward]].
