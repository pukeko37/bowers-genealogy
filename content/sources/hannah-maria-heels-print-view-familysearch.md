## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1814, Lockinge, Berkshire
- **Died:** 1886, Luton, Bedfordshire
- **Alternate name:** "Also Known As Batchelor" — confirms marriage to Abraham Batchelor
- **Residences:** 1841, 1851, 1861 Whipsnade; 1871, 1881 Studham; On The Green; By Whipsnade Green

## Notes

Hannah Maria's alternate name "Batchelor" confirms her as the wife of [[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham Batchelor]]. Her residence pattern is identical to Abraham's: Whipsnade 1841–1861, Studham 1871–1881. Born in Lockinge, Berkshire — a village 30+ miles from Whipsnade. How she came to Whipsnade is unknown, but the connection may run through the Heeles family: Samuel Heeles (Chequers tenant 1843) shares her surname and was in Whipsnade in the same period. If Samuel was her father or brother, she arrived in Whipsnade through his pub connection, and the licence passed to her husband Abraham.
