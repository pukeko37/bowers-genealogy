## Critical inference: Martha Sarah Bunker married Joseph Sinfield

The 1871 census shows Comfort Ann (as "Jane Bunker") living as **sister-in-law** to Joseph Sinfield. His wife is **Martha Sinfield, born Millbrook, Bedfordshire, age 30** — which maps exactly to **Martha Sarah Bunker** (born 1841, Millbrook). By 1881 and 1891 the same couple (Joseph Sinfield, now a Police Constable, and Martha, now aged 40–48) are in Luton with Comfort Ann still present as "sister."

This is almost certainly confirmation that **Martha Sarah Bunker (1841–1901) married Joseph Sinfield** and appears in census records from 1871 onwards as "Martha Sinfield." The Martha Sarah Bunker wiki page has no record of this marriage, and the existing sources did not reveal it. The Luton death in 1901 attributed to "Martha Sarah Bunker" would in fact be a death registered as Sinfield.

The Sinfield surname connection also raises an interesting genealogical echo: Thomas Bunker (1738) married **Martha Sinfield** in 1761 in Millbrook. Now, a Bunker great-granddaughter (Martha Sarah) marries a **Sinfield** in Bedford ~1870. Whether Joseph Sinfield was descended from the Millbrook Sinfields of 1761 is unconfirmed, but geographically plausible.
