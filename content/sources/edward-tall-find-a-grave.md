Find a Grave memorial ID 245670355. Source: Foster Hill Road Cemetery burial register and Howard Chapel register, Bedford. Contributed by Annibee.

**Edward Tall** died aged 63, buried **19 July 1870**, Foster Hill Road Cemetery, Bedford.

Address at death: **Albert Street, St Peter's, Bedford**.

Howard Chapel congregation member. The Howard Chapel burial ground was closed in 1855; after that date, Howard Chapel deaths were interred at Foster Hill Road Cemetery while still being recorded in the chapel's register. The existing wiki entry stating Edward was "buried at Howard Chapel" is therefore imprecise — he was buried at Foster Hill Road, registered at Howard Chapel.

No stone on this grave.

**Sarah Ward's death** confirmed: *"His widow Sarah died 6 January 1894 at Maulden."* Announced in the Bedford Mercury on Saturday 13 January 1894. This matches the existing sarah-ward-1806 page exactly.

Birth date note: the memorial gives ~1807 (calculated from age at death in burial register). FamilySearch has 9 November 1806. The difference is within normal margin for burial register age-at-death calculations.
