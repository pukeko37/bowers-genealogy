## Summary

Probate index entry for Beulah Rebecca Lucy Thody (née Bunker), from the England and Wales National Index of Wills and Administrations, 1858–1957. Screenshot of the printed index page.

## Transcription

> THODY Beulah Rebecca Lucy of 9 Oakley-road Luton Bedfordshire (wife of Harry Thody) died 29 June 1948 Probate London 26 August to John Quint Clayton solicitor and Joseph Taylor chartered accountant. Effects £1920 0s. 4d.

## Key Facts

- **Full name and address:** Beulah Rebecca Lucy Thody, of 9 Oakley Road, Luton, Bedfordshire
- **Relationship noted in index:** wife of Harry Thody
- **Death date:** 29 June 1948 (confirms existing)
- **Probate granted:** 26 August 1948 — 58 days after death
- **Probate place:** London
- **Executors:** John Quint Clayton, solicitor; Joseph Taylor, chartered accountant
- **Estate value:** £1,920 0s. 4d.

## Significance

**9 Oakley Road confirmed.** The address matches the house named "Carnoy" in the 1939 National Register and Beulah's memorial card — she died in the home she had shared with Harry throughout their marriage.

**Executed by professionals, not Harry.** The probate was handled by a solicitor (John Quint Clayton) and a chartered accountant (Joseph Taylor) — not by Harry himself. This is unusual for a spouse's estate and suggests Beulah had assets structured in a way that required professional management, possibly connected to the Thody Bros business. The same two names — Clayton and Taylor — appear in the FamilySearch sources record for Beulah, confirming the match.

**Estate of £1,920.** A respectable sum in 1948, well above the contemporary median estate. Harry continued running Thody Bros after her death; the estate may have included a share in the business or other investments.

**She left a will** — the entry is "Probate" not "Administration."
