Eleven clippings from bunkersmillbrook.wordpress.com, a genealogy blog maintained by a descendant of Caleb Bunker (son of James 1807) who has researched all Bunker families resident in Millbrook. Posts dated 2013. This is a secondary source — family genealogy research, not primary records — and should be treated accordingly: useful for confirmation, collateral context, and research leads, but not for asserting new facts without primary-source corroboration.

## Key findings

**Millbrook Station (1846):** A railway station was opened at Millbrook in 1846. Initially called Marston Station, it became Ampthill Station c.1850, then Millbrook Station in 1877. The station predates James Bunker's death by six years and was operational during the period when the older children began leaving for Bedford — relevant context for the migration.

**Pre-1858 Methodist community:** The Methodist chapel article states explicitly: "There had been a group of Methodists meeting in Millbrook since at least the mid 18th century." The 1858 chapel formalised a long-standing informal community. The mid-18th century dates to James Thomas Bunker's birth (1764) — he was born into a village where Methodism was already active.

**"10 children" claim:** The blog states James (1807) and Elizabeth Watts "had 10 children in Millbrook." Our wiki records 9. The FamilySearch sources page for James Bunker separately notes an "Annie Bunker" not on our list. This may represent a 10th child who died young. Flagged as open question on [[james-bunker-1807]].

**Migration to Bedford began in 1840s:** The blog states "the oldest children moved in the 1840s" — before James died in 1852. John's absence from the 1851 census already implied this; the blog confirms it was a process underway before James's death.

**The collateral Bunker lineage in Millbrook:** The blog documents a second, entirely separate Bunker line descended from Edward Bunker (1742, son of Thomas 1704) that persisted in Millbrook from the 1840s through to 1911. This line is collateral to our research (no overlap with Caleb or Frederick Bunker's line) but contextualises the Millbrook Bunker community.
