## Notes

Mary Eusden was christened at Trinity Church, Ely — some distance from the Fenstanton area where she later lived and died. Her father Thomas Euseden (1764–1842) is documented as a separate FamilySearch record. Mary died around 1841, when her eldest daughter Ann was twelve. Ann Norman's choice to name her son "Alfred **Eusden** Norman" in 1865 is a direct memorial to her mother's maiden name, kept alive 24 years after Mary's death. The Eusden/Ewsden spelling variation is consistent with phonetic transcription.
