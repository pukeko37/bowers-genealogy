## Key Facts

- **Birth registration:** 1896, Kettering Registration District — indexed as "Buelah Rebecca L Bunker"
- **Born:** 2 August 1896, Kettering, Northamptonshire (confirmed by 1939 National Register entry)

### 1901 Census — Ilford, Essex
Household: Fred W.S. Bunker (31, bricklayer, born Amphill); Eliza Bunker (39, born Haynes); children: Beulak L Bunker (4, born Kettering, indexed as "Beulak"), Roland H. Bunker (3, born Kettering). The family had moved from Northamptonshire to Ilford, Essex by this date.

### 1911 Census — Luton, Bedfordshire
Household: Frederick Wen Bunker (41, bricklayer, Ampthill) head; Eliza Bunker (49, Haynes, wife); Beulah Rebecca Ivy Bunker (14, born Kettering, apprentice dressmaking); Roland Herbert Bunker (13, born Kettering). Note: source records middle name as "Ivy"; the attached reason states it should be "Lucy." Dora Emily Bunker (20, born Bucks Wavingdon) also on the record but listed separately — probably a lodger or visitor, not a sibling.

### Marriage Certificate — 2 August 1920
Certified copy from General Register Office. No. 253. Parish Church, Little Gaddesden, Hertfordshire. By licence.

- **Harry Thody:** age 25, Bachelor, Upholsterer, residing Little Gaddesden; father William Allan Thody, Deceased
- **Beulah Rebecca Lucy Bunker:** age 24, Spinster, residing 50 Hampton Road, Luton; father Frederick William Tall Bunker, Bricklayer

Married by Rector Thomas Syondze. Witnesses: **R. H. Bunker** ([[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland Bunker]], Beulah's brother) and **N. W. Norman** ([[norah-winifred-norman-1902|Norah Winifred Norman]], Ivy Norman's youngest sister, aged 18).

The two witnesses together represent the two families about to be joined: Roland stands for the Bunkers, Norah for the Normans. [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy]] and Norah were already close to Harry before Roland and Ivy married in June 1921.

Note: 2 August was Beulah's 24th birthday. She married on her birthday.

### 1939 National Register — 9 Oakley Road, Luton
Beulah R L Thody: born 2 Aug 1896; married; occupation "Unpaid Domestic Duties." Household also includes Harry (transcribed as "Marcy Thody" — indexing error), Eliza Bunker (aged 80, Beulah's mother), and three redacted "UNKNOWN" entries (the living children).

### Death — 29 June 1948
**Death registration:** 1948, Luton, Bedfordshire. Age 51.

**Memorial card:** *"In Loving Memory of Beulah Thody whose spirit departed most peacefully on 29th June, 1948 Aged 51 Years Laid to rest in Luton General Cemetery on Saturday, 3rd July, 1948. 'Carnoy,' 9 Oakley Road, Luton."*

The house was named **Carnoy** — almost certainly after Carnoy, a village on the Somme where Harry served during WWI.

### Probate — 26 August 1948
Beulah Rebecca Lucy Thody; died 29 June 1948; probate granted London. Also on record: John Quint Clayton, Harry Thody, Joseph Taylor.
