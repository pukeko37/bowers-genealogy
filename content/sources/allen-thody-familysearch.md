## Key Facts Established

**Birth:** 5 October 1813, Spain (from Louis Albert Thody's family "black book" — a private manuscript history compiled by Allen's grandson). No original document has been found.

**Marriage:** 19 October 1834, St Mary Cardington, Bedfordshire. Transcribed verbatim from the parish register: "Allen Thody of this Parish and Elizabeth Saunders of this Parish were married in this Church by Banns… Allen Thody x his mark, Elizabeth Saunders X her mark… In the Presence of John Arnold X his mark, Elizabeth Arnold X her mark." Both parties illiterate; witnesses also signing by mark.

**Census run 1841–1891** (Cardington / Eastcotts / Cotton End, Bedfordshire throughout):
- 1841: Cardington; age 25; birthplace "Foreign"
- 1851: Eastcotts; age 37; Farm Labourer; birthplace "Spain British Subject"; household includes wife Elizabeth (39) and five children
- 1861: Eastcotts; age 48; Agricultural Labourer; household: wife Elizabeth (49), son George (16)
- 1871: Cotton End, Eastcotts; age 57; Agricultural Labourer; household: wife Elizabeth (59)
- 1881: Eastcotts; age 67; Agr Laborer; birthplace recorded as "St Madeira, Portugal" — clearly an enumerator error, contradicted by all other sources; household: wife Elizth (69)
- 1891: Eastcotts; age 77; Agricultural Labourer; birthplace "Spain British Subject"; household: wife Elizabeth (79), daughter Elizabeth Rogers (43), daughter Ann Thody (56), granddaughter May Rogers (3)

**Son's marriage (1894):** Allen Thody listed as father on William Allen Thody's marriage certificate, 6 February 1894, Whipsnade; William's age 43 (birth year c. 1851).

**Death:** 22 March 1898, Cotton End, Eastcotts; "Allen Thody, Male, 84 years, Farm Labourer, cause of death Decay Certified by A. F. Goldsmith M.R.C.S."; informant: Ann Thody, daughter, present at the death, Cotton End. Registered 24 March 1898.

## Notes

The 1881 birthplace "St Madeira, Portugal" is an outlier and is likely a mishearing — all other sources consistently record Spain. The age 84 at death aligns with an 1813/14 birth year.
