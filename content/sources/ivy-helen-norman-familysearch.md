## Biographical Narrative (from FamilySearch)

Rich narrative covering Ivy's entire life. Key threads:

- Grew up in Hosier Lane near Smithfield Market, City of London. Father Charles was a foreman at the Carter Paterson stables.
- Father died of tongue cancer aged 48 in 1907, when Ivy was 7. Family supported by older children and extended family.
- The Mason family (neighbours in Hosier Lane) married into the Norman family — John Mason married sister Mabel, Isaac Mason married sister Alice. They ran shops in Brixton and Clapham.
- After Charles's death, the family moved to the Kingsland/Hackney area. The 1911 census entry (RG14/477, schedule 236) shows 10 Normans plus one visitor (Mary Elizabeth Angus). The three Mason brothers were enumerated at Lambeth, Norwood. See [[norman-family-1911-census]].
- Ivy and sister Norah visited Studham with Ellen's step-sister Lucy Thody at Rose Cottage.
- Ivy was an excellent mental arithmetician. Worked as a teller at Westminster Bank from about age 14 through WW1 until marriage in 1921.
- Met husband Roland through Lucy Thody's son Harry. Harry married Ron's sister Beulah in 1920.
- Married Ron July 1921, stayed at Rose Cottage Studham, then Portsmouth (Ron in Royal Navy).
- Sons born in Portsmouth: Norman (1926), Alan (1928).
- Ron discharged from Navy 1930, went into furniture business with Harry Thody in Luton. Family moved to Harpenden, Park Hill.
- Ivy managed the business books. During WW2, Ron worked at de Havilland in Hatfield, Ivy ran the furniture shop.
- Sister Lillian lived at Park Hill during the war. Later, Mabel, John Mason and Nellie also moved in after their Brixton shop was damaged by a V1 bomb.
- Ron and Ivy moved to Auckland, New Zealand in 1970 to join son Alan, his wife Jacqui, and their children.
- Ivy died in Auckland in 1994.
