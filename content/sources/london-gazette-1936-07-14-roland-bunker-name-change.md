Notice published in *The London Gazette*, Issue 34304, page 4550, dated 14 July 1936. The notice spans two screenshots (also: `Screenshot 2026-05-07 at 22-30-13 Page 4550 Issue 34304 14 July 1936 London Gazette The Gazette.png`).

**Full text (reconstructed from two-part screenshot):**

> I, ROLAND HERBERT BOWERS, of 32, Park Hill, Harpenden, in the county of Hertford, House Furnisher, a born British subject, heretofore called Roland Herbert Bunker, hereby give notice that I have renounced and abandoned the name of Roland Herbert Bunker, and that I have assumed and taken and henceforth on all occasions, shall use and at all times to sign and use and to be called and known by the name of Roland Herbert Bowers in lieu of and in substitution for my former name Roland Herbert Bunker; and I also hereby give notice that such change of name is formally declared and evidenced by a deed poll under my hand and seal dated the 8th day of July, one thousand nine hundred and thirty-six, duly executed and attested, and that such deed poll was enrolled in the Central Office of the Supreme Court of Judicature on the 9th day of July, one thousand nine hundred and thirty-six.—Dated this 10th day of July, one thousand nine hundred and thirty-six.
>
> ROLAND HERBERT BOWERS, formerly (096) Roland Herbert Bunker.
