Entry 693/A from the *National Roll of the Great War 1914–1918*, accessed via a FamilySearch-linked source clipping.

**Full text:**

> THODY, H., Private, Royal Army Service Corps. He volunteered in September 1914, and in the following year was drafted to France, where he served on the Somme, at Arras, Passchendaele, Lens and Cambrai, engaged in transporting supplies to the front lines, and was wounded. He also operated through the Advance into Germany, and was demobilised in February 1919. He holds the 1914–15 Star, and the General Service and Victory Medals. Studham, near Dunstable. 693/A.

- Home address at time of entry: Studham, near Dunstable

**Notes:**

The National Roll records only his RASC service. Other sources (FamilySearch) record an earlier affiliation with the Bedfordshire Regiment (No. 19831) before transfer to RASC. The National Roll records the principal or final unit, so there is no contradiction.

The Somme service is consistent with the house name "Carnoy" (9 Oakley Road, Luton, recorded in the 1939 Register) — Carnoy is a village on the Somme.

This is the primary documentary source for Harry's WWI service record, underlying the FamilySearch narrative entries.
