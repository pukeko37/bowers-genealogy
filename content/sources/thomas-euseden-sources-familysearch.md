The FamilySearch citation reads: *"Entry for Richard Tabram and Thomas Euseden, 1841."* Thomas was in his son-in-law Richard Tabraham's household at Bell Lane.

The census age of 70 implies birth ~1767–1771, but the 1841 census commonly rounded ages down to the nearest five years. The burial record (below) gives age 78 in November 1842, implying birth ~1764 — consistent with the FamilySearch birth year and more reliable.

## Significance

Confirms that Thomas Euseden lived with Richard Tabraham at Bell Lane, Fenstanton in 1841 — the same household as his grandchildren Ann, Susan, John, and infant George Richard Tabram. Mary (Thomas's daughter and Richard's wife) had died around this same time, aged ~34. Thomas himself followed seventeen months later, in November 1842.
