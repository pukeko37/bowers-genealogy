## Notes

Isaac Mason (known as "Ike") was the youngest of the three orphaned Mason brothers, sons of [[mason-james-1874|Richard Mason and Catherine James]]. Born in Clerkenwell, he was living at St Sepulchre Newgate in 1891 — the street bordering Smithfield, adjacent to Hosier Lane where the Norman family lived. This proximity is the most likely explanation for the Mason-Norman acquaintance that led to two marriages.

By 1921, Isaac was established as a newsagent at 4 Yukon Road, Balham — the household that also included his wife [[alice-norman-1890|Alice Norman]], his wife's aunt [[elizabeth-birchmore-1862|Elizabeth Birchmore]], and which Nellie Norman visited to help in the shop. He died in 1955, one year after Elizabeth Birchmore. Alice was widowed at 65 and eventually moved to Kent.
