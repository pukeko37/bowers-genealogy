National Probate Calendar entry for Adeliza Tabram, clipped 2026-05-14.

**Entry text:** "TABRAM Adeliza of Fenstanton St Ives Huntingdonshire (wife of John Tabram) died 8 February 1938 at 1 Southoe Buckden Huntingdonshire Probate London 4 April to Ethel Lomas married woman. Effects £609 15s. 8d."

**Deceased:** Adeliza Tabram (née Fuller), wife of [[john-tabram-1861|John Tabram (1861–1939)]].

**Death:** 8 February 1938 at 1 Southoe, Buckden, Huntingdonshire. She died a year before her husband John, who died at the same address on 7 February 1939.

**Probate:** London, 4 April 1938, to **Ethel Lomas, married woman** — their daughter Ethel Fuller Tabram (1883–), who married a Lomas. She administered both parents' estates: her mother's in April 1938, her father's in March 1939.

**Effects:** £609 15s. 8d. — slightly more than her husband's estate of £506 12s. 4d. at his death the following year.

**Address note:** The probate index describes Adeliza as "of Fenstanton St Ives" — reflecting her long-term parish — even though she died at Buckden. This suggests the family moved from Fenstanton to Buckden (1 Southoe) at some point between the 1911 census and her death in 1938, possibly in old age to be near daughter Ethel.
