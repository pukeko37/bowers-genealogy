Geni.com profile for William Abel Matthews Sr. (1848–1917).

## Vital Data

- **Full name:** William Abel Matthews Sr.
- **Born:** 18 March 1848, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire, England
- **Died:** 16 August 1917 (aged 69), Virgin, Washington County, Utah
- **Buried:** Virgin, Washington County, Utah

## Family

- **Parents:** Abel Matthews and **Mary Ann Walker** (this is the fullest rendering of the mother's name seen so far — confirms "Ann" as a middle name, not a separate person)
- **Wife:** Rebecca Vince
- **Children:**
  - William Abel Matthews Jr. (born 1871, Lehi, Utah County, Utah)
  - Betsy Edith Matthews (born 1884)
  - Ralph Vince Matthews (born 1893)

## Significance

William is the youngest child of [[able-matthews-1813|Able Matthews]] and [[mary-walker-1817|Mary Walker]] confirmed in the 1851 Whipsnade census (aged 3). This record confirms that William emigrated with his father and settled in **Virgin, Washington County, Utah** — the same town as his uncle [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis Matthews]]. His first son (William Jr.) was born in Lehi in 1871, suggesting William initially settled in Lehi (where Able lived) before moving south to Virgin.

The mother's name "Mary Ann Walker" is the fullest form seen. Previous sources give "Mary Walker" or "Mary Mathews" (census surname after marriage).
