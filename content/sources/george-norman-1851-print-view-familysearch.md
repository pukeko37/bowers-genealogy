The London residences (Wandsworth 1881, Croydon 1891) and Stilton 1901 residence below do **not** belong to our George Norman. The corrected picture is in [[george-norman-1851-1903-print-view-familysearch]] and [[george-norman-1851-sources-familysearch]]. George Norman (1851–1903) lived in West Hartlepool from 1875, married there, had ten children there, and died there in September 1903. He never went to London and never returned to Huntingdonshire.
