## Notes

Richard Tabraham was born and died in Elsworth/Fenstanton, the core of his family's geography. He married [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary Eusden]] on 3 August 1828 in Fen Stanton. Mary died ~1841, leaving Richard a widower with four children including their eldest [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann]], aged twelve. The name "Tabram" is a common 19th-century variant — the family appears variously as Tabraham, Tabram, and Tabrahem in records.

The burial date anomaly (29 January before 26 February death) is almost certainly a transcription error. His residence at Bell Lane, Fenstanton is a specific street-level address.
