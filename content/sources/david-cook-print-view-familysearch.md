## Notes

David Cook was born in Kensworth — the same village as his sister [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] (b. 1820), who later married GBP Sr. He married [[charlotte-matthews-1823|Charlotte Matthews]], sister of [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] (GBP Sr's first wife). Both Cook and Matthews families were anchored to Whipsnade Green / Houchen End — the same hamlet recorded for Thomas Matthews, Mary Wilson, and GBP Sr himself.

David is listed in Ruth Cooke's FamilySearch record as one of her siblings (children of Daniel Cook, 1790–1857, and Mary Birchmore, 1785–?).
