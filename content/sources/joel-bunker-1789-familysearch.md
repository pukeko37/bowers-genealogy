- **Second marriage:** Elizabeth Inskipp (1792–Deceased), 16 April 1839, Flitton with Silsoe, Bedfordshire

## On the birth date discrepancy

The christening at Millbrook on 26 April 1789 conflicts with the FamilySearch estimate of 1782–1786 derived from census records. A delayed christening — registering a child with the Church of England years after birth — is consistent with the nonconformist leanings evident in this family's naming pattern (Jesse, Joel, Thomas Jehu, Jonas). The Millbrook page notes a "1806 mass Church of England christening of seven Bunker children in a single day" in the James Thomas Bunker line — the same practice. Joel may have been born c.1782–1786 and christened at Millbrook in 1789 at the age of 3–7.

If Joel was born c.1782, Job Bunker (listed as 1804) was born when Joel was ~22, before the 1809 marriage — making Job likely a pre-marital child rather than a dating error.

## Earlier FamilySearch errors (now corrected)

The first clipping ("Joel Bunker 1789–Deceased") showed "Martha Bunker (1756)" — Joel's own mother — listed as a spouse, and an "[Unknown Name]" artefact. Both were cleaned up before the second clipping was taken. The second clipping added the death date (1861 Ampthill). The third added the birth range, second marriage, residences, and occupation.

## Significance

Joel is the first of Joseph (1754) and Martha Garratt's nine adult children traced into the next generation. His son **Joseph Bunker (1810)** is old enough to be the father of the **Joseph Bunker (~1837)** in the Millbrook Victorian census records — which would confirm the cordwainer line's persistence in the village.
