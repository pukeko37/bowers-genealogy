FamilySearch sources page for Ann Geary (1851–1922), aggregating 19 attached sources including her christening, census returns 1861–1911, marriage registration, and children's christening records.

**Key facts confirmed:**

- Christened 7 Dec 1851, Haynes, Bedfordshire. Parents: John Geary and Lucy.
- 1861 census: Scholar, age 9, Church End, Haynes. In John and Lucy Geary household.
- 1871 census: Platter, age 19, at parental home, Haynes.
- Had an illegitimate son **Francis Ernest Geary** (born ~1870, no father named), christened 11 May 1873 at Haynes alongside his aunt Lydia Rose Geary — both receiving late christenings on the same day. He was raised in John and Lucy's household: the 1871 census records him as "Ernest Geary" age 1, and the 1881 census as "Ernest Geary" age 11, in the parental home. He does not appear in Ann's Whitmore household and disappears from records after 1881.
- Marriage registration 1873 at Bedford — possibly a misattached record; Ann's 1911 census states married 35 years (→ ~1876 marriage to William Whitmore).
- Married **William Whitmore** (born Bolnhurst, Bedfordshire; occupation corn dealer labourer / gardener).
- Children's christenings (all at St Paul, Bedford): Alice Maud Jane (Feb 1882), Florence Evelyn (Jun 1883), Sarah Jane (Dec 1886), Percy Edwin (Mar 1889), Edith May (Jan 1892).
- 1881 census: Ann Whitmore, age ~27, Haynes, wife, Bedford Western Ward.
- 1891 census: Ann Whitmore, age 38, Charwoman, Bedford Western Ward. Household: husband William (Corn Dealer Laborer), Phoebe (16), Ethel (12), Florence (8), Jane (4), Percy (2).
- 1901 census: Annie Whitmore, age 45, Bedford Western Ward St Paul. Household: husband, Phoebe (25), Jane (14), Percy (12), Edith (9).
- 1911 census: Ann Whitmore, age 55, Bedford, married 35 years. Household: husband William (55), Phoebe (35), Percy (22), Edith (19), Jeanie (24).
- Died approximately 1922 (no death record found in sources).
