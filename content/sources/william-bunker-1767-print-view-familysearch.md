## Notes

**Sarah Bunker (1807):** FamilySearch attributes her to the Sarah Beal marriage, but the Beal marriage did not take place until 1808. She was christened 28 June 1807 — the same year Ann Denton died. She is almost certainly Ann Denton's last child, possibly born at the cost of her mother's life. The FamilySearch attribution appears to be an error.

**Further children:** The sources page records events at Millbrook in October 1819 and October 1821 not accounted for by the four named children above. The October 1819 event is likely **Samuel Bunker** (born ~1820), who appears in the 1841 census aged ~20 in William's household. See [[william-bunker-1767-sources-familysearch]].
