FamilySearch print view for Sarah Ward, Frederick's maternal grandmother who raised him.

**Vitals:** Born 27 March 1806, Flitwick, Bedfordshire (christened 12 April 1807, Flitwick). Died 6 January 1894, Maulden, Bedfordshire.

**Residences:** 1851 Maulden · 1861 Bedfordshire · 1871 Maulden · 1881 Maulden · 1891 Maulden · Derby Street, Bedford (address also shared with [[edward-tall-1806|Edward Tall]]).

Sarah lived her entire life in and around Maulden, Bedfordshire. She died there in 1894, aged 87. Frederick (age 1 in 1870) was entrusted to her care after both his mother Rebecca and grandfather Edward died in July 1870. The 1871 census likely shows Frederick with Sarah in Maulden. By 1881 Frederick had moved to Matlock with his father Caleb.

Note: The existing narrative describes Sarah as "née Tall" — if correct, this would make her a Tall by birth who married a Ward, and Edward Tall a relative (possibly brother). The clipping does not confirm her maiden name. The shared address and Maulden residences with Edward Tall suggest they were more likely husband and wife (Sarah Ward married to Edward Tall), with Rebecca taking her father's surname Tall.
