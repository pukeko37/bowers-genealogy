FamilySearch aggregated sources page for William Thody (LBJC-CG6), 18 sources total. This is the most significant new document in this ingest — it confirms Allen Thody's father and resolves the long-standing open question about Allen's Spanish birthplace.

## Key Facts Established

**Birth:** About 1774, Cardington, Bedfordshire. Source: *The Inhabitants of Cardington in 1782* (Bedfordshire Historical Record Society, 1973), p. 183 — "C36.5 3d William [Thody], at School by Mrs. Howard. [age on 1 January 1782] 7 [years]." Text notes ages as of 1 January 1782; a birth year of 1774 follows. He is identified as the son of **Samuel Thody** and **Elizabeth Pearsey**.

**Military service:** 40th Regiment of Foot (1st Battalion). Married Alice Gardner at St Clement's, Hastings, 27 January 1804 (both signing by mark); she is described as "Spinster of the same parish," William as "Bachelor of this Parish." A daughter **Elizabeth Thody** was baptised at St Clement's Hastings, 19 October 1804 (father William, mother Alice). Alice presumably died between 1804 and 1809.

**Second marriage:** Elizabeth (née unknown; formerly wife of James Meyers), at **Badajoz, Spain**, c. 1809 — confirmed by a 5 December 1814 legal examination at Cardington. Elizabeth Thoady, wife of William Thoady (described as "now a Private Soldier in the 40th Regiment of Foot, serving in America"), petitioned for parish support for her son William Myers (aged 5 years, 2 months), born to her and her first husband James Meyers "at Elves [Elvas] in the Kingdom of Portugal." She states she "married her present husband about five years ago at Badajoz in Spain." This marriage c. 1809 places Allen Thody's 1813 birth squarely within it.

**Battle of Waterloo, 1815:** William received the Waterloo Medal, awarded to all ranks present at Ligny, Quatre Bras, and Waterloo (16–18 June 1815). He served in Captain J. H. Barnett's Company, 1st Battalion, 40th Regiment of Foot. His discharge papers state he was "in the action at Waterloo." He was wounded and subsequently honourably discharged.

**Chelsea Pensioner:** Admitted to the Royal Hospital Chelsea, 19 September 1816 (WO 23/48). Discharge papers (WO 97, Box 566) describe him as: birthplace Carrington [sic] Bedford; rank Private; Regiment 40th; attestation age 49 [implying service since c. 1767, likely an error — probably c. 1795–1800].

**1841 census:** Turk's Row, St Luke Chelsea, Middlesex; age 65; in household with James Gibbs, Catherine Gibbs, and others — almost certainly Chelsea Pensioners' accommodation.

**Third marriage:** 24 February 1845, Holy Trinity Brompton, Middlesex. William Thoady, "of full age, Widower, no occupation," married **Elizabeth Dance**, "of full age, Widow." Both signed by mark. The annotating researcher notes that the wife's given name appears as "Elizabeth" on the certificate but she is consistently called **Jane** in all later records (1851 census; informant on his death certificate). The researcher believes this was likely a scribal error during the ceremony.

**1851 census:** St Luke Chelsea; age 77; Pensioner; Widower listed as head; wife Jane Thody (60, born Wiltshire). The "Widower" classification appears to be a census error, as Jane is listed with him. James Smith (80, born Wiltshire) also in household — possibly Jane's father.

**Death:** 7 August 1858, 15 Paradise Walk, Chelsea; recorded as "William Thoady, Male, 73 years of age, occupation Out Pensioner, Royal Hospital, cause of death Natural exhaustion certified." Informant: Jane Thoady (signed by mark), present at the death. Registered 9 August 1858. The age of 73 is noted as about 10–11 years too low; the 1851 census gives age 77, consistent with a birth year of c. 1774.

**Burial:** 13 August 1858, Victoria Park Cemetery, Hackney, Middlesex (abode given as 15 Paradise Walk, Chelsea).

## Notes on the Peninsular War context

The 40th Regiment of Foot served in Portugal and Spain throughout the Peninsular War (1808–1814). The reference to William "serving in America" in December 1814 is consistent with the regiment's subsequent deployment to North America in 1814 (the War of 1812), returning to England in 1815 in time for the Waterloo campaign. Allen Thody was born 5 October 1813 in Spain — during the final stages of the Peninsular War, almost certainly while his father was on campaign.
