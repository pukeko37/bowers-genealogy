## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1865, Swavesey, Cambridgeshire
- **Died:** 1896, Swavesey, Cambridgeshire (aged ~31)
- **Birth registration:** St Ives, Huntingdonshire
- **Residences:** Swavesey 1871, 1881, 1891

## Notes

Alfred Eusden Norman was born and died in Swavesey. His middle name "Eusden" is a deliberate memorial to his mother Ann Tabram's mother Mary Eusden (1807–1841), who died when Ann was twelve. He died in 1896, aged about 31, and never left Swavesey. He predeceased his father Thomas (died 1905) by nine years.
