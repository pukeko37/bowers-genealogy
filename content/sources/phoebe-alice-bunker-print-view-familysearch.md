Seventh child of James Bunker (1807) and Elizabeth Watts. Moved to Pilsley, Derbyshire — close to Tupton, where George Bunker had settled — then on to Nottinghamshire. Long-lived, dying in 1923 aged ~80. The Derbyshire migration of Phoebe and George was part of the same family movement that brought Caleb to Matlock. Caleb's elder sister.
