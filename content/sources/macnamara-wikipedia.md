Wikipedia article on Arthur Macnamara (1831–1906), supplemented by the Eaton Bray community forum (eatonbray.com) and Bedford Borough Council community history pages.

## Key Facts

- Arthur Macnamara (1831 – 11 Feb 1906); Squire of Billington, Bedfordshire
- Family holdings: Caddington Hall (Hertfordshire), Eaton Bray estate (Bedfordshire), Llangoed Hall (Llyswen, Breconshire, Wales)
- Married 28 Sep 1854: **Lady Sophia Hare**, daughter of the Earl of Listowel; ceremony at her family's County Cork estate
- 1880s: acquired land at Billington, built Victorian manor house with "AM" cypher on farm buildings; attempted to reconstruct Eaton Bray castle (abandoned)
- Died 11 Feb 1906, Billington, cirrhosis of the liver; found bankrupt posthumously
- Lady Sophia sold properties, moved to Heath and Reach, died 1912, buried in Ireland
- Estate (Pedley Settled Estates) wound up over nine years; sold at auction November 1915
- Annotated sale particulars at Bedfordshire Archives, ref Z214/3

## Additional context (Eaton Bray forum)

- Macnamara acquired Park Farm Estate (including Rye Farm, Eaton Bray) in 1877 from the Huxley family
- Rye Farm (104 acres) sold at 1915 auction to sitting tenant Jeffrey Sharratt for £2,000
- Estate described locally as going bankrupt due to Macnamara's "gracious living"
