FamilySearch sources page for Elizabeth Fear (GM8R-YTT). Eight sources attached.

**Marriage, August 1815** — Three records: Cambridge Parish Registers (13 Aug 1815, as "Elizh Fear or Fea?r"), Cambridge Parish Registers (20 Aug 1815, as "Elizabeth Fear"), and Cambridgeshire Bishop's Transcripts (20 Aug 1815, as "Elizabeth Fear"). All at Swavesey; she is listed as Single in the later records. Married Thomas Norman.

**Son's christening, 15 October 1815** — Two records show Elizabeth as mother of Thomas Norman christened at Willingham, 15 Oct 1815. The Cambridgeshire Bishop's Transcripts record gives her name as "Elizabeth Norman" (post-marriage surname). The Cambridge Parish Registers record also lists her as "Elizabeth Norman." The England, Births and Christenings record gives her simply as "Elizabeth."

**Death, 29 July 1832** — Two records: Cambridgeshire Bishop's Transcripts and Cambridge Parish Registers both give "Elizabeth Norman" buried Swavesey, 29 July 1832, age 37 (b. est. 1795). Note: the burial record uses her married name "Elizabeth Norman," consistent with her having been married to Thomas Norman since 1815.
