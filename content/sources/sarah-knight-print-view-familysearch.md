FamilySearch full print view for Sarah Knight (FamilySearch ID: LC5J-27D) — Samuel Capel's wife; Caleb Bunker's great-great-grandmother on the Watts maternal line.

**Vitals:**
- Born: 10 July 1755 (christened 13 July 1755), Cranfield, Bedfordshire
- Died: about 1839, Millbrook, Bedfordshire

**Marriage:** Samuel Capel (1750–1808), 15 November 1772, Cranfield.

**Parents:** William Knight (1729–Deceased) and Mary Gurney (1733–Deceased), married 21 September 1754, Cranfield, Bedfordshire. Siblings: Hannah Knight (1758–?), William Knight (1761–1813), Rachel Knight (1764–1766), Thomas Knight (1770–1800).

**Significance:** Sarah was born in Cranfield and died in Millbrook — both Bedfordshire villages in the same cluster as James Bunker's Millbrook family. Her marriage in 1772 pre-dates the Bunker family's arrival in Millbrook by over fifty years, establishing the Capel/Knight family as long-established local inhabitants before Elizabeth Watts married into the Bunkers.

**Note:** Sarah's son John Knight Keppel (1789) carries both the Knight and Keppel/Capel surnames — the same convention of embedding mother's maiden name seen elsewhere in the research (cf. Herbert Thomas **Barnsley** Bunker, Alfred **Eusden** Norman).
