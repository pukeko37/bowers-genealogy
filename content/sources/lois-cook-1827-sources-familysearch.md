## Note on Name Confusion

This Lois Cook (1827–1903, married Harrowell) must not be confused with [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois Brandsom]] (1843–1924, married Charles Cook). The Harrowell Lois is the youngest sibling of Ruth Cooke and David Cook. The Brandsom Lois is entirely unrelated — she is of the Bransom/Brandsom family of Edlesborough.
