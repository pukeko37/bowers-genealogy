## Key Facts

- **Full name and address:** Harry Thody, of 15 Bedford Avenue, Silsoe, Bedfordshire
- **Death date:** 29 September 1986 (confirms existing)
- **Probate granted:** 25 February 1987 — nearly five months after death
- **Probate place:** London
- **Estate value:** £449 (the "B" suffix is a registry reference, not currency)
- **Registry reference:** 8780001185C

## Significance

**Address at death: 15 Bedford Avenue, Silsoe.** Harry's precise address in Silsoe had not previously been recorded in the wiki. He moved to Silsoe at some point after Beulah's death and Thody Bros winding up (1970); the probate record confirms he was there at his death in 1986.

**Modest estate of £449.** By 1986 standards this is a very small estate, suggesting Harry's assets had been largely dispersed or spent in his final years. He was 92 at death.

**He left a will** — the entry is "Probate" not "Administration." The will's contents (executor, beneficiaries) are not given in the index entry.
