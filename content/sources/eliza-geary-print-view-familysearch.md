FamilySearch person GW1V-RQH. Eliza Geary, born 25 July 1859, Haynes, Bedfordshire; christened 27 November 1859 Haynes; birth registration 1859 Ampthill. Died 19 January 1942, Luton, Bedfordshire.

**Residence history:** 1861 Haynes · 1871 Haynes · 1881 Luton · 1891 Maulden · 1901 Ilford, Essex · 1911 Luton · 1939 9 Oakley Road, Luton (with Harry Thody and Beulah).

Key finding: **1891 residence is Maulden** — the same village where Frederick Bunker was raised by Sarah Ward. This is almost certainly where Frederick and Eliza met, one year before their 1892 Ampthill marriage.

**1881 Luton residence** predates the marriage by 11 years; Eliza was 22 and may have been working there.

Marriage: Frederick William Tall Bunker, 1892, Ampthill, Bedfordshire.

Children: Beulah Rebecca Lucy Bunker (1896–1948) and Roland Herbert Bunker (1898–1978).

Parents: John Geary (1825–1895) and Lucy Morris (1826–1895). Siblings listed: Eliza (1849–1856, died young — our Eliza was named for her), Ann (1851–1922), Phoebe (1854–1910), Emma (1856–1874), Elizabeth (1858–1936), John (1862–?), Ellen (1864–1865), Albert (1866–1942), Lydia Rose (1868–1932), Frederick Louis (1871–1872).
