## Name History

Born illegitimately as George Birchmore Plummer, given mother's surname Plummer. Registered as George Birchmore in 1841 census. Around 1860–61, after marriage to Eliza and birth of daughter Ellen (christened Ellen Plummer) and death of his father, begins using George Birchmore Plummer. In his 1859 marriage record, registered as "George Plummer Birchmore" — the surnames were used interchangeably for a period. His son from his first marriage continued using the original name.
