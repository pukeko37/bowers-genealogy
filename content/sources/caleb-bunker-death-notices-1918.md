FindMyPast newspaper search results showing six items relating to Caleb Bunker's death in May 1918. Image quality is poor; OCR is inconsistent, but the key facts are cross-confirmed by multiple independent notices.

## Death date and place confirmed: 22 May 1918, Lullington

Two notices in the *Derbyshire Advertiser and Journal* (published 31 May and 1 June 1918) give the clearest readings:

- **31 May 1918:** *"May 22, at Lutlington [Lullington], Caleb Bunker late of Osmaston-road, Derby."*
- **1 June 1918:** *"Bunker — Hay [May] 22, Lullington, Caleb Bunker, late of Osmaston-road, Derby."* (OCR renders 'M' as 'H' throughout; "Hay 22" = "May 22".)

A notice in the *Derby and Chesterfield Reporter* (24 May 1918) reads: *"Passed away suddenly, May 22nd, & Caleb Bunker, late of Oas[maston Road] Derby."*

**Death date: 22 May 1918. Death place: Lullington, Derbyshire** (registered in the Burton upon Trent, Staffordshire GRO district, which covers Lullington parish). The death was sudden — all notices use "passed away suddenly."

## Home address confirmed: Osmaston Road, Derby

All legible notices describe him as "late of Osmaston-road, Derby." This supersedes the **60 High Street, Derby** address from the 1911 census; Caleb had moved to Osmaston Road at some point between 1911 and his death in 1918.

## Obituaries

- **23 May 1918** — *Derby Daily Telegraph*: first notice; partially legible, mentions "Lullington" and "passed away suddenly."
- **24 May 1918** — *Derby and Chesterfield Reporter* (two separate items, one gives "May 22nd" clearly).
- **24 May 1918** — *Derby Daily Telegraph*: mentions "Saturday, May 25" — this is the burial date (25 May 1918 was a Saturday), consistent with the Find a Grave record.
- **31 May 1918** and **1 June 1918** — *Derbyshire Advertiser and Journal*: clearest OCR; give "May 22" and "Osmaston-road, Derby" legibly.

## Burial

**25 May 1918**, Nottingham Road Cemetery, Chaddesden, Derby — confirmed by Find a Grave (already in [[caleb-bunker-1845-sources-familysearch]]) and consistent with the 24 May *Derby Daily Telegraph* reference to "Saturday, May 25."

## Notes on image quality

The image is a FindMyPast search results page showing article snippets, not full-page scans. The OCR is poor throughout; dates and names were identified by cross-referencing multiple notices rather than relying on any single reading. The 24 May *Derby and Chesterfield Reporter* item giving "May 20" in one snippet and "May 22nd" in another likely reflects OCR variation on the same text; "May 22nd" appears in the clearer of the two and is confirmed by the June notices.
