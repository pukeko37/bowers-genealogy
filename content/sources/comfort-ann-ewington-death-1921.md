FreeBMD transcription of the GRO death registration index entry.

**Entry:** Ewington, Comforrt A. [transcription error: "Comforrt" for "Comfort"], age 72, Luton, Deaths Mar 1921, GRO vol 3b, page 434.

Confirms Comfort Ann Ewington (née Bunker) died in the **March quarter 1921** in **Luton**, aged **72**. Age 72 at death is consistent with her christening of November 1848 — she would have turned 72 in November 1920, three to four months before her death.

She died under her married name Ewington, in the same town where she had lived since at least 1881 and where her sister [[martha-sarah-bunker-1841|Martha Sarah]] had died twenty years earlier.
