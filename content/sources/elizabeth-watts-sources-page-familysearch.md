## Key new findings

**James Bunker was an Agricultural Labourer.** The 1851 census is the first and only surviving document giving his occupation. He farmed in Millbrook until his death in 1852, while Elizabeth worked as a lace maker — a pattern common in Bedfordshire, where lace-making provided a domestic income to supplement a labourer's wages.

**Lydia Brown's final home.** The 1841 census places her in James and Elizabeth's Millbrook household, aged 70, just over a year before her death on 6 September 1842. She was part of the same household as her grandchildren John, Lydia, George, David, Charles, and infant Martha.
