## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1868, Swavesey, Cambridgeshire
- **Died:** 1954, Cambridgeshire
- **Birth registration:** St Ives, Swavesey
- **Residences:** Swavesey 1871, 1881, 1891, 1901, 1911

## Notes

Robert Henry Norman was the youngest of Thomas and Ann's children. He remained in Swavesey his entire life, through all census years from 1871 to 1911. He lived to 1954 — long enough that he and his nephew [[george-stewart-main-1919|George Main]] (born 1919) were alive at the same time for 35 years. He is the longest-lived of Charles's siblings.
