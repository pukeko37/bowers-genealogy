FamilySearch person M9S7-BXG. Print view and 8-source sources page.

**Vitals:** Born 28 May 1790, Bedfordshire. Christened 7 April 1806, Millbrook (mass christening). Died 31 May 1848, Millbrook, Bedfordshire. GRO death registration: vol 6, page 45, Bedford district.

**Residence:** 1841 census at West End, Stevington, Bedfordshire.

**Marriage:** Catherine Bull, 19 May 1815, Stevington, Bedfordshire.

**Children** (all christened Stevington 1824):
- Ebenezer Bunker (1820–Deceased)
- Ephraim Bunker (1821–1887) — died Crookwell, New South Wales, Australia (5 July 1887, aged 67); confirmed by "Australia Deaths and Burials, 1816-1980"
- Ruth Bunker (1822–Deceased)
- Lydia Bunker (1826–Deceased)

**Note:** The four children were all christened together at Stevington on 26 September 1824 — another mass christening, mirroring the 1806 Millbrook pattern. Confirms Nonconformist practice continued into the next generation.

**Australian connection:** Ephraim emigrated to Australia alongside his first cousin **Ebenezer Bunker** (1822–1902), son of Joseph (1792). They were grandsons of James Thomas Bunker who left Stevington together for New South Wales. Ephraim married in Bedfordshire before departure; Ebenezer married in Australia (to be confirmed). Ephraim's death record at Crookwell, NSW is attached here. Substantial research on the Australian families exists and may be ingested at a later stage.
