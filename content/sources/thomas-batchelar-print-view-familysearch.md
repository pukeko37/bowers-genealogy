## Key Facts

- **Born:** 9 November 1803, Dagnall, Buckinghamshire
- **Christened:** 22 June 1804, Ivinghoe, Buckinghamshire
- **Died:** 22 December 1872, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire
- **Probate:** 5 March 1873
- **Occupation:** Labourer
- **Residences:** 1841, 1851, 1861, 1871 Whipsnade; Ellesborough Green (Buckinghamshire)
- **Alternate name:** Thomas Batchelor

## Notes

Thomas Batchelar is almost certainly Eliza Batchelor's father: he shares her surname (variant spelling), lived in Whipsnade throughout, attended GBP Sr and Eliza's 1859 marriage, and GBP Sr was recorded living in Thomas's household in the 1861 census. He was born in Dagnall, Buckinghamshire — the same birthplace recorded in the earlier GBP Sr sources list clipping. Ellesborough Green is another Buckinghamshire village in the same area (Chilterns), suggesting Thomas maintained connections there.

The relationship to Abraham Batchelor (who held the Chequers licence before GBP Sr) is not yet confirmed but likely fraternal — both from the same Dagnall/Chilterns area.
