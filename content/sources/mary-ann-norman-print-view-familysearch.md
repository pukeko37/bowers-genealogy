## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1 November 1861, Swavesey, Cambridgeshire
- **Died:** 1951, Cambridgeshire
- **Birth registration:** St Ives, Swavesey
- **Residences:** Swavesey 1871, 1891, 1901, 1911; Chesterton, Cambridgeshire 1939
- **Marital status:** Widowed (by 1939)
- **Occupation:** Old Age Pensioner

## Notes

Mary Ann Norman was Charles Norman's older sister by two years. She lived in Swavesey through all census years 1871–1911, then moved to Chesterton (near Cambridge) by 1939, likely in widowhood. She died in 1951 aged ~89, outliving her brother Charles (died 1907) by 44 years. She is the only Norman sibling recorded as having been married (widowed by 1939).
