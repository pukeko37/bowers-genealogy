She was almost certainly employed there as a domestic servant. She was **not** in James Bunker (1777)'s 1841 household.

This directly contradicts the Millbrook page's attribution of "Rachel (~17)" to James (1777)'s household as his daughter by Catherine Blunt. No christening record for a Rachel as daughter of James (1777) has been found. The Rachel in James's household remains unidentified.

## Probable parentage

The most plausible candidate for "William Bunker + Sarah" in Millbrook 1824 is **[[william-bunker-1767|William Bunker (1767)]]** and his second wife **Sarah Beal** (married 1808). William was ~57 and Sarah ~48 at Rachael's birth — late but not impossible. If correct, Rachael would be a younger sibling of Samuel (~1820), who was in William's 1841 household while Rachael was in service at Village Great Farm.

This identification is inferred from circumstantial evidence; the christening record gives only "William Bunker" and "Sarah" without further details.
