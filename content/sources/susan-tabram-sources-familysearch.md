**Death:** 1911, registered in the **Chesterton registration district, Cambridgeshire** (vol. 3B, p. 465). Age given as 80, consistent with birth c. 1831. "Chesterton" is the registration district name — the actual place of death within the district is not specified by the index. Fenstanton, where Susan lived through 1901, falls within the St Ives registration district in Huntingdonshire; the Chesterton district in Cambridgeshire covered Cambridge and surrounding villages. Her death being registered there suggests she had moved out of Fenstanton at some point after 1901, possibly after John's death in 1909.
