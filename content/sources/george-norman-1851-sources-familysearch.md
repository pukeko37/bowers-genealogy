## Notes

**Correction to prior wiki entry:** The earlier source summary and person page for George Norman were based on a conflated FamilySearch record (`Print • George Norman (1851–Deceased)`) that merged two different people. That record included London residences (Wandsworth 1881, Croydon 1891) and a Stilton, Huntingdonshire 1901 residence that belonged to a different George Norman. The present sources page and the 1851–1903 print view confirm that our George Norman was in West Hartlepool continuously from 1875 until his death in September 1903. He never went to London, never returned to Huntingdonshire.

**Joint christening at Fen Stanton, 1853:** Confirmed by this source. George was born 1851 but not christened until 3 July 1853 — the same day and place as his infant brother Richard. Ann Tabram chose her own home parish for the ceremony.

**Both brothers in Hartlepool 1875:** George married at West Hartlepool on 7 December 1875; Richard married at Hartlepool in October 1875. The two eldest Norman brothers moved to Hartlepool together and both married there within two months of each other.

**Occupation trajectory:** Grocers Porter (1881) → Grain Porter NE Railway (1891) → Malster at a brewery (1901). A working man in the port and industrial economy of West Hartlepool.

**1901 wife name discrepancy:** The 1901 census records the wife as "May" (age 47, born Hartlepool). The print view consistently names her Margaret J Garry (born 1854, died 1933). Ages and birthplace match; "May" is most likely a pet name or middle name for Margaret.

**1901 child discrepancies:** "Poll" (female, 15) does not appear in the print view children list — possibly a child who died after 1901 or was not captured in FamilySearch. George H Norman (born 1888) does not appear in 1901, suggesting he may have died in childhood. "Prand" (age 8) is likely a transcription error for Francis/Frances (born 1892, would be 9).
