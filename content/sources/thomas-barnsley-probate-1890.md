## Summary

Administration with the Will index entry for Thomas Barnsley, from the England and Wales National Index of Wills and Administrations, 1858–1957. Screenshot of the printed index page.

## Transcription

> BARNSLEY Thomas. Personal Estate £50 1s. 7d.
>
> 3 October. Administration (with the Will) of the Personal Estate of Thomas Barnsley formerly of Matlock Bath in the Parish of Matlock but late of Church-street Bradwell in the County of Derby who died 11 August 1890 at Bradwell was granted at Derby to Joseph Barnsley of Pontefract-place Leeds in the County of York Oil and Tallow Manufacturer the Son and one of the Next of Kin.

## Key Facts

- **Full name and address:** Thomas Barnsley, formerly of Matlock Bath, then of Church Street, Bradwell, Derbyshire
- **Death date:** 11 August 1890, Bradwell (confirms existing)
- **Record type:** Administration with the Will — he left a will, but for procedural reasons administration was granted rather than simple probate
- **Administration granted:** 3 October 1890, Derby
- **Administrator:** Joseph Barnsley of Pontefract-place, Leeds, Yorkshire — described as Oil and Tallow Manufacturer; the Son and one of the Next of Kin
- **Estate value:** £50 1s. 7d.

## Significance

**Joseph Barnsley — new person identified.** The record names Thomas's son Joseph Barnsley, who was based in Leeds at the time of Thomas's death and worked as an oil and tallow manufacturer. Joseph is a sibling of [[margaret-barnsley-1850|Margaret Barnsley]] (Caleb Bunker's wife). He is not yet in the wiki.

**Thomas had a will.** The entry is "Administration with the Will" rather than plain probate — this form occurs when an executor named in the will was unable or unwilling to act, requiring court-appointed administration. The will itself may survive at the Principal Probate Registry.

**Two addresses.** Thomas lived latterly in Bradwell (Church Street) but the record notes he was "formerly of Matlock Bath" — the two Derbyshire addresses in his life correspond to the census pattern (Matlock area in 1871–1881, back to Bradwell by death in 1890).

**Estate of £50 1s. 7d.** A modest sum, similar in scale to Ruth Cooke's £45 estate.
