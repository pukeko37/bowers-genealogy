London Museum collection object 86.72/1 — a dairyman's handcart from Jones Dairy, Fleet Street, London.

**Object details:**
- Used by Jones Dairy to deliver milk around Fleet Street to domestic and business customers
- Still includes: measures for 1 pint, 1.5 pints, and 1 gill (¼ pint); two hot-milk containers
- Production date: 1900–1909
- Dimensions: H 1040mm × L 1360mm × W 850mm

**Jones Dairy:** Built 1902; had eight dairy cows; described as "one of the first Welsh dairy shops in London"; closed 1939. Located in Fleet Street — approximately 0.5 miles from Hosier Lane, Smithfield.

**Relevance to Mason family:**
Jones Dairy (Welsh, Fleet Street, 1902–1939) was operating in the same part of central London as the Mason milk shop at Hosier Lane (1874–1900). The Welsh dairy geography extended through the Smithfield/City/Fleet Street corridor as well as the Paddington/Marylebone cluster. Both operations used handcart delivery and measured milk into customers' containers — the mode of retail confirmed by multiple sources. The Mason shop predated Jones Dairy by over 25 years, operating in the same trade tradition.
