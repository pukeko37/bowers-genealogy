FamilySearch person ID MM1Z-SW2. Sources page for Caleb Bunker (1845–1918) listing seventeen attached records.

**Birth registration:** 1845, Ampthill district, volume VI, page 5. Christening: 19 October 1845, Millbrook.

**1851 census:** Millbrook, age 5. In household with James Bunker (43, ag lab), Elizabeth Bunker (41, Cranfield), and siblings: Phoebe (8), Charles (11), Comfort (2), Martha (10), George (15), David (13). HO107/1754/425.

**1861 census:** Dane Street, St Paul Bedford, age 15, shoe maker. (Already captured in [[caleb-bunker-1861-census]].)

**Marriage to Rebecca Tall:** 1868, Bedford, volume 3B, page 793. (Christmas Day 1868.)

**1871 census:** Lodger in Joseph Fletcher household, Matlock, Derbyshire. Name indexed as "Caleb Bunken" (transcription error). Age 28. Birth year estimated 1843. Birthplace: Ampthill, Bedfordshire. Marital status: **Widowed**. Occupation: **Porter**. Relationship: Lodger. Joseph Fletcher (56, gardener, Kilburn Derbyshire), Ann Fletcher (55, Derby). Also in household: George Fletcher (11), James K Hawker (22, Kempston Bedfordshire), John E Riley (16, Kirkstall Yorkshire). FamilySearch ark:/61903/1:1:VB6Y-XBG. Enumeration district 11f, Matlock.

**Note on 1871 occupation:** Caleb arrived in Matlock after Rebecca's death in July 1870. In 1871 he is a "Porter" — a labourer's role, not a skilled trade. The shoe-making craft he trained at in Bedford evidently took time to re-establish in Derbyshire. By 1881 he had set up in his own trade.

**Marriage to Margaret Barnsley:** 1871, Bakewell district, volume 7B, page 1283. Registered in the Oct-Dec quarter. Bakewell is the registration district covering Matlock.

**1881 census:** Caleb Bunker, head of household, Matlock, Derbyshire. Age 36. Occupation: **Toys Small Dealer Boot & Shoe Maker** (combined occupation). Wife Margaret (30, Ashton-u-Lyne). Children present: Fredrick W T (11, Ampthill Bedfordshire), Annie E (8, Matlock Bath), Herbert T B (6, New Tupton Derbyshire), Maggie E (5, Derbyshire), Agnes M (3, Matlock Bath), Mary G (0, Matlock Bath). Boarders: Annie Richardson (15, Froggatt), Mary H Turnham (39, Thame), Mary A M Thunben (8, Sutton). RG11/3450/35, page 18. — This confirms Frederick Bunker (born 1869) was living with Caleb and Margaret in Matlock by 1881.

**1891 census:** Caleb Bunker, head of household, St Peter's, Derby. Age 45. Occupation: **Boot & Shoe Maker**. Children present: Annie E (18), Maggie E (15), Agnes M (13), Herbert T B (16), May G (11), Ernest C (9). RG12/2727/43.

**1901 census:** Derby, head. Age 55. Occupation: **Boot Shoe Maker**. Children at home: Maggie E (25), Agnes M (23), May G (20), Ernest C (18). Piece 3215, folio 138, page 21.

**1911 census:** **60 High Street, Derby.** Head. Age 65. Occupation: **Boot Maker and Small Shopkeeper**. Wife Margaret (60). Daughter **Annie Elizbth Borrington** (38, Matlock Bath) — Annie Elizabeth had married by this date, surname Borrington. Also in household: Maria Tomlinson (57), John Tomlinson (30), Fredrick Tomlinson (20), Alfred William Horsley (45), Frank O'Brien (33) — several lodgers, consistent with a small shopkeeper running lodgings at the same address. RG14/735.

**Newspaper obituaries:** Two Derby paper notices, 23 May 1918 and 24 May 1918. Herbert T B Bunker obituary notice (13 March 1917) also names Caleb as parent.

**Death:** 1918, Burton upon Trent, Staffordshire, age 73. Volume 6B, page 393.

**Burial:** Find a Grave — Nottingham Road Cemetery, Chaddesden, Derby, 25 May 1918.
