## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1829, Fen Stanton, Huntingdonshire
- **Christened:** 3 January 1829, Fen Stanton
- **Died:** 11 July 1897, Swavesey, Cambridgeshire
- **Alternate name:** Ann Tabram (birth name)
- **Residences:** Elsworth, Cambridgeshire 1841; Swavesey 1851, 1861, 1871, 1881, 1891
- **Other residences noted:** Fen-Stanton, Huntingdon; Paduck Gve (Paddock Grove — an informal address)

## Notes

Ann Tabram was the eldest child of [[richard-tabraham-1807|Richard Tabraham]] and [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary Eusden]], both of the Fen Stanton/Elsworth area. Her mother died ~1841 when Ann was twelve, and she moved to Swavesey by 1851 — likely on or before her 1850 marriage to Thomas Norman. She died in Swavesey in 1897, aged 68, two years after her husband Thomas. FamilySearch uses "Tabram" — a spelling variant of Tabraham common in 19th-century records.
