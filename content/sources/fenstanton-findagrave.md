Two clippings cover Fenstanton:

1. **Fenstanton Cemetery** (cemetery ID 2604713) — 10 records, all modern (20th–21st century) or war memorials. No research family names.

2. **St Peter and St Paul Churchyard, Fenstanton** (cemetery ID 859648) — 43 records. The church is confirmed as Fenstanton by the presence of Lancelot "Capability" Brown (1716–1783), who is famously buried there. No Euseden, Tabraham, or Asplan entries. The listing appears incomplete for the 18th–19th century period when Thomas Euseden (1764–1842) and Mary Euseden (1771–1825) were buried here.

**Null result.** Despite the wiki recording the Euseden burials at Fenstanton, neither listing contains their names. Find a Grave coverage for this churchyard is clearly thin for the relevant period. The open questions about the Euseden/Tabraham ancestry cannot be addressed from this source.

The Brown family vault entries (Capability Brown and descendants) dominate the churchyard listing — as expected for the most famous resident of the village.
