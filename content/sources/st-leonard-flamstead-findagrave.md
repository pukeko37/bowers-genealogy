## New discovery: William Burchmore (1791–1856)

William Burchmore (born 1791, died 17 Jan 1856) appears in the listing but has no person page and is not recorded in the family pages. Born 1791 — five years after George (1786) — he is almost certainly another son of George Birchmore (1762) and Susannah Redding, making him a previously unknown sibling of George (1786). His birth year fits within George (1762)'s marriage period (wed 1784). See [[birchmore-redding-1784]].

## Other Flamstead Birchmores (parentage not established)

The listing contains several further Burchmores whose place in the family tree is unclear:

- Charlotte Burchmore (1805–20 Sep 1885)
- Ann Burchmore (1808–4 Aug 1853)
- Mary Ann Burchmore (1813–30 Aug 1891)
- John Burchmore (dates unknown)
- Sarah Kingston Burchmore (d. 16 Sep 1905) — married name, née Kingston
- George Burchmore (d. 1788) — predates George (1762)'s own death by 55 years; possibly Thomas (1729)'s father

Charlotte (1805) and Ann (1808) predate the 1815 Norton marriage, so they are not children of George (1786) + Ann Norton. They may be children of William Burchmore (1791), or of George (1762) born late in his marriage. Mary Ann (1813) is born the same year as GBP Sr — they cannot be siblings, since GBP Sr's mother was Elizabeth Plummer (not a Flamstead Burchmore).

## Other notable Flamstead families

**Spary**: A prominent Flamstead farming dynasty — John Spary (1777–1843), his wife Elizabeth Bedford Spary (1779–1859), and several children (Edward 1815, Eliza Jekell 1808, Nathaniel James 1841, William 1846, George 1844). Mary Spary (1813–1872) married William Ecutt; both buried here. No documented connection to our research families.

**Sebright**: The Sebright baronets, lords of the Beechwood estate, Flamstead — multiple entries from 1666 to 1895. The manorial family of the parish. No direct genealogical connection to our families.

**Rosier**: Daniel Rosier (1788–1839), Fanny Rosier (1791–1876), Thomas Rosier (1816–1828), Ann Rosier Fryer (1818–1859). A Flamstead family with no established connection to our research.
