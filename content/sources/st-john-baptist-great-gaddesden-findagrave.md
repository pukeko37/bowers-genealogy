Find a Grave memorial listing for St John the Baptist Churchyard, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire (cemetery ID 2663040). 125 records across 7 pages.

Great Gaddesden is the parish immediately east of Flamstead. Thomas Birchmore (1729–1806) married Sarah Andrew here in 1758 — suggesting Sarah Andrew was from this parish. Joseph Batchelor (1755) married **Sarah Bingham** in Flamstead; the Bingham family in this listing is a plausible family of origin for her.

## Key finding: the Bingham family

The listing contains a multi-generation Bingham family at Great Gaddesden:

|------|-------|-------|
| Rev William Bingham | 1742–31 Dec 1819 | Senior male; clergyman |
| Agnata Bingham | 1747–7 Jul 1827 | His wife |
| Martha Caroline Buckley Bingham | 1776–15 Oct 1791 | Died aged 15; probable daughter |

| John Batt Bingham | 1788–10 Apr 1872 | Probable son |
| Frances Bingham | 1798–23 Sep 1874 | Probable daughter |
| LT Henry Bingham | 1837–9 Jan 1860 | Probable grandson (son of John Batt?) |
| Henry Buckley Bingham Smith Bingham | 1866–18 Dec 1920 | Great-grandchild generation |

**Sarah Bingham (born 1758)** — the wife of Joseph Batchelor of Flamstead — fits into this family in one of two ways:
- She was a **younger sister** of Rev William Bingham (1742), sharing the same parents (a Bingham born c.1710–1720 who does not appear in the listing, either because unmarked or predating Find a Grave coverage).
- She was an **early daughter** of Rev William Bingham (1742) himself, born when he was 16 — possible but young.

The sibling hypothesis is more likely given William's probable age at ordination. A parent born c.1715 could have had William in 1742 and Sarah in 1758 — a 16-year span well within the bounds of a single marriage.

**Research lead:** The Great Gaddesden parish registers (Hertfordshire Archives) should contain a baptism entry for Sarah Bingham c.1758. This would confirm or deny the connection to this family, and establish her parents' names — which would in turn open a new line of ancestry behind Joseph Batchelor's wife.

## No Birchmore or Andrew entries

Thomas Birchmore (1729–1806) married Sarah Andrew at this church in 1758, which by convention suggests Sarah Andrew was the parish native. However, no Andrew family appears in the listing — the Find a Grave coverage for this churchyard appears thin for the 18th century. The absence does not rule out the Andrew connection; it likely reflects incomplete transcription.

## Meacher family

Eleanor King Sheeres Meacher (1850–1936) and John Howard Meacher (1872–1908) are buried here. The Chequers Inn ownership record shows a "Meacher" held the pub by 1857. The geography (Great Gaddesden is about 8 miles from Whipsnade) and the generation (Eleanor Meacher born 1850 could be a daughter of the 1857 Chequers owner) make this a plausible family connection, but the link is speculative without further evidence.
