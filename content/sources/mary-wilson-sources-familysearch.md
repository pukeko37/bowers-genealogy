## Notes

**Birthplace discrepancy for Mary:** The LDS record gives Kensworth, Beds; the 1851 census gives Stanbridge, Bedfordshire; the FamilySearch main record gives Stanbridge. Kensworth (Hertfordshire) and Stanbridge (Bedfordshire) are several miles apart. The census is generally more reliable for birthplace. The LDS record may be a mis-transcription, or the original entry may have said "Kensworth" (where the family was living when the children were born).

**LDS baptism date:** One notes field in the sources reads "4 Jan 1850" but the indexed membership record gives **4 January 1851**. The indexed version is authoritative.

**Abel/Able spelling:** FamilySearch records spell the name "Abel"; the family convention has been "Able." Both refer to the same person.

**David Matthews (1817) is distinct from Daniel Willis Matthews (1820):** Two close siblings with "D" names — David was christened 1817, Daniel three years later.
