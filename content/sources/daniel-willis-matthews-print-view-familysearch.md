## Key Facts

- **Born:** 24 June 1820, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire
- **Christened:** 17 December 1820, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire
- **Died:** 15 April 1903, Virgin, Washington, Utah, United States
- **Buried:** 18 April 1903, Virgin, Washington, Utah
- **LDS Baptism:** 12 September 1846
- **Emigration:** 13 June 1853 (departure from Liverpool)
- **Immigration:** 6–16 October 1853, Utah

## Emigration Note (verbatim from record)

> Sailed from Liverpool with family on "Ellen Marie" amongst passenger list of 332 converts to The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints arriving in New Orleans 6 March 1853.

## Residences in Utah

- **1880:** Virgin City, Kane, Utah
- **1900:** Grafton / Harmony / Rockville / Toquerville / Virgin City Precincts, Washington, Utah

## Notes

Daniel Willis was baptised LDS in 1846, seven years after Anne chose to stay in England and marry GBP Sr. He emigrated in 1853 — five years before Anne's death. The ship "Ellen Marie" carried 332 LDS converts from Liverpool to New Orleans, then the overland journey to Utah took until October. He settled in southern Utah (Washington County, Virgin area) and died there in 1903, aged 82.
