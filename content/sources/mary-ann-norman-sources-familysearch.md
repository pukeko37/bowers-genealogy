## Notes

- Surname spelled "Cawser" in 1891, "Causer" from 1901 onward — same family
- Husband George Causer was born Staffordshire ~1860, occupation Engine Driver; died between 1891 and 1901 (widowed by 1901)
- Six children total, born Swavesey 1885–1895
- The 1939 address "Middle Watch" is a hamlet in Swavesey, not a move to Chesterton. Middle Watch is also the confirmed address of her brother [[robert-henry-norman-1868|Robert Henry Norman]] — she remained in Swavesey near or with her youngest sibling. The FamilySearch record appears to have labelled the location as "Chesterton" in error.
- Elizabeth Ann Causer born 19 June 1885 — confirmed exact birth date; age 54 in 1939 is correct. Earlier apparent discrepancy was a transcription error.
