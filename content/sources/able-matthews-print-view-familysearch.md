## Notes

Able emigrated in 1855 — two years after his brother Daniel Willis (1853). The FamilySearch marital status of "Single" contradicts the biography, which records a wife Mary Ann Walker Matthews and at least one daughter Ellen born in St. Louis. He was widowed in St. Louis before reaching Utah. He appears to have emigrated with his own family unit (wife and children), not with Daniel Willis. Naturalised in Utah in 1866 and again 1878 (possibly a re-affirmation of citizenship).
