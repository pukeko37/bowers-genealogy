National Probate Calendar (England and Wales) entry for Edward Ernest Bass.

> BASS Edward Ernest of 16 Maulden-road Flitwick Bedfordshire died 14 August 1936 Probate London 29 September to Lucy Washington Bass widow. Effects £156 4s. 7d.

**Key facts:**
- Address at death: **16 Maulden-road, Flitwick, Bedfordshire** — on the road leading toward his birth village of Maulden
- Died: **14 August 1936**
- Probate granted: **29 September 1936**, London
- Administrator: **Lucy Washington Bass, widow** — she is described as widow, meaning she was his wife at death
- Effects: **£156 4s. 7d.**

The wife named here, Lucy Washington Bass, does not appear in any census record through 1911, where his wife is recorded as Mary Ann Bass (née Dennis, married ~1885). Lucy Washington was almost certainly a second wife, married after 1911 following Mary Ann's death at an unknown date.
