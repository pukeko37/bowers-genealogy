## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1797–1801 (uncertain range)
- **Died:** 1845, Luton, Bedfordshire
- **Residence:** 1841 Whipsnade; On The Green

## First Marriage: Anne (1803–1840)

Married before 1824, near Totternhoe, Bedfordshire. Anne died 1840. Children:
- Mary Ann Heels (1825)
- Rebecka Heels (1826–1911)
- Elizabeth Heels (1834)
- Emma Heels (1838–1848)

## Second Marriage: Hannah Maria Heels (1814–1886)

Married about 1842. One child: Thomas Heels (1843–?).

Samuel died in 1845 — two years after the Chequers pub auction in which he was listed as tenant paying £16/year rent. His widow [[hannah-maria-heels-1814|Hannah Maria]] subsequently married [[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham Batchelor]], who took over the Chequers licence.

## Notes

Samuel and Hannah share the surname Heels — she was almost certainly born Heels, making them distant relatives who married. Her Lockinge, Berkshire origins and his Bedfordshire/Whipsnade base suggest separate branches of the same family. Samuel died the year after the 1843 pub auction, meaning the Chequers licence passed to his widow Hannah and then to Abraham Batchelor through their marriage. This is the cleanest explanation of the Heeles → Batchelor pub transition.
