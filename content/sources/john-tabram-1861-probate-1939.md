National Probate Calendar entry for John Tabram (1861–1939), son of [[john-tabram-1836|John Tabram (1836)]] and Elizabeth Lewis.

**Transcription:** TABRAM John of 1 Southoe Buckden Huntingdonshire died 7 February 1939 Probate London 4 March to Ethel Fuller Lomas married woman. Effects £506 12s. 4d.

**Address:** 1 Southoe, Buckden, Huntingdonshire. Buckden is a village in Huntingdonshire roughly ten miles south-west of Fenstanton — John had left his birth parish at some point after 1901 (when his father was last recorded in Fenstanton).

**Death:** 7 February 1939. Confirms the FamilySearch birth/death dates of 1861–1939.

**Probate:** Granted London, 4 March 1939. Administration to **Ethel Fuller Lomas, married woman**. "Lomas" is not a Tabram name — she is most likely a daughter of John who married a man named Lomas. "Fuller" may be her mother's maiden name carried as a middle name, or simply a middle name. As a "married woman" (not widow) she was alive and still married to Mr Lomas at the time of probate.

**Effects:** £506 12s. 4d. A modest estate — broadly equivalent to a year or two of a skilled worker's wages — but substantially more than many in this family left. His aunt [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann Tabram]]'s descendants (the Norman family) left no probate at all in many cases.
