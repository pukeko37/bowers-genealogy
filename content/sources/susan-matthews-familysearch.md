## Key facts extracted

**Birth:** 17 September 1800 (print view); christened 26 October 1800, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire.

**Parents:** Father **Peter Matthews**; Mother **Mary** (identified from the print view as [[mary-walker-1766|Mary Walker]]).

**Marriage:** Susan Matthews married Thomas Batchelor on 22 May 1826 at **Shenley, Hertfordshire** (not Hertfordshire generically — specifically Shenley).

**Children** (all christened Whipsnade):
- Emma Batchelor — christened 8 April 1827
- Eliza Batchelor — christened 6 December 1829
- Ann Batchelor — christened 22 December 1833
- Elizabeth Batchelor — christened 24 July 1842

**Census record (1841):** Beech Bucket Wood, Whipsnade. "Susanna Batchelor," age 40.

**Census record (1851):** Whipsnade. Susan Batchelar, age 50, birthplace "Whipsnade, Bedfordshire," occupation **Grocer**.

**Death:** 20 November 1857, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire.

**The 1871 census:** A Susan Batchelar (age 71, birthplace Luton, Bedfordshire) appears in the 1871 Whipsnade census with Thomas. This is *not* Susan Matthews — she died in 1857. This entry belongs to Thomas's second wife, **Susan Durent née Maynard**, whom he married in January 1868. FamilySearch attaches this census incorrectly to Susan Matthews.
