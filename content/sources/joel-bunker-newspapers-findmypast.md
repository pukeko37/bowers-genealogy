FindMyPast newspaper search for "Joel Bunker", 1800–1849. Seven results, six of which clearly relate to the same Joel Bunker — publican of the **Green Man Inn, Eversholt, Bedfordshire** (near Woburn). One result (Feb 1830, Northampton Mercury) is a name in a list, possibly the same man.

## Confirmed references

**1 October 1833, Morning Herald (London)**
Auction notice for freehold land and cottages at Eversholt. "To view, to the tenant, Mr. Joel Bunker, at the Green Man, near the church, Eversholt." Confirms Joel was tenant-publican of the Green Man by 1833 at the latest.

**22 October 1836, Bucks Gazette**
Death notice: *"On the 11th, aged 49 years, Mrs. Bunker, wife of Mr. Joel Bunker, Green Man Inn, Eversholt, near Woburn."*
This is the death of [[martha-taylor-1786|Martha Taylor]], Joel's first wife. She died **11 October 1836, aged 49** — placing her birth at approximately 1787. FamilySearch shows her death as 1836 and birth as 1786, consistent.

**24 March 1838, Bucks Gazette**
Marriage notice: *"Sunday se'nnight, at Eversholt, Beds, Mr. Stephen Danby, excise officer, of Hockliffe, to Eliza, eldest daughter of Mr. Joel Bunker, Green Man Inn, Eversholt."*
Confirms Joel is still at Eversholt in early 1838. His daughter Eliza married Stephen Danby, an excise officer — corroborates Eliza Bunker (1815–1885) in the family list.

**13 February 1830, Northampton Mercury**
Joel Bunker appears in a list alongside other named individuals. Context unclear — possibly jurors, subscribers, or a similar roll.

**2 November 1844, Northampton Mercury**
Court report: *"John Smith, John Brazier, and William Brazier were each fined 6s. and 10s. costs, for on the 11th October, assaulting Joel Bunker, of the Green Man public-house, Eversholt."* Joel was the victim; three men prosecuted.

**23 November 1844, Northampton Mercury**
Weights and measures court: *"Joel Bunker, of the Green Man, Eversholt, had 73 measures taken from him. He was only fined £1, and 7s. 6d. costs."* He had short measures — minor trade infraction.

**19 September 1846, Northampton Mercury**
Auction notice: *"To be sold by auction... the premises, the property of MR. JOEL BUNKER, the Green Man Public-House, Eversholt (aforesaid), who is leaving the neighbourhood, ALL the genuine HOUSEHOLD FURNITURE."*
Joel departed Eversholt in September 1846, leaving behind the household contents. His reasons for leaving are not stated.

## Summary of Joel's career at Eversholt

Joel ran the Green Man Inn at Eversholt for at least thirteen years (1833–1846). Martha died there in 1836. He remarried Elizabeth Inskipp in 1839 and continued at the pub until September 1846 when he sold up and left. The 1841 census (Eversholt) and FamilySearch residence data confirm his presence there. His later occupation is recorded as "Formerly Publician And Serv" — he retired from the trade.
