## Summary

Probate index entry for Thomas Batchelar, from the England and Wales National Index of Wills and Administrations, 1858–1957. Screenshot of the printed index page.

## Transcription

> BATCHELAR Thomas. Effects under £200.
>
> 5 March. The Will of Thomas Batchelar late of Whipsnade in the County of Bedford Shepherd who died 22 December 1872 was proved at the Principal Registry by Henry Perkins of Whipsnade Farmer the sole Executor.

## Key Facts

- **Full name and address:** Thomas Batchelar, late of Whipsnade, Bedfordshire
- **Occupation:** Shepherd
- **Death date:** 22 December 1872, Whipsnade (confirms existing)
- **Record type:** Probate (he left a will)
- **Probate granted:** 5 March 1873, Principal Registry
- **Executor:** Henry Perkins of Whipsnade, Farmer — sole executor
- **Estate value:** Under £200

## Significance

**Occupation: Shepherd, not agricultural labourer.** The probate record describes Thomas as a Shepherd — a specific, skilled pastoral role rather than the generic "agricultural labourer" recorded in census returns. Shepherds on the chalk downland of Whipsnade/Dunstable Downs were highly regarded specialists. This is a meaningful correction to Thomas's occupational record.

**Executor: Henry Perkins, Whipsnade Farmer.** Henry Perkins was a farmer in Whipsnade who Thomas trusted enough to name as his sole executor. He was not a family member recorded in the wiki. The choice of a respected local farmer rather than a relative as executor may suggest Thomas had no surviving close kin in Whipsnade at the time — or that Perkins was a close associate or employer.

**He left a will.** The entry is "Probate" confirming Thomas had a will; it was proved at the Principal Registry (London). The will's contents (beneficiaries, specific bequests) are not given in the index.

**Estate under £200.** The threshold for the "under £200" bracket is consistent with a working man who owned personal effects and perhaps savings but no freehold property.
