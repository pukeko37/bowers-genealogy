**Vitals:**
- Born November 1806 at Wootton, Bedfordshire; christened 17 May 1807 at Wootton by Bedford
- Died 4 January 1884 at Haynes, Bedfordshire; buried St Mary's Churchyard, Haynes
- Married **Joseph Geary** 20 April 1824 at Clophill, Bedfordshire

**Parents:**
- Father: **William Spring** (1779–Deceased), christened Shillington, Bedfordshire. See [[william-spring-1779]].
- Mother: **Sarah Bottoms** (1786–1829). See [[sarah-bottoms-1786]].

**Note on name:** The christening record gives the name as "Rhoda or Phoebe Spring or Bottoms" — the dual surname (Spring or Bottoms) reflects her parents' relationship; Sarah Bottoms does not appear to have formally married William Spring before his later marriage to Mary (about 1808). Rhoda was born in Wootton, a different parish from the Spring family base at Shillington.

**Residence history:** Haynes (Plum's Lane) from at least 1841 through 1881 — the family home for her entire married life. Died and buried at Haynes in 1884.
