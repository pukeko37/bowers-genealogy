Area guide published in the Hertfordshire Advertiser. Covers the character, property, and amenities of Kinsbourne Green.

**Key facts extracted:**

- Kinsbourne Green is a small hamlet to the north-west of Harpenden town centre, in the parish of Harpenden Rural (created 1898 when old Harpenden Parish split into urban and rural districts)
- "In the early 20th century, Kinsbourne Green was a small hamlet, quite detached from Harpenden" — character of separation from the town persists
- **The Annables Estate:** "A development of 32 individually designed detached houses in Kinsbourne Green on two privately owned roads, Chamberlaines and Spring Road. The first house on the estate was built in the 1930s and since then individual plots have been sold off and properties built." The privately owned roads give the estate a private/gated character. Current prices: five-bedroom detached on the estate marketed at £2m.
- Property prices overall average ~£950,000; detached properties over £1.3m currently
- Good access to M1 (junction 9, ~10 minutes) and A1081; Harpenden station (Thameslink to St Pancras, 25–30 minutes) requires a short drive or infrequent bus
- Only one pub remains: The Fox
