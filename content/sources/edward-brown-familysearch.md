## Critical note: two daughters named Lydia

The FamilySearch tree for Edward Brown shows **two daughters named Lydia** — born 1765 and 1769. The first (1765) has no marriage or death record and presumably died young; the second (1769) is our Lydia who married James Thomas Bunker at St Paul's Walden on 31 December 1788. This naming pattern — giving a second child the same name as an earlier child who died — is common in this period.

This resolves a prior error in the wiki: the `lydia-brown-print-view-familysearch` source summary had noted a "correction" of Lydia's birth year from "abt 1769" to "14 April 1765" based on a Cranfield parish register entry. That 1765 baptism belongs to the sibling Lydia (K8SK-JJX), not our Lydia (LYHK-W98). Our Lydia's birth year is correctly **abt 1769**.

## Significance

This clipping resolves the long-standing open question of Lydia Brown's origins. She was from **Cranfield, Bedfordshire** — not Hertfordshire as earlier sources suggested (the Hertfordshire attribution arose from her marriage at St Paul's Walden, just over the Hertfordshire border). Her parents were Edward Brown (abt 1732) and Lydia (maiden name unknown), who raised their family at Cranfield throughout the 1758–1769 period.

The St Paul's Walden marriage (1788) remains slightly puzzling given that both families were Bedfordshire-based. St Paul's Walden is in Hertfordshire, roughly equidistant from Millbrook and Cranfield.
