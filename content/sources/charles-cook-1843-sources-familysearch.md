FamilySearch sources page for Charles Cook (FS ID G4LP-JD7), 13 sources attached. This is NOT the same as [[charles-cook-1847|Charles Cook (1847)]], Ruth Cooke's son.

## 1851 Census (two records)

First record: Charles (8) with parents George Cook and Susan Cook — his birth family.

Second record: Charles (8, born Whipsnade) in Daniel Cook household alongside Ruth (31) and Mary Sanders Cook (2). This indicates Charles was visiting or lodging in Ruth Cooke's household in 1851 — proximity that later caused confusion between the two Charles Cooks.

## Marriage (21 March 1868)

Married [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois Brandsom]] at Whipsnade. Her father Jeremiah Brandsom was present.
