Notice published in *The London Gazette*, Issue 24893, page 5361, dated 19 October 1880.

**The Bankruptcy Act, 1869. In the County Court of Derbyshire, holden at Derby.**

In the Matter of Proceedings for Liquidation by Arrangement or Composition with Creditors, instituted by **Caleb Bunker**, of Matlock Bath, in the county of Derby, Boot and Shoe Maker, and Toy and Smallware Dealer, trading as **Thomas Barnsley**.

Notice is hereby given that a First General Meeting of the creditors of the above-named person has been summoned to be held at the offices of Mr. James Potter, of All Saints-chambers, No. 22, Irongate, in Derby, on the 2nd day of November, 1880, at one o'clock in the afternoon precisely. Dated 14th day of October, 1880.

**Solicitor:** James Potter, All Saints-chambers, No. 22, Irongate, Derby.

**Key facts extracted:**

- Caleb Bunker's address at the time of proceedings: **Matlock Bath**, Derbyshire (more specific than the "Matlock" of earlier records)
- Business description: **Boot and Shoe Maker, and Toy and Smallware Dealer** — matches his 1881 census occupation exactly
- Trading name: **Thomas Barnsley** — Caleb operated his business under the name of his father-in-law, [[thomas-barnsley-1817|Thomas Barnsley]] (1817–1890)
- Proceedings type: **Liquidation by Arrangement or Composition** — not full bankruptcy, but a negotiated settlement with creditors
- First creditors' meeting: 2 November 1880, Derby
- Solicitor: James Potter, Derby
