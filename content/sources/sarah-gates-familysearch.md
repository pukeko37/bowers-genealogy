## Key Facts Established

**Christening:** 27 November 1803, **Aldbury, Hertfordshire** — not a Bedfordshire woman by birth; Aldbury is near Tring, a few miles across the county border.

**Parents:** Joseph Gates (1781–Deceased, FamilySearch MLYK-J2H) and **Elizabeth Inward** (1780–1843, MLYK-J2N), married 9 June 1803 at Hertfordshire — Sarah was born just months after her parents wed.

**Siblings:** Hannah Gates (1812), Elizabeth Gates (1814), Mary Gates (1817), Charlotte Gates (1822–1846), Josiah Gates (1826).

**Marriage:** 17 January 1825 at **Studham, Bedfordshire**. The marriage register entry shows "Sarah Yates or Gates" — the register clerk had some uncertainty about the surname. The corresponding entry for her husband is simply "Matthews."

**1841 census (Houchen End, Whipsnade):** Sarah (35), Peter (35), Hannah (6), William (4) — and also **Charlotte Gates (15)**, Sarah's younger sister, living in the household. Charlotte died in 1846 aged ~24.

**1851 census (Whipsnade):** Sarah (47), Peter (48); children listed are David (9), Elizabeth (5), Hannah (16). Sarah's birthplace is recorded as "Arbury, Buckinghamshire" — almost certainly a misrecording of Aldbury, Hertfordshire. William (13) and Sarah Elizabeth (~7) are not in the indexed entry; William may have been in service elsewhere.

**Death:** 1851, Bedfordshire — she died in the year of the census; the 1851 entry is her last record.

## Notes

The Gates family connection to Aldbury, Hertfordshire, explains the Studham marriage venue: Studham was the nearest Bedfordshire parish to Aldbury, and a natural meeting point between the Whipsnade Matthews family and a Hertfordshire family.

Charlotte Gates's presence in the 1841 household is a small but human detail — Sarah brought her younger sister into the home, and Charlotte died there four or five years later, in 1846.
