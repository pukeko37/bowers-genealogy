FamilySearch print view for Frances Green (GH94-P81).

**Vitals:** Born 28 May 1769, Swavesey, Cambridgeshire. Christened 28 May 1769, Swavesey. Died 1810, Over, Cambridgeshire, England.

**Spouse and children:** Married [[robert-norman-1769|Robert Norman]] 16 November 1788, Swavesey. Seven children (same list as in Robert Norman's print view).

**Parents:** [[frances-green-1769|Frances Green]]'s father was Francis Mann (1746–1819) and her mother was Elizabeth Green (1745–1824). Her surname is complex: she was baptized as "Frances Marks" (or "Frances Mann") with the father's name recorded variously as "Francis Marks" or "Francis Mann," and her mother's maiden name was Green. The marriage record in 1788 gives her as "Frances Green or Mann" — she apparently used her mother's maiden name Green as her own surname at times. FamilySearch lists her under "Frances Green."
