## Name History (from Brief Life History)

Born illegitimately as George Birchmore Plummer, given mother's surname Plummer. Registered as George Birchmore in 1841 census. Around 1860–61, begins using George Birchmore Plummer after marriage to Eliza Batchelar, birth of daughter Ellen (christened Ellen Plummer), and death of his father George Birchmore. In his 1859 marriage record, registered as "George Plummer Birchmore" — surnames used interchangeably for a period. His son from the first marriage continued using George Birchmore Plummer and appears on marriage records in 1871.
