## Analysis

**The 1828 entry confirms our Mary.** "Mary — Rd TABRAM — Fenstanton" matches the known marriage of [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary Eusden]] to [[richard-tabraham-1807|Richard Tabraham]], 3 August 1828, Fen Stanton.

**The Eusden family heartland is Huntingdonshire, not Cambridgeshire.** The county-wide index makes clear the family was rooted in Huntingdonshire. The Ely christening of Mary (1808) may reflect a temporary residence by her father Thomas, or her mother's family being from Ely.

**Two distinct clusters:**

*Ellington cluster (1762–1772)* — Rt, Mary, Wm, and Eliz all marrying at Ellington within ten years. These are almost certainly a sibling group, one generation above Thomas Euseden (born 1764). The 1762 "Rt" who married Eliz Bellamy is a strong candidate for Thomas's father, with Thomas (b. 1764) being the first child of that marriage.

*Fenstanton cluster (1825–1833)* — William, Mary, Sarah, Thomas, and Henry all marrying at Fenstanton within eight years. This is almost certainly the sibling group that includes Mary Eusden: all children of Thomas Euseden (1764–1842). The tight geographic and temporal clustering is compelling. The 1833 "Thos" is ambiguous: could be Thomas the father (~69, possibly remarrying after his wife died) or a son also named Thomas.

**The 1836 entry** — "Mary Ann w [widow] married Jn Wm SKINNER at Kimbolton" — is a widowed Mary Ann Eusden. She could be a daughter of Thomas (younger than Mary, marrying post-1837 date-range) or the widow of one of the male Eusdens from this generation.

**Research leads opened:**
- Check HEWSDEN index (as directed by the source header) for pre-1762 Eusden records
- Parish register of Ellington — find the marriage of "Rt Eusden and Eliz Bellamy" (1762) to identify Thomas's parents
- Parish register of Fenstanton — confirm William (1825), Sarah (1829), Thomas/Henry (1833) as Thomas Euseden's children
- 1841 census — Thomas Euseden (b. ~1764, d. 1842) should appear with surviving children, possibly William or Henry
