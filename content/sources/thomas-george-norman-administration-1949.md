## Summary

Administration index entry for Thomas George Norman, from the England and Wales National Index of Wills and Administrations, 1858–1957. Screenshot of the printed index page.

## Transcription

> NORMAN Thomas George of 106 Hertford-road Hackney London died 5 March 1949 at The Hospital Hackney Administration London 10 June to Mary Elizabeth Norman widow and Gwendoline May Norman spinster. Effects £8889 11s. 9d.

## Key Facts

- **Full name and address:** Thomas George Norman, of 106 Hertford Road, Hackney, London
- **Death date:** 5 March 1949 (precise date; previously only "1949")
- **Death place:** The Hospital, Hackney — died in hospital, not at home
- **Record type:** Administration (intestate — he died without a will)
- **Administration granted:** 10 June 1949 — three months after death
- **Administration place:** London
- **Administrators:** Mary Elizabeth Norman (widow) and Gwendoline May Norman (spinster)
- **Estate value:** £8,889 11s. 9d.

## Significance

**Intestate.** The record is "Administration" not "Probate," meaning Thomas left no will. The estate passed by intestacy rules to his wife and daughter.

**Address at death: 106 Hertford Road.** Thomas's 1921 census address was 106 Hertford Road, Hackney; his 1939 address was 6 Englefield Road, Kingsland. The probate record gives his address at death as 106 Hertford Road again — suggesting he returned to the earlier address, possibly after the Englefield Road house was bombed in WW2 and he had been living in the baker's shop.

**Died at The Hospital, Hackney.** He did not die at home — he was hospitalised. No further information on the cause of death is given in the index.

**Gwendoline May Norman (spinster)** is confirmed as still unmarried at Thomas's death in 1949 — she was 33 or 34. She never married; her lifelong singlehood is confirmed by the "spinster" designation here.

**Estate of £8,889.** Substantially larger than the estates of most family members in the wiki. This is consistent with Thomas having run manufacturing businesses and a wartime bakery.

**Corrects existing source.** The print view source [[thomas-george-norman-print-view-familysearch]] recorded "Probate: 10 Jun 1949, London" — the date is correct but the type should be Administration. Thomas died intestate.
