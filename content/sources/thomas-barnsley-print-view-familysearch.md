Thomas Barnsley was born in Bradwell in the Peak District, migrated to the cotton/weaving industries of Ashton-under-Lyne in Lancashire (as many Derbyshire workers did), and later returned to Derbyshire — first to Matlock, then Darley Dale. His daughter Margaret was born in Ashton-under-Lyne in 1850 during the Lancashire period. He died at Bradwell, his birthplace, in 1890, with probate granted in Derby. Margaret Barnsley is the probable second wife of Caleb Bunker.
