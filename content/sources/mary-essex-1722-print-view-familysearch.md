FamilySearch person L565-SD7. Mary Essex, born 1722 at Cottenham, Cambridgeshire; christened 10 October 1722; buried 17 October 1722 (likely a data error — probably the burial of another Mary Essex in the same year). Died 1765 at Cottenham.

**Parents:** John Essex (1703–1762) and Alice (1705–1727). Mary's siblings: Alice Essex (1720, died same year), John Essex (1721), Mary Essex (our Mary, 1722), Philip Essex (1723–1742), Alice Essex (1724), Jane Essex (1725), Thomas Essex (1726).

**Marriage to Richard Norman:** 27 April 1740 at Cottenham. Children: William Norman (1739–1822), Richard Norman (1745–Deceased).

**Note on burial date:** The burial date of 17 October 1722 — one week after the christening — likely belongs to a different infant named Mary Essex, or is a FamilySearch data entry error. Mary Essex (our subject) clearly lived to 1765, as she appears as Richard Norman's wife in subsequent records.
