## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1850, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire (FamilySearch record corrected; see note)
- **Christening:** 09 Jun 1850, St Pancras Old Church, Saint Pancras, London
- **Birth Registration (Custom Event):** 1850, St Pancras — origin of earlier birthplace confusion
- **Died:** 1920, St Thomas, Devon, England (St Thomas is a district of Exeter)
- **Also known as:** George Birchmore
- **Marriage:** 25 July 1871, Kentish Town, Middlesex — to Elizabeth Sarah Morse
- **Residences:** 1851 Whipsnade; 1871 Islington; 1881 St Pancras; 1891 St Pancras; 1901 St Pancras; 1911 St Pancras, St Jude, Gray's Inn Road
- **Occupation:** Dairy Farmer (1881); Milkman (1891); Milk Carrier (1911) — confirmed by custom fact: "Marriage Certificate names George Birchmore as father of George Birchmore Plummer, a milkman."
- **Children:** Rosa (1874–1934), Edith Eleanor (1876–1947), George William (1880–1943), Florence Eliza (1884–1951)

## Notes

**Birth and christening anomaly.** The FamilySearch record now correctly gives birth as 1850, Whipsnade — consistent with census evidence (1851, 1861, 1871 all cite Whipsnade; 1901, 1911 give "Whipsnade, Bedfordshire"). However, the christening is recorded at St Pancras Old Church, 9 Jun 1850, and a separate "Birth Registration" custom event cites St Pancras. This is internally inconsistent: Whipsnade was in the Luton registration district, not St Pancras. It is possible GBP Sr was in London in mid-1850 and had the child christened at St Pancras, or the birth registration entry is a separate data error. The census birthplace evidence is taken as the reliable indicator.

**Marriage confirmed.** George Jr and Elizabeth Sarah Morse married 25 July 1871, Kentish Town, Middlesex — prior to Rosa's birth in 1874. This closes the open question about whether they formalised their relationship. They had been together as a couple in St Pancras from at least 1871.
