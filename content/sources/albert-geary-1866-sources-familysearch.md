FamilySearch sources page for Albert Geary (1866–1942), aggregating 15 attached sources including his christening, census returns 1871–1939, marriage registrations, and burial record.

**Key facts confirmed:**

- Born 26 July 1866 (confirmed by 1939 National Register). Christened 16 Sep 1866, Haynes, Bedfordshire. Parents: John Geary and Lucy.
- Birth registration 1866, Ampthill, Bedfordshire (Volume 3B, p. 356).
- 1871 census: age 4, Haynes (listed as "Platter" — almost certainly an enumerator's error given his age).
- 1881 census: age 14, at home, The Warren, Haynes. Siblings at home: Ernest (11) and Lydia (12).
- First marriage: to **Charlotte** (born ~1860, Maulden, Bedfordshire). Married probably ~1890.
- 1891 census: Married, Woodman, age 23, **Maulden**. Wife Charlotte (31, Bedfordshire), infant son Ernest (days old).
- 1901 census: Albert Gearey, Railway Platelayer, age 32, **Luton**. Wife Charlotte (40, Maulden), children Ernest (10, Haynes), Elizabeth (7, Haynes).
- 1911 census: Gardener, age 42, Luton. Wife Charlotte (52, Maulden), son Ernest C (20, Maulden), daughter Elizabeth A (17, Maulden). Also lodger Emma Keech (24, Maulden).
- Second marriage: married **Caroline Stanbridge** in 1928 at Luton (Charlotte had died by then, date unknown).
- 1939 National Register: age 73, Gardener, 76 Windsor Street, Luton. Wife Caroline E Geary (63). Also Percy F Stanbridge (28) and a Stanbridge female (25) in household.
- Death registration: 1942, Bedfordshire (Volume 3B, p. 947). Age 75. Buried Luton General Cemetery, 19 March 1942.

**Note:** Albert followed his father's occupation (Woodman) in 1891 at Maulden — the same village his parents had moved to. He married a Maulden woman (Charlotte), then shifted occupation to Railway Platelayer and later Gardener as the family relocated to Luton.
