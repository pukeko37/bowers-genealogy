FamilySearch sources view for Thomas Barnsley (GWYY-7LN), clipped 1 June 2026. Aggregates census, vital, and probate records. Key extractions:

## Marriage

Thomas Barnsley married **Eliza Bradwall** on 28 May 1838 at Hope, Derbyshire — a Church of England parish ceremony. His father was **Luke Barnsley**. Eliza's father was John Bradwall. Both Thomas and Eliza were from Bradwell, a village in the Hope Valley, Derbyshire — a local match made before the family moved to Lancashire.

## Children (all christened Ashton-under-Lyne, St Michael and All Angels)

- **Elizabeth Barnsley** — christened 11 October 1840
- **Martha Barnsley** — christened 2 June 1844
- **Joseph Barnsley** — born 23 March 1847, christened 9 May 1847; married Maria Ellen Boon in Lancashire on 17 May 1869; later an oil and tallow manufacturer in Leeds (Pontefract-place)
- **Margaret Barnsley** — born 14 April 1850, christened 12 May 1850; later married [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]]

## Census occupations

**1841** — Ashton-under-Lyne (Cricketty). Household: Thomas (age 20), Eliza (20), Elizabeth (1). No occupation recorded (1841 census format).

**1851** — Ashton-under-Lyne. Age 34. Occupation: **Dealer In Old And New Clothes**. Household: Thomas, Eliza wife (30, born Bradwell), Elizabeth daughter (10), Joseph son (4), Margaret daughter (0), plus Caroline Bradurle (28, born Bradwell, Derbyshire) — likely a sister-in-law (Eliza's sister). Reference: HO107/2233.

**1871** — Matlock, Derbyshire. Age 54. Occupation: **Stationer**. Household: Thomas, Eliza wife (52, born Bradwell), Margaret daughter (20, born Ashton-under-Lyne). Enumeration district 11f, household 74. This is the census in which Caleb Bunker also appears in Matlock — the year Caleb arrived and married Margaret.

**1881** — Darley aka North Darley (Darley Dale), Derbyshire. Age 64. Occupation: **Formerly Shop Keeper**. Household: Thomas, Eliza wife (62, born Bradwell). Reference: RG11/3449/52. Thomas describes himself in the past tense — retired from retail. The Matlock Bath bankruptcy (October 1880) had occurred six months before this census. By April 1881 the shop was gone and Thomas had withdrawn to Darley Dale.

## Probate cross-reference

The FamilySearch probate record (3 October 1890) confirms death date 11 August 1890. Full text from the probate screenshot: *"formerly of Matlock Bath in the Parish of Matlock but late of Church-street Bradwell"* — confirming Thomas had been specifically at Matlock Bath (where Caleb's business was registered), and retired to Bradwell (his birthplace). Administration granted at Derby to Joseph Barnsley of Pontefract-place, Leeds. See [[thomas-barnsley-probate-1890]].
