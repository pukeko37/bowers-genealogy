FamilySearch sources page for Frances Green (GH94-P81). Three sources attached.

**Birth/christening, 28 May 1769** — England, Cambridgeshire Bishop's Transcripts. Indexed as "Frances Marks," female, born Swavesey, Cambridgeshire. Father listed as "Francis Marks," mother "Elizabeth Green." A second record (England, Births and Christenings, 1538–1975) gives the same date and place but names her "Frances Mann" with father "Francis Mann." The discrepancy in the father's surname (Marks vs. Mann) may reflect a clerical variant or dual surname usage common in 18th-century Cambridgeshire.

**Marriage, 16 November 1788** — England, Cambridge, Parish Registers. Frances Green (indexed as "Frances Green or Mann") married Robert Norman at Swavesey. She is listed as the wife in the record for Robert Norman. The "or Mann" designation echoes the birth record's uncertainty between Green and Mann.

**Name note:** Frances was baptized under her father's surname (Marks/Mann) but appears in records under her mother's maiden name (Green). This surname borrowing from the mother's line is unusual but not unknown in 18th-century England.
