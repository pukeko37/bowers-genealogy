## Corrections to the Millbrook page

**Rachel (~17) was NOT in this household.** No Rachel appears in the census. The Millbrook page's prior attribution of "Rachel (~17)" as James's daughter by Catherine Blunt was wrong and unsubstantiated — confirmed absent.

**Naomi WAS in this household** (age 25, as expected). An earlier session incorrectly removed her; she is confirmed present.

**The Chapmans were Rebecca Bunker's family**, not a separate household — see above.

## On Richard Bunker (35)

Richard's census age of 35 gives an estimated birth year of 1802–1806. The Millbrook page had him as "Richard (c.1800)" — his actual birth was probably c.1804–1806. He is James's son by his first wife **Sarah Pedder** (the other son being John 1803). No person page yet exists for him.

## On the grandson Richard (4)

Born c.1837. Confirmed as Naomi's pre-marital son Richard Bunker (1837), who was living with his grandfather.
