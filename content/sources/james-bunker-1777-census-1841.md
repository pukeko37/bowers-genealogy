## Corrections to the Millbrook page

**Rachel (~17) was NOT in this household.** She does not appear in the census record. The Millbrook page's prior attribution of "Rachel (~17)" to this household was wrong — confirmed absent.

**Naomi was NOT in this household.** Despite FamilySearch attaching her 1841 census record to this household (MQRS-LCX), the user's direct reading of the census image confirms she is not present. FamilySearch has likely indexed her from the same census page/folio as James's household without her being in his household. Naomi's actual 1841 location is currently unknown.

## On Richard Bunker (35)

Richard's census age of 35 gives an estimated birth year of 1802–1806. The Millbrook page had him as "Richard (c.1800)" — his actual birth was probably c.1804–1806. He is James's son by his first wife **Sarah Pedder** (the other son being John 1803). No person page yet exists for him.

## On the grandson Richard (4)

Born c.1837. Confirmed as Naomi's pre-marital son Richard Bunker (1837), who was living with his grandfather.
