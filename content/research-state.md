## The Three Threads

### Thread 1: Norman / Birchmore / Cook / Thody

The core family thread. Converges at **Ivy Helen Norman** (1899–1994) and her world.

**Norman-Tabram (Cambridgeshire):** Thomas Norman (1827–1905) + Ann Tabram (1829–1897), Swavasey. Seven children: Charles went to London; George (1851) and Richard (1853) went to West Hartlepool; Tom, Mary Ann, Alfred, and Robert Henry stayed near Swavesey.
- Coverage: All seven children have person pages. The Tabraham-Eusden ancestry goes back to Thomas Euseden (1764–1842) in Fenstanton, with the Ellington generation above still unresearched.
- **Gap: The Hartlepool branch.** George and Richard had 21 children between them — none has a person page.

**Birchmore-Plummer (Flamstead/Whipsnade):** Ellen Birchmore's father GBP Sr (1813–1884) had three marriages and an illegitimacy story. Well-documented back to George Birchmore (1762), Row End Farm, Flamstead. Ellen's mother Eliza Batchelor and the Batchelor/Bachelor/Heels families of Whipsnade are documented. The full Chequers Inn licensee sequence is established.

**Cook / Thody (Whipsnade/Bedfordshire):** Ruth Cooke (1820–1904) is the hub: her daughter Mary Saunders Cook → Bransom family; her daughter Lucy Cook (1856) → William Allen Thody → Harry Thody → Thody-Bunker 1920 → Ivy Norman meets Roland Bunker.
- William Allen Thody's transatlantic life (US Army enlistment 1871, desertion, Chicago marriage, two return crossings) is fully documented.
- The 1894 Thody-Cook wedding witness network is resolved.
- Clarence Thody's WWI death (Captain, Manchester Regiment, 30 Aug 1918, Riencourt-lès-Bapaume) is fully documented.

**Mason connection:** Richard Mason + Catherine James (Welsh Cardiganshire dairy family, Hosier Lane Smithfield). Two sons married Norman sisters (John → Mabel 1912; Isaac → Alice 1918). Richard William Mason is documented as a lifelong bachelor. All three brothers converge on Daysbrook Road, Streatham in their final years. Fully documented.

**Matthews/LDS (Whipsnade/Utah):** Anne Matthews (GBP Sr's first wife) and the ten children of Thomas Matthews + Mary Wilson. Three Utah emigrants (William, Daniel Willis, Abel; 1853–1861) are fully documented including death records. Charlotte Matthews stayed and married Ruth Cooke's brother David Cook.

**Thread 1 open questions:**
- Hartlepool branch: 21 undocumented children of George Norman and Richard Norman (top priority)
- Richard Norman (1853): death date and full occupation unknown
- Tom Norman at Eaton Socon 1911: probable Crown Beerhouse, but 1911 census image not yet verified

### Thread 2: Bunker

Roland Bunker's line. The Millbrook, Bedfordshire ancestral home runs from Thomas Bunker (1704) through six generations to Roland (1898).

**Main line:** Thomas (1704) → Thomas (1738) → James Thomas (1764) → James (1807) → Caleb (1845) → Frederick (1869) → Roland (1898). Fully documented throughout. The double bereavement at Maulden (July 1870: Rebecca Tall and her father Edward Tall died nine days apart) left Frederick as an infant in his grandmother Sarah Ward's care; Caleb moved to Matlock and remarried within eighteen months.

**Caleb's second family (Barnsley branch):** Six children of Caleb + Margaret Barnsley. Herbert T.B. Bunker (1875–1917) and Ernest Caleb Bunker (1882–1967) are documented; the Derbyshire/Bristol dispersal is told. Caleb's 1880 bankruptcy (trading as Thomas Barnsley) is documented.

**Caleb's siblings:** All eight siblings have person pages. The New Tupton convergence (~1875: George, Caleb, Phoebe all had children born there) is documented. The Wesleyan Methodist thread through the Bunker family (1806 mass christening → 1858 Millbrook chapel → 1860 Wesleyan christening → 1868 Howard Chapel wedding → 1913 Horfield Wesleyan) is noted across pages but not written up as a dedicated analysis.

**Watts/Capel ancestry:** Elizabeth Watts (James Bunker's wife) documented back through John Watts (1790–1814), Sarah Capel (three marriages), Samuel Capel (1750–1808), Sarah Knight (1755–1839).

**Bunker-Reade deep ancestry:** Thomas Bunker (1704) + Mary Reade (1728, Toddington). 12 children fully documented. Mary Reade's parents unknown.

**Thread 2 open questions:**
- The name change: why Roland Bunker → Bowers in 1936? Deed poll confirmed; motive unknown. Frederick died 1932 — timing may be relevant.
- David Bunker (1837–1920, Peterborough): 83-year life; only the print view ingested
- Martha Sarah Bunker (1841–1901): obituary published in Luton newspaper; not yet found
- The three Lane daughters (Edith, Mary, Elizabeth — Lydia Bunker's children; father died 1872): unresearched
- Mary Reade (1702–1775): parents unknown; Toddington 1728 register not checked
- `batchelor-bingham-flamstead` family page: Joseph Batchelor (1755)'s parents; created 2026-07-01
