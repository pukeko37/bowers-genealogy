Steppingley is a village in Bedfordshire, about 3 miles northeast of Ampthill. It was the settled home of William Bunker (1740) — seventh child of Thomas Bunker (1704) — and then of at least four generations of descendants. William married Ann Peet here in 1768, and his children and grandchildren remained in Steppingley through the nineteenth century, including Thomas (1767), William (1776), Joseph (1798), and Samuel (1818). The [[bunker-sear-1840|Bunker-Sear]] family who produced Ruth Bunker (1840) were among the last of this line in the village.
