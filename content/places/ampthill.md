Ampthill is a market town in Bedfordshire, about 8 miles south of Bedford. It lies at the heart of the Bunker family's Bedfordshire geography — the parish where John Bunker (1803) married Maria West in 1824, where John Watts Bunker married Ann Woodland in 1854, where John Bunker (1746) is buried, and where several Bunker children were christened in the nineteenth century.

The town sits between Millbrook (3 miles west) and Bedford (8 miles north), making it a natural gathering point for the Bunker families dispersed across the Greensand Ridge.
