Stevington is a village in Bedfordshire, about 5 miles northwest of Bedford. It was home to a branch of the Bunker family rooted there from the late eighteenth century: [[james-thomas-bunker-1764|James Thomas Bunker]] (1764) came from nearby parishes, and his son [[joseph-bunker-1792|Joseph Bunker]] (1792–1853) was buried at Stevington Baptist Chapel. [[james-bunker-1824|James Bunker]] (1824–1904), Joseph's son, lived his entire life at Silver Street, Stevington, and is the father of [[ruth-bunker-1855|Ruth Bunker]] (1855) who emigrated to Quebec.
