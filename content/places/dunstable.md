Dunstable is a market town in south Bedfordshire, at the junction of the ancient Icknield Way and Watling Street. It was the nearest significant town to Whipsnade, a few miles to the north-west, and served as a natural gathering and legal point for the communities of the chalk downland parishes.

[[able-matthews-1813|Able Matthews]] and [[mary-walker-1817|Mary Ann Walker]] were married at Dunstable on 22 November 1840, with James Walker (probably Mary's father) as witness. The choice of Dunstable rather than Whipsnade or Kensworth is consistent with it being the civic centre for the area — registrar's office, market, and transport connections all concentrated here.

William Allen Thody of the [[thody-saunders-1834|Thody family]] is identified in the CWGC record of his son Clarence as "of Dunstable," suggesting the family had moved into Dunstable by the time of WWI.
