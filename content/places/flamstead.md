Flamstead is a village in Hertfordshire, approximately 5 miles west of St Albans. It sits close to the Bedfordshire border — Whipsnade is about 5 miles to the north-west, and the two parishes exchanged families across several generations.

## The Birchmore family

The Flamstead Birchmores (spelled "Burchmore" in most local records) were a yeoman farming family rooted here for at least four generations. They farmed at **Row End Farm** (also Roe End Farm) throughout the 18th and early 19th centuries.

- Thomas Birchmore (1729–1806) and Sarah Andrew — George (1762)'s parents; both buried at St Leonard
- [[george-birchmore-1762|George Birchmore]] (1762–1843) — farmed Row End Farm his entire life; buried St Leonard
- [[george-birchmore-1786|George Birchmore]] (1786–1838) — farmed Roe End Farm; buried St Leonard; father of GBP Sr
- William Burchmore (1791–1856) — probable third son of George (1762); buried St Leonard; newly identified from the churchyard listing
- George Burchmore (1817–22 Aug 1866) — legitimate son of George (1786) + Ann Norton; buried St Leonard

The illegitimate birth of [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]] in 1813 (father: George 1786; mother: Elizabeth Plummer) brought the Flamstead line into the Bedfordshire research through GBP Sr's later life in Whipsnade and Studham.

## Matthews connection

The [[matthews-durrant-1761]] family — Peter Matthews (1734) and Susannah Durrant — were married at Flamstead in 1761 before settling at Kensworth. This is the only other research family with a direct Flamstead link.

## St Leonard Churchyard

152 memorials are recorded in Find a Grave (as of June 2026), spanning 1413 to 2011. The major local families represented are Burchmore, Spary (extensive farming dynasty), and the Sebright baronets (lords of the Beechwood estate). No Batchelor entries appear, which is notable given the open question about Joseph Batchelor (1755) and the [[batchelor-bingham-flamstead]] family.
