Eaton Bray is a village in Bedfordshire, approximately 4 miles south-west of Whipsnade in the Vale of Aylesbury. Its parish church is St Mary the Virgin.

## Woodcraft family

The Woodcraft family had a documented presence at Eaton Bray, represented in the St Mary the Virgin churchyard by WWI veterans Harry Woodcraft (1890–16 Feb 1917) and Ernest Woodcraft (dates unknown). These men are likely two or three generations removed from the family's Victorian core.

The significance for our research: **Alice Woodcroft** (born abt 1791) married [[thomas-bachelor-1781|Thomas Bachelor]] at Whipsnade on 2 August 1816. Her origins are undocumented but she was from "Whipsnade or the surrounding area." Eaton Bray is now the leading candidate for her home parish, sitting squarely in the corridor between Whipsnade and Leighton Buzzard where the Bachelor/Batchelor family had their roots.

To confirm: the Eaton Bray parish registers (Bedfordshire Archives) should be searched for Woodcraft baptisms in the 1760–1800 range, and for any Woodcroft/Woodcraft in the early 19th century. See [[bachelor-woodcroft-1816]] and [[st-mary-virgin-eaton-bray-findagrave]].

## St Mary the Virgin Churchyard

398 memorials recorded in Find a Grave (as of June 2026) — well-transcribed relative to comparable Bedfordshire/Cambridgeshire churchyards. Dominant families: Ashwell, Wood. No entries for Batchelor, Matthews, Cook, or other primary research families beyond Woodcraft.
