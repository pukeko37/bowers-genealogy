Studham is a village in Bedfordshire, immediately east of Whipsnade. The parish church is **St Mary the Virgin**.

[[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] served as Police Constable at Studham from at least 1901 and died there on 1 February 1911, buried at St Mary the Virgin. His widow [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] remained at Rose Cottage, Studham, until her death in early 1930 (gravestone: 25 January 1930). Both are buried in the churchyard. A memorial inscription for their son [[clarence-james-thody-1884|Captain Clarence James Thody]] (killed France, 1918) was also placed at the church, though his body rests at Manchester Cemetery, Riencourt-lès-Bapaume.

Ivy and Norah Norman often visited Lucy at Rose Cottage. After their marriage in 1921, [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Ron]] and [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy Bunker]] stayed there before moving to Portsmouth.

The churchyard also holds Noel Jesse Turvey (1896–1978) and Millie Turvey (1898–1973), likely a branch of the Whipsnade Turvey family who settled at Studham.
