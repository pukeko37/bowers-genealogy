St Pancras is a district in the London Borough of Camden, immediately north of the City of London. Its southern edge — Gray's Inn Road, King's Cross — borders Islington and is roughly one mile north of Smithfield Market.

## Family Connections

**[[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer Sr]]** married [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]] at Old Church, Saint Pancras on 18 June 1859. This was a deliberate excursion from Whipsnade; neither party was a London resident. His son George Jr was christened in Whipsnade in 1850 and is consistently cited as Whipsnade-born in the census record.

**[[birchmore-morse-1874|George Birchmore Plummer Jr and Elizabeth Morse]]** lived in St Pancras their entire London lives — 1881 through 1911, latterly at St Jude, Gray's Inn Road. George Jr worked as a milkman in this area.
