Hemingford Abbots is a small village in Huntingdonshire, immediately south of Fenstanton and adjacent to Hemingford Grey on the River Great Ouse. It is the birthplace of [[adeliza-fuller-1862|Adeliza Fuller]] (1862–1938), whose parents Thomas and Rachael Fuller were from this parish. Adeliza married [[john-tabram-1861|John Tabram]] and lived in Fenstanton.
