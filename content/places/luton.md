Luton, Bedfordshire. [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick Bunker]] worked here as a bricklayer and builder, settling by 1911 and dying there in 1932. His wife [[eliza-geary-1859|Eliza Geary]] and their children [[beulah-bunker-1896|Beulah]] and [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland]] grew up here. Frederick and Eliza later became estranged but remained separately in Luton. In 1939, Eliza was living at 9 Oakley Road, Luton with Beulah and [[harry-thody-1894|Harry Thody]]. Eliza died there in 1942.
