Haynes is a village in Bedfordshire, about 4 miles south of Bedford. It was the home parish of the Geary family across two generations: [[joseph-geary-1804|Joseph Geary]] (1804–1890) and [[lucy-morris-1826|Lucy Morris]] settled here, and their son [[john-geary-1825|John Geary]] (1825–1895) was buried at the parish church. Eliza Geary — who married Frederick Bunker and raised Roland Bunker in Luton — grew up in Haynes before the family moved to Maulden in later life.
