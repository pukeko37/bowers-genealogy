After Charles Norman's death in 1907, the Norman family moved to the Kingsland area of Hackney, just north of the City of London — specifically the De Beauvoir Town neighbourhood of St John at Hackney. The 1911 census (RG14/477, schedule 236) places 11 people in one dwelling: Ellen Norman, all nine children, and one visitor — Mary Elizabeth Angus, Thomas George Norman's future wife.

The Masons were not part of that household. The three Mason brothers (Richard William, John James, Isaac) were enumerated separately at Lambeth, Norwood in 1911. Family oral history gave the impression of a joint Norman-Mason household at this address, but the census does not support it. John Mason married Mabel Norman in Hackney the following year (1912), which may be the origin of the confusion.
