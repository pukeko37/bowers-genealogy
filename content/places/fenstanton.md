Fenstanton (also written Fen Stanton) is a village in Huntingdonshire, five miles north-east of Swavesey across the county boundary. It was the home parish of the **Tabraham family** — the maternal grandparents of [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] — and appears repeatedly in the Norman family's records across four generations.

For the full narrative of the Norman family's roots in this area, see [[swavesey]], which covers both Swavesey and Fenstanton together.

## The Tabraham family

Richard Tabraham and Mary Eusden settled in Fenstanton on their marriage in 1828 and raised four children there. Mary died around 1841; Richard remained at Bell Lane, Fenstanton through the 1851 and 1871 censuses, and died in Elsworth in 1874. Of their four children, two — [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann]] and [[john-tabram-1836|John]] — were born at Fenstanton; [[susan-tabram-1831|Susan]] was born in the Fenstanton area around 1831. All three surviving siblings were recorded in Fenstanton households through the 1840s and 1850s.

Ann left for Swavesey on her marriage to Thomas Norman in 1850. John and Susan remained in Fenstanton for the rest of their lives.

## John Tabram and the Fenstanton Tabrams

[[john-tabram-1836|John Tabram]] (1836–1901) married **Elizabeth Lewis** at Fenstanton on 8 November 1857 and worked as an agricultural labourer throughout his life without ever leaving the parish. He and Elizabeth had seven children, three of whom died young. The 1871, 1881, 1891, and 1901 censuses all find him in Fenstanton. He is the last of the Tabraham generation to appear in the Fenstanton record.

## Susan Tabram and the Levitt family

[[susan-tabram-1831|Susan Tabram]] (abt 1831–1911) married **John Levitt** (1830–1909) at Fenstanton on 11 October 1859. The Levitts were themselves a Fenstanton family — John was born there and his family was locally rooted. Susan and John lived their married life in Fenstanton and Fen End, the hamlet within the parish, appearing in every census from 1861 through 1901 at Fen Stanton addresses.

Their six children were all christened at Fenstanton parish church:

| Child | Christened |
|---|---|
| William Levitt (1860–1944) | 13 May 1860 |
| Mary Ann Levett (1863–1863) | 3 May 1863 — died in infancy |
| Lizzy Levitt (1865–) | 4 June 1865 |
| George Levitt (1867–) | — |
| Mary Eusden Levitt (1869–) | 3 October 1869 |
| Annie Tabrain Levitt (1874–) | 31 May 1874 |

Mary Eusden Levitt's name commemorated Susan's mother [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary Eusden]], who had died in this same parish thirty years earlier. Annie's middle name "Tabrain" preserved the Tabram surname in the next generation — a deliberate family naming act that parallels her aunt [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann]]'s naming of Alfred Eusden Norman in Swavesey in 1865.

John Levitt died in 1909. Susan, widowed at about 78, died in 1911; her death was registered in the Chesterton registration district, Cambridgeshire, suggesting she had moved out of Fenstanton in her final years — possibly to be near one of her children.

## Key events at Fenstanton

- **1828** — [[richard-tabraham-1807|Richard Tabraham]] and [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary Eusden]] married at Fenstanton on 3 August
- **1829** — [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann Tabram]] born; Bell Lane, Fenstanton, Richard Tabraham's address
- **1841** — Mary Eusden died at Fenstanton, aged ~34
- **1842** — [[thomas-euseden-1764|Thomas Euseden]] (Mary's father) buried at Fenstanton, aged ~78
- **1851** — Susan Tabram recorded in Fenstanton, unmarried, occupation Housekeeper
- **1853** — [[george-norman-1851|George Norman]] and [[richard-norman-1853|Richard Norman]] christened together at Fenstanton on 3 July — Ann Tabram's home parish, chosen over Swavesey
- **~1854** — [[ann-elizabeth-randall-1854|Ann Elizabeth Randall]] born at Fenstanton — later married Richard Norman in 1875
- **1857** — John Tabram married Elizabeth Lewis at Fenstanton, 8 November
- **1859** — Susan Tabram married John Levitt at Fenstanton, 11 October
- **1860–1874** — Six Levitt children christened at Fenstanton parish church
- **1871** — Richard Tabraham (64) still recorded at Fenstanton in the census
- **1874** — Richard Tabraham died, Elsworth
- **1901** — John Tabram last recorded in Fenstanton census; John and Susan Levitt also in Fen Stanton
- **1909** — John Levitt died
- **1911** — Susan Levitt died (Chesterton registration district)
