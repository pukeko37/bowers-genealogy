The [[norman-birchmore-1883|Norman-Birchmore family]] first lived in Hackney after Charles and Ellen married in 1883. The Carter Paterson haulage operation was centred here. The family later moved to [[hosier-lane-city-of-london|Hosier Lane]] in the early 1890s.
