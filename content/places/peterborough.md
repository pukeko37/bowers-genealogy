Peterborough is a city in Cambridgeshire, a major Great Northern Railway junction in the nineteenth century. [[david-bunker-1837|David Bunker]] (1837–1920) settled here by 1881 working as a railway storekeeper, and spent the last four decades of his life in the city. He was the only Bunker sibling of his generation to migrate northeast rather than to Derbyshire or London.
