Elizabeth Julia Sinfield (born ~1867, Bedford), younger daughter of Joseph Sinfield and [[martha-sarah-bunker-1841|Martha Sarah Bunker]]. Married **George Linger** on 24 December 1886 in Luton. Their son Arthur Linger (born ~1888) appears in the 1891 Sinfield household aged 3.
