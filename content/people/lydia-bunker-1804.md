Lydia Bunker (4 February 1804 – 31 March 1883) was the sixth child of [[james-thomas-bunker-1764|James Thomas Bunker]] and [[lydia-brown-1769|Lydia Brown]], and the eldest surviving sibling older than [[james-bunker-1807|James Bunker]] (1807) to reach old age. She lived almost her entire adult life in Bedford and died there aged 79.

She married **Thomas W Inskip** (1800–1875) on 25 March 1832 at St Paul's Church, Bedford — two years after her youngest brother James married Elizabeth Watts at Millbrook. She had seven children, all registered in the **Non-Conformist Record Indexes (RG4-8)** at **Mill Street Baptist, Bedford**:

The census trail places the family on Mill Street or in St Paul's parish, Bedford, across 1841, 1851, and 1861. Lydia was widowed in 1875 and died eight years later. She was buried at **Foster Hill Road Cemetery, Bedford** — the same cemetery as her sister-in-law [[elizabeth-watts-1810]] (died 1868) and the Tall family (connected to [[caleb-bunker-1845]] via Rebecca Tall).

**Nonconformist denomination:** The children's Baptist registrations distinguish this branch from the main Bunker line's Wesleyan Methodist affiliation. Thomas Inskip was Baptist; whether Lydia herself converted or was already inclined toward Baptist practice is unknown.

**Not to be confused with:** [[lydia-brown-1769]] (her mother) or Lydia Bunker (1826, daughter of her brother [[caleb-bunker-1790]]).
