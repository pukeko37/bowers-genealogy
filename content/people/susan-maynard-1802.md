Susan Maynard was born in early March 1802 in Luton, Bedfordshire, to **George Maynard** and **Sarah Rumball** (married Luton, 14 February 1794). The family were Nonconformists; her birth is recorded in the Baptist register (RG4-8). Her known siblings are Mary Maynard (1795–1884) and Keziah Maynard (1800). She had three marriages, all in or connected to Whipsnade, and she appears to have spent most of her adult life in that village.

### Marriage 1 — William Woodcraft

Susan married **William Woodcraft** (1777–1835) on 20 May 1827 at Whipsnade. He was considerably older — about 50 at the time of the marriage. They had five children, all christened at Whipsnade: Ann (1828), George (1829), James (1832), Amos (1834), and Charles (1836). If FamilySearch's death date for William (1835) is correct, Charles was born after his father's death.

The 1841 census places Susan — recorded as "Susan Woodcroft," age 40 — at **Frog Corner, Whipsnade**, the hamlet that contained the Chequers Inn. Her household included George (11), James (9), Amos (6), and Charles (5), consistent with the known children. Also in the household were Thomas Woodcroft (25) and Charlotte Woodcroft (15) and David Woodcroft (20) — too old to be Susan's children by William from the 1827 marriage. They may be William's children from a prior relationship, but this is unconfirmed.

### Marriage 2 — Edmund Durrant

Susan married **Edmund Durrant** (born c.1795, Whipsnade; agricultural labourer) on 9 June 1849 at Whipsnade. Her father **George Maynard** is named in the marriage record as a party, confirming his paternity. The Durrant surname is what she carried when she remarried in 1868 — the "Susan Durent" of Thomas Batchelar's record is this marriage.

The 1851 census records them as "Edmund and Susanna Durrant" at Whipsnade. Edmund was head of household, aged 56, Ag Lab; Susan is listed as wife, aged 51, with occupation **"Labs Wife And School Mistress"** — she was running a small school in Whipsnade, a notable independent role for a woman in a hamlet of this size. Still in the household in 1851 were Thomas Woodcraft (35) and Charlotte Woodcraft (27), older children from the first marriage.

Edmund Durrant died between 1851 and 1868; no death record has been located.

### Marriage 3 — Thomas Batchelar

Susan married [[thomas-batchelar-1803|Thomas Batchelar]] (1803–1872) on 11 January 1868 at Whipsnade. This was her third marriage and his second. She was about 65; he was 64. Her father George Maynard is again named in the marriage record. By this time she was known as Susan Durent (her second married name). No children from this marriage are recorded.

The 1871 census shows Susan Batchelar, aged 71, birthplace Luton, Bedfordshire, in Thomas's household at Whipsnade. Thomas died 22 December 1872. Susan's fate after his death is not established.

### Notes

- The birthplace "Setan, Bedfordshire" in the 1851 census is almost certainly a transcription error for Luton.
- The Frog Corner address in 1841 is the same hamlet as the Chequers Inn — though Susan was there as a widow before Abraham Batchelor and Hannah Maria Heels took over the pub in the mid-1840s.
- Her father George Maynard appears in both her 1849 and 1868 marriage records, confirming his paternity across more than thirty years.
