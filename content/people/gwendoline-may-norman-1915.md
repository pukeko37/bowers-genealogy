Gwendoline May Norman was born in 1915, the only child of [[thomas-george-norman-1886|Thomas George Norman]] (1886–1949) and [[mary-elizabeth-angus-1885|Mary Elizabeth Angus]] (1885–1969). She never married — described as "spinster" in her father's 1949 administration record, when she was 33 or 34.

When her father died on 5 March 1949, Gwendoline and her mother Mary Elizabeth jointly administered his estate (£8,889 11s. 9d.). The family's house at 6 Englefield Road, Kingsland, Hackney had been bombed during WW2; Thomas had run a baker's shop in Dalston/Kingsland during the war, and after the bombing the family moved into the shop to live. Gwendoline continued living in the converted baker's shop for over 40 years after the bombing.

In 1984, Andrew Bowers and David Bowers visited her at the baker's shop premises in Kingsland. Eventually she moved to Kent, where she was cared for by her cousin [[george-stewart-main-1919|George Stewart Main]] until her death in 2000.
