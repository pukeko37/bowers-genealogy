George Woodcraft (christened 6 December 1829, Whipsnade), second child of [[william-woodcraft-1777|William Woodcraft]] and [[susan-maynard-1802|Susan Maynard]]. He appears in his mother's 1841 household at Frog Corner, Whipsnade, aged 11; no records traced thereafter.
