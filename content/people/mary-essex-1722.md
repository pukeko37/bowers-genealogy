Mary Essex was born in 1722 at Cottenham, Cambridgeshire (christened 10 October 1722), the daughter of John Essex (1703–1762) and Alice (1705–1727). She died in 1765 at Cottenham.

She married a **Richard Norman** on 27 April 1740 at Cottenham. That marriage is well-attested in the Cambridgeshire Bishop's Transcripts. What is uncertain is *which* Richard Norman — see [[norman-essex-1740]] for the full discussion of the attribution problem.

In brief: FamilySearch has attached this marriage to two different Richard Norman profiles. The more credible attribution is to FamilySearch profile L565-ZVH (Richard Norman, 1718–1753), not to our [[richard-norman-1723|Richard Norman]] (P68D-T5X, 1723–1783). If the marriage belongs to L565-ZVH, Mary would have been widowed in 1753 and lived her remaining twelve years in Cottenham — consistent with her dying there in 1765.

**Note on FamilySearch burial entry:** A burial date of 17 October 1722 — one week after Mary's christening — appears in the FamilySearch record. This most likely belongs to a different infant named Mary Essex in the same parish and year. Mary Essex clearly survived to adulthood.
