Mary Essex was born in 1722 at Cottenham, Cambridgeshire (christened 10 October 1722), the daughter of John Essex (1703–1762) and Alice (1705–1727). She died in 1765 at Cottenham.

She married [[richard-norman-1723|Richard Norman]] on 27 April 1740 at Cottenham — both were from the same parish. Their known children were William Norman (1739–1822) and Richard Norman (1745). See [[norman-essex-1740]].

Mary died in 1765, twenty-five years before her husband Richard (who died 1783). Richard remarried Mary Hall in 1749, and it was through that second marriage that [[robert-norman-1769|Robert Norman]] — the ancestor of [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy Helen Norman]] — was born.

**Note on FamilySearch burial entry:** A burial date of 17 October 1722 appears in the FamilySearch record — one week after Mary's christening. This most likely belongs to a different infant named Mary Essex and is a data entry error. Mary Essex clearly survived to adulthood and to 1765.
