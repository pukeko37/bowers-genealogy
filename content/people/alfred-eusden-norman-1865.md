Alfred Eusden Norman was born in 1865 in Swavesey, Cambridgeshire, the sixth child of [[thomas-norman-1827|Thomas Norman]] and [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann Tabram]] in the [[norman-tabram-1850|Norman-Tabram family]]. His birth was registered at St Ives, vol 3B.

His middle name "Eusden" is a deliberate memorial to his maternal grandmother [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary Eusden]], who had died in 1841 — 24 years before Alfred was born. Ann Tabram's choice to preserve her mother's maiden name in her sixth child's name speaks to the loss she felt at losing her mother when she was only twelve.

Alfred never left Swavesey. He appears in every census there from 1871 (age 5, scholar) through 1891 (age 25, agricultural labourer). He was single throughout. His death was registered in 1896, age 30, in the Cambridge registration district — Cambridge GRO covered Swavesey. He predeceased both his parents — his mother Ann died in 1897, his father Thomas in 1905.

Note: in the 1881 census the Norman family's address is given as "58 High St, Boxworth End (Public House)" — the household was living at or adjacent to a pub on Swavesey High Street.
