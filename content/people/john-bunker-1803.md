John Bunker was born in 1803 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire, the son of [[james-bunker-1777|James Bunker (1777–1864)]] by his first wife Sarah Pedder. He died in 1889 at approximately 86 — nearly as long-lived as his father. FamilySearch ID: G9RB-9PR.

He married **Maria West** (born 1806, Ampthill) on 15 August 1824 at Ampthill — he was 21, she was 18. She was an Ampthill woman, not from Millbrook. They had at least nine children, all christened at Millbrook. John appears in the 1861 census with his own household near his elderly father James, and was in Millbrook through at least that point. He almost certainly served as the anchor of the Millbrook Bunker presence through the Victorian decades while his cousins from the James (1807) line left for Bedford and Derbyshire.

## Notes

John's mother is identified in the James (1777) page as Sarah Pedder. His brother from the same marriage is Richard (~1800). James later married Mary Somerfield and Catherine Blunt, so John's half-siblings include Naomi (~1813) and Rachel (~1824).

John himself served as **clerk and sexton of Millbrook parish church** — confirmed by his son Samuel's obituary (*Bedfordshire Times*, 20 June 1913): "His father also [served in the same capacity]." Samuel then held the role for about 20 years after him. The sexton's role ran in the family across two generations.
