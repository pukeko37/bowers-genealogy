Edwin Bransom was born on 15 September 1850 in Whipsnade, Bedfordshire, the sixth child of [[jeremiah-bransom-1808|Jeremiah Bransom]] (1808) and [[sarah-watts-1816|Sarah Watts]] (1816). He married [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]] in 1871 and together they raised seven children in Whipsnade. He died in 1928 aged approximately 82.

He appears in the Whipsnade census in every recorded year from 1851 through 1911 — a lifelong resident of the village, as were his wife and his parents before him.

## Family of Origin

Edwin was the youngest surviving child in the Bransom household. His sister [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois Brandsom]] (1843–1924) — seven years his senior — married [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook (1843–1908)]] in 1868, while Edwin himself married Mary Saunders Cook in 1871. The intertwining of the Bransom and Cook families in Whipsnade thus ran through two separate marriages.

## Children

Edwin and Mary had seven children, all raised in Whipsnade:

1. [[albert-bransom-1871|Albert Bransom]] (1871–1940) — christened 8 October 1871
2. [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]] (1873–1963) — born 20 September 1873, christened 5 November 1873
3. [[ebenezer-bransom-1875|Ebenezer Bransom]] (1875–1965) — born 31 August 1875, christened 25 October 1875
4. [[clara-lucy-bransom-1877|Clara Lucy Bransom]] (1877–1957) — born 14 September 1877, christened 2 December 1877. Married Cyril John Baldock 1912; worked as a Book Sewer at Watertow & Co Printing Works, Dunstable.
5. [[margaret-anne-bransom-1880|Margaret Anne Bransom]] (1880–1964)
6. [[walter-william-bransom-1881|Walter William Bransom]] (1881–1963) — born 26 November 1881, christened 5 February 1882
7. [[minnie-rose-bransom-1884|Minnie Rose Bransom]] (1884–1960) — born 24 January 1884, christened 13 April 1884

Florence was a witness at the 1894 Whipsnade marriage of [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] (Mary's sister) and [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]].

## Later Life

By 1901 Edwin and Mary were alone in the household — all children had left home. The 1911 census shows them with Florence (now "Florce Perry Bransone", aged 37) and Florence's daughter Florence Hilda Perry Bransone (aged 8), suggesting Florence had married a Perry. Edwin died in 1928; a death registration survives confirming age 82.
