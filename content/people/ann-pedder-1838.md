Ann Pedder was born around 1838 in Westoning, Bedfordshire, and married [[george-bunker-1836|George Bunker]] on 8 April 1858 at St Paul's Church, Bedford. Her maiden name is known from the marriage record; her parents and wider family are not yet documented.

Westoning is notable as the birthplace of George's ancestor [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]], though there is no known family connection — Ann and George appear to have met in Bedford, not through the Westoning community.

She appears in every census alongside George from 1871 through 1911, always recorded with Westoning, Bedfordshire as her birthplace. By 1911 she and George had been married 53 years. Her death date is unknown; she was alive and in Derby in the 1911 census, aged approximately 73.
