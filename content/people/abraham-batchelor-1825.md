Abraham Batchelor was born in 1825 in Whipsnade, christened 20 March 1825. He was the son of [[thomas-bachelor-1781|Thomas Bachelor]] and Alice Woodcroft. He married [[hannah-maria-heels-1814|Hannah Maria Heels]] (1814–1886), who came from Lockinge, Berkshire. See [[batchelor-heels-whipsnade]].

## The Chequers Inn

Abraham was almost certainly the licensee of the Chequers Inn, Whipsnade, in the period between Samuel Heeles (tenant 1843) and [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]] and [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] (who took the licence at their December 1867 marriage). The evidence is circumstantial but coherent:

- Abraham and Hannah were in Whipsnade in every census 1841–1861 — the right period
- Hannah's surname Heels connects her to Samuel Heeles, the 1843 tenant — likely a family transfer of the licence
- Abraham's addresses include "By Whipsnade Green" and "Frog Corner" — the hamlet where the Chequers sits
- After 1867, Abraham and Hannah moved to Studham (1871, 1881 census) — consistent with vacating the pub
- Abraham returned to Whipsnade in 1891 and died in 1892 (death registered in the Luton district)

## Relationship to Eliza Batchelor

Abraham is related to [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]] (GBP Sr's second wife, 1829–1867) — making GBP Sr's connection to the Chequers pre-date his marriage to Ruth Cooke. The exact relationship between Abraham's line (through [[thomas-bachelor-1781|Thomas Bachelor]], Flamstead) and Eliza's line (through [[thomas-batchelar-1803|Thomas Batchelar]], Dagnall) is the outstanding open question. Both Batchelar/Bachelor families are from the same 4-mile cluster of Chilterns villages.
