Charles Bunker (born ~1866, Bedford), youngest child of [[david-bunker-1837|David Bunker]] and Marian Bentley; named for his uncle [[charles-bunker-1839|Charles Bunker]] of London. No records traced beyond early censuses. Not to be confused with [[charles-bunker-1839|Charles Bunker (1839)]], his uncle, or [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman (1859)]], a different family.
