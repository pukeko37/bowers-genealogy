Mabel Bunker was born in 1883 in Millbrook, the tenth child of [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker (1840–1914)]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]]. She was the youngest of the daughters.

**1901**: Mabel (aged ~18) is recorded as a domestic servant — the 1901 census places her away from home, working in service. The most likely location is Luton or the surrounding area, given subsequent events.

**1902**: A daughter, [[grace-bunker-1902|Grace Bunker]], was born in Luton in Q3 1902 — probably Mabel's illegitimate child. Grace was registered under the Bunker name, indicating Mabel was unmarried at the time. Grace was raised by Joseph and Mary Ann at Millbrook.

**1904**: Mabel married **Mark Edward Cook** at Millbrook in May 1904. She was approximately 20 or 21. Nothing is known about Mark Cook's background.

**1911**: Mabel and Mark Cook are living in Luton. Grace (age 8) is not with them — she remains with her grandparents at Millbrook. Mabel's death date and any children of the Cook marriage are unresearched.
