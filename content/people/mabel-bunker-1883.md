Mabel Bunker was born on 25 March 1883 in Millbrook, the tenth child of [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker (1840–1914)]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]]. She was christened 29 April 1883 at Millbrook.

**1891**: Mabel appears at home in Millbrook with her parents, aged eight.

**1901**: She is recorded in the household of Frederick L Sharpin — a Church of England clergyman — in Millbrook, working as a **house parlour maid**. She was still in her home parish, employed in the vicarage or rectory household. The Sharpin children included several born in India, suggesting a clergyman with colonial service.

**1902**: [[grace-bunker-1902|Grace Bunker]] was born in Luton in Q3 1902. Mabel had moved to Luton at some point between the April 1901 census and Grace's birth. Grace was born under the Bunker surname, consistent with Mabel being unmarried at the time.

**1904**: Mabel married **Mark Edward Cook** (born 1883, Wotton, Bedfordshire) on 21 May 1904 at Millbrook — returning to her home parish for the ceremony. After the marriage, Grace was left in Millbrook with Joseph and Mary Ann.

**1911**: Mabel and Mark are living in Luton. Mark is working as a stone mason's labourer. Two children are with them: Victor Thomas (aged 5, born Bedford) and Albert John (aged 2, born Luton). The census notes "5 children born, 7 years married" — suggesting five children had been born to date, though only two are visible in this household.

**1921**: The family is at 15 Ashton Street, High Town, Luton. Grace — listed as **Grace Bunker Cook** — is now with her mother, confirming Mabel as her parent. Also in the household: Victor Thomas, Herbert Henry, Annie Mabel, Albert John, and Mark Joseph Cook. This is the last documented record of Mabel.

Mark Edward Cook is recorded as dying in 1955. Mabel's death date is not established.
