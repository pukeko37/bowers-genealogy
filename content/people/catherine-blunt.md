Catherine Blunt (born c.1792–1796, Bedfordshire) was the third wife of [[james-bunker-1777|James Bunker (1777)]]. In the 1841 census she appears as "Cathrine Bunker" aged 45 at Village Sand End, Millbrook. She was also with James in the 1851 and 1861 censuses at Millbrook. Her maiden name, origins, and death date are not documented. No children of James and Catherine have been confirmed by a christening record; the previously assumed "Rachel" daughter has not been found in census or parish records.
