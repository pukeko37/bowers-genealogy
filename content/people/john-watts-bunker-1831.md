John Watts Bunker was born 10 December 1831 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire, the eldest child of [[james-bunker-1807|James Bunker]] and Elizabeth Watts. He was christened 10 February 1832 in Millbrook. He died in 1899 in Ampthill, Bedfordshire, aged ~67.

His middle name Watts was his mother's maiden name — a family naming custom also seen in Caleb's son Frederick William **Tall** Bunker, who carried his mother's surname as a middle name.

After his father died in 1852, the family moved from Millbrook to Bedford. John remained in the Bedford area — recorded there in 1871 and in Bedford Western Ward St Mary in 1881. He died in nearby Ampthill, close to his Millbrook birthplace. He was Caleb's eldest brother.
