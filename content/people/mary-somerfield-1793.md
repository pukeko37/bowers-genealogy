Mary Somerfield (abt 1793–unknown) was the second wife of [[james-bunker-1777|James Bunker (1777)]], marrying him on 25 June 1811 at Millbrook. Her origins are not documented. Together they had five children in Millbrook between 1813 and 1818. See [[bunker-somerfield-1811]].
