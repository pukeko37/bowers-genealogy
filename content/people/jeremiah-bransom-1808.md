Jeremiah Bransom (born 1808) was an Agricultural Labourer of Whipsnade, Bedfordshire. He married [[sarah-watts-1816|Sarah Watts]] on 7 November 1833 at Edlesborough, Buckinghamshire. Together they had at least six children, all raised in the Whipsnade area.

He is listed as head of household in the 1851 census, with his wife Sarah and several of their children. His son [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin]] was christened the same day as his birth in 1850, with Jeremiah recorded as "Jeremiah Brandsom" on the christening record.

Jeremiah was present at his daughter [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois]]'s marriage to [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook]] on 21 March 1868 at Whipsnade.

His children were: Frederic (1836), Betsy (1838), Elizabeth (1838), Alfred (1840), [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois]] (1843–1924), and [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin]] (1850–1928).
