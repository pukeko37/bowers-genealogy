Mark Ashby Bunker was born in 1789 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire, the ninth child of [[edward-bunker-1742|Edward Bunker]] (1742–1814) and Mary Ashby (1745–1813). His given name preserves his mother's maiden name — a common 18th-century practice that kept the maternal family identity alive.

Before his transportation he had fathered at least three illegitimate children in the area: Seth Wheeler (1809, Millbrook), Fanny Johnson (1814, Aspley Guise), and Mark Ashby Bunker jr (1815, Cranfield). Two Bedfordshire Quarter Sessions recognizances document the latter two: QSR/22/1814/114 (Fanny Johnson, mother Elizabeth Margess/Burgess) and QSR/22/1816/157.

In 1817 he was convicted of sheep stealing and transported to Australia for life. He does not appear in Millbrook records again. After his release from prison in Australia he married and had eight further children there. He died in 1868 aged approximately 79.
