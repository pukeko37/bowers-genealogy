Alfred Dumpleton was born in 1848 at Chalgrave, Bedfordshire. He appears in the Chalgrave parish in the 1851 and 1861 censuses. By 1871 he had moved to Sundon, Bedfordshire, where he met and married [[emma-hucklesby|Emma Hucklesby]] on 22 February 1870 at Sundon. By 1881 the family had settled at Kensworth, where they remained through the 1891 and 1901 censuses. Alfred died in 1917, probably at Kensworth. He does not appear in the Kensworth Find a Grave records ingested to date.

Alfred and Emma had five children: four sons and [[alice-dumpleton-1883|Alice Mabel]] (1883), their only daughter. Arthur Thomas Dumpleton (1879–1951), buried at Kensworth, is likely one of the four sons. See [[dumpleton-hucklesby]].
