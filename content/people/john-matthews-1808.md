John Matthews was christened 28 February 1808 at Kensworth, Hertfordshire, the fourth known child of [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews]] and [[mary-wilson-1779|Mary Wilson]]. He was the last of the children christened in Kensworth before the family moved to Whipsnade.

He appears in the **1841 census** at Houchen End, Whipsnade, aged 30 (birth rounded, christening record gives 1808). He was still living at home with his parents and his younger brother Daniel (then 20). By 1851 he had left — only William remained in the parental household at that census.

He **died in 1849 and was buried at St Mary Magdalene, Whipsnade** — confirmed by both the National Burial Index for England & Wales and the Bedfordshire Burials index (burial year recorded as 1849, birth given as 1809, a minor rounding variant consistent with the 1808 christening). He died between the two censuses, which explains his absence in 1851. He never emigrated.
