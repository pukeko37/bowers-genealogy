Alice Norman was born in 1890, the fifth child of [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] and [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen Birchmore]]. She was 17 when her father died in 1907.

Alice married Isaac Mason (1882–1955) in 1918, forming the [[mason-norman-1918|Mason-Norman family]]. Isaac was the youngest of the orphaned Mason brothers from Hosier Lane — and the brother of [[mabel-annie-norman-1883|Mabel's]] husband John. Brothers married sisters. Isaac ran a newsagent at 4 Yukon Road, Balham. They had one son, George R Mason (b. 1919).

Alice and Isaac took in her aunt Elizabeth Birchmore (1862–1954), Ellen's sister, who lived with them for over 33 years. In the 1939 Register, the household at Yukon Road was Alice, Isaac, and Elizabeth (then aged 77).

Isaac died in 1955. Alice was widowed and eventually moved to Kent, where she was cared for by her nephew George Stewart Main. Alice died in 1980 in Kent, aged 90.
