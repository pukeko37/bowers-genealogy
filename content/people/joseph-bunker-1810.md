Joseph Bunker (born 20 May 1810, Alwalton, Huntingdonshire) was the second child of [[joel-bunker-1789|Joel Bunker]] and [[martha-taylor-1786|Martha Taylor]]. His birth in Huntingdonshire — near Peterborough, about 50 miles north of Millbrook — shows the family were still on the move sixteen months after their January 1809 marriage at Millbrook. By December 1812 they had settled in Eversholt, Bedfordshire.

Joseph married **[[fanny-jakins|Fanny Jakins]]** on 7 March 1846 at Eversholt, where his father Joel was still running the Green Man Inn. Joel left Eversholt just six months later. Joseph's subsequent life and death are not yet documented.
