Richard Bunker was born on 19 December 1859 in Millbrook, the eldest child of [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker (1840–1914)]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]]. He was christened 4 March 1860 at Millbrook and registered at Ampthill.

He grew up in Millbrook, appearing in the household in both the 1861 and 1871 censuses. By 1891 he had migrated to the South Normanton area of Derbyshire — part of a broader movement of the Bunker brothers north to the coalfields during the late 1880s (see [[south-normanton]]).

**1882**: Richard married **Emma Page** on 27 March 1882 at Millbrook. Their first child, Harold William *Page* Bunker, had already been born in 1881 — a pre-marital child acknowledged in the "Page" middle name drawn from Emma's maiden name. They went on to have nine more children between 1883 and 1901.

By 1901 Richard had returned to Bedfordshire, living at Flitwick (about eight miles from Millbrook), and was still there in 1911. The reason for the return from Derbyshire is not documented.

Of his ten children, two sons died in the First World War: Harold William Page Bunker (1881–1917) and Joseph Bunker (1894–1917).

His date of death is unknown; he appears as "Deceased" in FamilySearch without a date.
