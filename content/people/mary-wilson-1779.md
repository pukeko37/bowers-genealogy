Mary Wilson was born 21 December 1779, most likely in Stanbridge, Bedfordshire (1851 census). She married [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews]] on 3 June 1801. They lived at Houchen End / By Whipsnade Green, Whipsnade, through at least 1851. Her death date is unknown.

### Children

The FamilySearch sources page reveals seven children confirmed by christening records, plus at least two others (Susan and Able) from separate sources — nine in total, consistent with family tradition of "at least nine":

- [[susan-matthews-1800|Susan Matthews]] (b. Sep 1800, before the formal marriage)
- [[sarah-matthews-1803|Sarah Matthews]] (christened 30 Jan 1803, Kensworth)
- [[william-matthews-1806|William Matthews]] (christened 20 Apr 1806, Kensworth)
- [[john-matthews-1808|John Matthews]] (christened 28 Feb 1808, Kensworth)
- [[able-matthews-1813|Able Matthews]] (b. 18 Apr 1813)
- [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] (christened 7 May 1815, Whipsnade)
- [[david-matthews-1817|David Matthews]] (christened 12 Jun 1817, Whipsnade)
- [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis Matthews]] (christened 17 Dec 1820, Whipsnade)
- [[charlotte-matthews-1823|Charlotte Matthews]] (christened 29 Jun 1823, Whipsnade)

### Census records

**1841** (Houchen End, Whipsnade): Thomas (70), Mary (60), John (30), Daniel (20).

**1851** (Whipsnade): Thomas (84, Pauper Ag Lab), Mary (70, Wife), William (45, Son). The 1851 census gives Mary's birthplace as Stanbridge, Bedfordshire.

### LDS baptism

Mary was baptised into the LDS church on **4 January 1851**, Hensworth Branch, British Mission — one month before the 1851 census. Her LDS membership record gives her birthplace as "Kensworth, Beds" which conflicts with the census entry of Stanbridge. The census is generally more reliable; the LDS record may be in error, or may have conflated Thomas's birthplace (Kensworth) with hers.

She appears in the 1851 census in Whipsnade, confirming she had not emigrated by that point. Whether she later followed sons Able and Daniel Willis to Utah, or remained with Thomas until his 1862 death, is not known. No death record has been found.

### Notes on birthplace

Two sources disagree:
- **Stanbridge, Bedfordshire** — 1851 census (more reliable)
- **Kensworth, Beds, England** — LDS membership record (possibly in error, or "Beds" misapplied to what is really Herts)
