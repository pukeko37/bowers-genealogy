Richard Tabraham was born before February 1807 in Elsworth, Cambridgeshire, and christened there on 1 February 1807. He died on 26 February 1874, also in Elsworth, having lived his life within the Elsworth/Fenstanton area of Cambridgeshire.

He married [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary Eusden]] on 3 August 1828 in Fen Stanton, forming the [[tabraham-eusden-1828|Tabraham-Eusden family]]. Their children were Ann (1829), Susan (1831), John (1836), and George Richard (1840). Mary died around 1841, leaving Richard to raise their four children alone in Fenstanton, where he lived through all subsequent census years: 1841, 1851, and 1871. His address was Bell Lane, Fenstanton.

His eldest daughter [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann]] had married Thomas Norman and moved to Swavesey by 1851. Richard died in 1874, the year his granddaughter the future London Norman family was taking shape — his grandson [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] was 14 years old when Richard died.

### Note on the surname

Richard appears in records as both "Tabraham" (christening, FamilySearch primary) and "Tabram" (alternate). The burial record shows an anomalous date of 29 January 1874, which precedes his death of 26 February 1874 — almost certainly a transcription error, likely 29 March 1874.
