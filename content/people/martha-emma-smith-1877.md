Martha Emma Smith was born in 1877 and died in 1940 — outliving her husband [[herbert-thomas-barnsley-bunker-1875|Herbert]] by 23 years. She married Herbert in 1901 at Kings Norton, Worcestershire, and had three children: [[sybil-may-bunker-1901|Sybil May]] (1901), [[herbert-lawrence-bunker-1905|Herbert Lawrence]] (1905), and [[grace-bunker-1909|Grace]] (1909). After Herbert's death in 1917 she was a widow for 23 years. See [[bunker-smith-1901]].
