David Bunker was born in 1837 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire (registered at Ampthill), the fourth child of [[james-bunker-1807|James Bunker]] and Elizabeth Watts. He died in 1920 in Peterborough, aged ~83.

Unlike his siblings who went to Bedford or Derbyshire, David moved northeast — to Huntingdonshire by 1871 and then to Peterborough, where he remained for the rest of his life (recorded there 1881–1911). He was long-lived, dying in 1920. He was Caleb's elder brother.
