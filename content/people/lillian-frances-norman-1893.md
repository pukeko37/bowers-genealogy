Lillian Frances Norman was born in 1893, the sixth child of [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] and [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen Birchmore]]. She was 14 when her father died in 1907.

Lillian never married. She worked as a clerk (1911) and later as a bookkeeper. In 1921 she was living at 143 De Beauvoir Road with her mother Ellen, sister Elizabeth Ann, and brother Charles Jr. By 1939 she had moved to Salisbury, Wiltshire — over 80 miles from London — living in a boarding house with other bookkeepers. She was the only sibling besides [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy]] to leave the London area.

During WW2, Lillian stayed at 32 Park Hill, Harpenden with Ivy and Roland, keeping house. Ivy loved this period of her life.

Lillian died in 1968 in Wandsworth, aged 75.
