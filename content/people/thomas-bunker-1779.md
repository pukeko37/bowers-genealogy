Thomas Bunker (1779–1849) was the second son of [[james-bunker-1749|James Bunker (1749–1818)]] and Elizabeth Pedder, and a grandson of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and Mary Reade. He was the replacement Thomas after an elder brother of the same name died in infancy in 1778.

He married **Mary Burton** (born 1785) at an unestablished date, probably around 1810–1815. Their son [[samuel-bunker-1818|Samuel Bunker]] (born ~1818) was born in Steppingley, Bedfordshire, showing that Thomas had moved from Millbrook (his family of origin) to Steppingley by the time of Samuel's birth. The reason for this migration is not documented; the Steppingley Bunker community (from the [[bunker-peet-1768|William 1740 line]]) may have provided a connection.

Thomas died 1849. No further details of his life are documented.

His son Samuel's family — the children of Samuel Bunker and Hannah Sear — are what made Steppingley the Bunker family home for the next generation. See [[bunker-sear-1840]] and [[ruth-bunker-1840]].
