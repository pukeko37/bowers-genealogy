Jacqueline Lucy Reynolds was born in 1930. She studied at Liverpool University (graduating around 1951), where she met [[alan-geoffrey-bowers-1928|Alan Bowers]]. She then took a post-graduate certificate in Child Care at the London School of Economics (1951–1952), during which she lived with Alan's parents Ivy and Roland at Journey's end, Harpenden — commuting 30 miles to London.

Jacqueline worked as a social worker. She and Alan married in 1954 at Newcastle-under-Lyme, forming the [[bowers-reynolds-1954|Bowers-Reynolds family]]. She worked at Worcester County Council's Child Care service while Alan trained at Worcester Royal Infirmary.

In 1957, the family emigrated to Auckland, New Zealand. The passage was arranged through Jacqueline's relative Laurie Copeland, a captain with the Port Line ships on the England–New Zealand trade route. Alan worked as ship's doctor, paying for one fare.

Their children: Andrew Nicholas Bowers (b. 1959), Richard Ian Bowers (b. 1962), and Philippa Jane Bowers (b. 1964). Jacqueline died in 2014, aged 84.
