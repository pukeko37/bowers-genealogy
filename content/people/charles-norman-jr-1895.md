Charles Norman Jr was born in 1895, the seventh child of [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] and [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen Birchmore]], named after his father. He was 12 when his father died in 1907.

Charles Jr married Irene Constance Nellie Isard in 1919. He worked as a clerk at the Port of London Authority and as a refrigerated storekeeper.

In 1921 he was living at 143 De Beauvoir Road with his wife, his mother Ellen, and sisters Elizabeth Ann and Lillian. He died in 1955 in Worthing, Sussex, aged 60, and is buried at Littlehampton Cemetery, West Sussex.
