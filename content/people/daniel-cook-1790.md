Daniel Cook was born in March 1790 in Whipsnade, Bedfordshire (christened 11 April 1790). He was an agricultural labourer his whole working life, appearing as such in the 1841 and 1851 census records in Whipsnade. He married [[mary-birchmore-1785|Mary Birchmore]] on 12 October 1810. Their seven children were [[sarah-cook-1811|Sarah]] (1811), [[james-cook-1813|James]] (1813), [[george-cook-1815|George]] (1815), [[david-cook-1816|David]] (1816), [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth]] (1820), [[jesse-cook-1821|Jesse]] (1821), and [[lois-cook-1827|Lois]] (1827).

Daniel died in 1857 in Flamstead, Hertfordshire — the adjacent parish to Whipsnade, suggesting he had moved in later life. His daughter Ruth (then 37) and her children were in Whipsnade at this point.
