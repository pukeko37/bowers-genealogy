Mathilde Elizabeth Parent (1853–1892), known as "Tillie," was the first wife of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]]. She was born in Canada, likely in Sandwich, Ontario — where at least one of her children was born and where she returned during the 1879–1884 period to care for her father.

She married William on 14 May 1873 in Chicago, Illinois, at the First Congregational Church. The marriage certificate names her as "Tillie E. Parent." By 1876 the couple had settled in Bedfordshire, England, where their son Louis Albert was born and christened.

Around 1879–1881 Tillie returned to Canada, taking young Louis Albert with her, leaving William and eldest son George Allen in Bedfordshire. She came back to England before 1884 (when Clarence was born at Houghton Regis), and the 1891 census shows her at Totternhoe, Bedfordshire, as William's wife, age 38, born "Sandwich, Canada Ont."

Tillie died in 1892, aged approximately 39. William married [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] two years later. The four children she had with William were: [[george-allen-thody-1874|George Allen]] (1874), [[louis-albert-thody-1876|Louis Albert]] (1876), [[clarence-james-thody-1884|Clarence James]] (1884), and [[lena-florence-thody-1889|Lena Florence]] (1889).

### Notes

- The 1877 christening record for Louis Albert lists the mother simply as "Matilda" — this is Mathilde/Tillie, not Matilda Nash. The name similarity between Tillie (Mathilde) and Matilda Nash has caused confusion in some sources.
- No death record or burial place found. She died 1892, probably in Bedfordshire.
