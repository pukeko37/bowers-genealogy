Charles Norman (1900–1987), eleventh and youngest child of [[richard-norman-1853|Richard Norman]] and [[ann-elizabeth-randall-1854|Ann Elizabeth Randall]]; born West Hartlepool. The third son his parents named Charles — the first two died young. He survived to 87, the most long-lived of the eleven children. No further records traced.
