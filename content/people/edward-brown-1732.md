Edward Brown (born about 1732, Cranfield, Bedfordshire) is the father of [[lydia-brown-1769|Lydia Brown (1769–1842)]], who married [[james-thomas-bunker-1764|James Thomas Bunker]] in 1788. He is Roland Bunker's great-great-great-grandfather on the Lydia Brown line.

He married **Lydia** (born about 1736, maiden name unknown). All their children were baptised at Cranfield parish church:

The two daughters named Lydia reflect the common practice of reusing a name after a child's death. The 1765 Lydia (K8SK-JJX) left no further record; the 1769 Lydia is our direct ancestor.

His death date and burial place are not recorded in the sources. Nothing further is known about Edward Brown beyond the children's baptism records.
