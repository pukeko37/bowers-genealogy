William Toll (also recorded as Tall) was born on **10 October 1830** in **Maulden, Bedfordshire** — the second child of [[edward-tall-1806|Edward Tall]] (1806–1870) and [[sarah-ward-1806|Sarah Ward]] (1806–1894), born three years after their 1827 marriage. He was a carpenter, the same trade as his father.

**Name spelling:** The FamilySearch record registers him as "William Toll." The GRO index has TOLE; FreeBMD has TOLL; the Foster Hill Road burial register and Howard Chapel register both have TALL. Tall and Toll are used interchangeably in the records — including within his own children's entries (Mary Ann *Tall*, Harriet *Toll*, Susan *Tall*).

**Residence:** 1851 Maulden (with family) · 1861 **St Pancras, Middlesex, London** (Swinton Street) · died 1862 at Derby Street, St Peter's, Bedford. By 1861 William had moved to London — specifically St Pancras, placing him in the same part of London where the Norman-Birchmore and GBP Jr families would later be centred, though a generation earlier and without documented connection.

**Marriage:** William married **Fanny Webb** on **4 January 1852** at Hitchin, Hertfordshire. Their three children:
- **Mary Ann Tall** (1853–1928)
- **Harriet Toll** (1859–1945)
- **Susan Tall** (1862–1867) — born the year William died; died aged five

Fanny was widowed in 1862 and later remarried **William Ansell** (registered Hitchin, Q4 1872).

**Death:** William died 10 July 1862, aged 31, at Derby Street, St Peter's, Bedford — having apparently returned from London. Buried 14 July 1862, **Foster Hill Road Cemetery, Bedford**. Howard Chapel register.

**Siblings:** [[mary-ward-tall-1827|Mary Ward Tall]] (1827), [James Toll (1832–1833)], [Susan Toll (1837–1848)], [[rebecca-tall-1844|Rebecca Tall]] (1844), [[frederick-charles-tall-1846|Frederick Charles Tall]] (1847). Three siblings died in childhood; Rebecca died aged 26 in 1870. William predeceased his father by 8 years and his sister Rebecca by 8 years.
