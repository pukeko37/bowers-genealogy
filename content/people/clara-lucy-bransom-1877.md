Clara Lucy Bransom (14 September 1877 – 1957) was the fourth child of [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]]. She was born at Whipsnade and christened on 2 December 1877 at the Wesleyan Methodist chapel, Dunstable.

## Life

The 1901 census finds her as "Clara L Bransom," aged 23, working as a General Servant Domestic in the household of Walter Lester (Watchmaker) in Dunstable.

By 1911 she was living as "sister-in-law" in the household of [[arthur-perry-1866|Arthur Perry]] in Dunstable. This is straightforwardly explained by the fact that her sister [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]] had married Arthur Perry in 1898: Clara was Florence's sister and therefore Arthur's sister-in-law. Clara was working as a **Book Sewer** at Watertow & Co Printing Works in Dunstable, and was presumably lodging with her brother-in-law while Florence was temporarily back at their parents' home in Whipsnade.

Clara married Cyril John Baldock in 1912 at Luton. She died in 1957 as "Clara L Baldock" in Luton, aged 79.
