Clara Lucy Bransom (14 September 1877 – 1957) was the fourth child of [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]]. She was born at Whipsnade and christened on 2 December 1877 at the Wesleyan Methodist chapel, Dunstable.

## Life

The 1901 census finds her as "Clara L Bransom," aged 23, working as a General Servant Domestic in the household of Walter Lester (Watchmaker) in Dunstable. By 1911 she appears as "Clara Branson," aged 33, working as a **Book Sewer** at Watertow & Co Printing Works in Dunstable. In 1911 she was living as "sister-in-law" in the household of Arthur Perry in Dunstable — suggesting a sibling had married into the Perry family. Her sister [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]] is similarly connected to a Perry in 1911, and the 1911 census shows Florence living with their parents as "Florence Perry Bransone," which may indicate a more complex family arrangement or that the Perry connection was a marriage by Florence rather than another sibling.

Clara married Cyril John Baldock in 1912 at Luton. She died in 1957 as "Clara L Baldock" in Luton, aged 79.
