Mary Elizabeth Angus was born 11 November 1885 in Little Thurlow, Suffolk. By 1891 the family had moved to West Ham, Essex, and by 1911 Mary was in Kingsland, London — where she met and married [[thomas-george-norman-1886|Thomas George Norman]].

Mary and Thomas married on 19 August 1911 at DeBeauvoir Town, Middlesex, forming the [[norman-angus-1911|Norman-Angus family]]. Their one child was Gwendoline May Norman (1915–2000).

The family lived at 106 Hertford Road, Hackney (1921) and 6 Englefield Road, Kingsland (1939). During WW2, their house at Englefield Road was bombed. The family moved into Thomas's baker's shop in Dalston to live.

Thomas died in 1949. Mary continued in the Hackney area. She outlived her husband by twenty years, dying in 1969 in Hackney, aged 83.

### Note

Mary's origins in rural Suffolk, via West Ham, are a reminder that London's East End population in this period was substantially drawn from East Anglia.
