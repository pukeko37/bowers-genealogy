David Matthews was born in 1853 at Kensworth, Hertfordshire. He is the "David Matthews (8)" recorded in [[thomas-matthews-1797|Thomas Matthews the younger]]'s 1861 household at Kensworth — his relationship to Thomas was not specified in the census, suggesting he was a grandchild, nephew, or other dependent rather than a direct son (Thomas was then ~63 and his wife Elizabeth ~66).

David was still at Kensworth in 1871. By 1876 he had married **Letitia** (born 1855, also at Kensworth), and in the 1881 census the household at Kensworth comprised David (28), Letitia (26), Louisa (4, born 1877), and Edwin (infant, born 1881). The 1891 census also places David and Letitia at Kensworth, with son Edwin (10). The family moved to **Luton** between 1891 and 1901, where David and Letitia appear in the 1901 and 1911 censuses.

Their son **Edwin Matthews** (christened 13 February 1881, Kensworth) married **Annie C.** and by 1911 was living at Luton with children Leslie E., Gladys E., and Constance N. Matthews. Edwin and Annie also appear in the 1911 Dunstable census with daughter Ethel Annie (born 20 February 1905, Kensworth — christened 16 April 1905).

David represents the last known Kensworth-born branch of the [[thomas-matthews-1797|Thomas Matthews the younger]] generation to remain in the immediate area before eventually dispersing to Luton.
