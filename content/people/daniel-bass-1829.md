Daniel Bass was born in **1829** in **Maulden, Bedfordshire** — the 1861 census records him aged 31 with birthplace Maulden. He worked as a **labourer**. He married [[mary-ward-tall-1827|Mary Ward Tall]] in October 1855 in Maulden, Bedfordshire, and they had three children: [[sarah-bass-1859|Sarah Bass]] (1859–1951), Florence Bass (1862), and Edward Ernest Bass (1864–1936). He died in 1868, aged ~39, leaving Mary a widow with three young children. His death year coincides with [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]] and [[rebecca-tall-1844|Rebecca Tall]]'s wedding at Howard Chapel, Bedford.

His origins beyond Maulden — parents, siblings, cause of death — are not yet established.
