Mary Ann Nash (c. 1880–1963) was the illegitimate daughter of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] and [[matilda-nash-1858|Matilda Nash]]. She was born in Kentish Town, London, c. 1880 — while William's wife [[mathilde-parent-1853|Mathilde Parent]] was temporarily in Canada.

In the 1891 census she appears in the Thody household at Totternhoe, Bedfordshire, as "Minnie Thody," aged 10, scholar, born Kentish Town, London. She is listed as "Niece [illegitimate daughter]" — a census enumerator's clarification.

She was born with her mother's surname Nash, but by 1891 was living as part of the Thody family, addressed as Thody. No further records of her life have been found.
