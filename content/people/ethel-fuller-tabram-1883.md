Ethel Fuller Tabram (born 1883, Fenstanton), second child of [[john-tabram-1861|John Tabram]] and [[adeliza-fuller-1862|Adeliza Fuller]]. Her middle name carries her mother's maiden name. She married a Lomas and appears in both parents' probate records (1938 and 1939) as executor — "Ethel Fuller Lomas." Her husband's first name is not recorded.
