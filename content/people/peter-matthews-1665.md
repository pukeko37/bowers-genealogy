Peter Matthews was born in 1665 in Bedford, Bedfordshire, and died in 1730 at Dunstable. He married **[[mary-jenkins-1665|Mary Jenkins]]** (1665–1748); no marriage date or place is recorded in available sources. They had at least seven children, all christened at Dunstable, including [[peter-matthews-1702|Peter Matthews (1702)]], who continued the line.

His own parentage is unknown — he is the earliest documented ancestor on this branch.
