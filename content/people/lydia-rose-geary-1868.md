Lydia Rose Geary was born in 1868 in Haynes, Bedfordshire — though her christening was not registered until 11 May 1873, an unusually late five years after her birth. She was the tenth of eleven children of [[john-geary-1825|John Geary]] (1825–1895) and [[lucy-morris-1826|Lucy Morris]] (1826–1895). She died December 1932 in Bedford and was buried at **Foster Hill Road Cemetery, Bedford**, the same cemetery as her sister [[phoebe-geary-1854|Phoebe]].

In 1871, aged 2, she appears at the parental home in Haynes. In 1881, aged 12, she is a Scholar at the family home at The Warren, Haynes, alongside brothers Albert (14) and Ernest (11).

**1911 census — caring for the Southgate family:** A year after the death of her sister [[phoebe-geary-1854|Phoebe]] (d. January 1910), Lydia is found in Bedford as "Aunt" in the household of **Walter Berta Southgate** (age 30, cab driver) — Phoebe's eldest son Bertie. The household also includes Phoebe's younger daughters May Southgate (16) and Amy Emma Southgate (13). Lydia appears to have moved in to help Bertie care for his younger siblings after their mother's death. With her is her son, **Jaspers Fred Geary** (9, born Luton), whose father's identity remains unknown.

**1921 census:** Lydia Rose is recorded at **12 Gordon Road, Southend-on-Sea**, recorded as widowed, aged 51 (born Haynes). She is there with her sister [[eliza-geary-1859|Eliza Bunker]] (née Geary) and her son Jasper Fred Geary (born Luton 1902, railway fireman). The occasion appears to have been a seaside holiday — Eliza was married and based in Luton.

No marriage record for Lydia has been found. In 1911 she gave her status as **Single** — the legally accurate status for an unmarried mother — despite having a nine-year-old son. By 1921 she was describing herself as **Widowed**, almost certainly a social fiction adopted to give respectability. This was a common Edwardian practice for women with illegitimate children who needed to account for the absence of a husband. Jasper carried her maiden name "Geary" throughout his life, not a married surname, which further points against any intervening marriage. There is no husband.

**Death:** December 1932, Bedfordshire. The death registration records her age as 63 (birth year ~1869, consistent with the 1868 birth given the late christening).

Lydia Rose died in 1932, the same year as her brother-in-law [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick Bunker]].
