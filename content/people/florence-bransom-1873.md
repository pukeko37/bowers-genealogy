Florence Bransom (20 September 1873 – June 1963) was the second child of [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] (1850–1928) and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]] (1848–1937), and thus the niece of [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]]. She was born at Whipsnade and christened on 5 November 1873 at the Wesleyan Methodist chapel, Dunstable.

## Life

Florence appears first in the historical record as "Florance Bransome," one of two witnesses at the 1894 marriage of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] and [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] at Whipsnade Parish Church. She was about 20. The other witness was [[walter-cook-1869|Walter Cook]], her first cousin through the Bransom family.

By 1891 she had moved from Whipsnade to Dunstable. In 1898 she married [[arthur-perry-1866|Arthur Perry]] — a Railway Porter from Harlington — at Luton. Their family is recorded at [[perry-bransom-1898]].

The 1901 census finds Florence with Arthur in Dunstable, age 27, with their infant son Percy (age 1). Three children in total are recorded: [[percy-arthur-perry-1900|Percy Arthur Perry]] (1900), [[florence-hilda-perry-1903|Florence Hilda Perry]] (1903), and [[elsie-perry-1907|Elsie Perry]] (1907).

The 1911 census presents an unexpected arrangement. Florence is listed back at her parents' household in Whipsnade as "Florce Perry Bransone," age 37, with daughter Florence Hilda (age 8). Meanwhile Arthur remained in Dunstable with Percy (11) and Elsie (4), and Florence's sister [[clara-lucy-bransom-1877|Clara Lucy]] was living there as his sister-in-law. Why the household split this way is not clear from available records.

Florence died in June 1963 in Luton, Bedfordshire, aged approximately 89.
