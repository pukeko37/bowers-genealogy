Florence Bransom (20 September 1873 – 1963) was the daughter of [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] (1850–1928) and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]] (1848–1937), and thus the niece of [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]]. She was born at Whipsnade and christened on 5 November 1873 at the Wesleyan Methodist chapel, Dunstable.

She appears in the historical record as "Florance Bransome," one of two witnesses at the marriage of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] and [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] at Whipsnade Parish Church on 6 February 1894. She was about 20. The other witness was [[walter-cook-1869|Walter Cook]] — the son of her uncle [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook (1843–1908)]]'s wife [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois Brandsom]], making Walter and Florence first cousins through the Bransom family.

By 1911 Florence was living in the household of her parents, listed as "Florce Perry Bransone," age 37 — evidently married or partnered with someone named Perry. Also in the household was her daughter, Florence Hilda Perry Bransone (age 8). Her brother [[walter-william-bransom-1881|Walter William]] and his wife may have had a related Perry connection; the 1911 census shows their sister [[clara-lucy-bransom-1877|Clara Lucy]] living as "sister-in-law" in the household of Arthur Perry in Dunstable — suggesting the Perry connection ran across multiple Bransom siblings. Florence's surname in later records has not been confirmed.

Florence lived to approximately 90 years old, dying in 1963.
