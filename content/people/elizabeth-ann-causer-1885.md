Elizabeth Ann Causer (19 June 1885–1974), eldest child of [[mary-ann-norman-1861|Mary Ann Norman]] and [[george-causer-1859|George Causer]]; born Swavesey. Lived with her widowed mother in Swavesey through at least 1939, never married; her 1939 register occupation is recorded as "Private Means." Died 1974.
