Rachael Bunker (born 21 January 1824, christened 15 April 1824 at Millbrook) was the daughter of a **William Bunker** and his wife **Sarah**, as recorded in the Millbrook christening register. The most probable identification of her parents is [[william-bunker-1767|William Bunker (1767)]] and his second wife Sarah Beal — which would make her a younger sister of Samuel (~1820) — but this is inferred, not confirmed.

In 1841 she was at **Village Great Farm, Millbrook**, in the household of the Green family, almost certainly in domestic service. She was not in James Bunker (1777)'s 1841 household, contrary to what the Millbrook page previously implied.

Her subsequent life after 1851 is not yet documented.
