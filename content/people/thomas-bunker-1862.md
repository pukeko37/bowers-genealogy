Thomas Bunker was born in 1862 in Millbrook, the second child of [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker (1840–1914)]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]]. He was christened 20 April 1862 at Millbrook.

He was at home in Millbrook in both the 1871 and 1881 censuses. By 1891 he had moved to Nottinghamshire — part of the wider migration of the elder Bunker brothers to the coalfields — and by 1901 he was in South Normanton, Derbyshire (see [[south-normanton]]).

**1885**: On Christmas Day 1885, Thomas married **Charlotte Roberts** at Moggerhanger, Bedfordshire. His younger brother Albert would marry an Emma Roberts at the same parish two years later, suggesting the Roberts women were sisters. No children of Thomas and Charlotte are documented.

Thomas died in early 1902 in South Normanton, aged approximately 40. He was buried there on 17 April 1902.
