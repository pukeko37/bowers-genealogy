Sarah Garner was the daughter of **William Garner Sr.** (1794–1862) and **Kitty Perry** (1796–1843), per the Find a Grave memorial. Kitty Perry died in 1843, when Sarah was ~21. She was christened **10 March 1822** in Caddington, Hertfordshire — a village on the Hertfordshire side of the county boundary near Whipsnade and Kensworth, where the Matthews family also had roots. (An earlier source gave 13 February 1822 as birth date; the christening date of 10 March 1822 is from the FamilySearch record.) Caddington is less than five miles from Whipsnade, making it likely that Sarah and [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis Matthews]] knew each other from the local community before their marriage.

She married Daniel Willis Matthews on **9 October 1842** at Kensworth, Hertfordshire. Both she and Daniel were baptised into the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints on **12 September 1846**, together at the Bedfordshire branch of the British Mission. Their LDS membership records give a "marriage date" of 10 June 1847, which is likely the date of their LDS ecclesiastical sealing rather than the civil registration.

The 1851 census records Sarah as aged 29, born "Waddside, Bedfordshire" — a census transcription of her Caddington birthplace (which straddles the Herts/Beds border and was sometimes attributed to Bedfordshire in administrative records).

Children known from christening records and other sources:
- **Eliza Matthews** (christened 9 Jul 1843, Whipsnade; later Eliza Mathews Taylor, died Tooele, Utah 1930)
- **Sarah Ann Matthews** (born 11 May 1844; died 1853 in Nebraska on the trail crossing)
- **Ann Matthews** (born ~1845, Whipsnade)
- **Mary Matthews** (born ~1849, Whipsnade)
- **Emma Matthews** (born 29 April 1851; died 1853 on the trail crossing, buried Pioneer Children's Memorial, Salt Lake City)

**Sarah emigrated with Daniel and the children in June 1853**, named in the Cyrus H. Wheelock Company records in the division under Captain Kendall alongside Daniel (32), Eliza (10), Sarah Ann (9), Mary (4), and Emma (2). She was aged 31 at departure.

**She died circa October 1853, on the trail, and never reached Salt Lake City.** This is confirmed by the FamilySearch record (death date: circa 1853 October) and specifically mentioned in the obituary of her daughter Eliza Matthews Taylor, who survived the crossing. Two of her daughters — Sarah Ann (9) and Emma (2) — also died during the same crossing. Of the five people in the family division, only Eliza (10) and Mary (4) survived to reach Utah.
