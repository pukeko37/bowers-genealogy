Samuel Fensome (abt 1841–1920) was a Bedfordshire man who married [[phoebe-alice-bunker-1843|Phoebe Alice Bunker]] in 1863. He was born in **Ravensden, Bedfordshire** — a small parish immediately north of Bedford — and was christened there on 31 May 1841. FamilySearch records his birth year as 1842 (likely from a census age rounding), but the christening and birth registration records give 1841.

**Origins.** His parents were **Thomas Fensom** (1813–1884) and **Sarah Barrick** (1821–?), who married in Ravensden on 12 August 1839. He had three known siblings: William Fensom (1845), Mary Ann Fensom (1847), and James Fensome (1850). The surname is spelled variously as "Fensom" and "Fensome" across records. In 1851 Samuel was still at home in Ravensden aged about 10.

**Marriage.** A marriage notice was published on 26 July 1863 in Bedford, and Samuel married **[[phoebe-alice-bunker-1843|Phoebe Alice Bunker]]** on 16 August 1863 at Saint Paul, Bedford. She was the daughter of the recently widowed Elizabeth Watts (née Bunker), living at Dane Street in Saint Paul's parish. Samuel would have been about 22, Phoebe 20. See [[bunker-fensome-1863]].

**Family and migration.** The couple remained in Bedford through at least 1873, where their first four children were born — Herbert Charles (1866), Edgar (1868), Alice Jane (1872), and Reginald Edwin (1873). Their fifth child, Sidney Thomas, was born in Pilsley in 1877, placing the migration **between 1873 and 1877**. They settled in **Pilsley, Derbyshire** for two decades, where four more children were born. The move to Pilsley placed them immediately next to New Tupton, where Phoebe's brothers [[george-bunker-1836|George Bunker]] and [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]] had family at the same period. Whether the Fensome migration followed the Bunkers to Derbyshire, or whether Samuel had independent reasons for going to Pilsley (coal-mining work is plausible), is not documented.

**Later years.** By 1911 Samuel and Phoebe had moved to **Hucknall Torkard, Nottinghamshire**, just across the county boundary from Pilsley. Samuel died in 1920 in **Mansfield, Nottinghamshire** — about ten miles north of Hucknall — probably in the area where at least one of their children was settled. Phoebe survived him by three years, dying in Yorkshire in 1923.

**Occupation:** Not recorded in current sources. Given the Pilsley area's coal-mining character, labouring work in the collieries is possible, but unconfirmed.

**Residence history:** Ravensden 1841–1851 · Bedford 1851–c.1875 · Pilsley, Derbyshire c.1875–1901 · Hucknall Torkard, Nottinghamshire by 1911 · died Mansfield 1920
