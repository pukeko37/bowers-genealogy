John Chapman (born c.1817–1821, Bedfordshire) married [[rebecca-bunker-1815|Rebecca Bunker]] on **8 June 1835** at Millbrook. He appears in the 1841 census aged 20 at Village Sand End, Millbrook, in his father-in-law James Bunker (1777)'s household. His origins are not documented. See [[chapman-bunker-1835]].
