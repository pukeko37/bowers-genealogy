David Matthews was christened in 1817 at St Mary Magdalene, Whipsnade, Bedfordshire — and died the same year in infancy. Both the National Burial Index for England & Wales and the Bedfordshire Burials index record his burial at Whipsnade in 1817, the same year as his baptism. He was the sixth known child of [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews]] and [[mary-wilson-1779|Mary Wilson]], and never survived to childhood.

His absence from all later records — the 1841 and 1851 censuses — is fully explained by his infant death. He is not a research gap.
