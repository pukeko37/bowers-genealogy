Phoebe Alice Bunker was born in 1843 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire (registered Ampthill), the seventh child of [[james-bunker-1807|James Bunker]] and [[elizabeth-watts-1810|Elizabeth Watts]]. She was christened 23 April 1843 in Millbrook. She died in 1923 in Yorkshire, aged approximately 80.

**Millbrook and Bedford.** Phoebe grew up in Millbrook. In 1851 she is in the family household aged 8. By 1861 the family had moved to Dane Street, St Paul's parish, Bedford, following her father's death in 1852, and she and her sister [[martha-sarah-bunker-1841|Martha]] were both working as **bonnet sewers** — one of the few domestic trades available to young women in the Bedfordshire economy alongside lace making.

**Marriage.** On 16 August 1863 Phoebe married **[[samuel-fensome-1842|Samuel Fensome]]** (1842–1920) at Saint Paul, Bedford — her home parish. That same year her sister [[lydia-bunker-1833|Lydia]] married Samuel Plackett Lane, also in Bedford: two Bunker sisters married in the same year in the same town. Together Phoebe and Samuel had **eight children**: Herbert Charles (1866–1950), Edgar (1868–1953), Alice Jane (1872), Reginald Edwin (1873), Sidney Thomas (1877–1954), Percy Louis (1879–1965), Maud M (1882), and Florence Sarah (1884). See [[bunker-fensome-1863]].

**The Derbyshire migration.** By 1871 the young family was still in Bedford; their fourth child Reginald Edwin was born there in 1873. Their fifth child, Sidney Thomas, was born in Pilsley in 1877 — pinning the migration to **between 1873 and 1877**. They migrated to **Pilsley, Derbyshire** — the same move that brought her brother [[george-bunker-1836|George Bunker]] and his family to neighbouring New Tupton. Pilsley and New Tupton are adjacent villages; the migrations were almost certainly coordinated. Phoebe's brother [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb]] had already been in Matlock since 1871 (about 15 miles south), and around 1875 both George and Caleb had children born in New Tupton. The Fensome family settled in Pilsley and remained through 1881, 1891, and 1901 — two decades in the same Derbyshire village. See [[new-tupton-derbyshire]].

**Later years.** By 1911 Phoebe had moved from Pilsley to **Hucknall Torkard**, Nottinghamshire — just across the county boundary. Samuel Fensome died in 1920. Phoebe survived him by three years, dying in **Yorkshire** in 1923. How she came to be in Yorkshire at the end of her life is not recorded — possibly in the care of one of her children or their families.

**Residence history:** 1843–1852 Millbrook · 1852–1871 Bedford · 1871–1901 Pilsley, Derbyshire · by 1911 Hucknall Torkard, Nottinghamshire · died Yorkshire 1923
