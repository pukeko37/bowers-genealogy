Ann Geary was born in September 1851 in Haynes, Bedfordshire (christened 7 December 1851), the second surviving child of [[john-geary-1825|John Geary]] and [[lucy-morris-1826|Lucy Morris]]. She died around 1922; no death record has been found but the FamilySearch record places her death as "about 1922."

She grew up in Haynes working as a **straw platter** — recorded as such in the 1871 census, aged 19, still at the parental home. Before leaving Haynes she had an illegitimate son, **Francis Ernest Geary** (born ~1870, no father named in the christening record). Francis Ernest was christened at Haynes on 11 May 1873 — the same day as his aunt Lydia Rose Geary, both receiving a late christening together. He was raised in his grandparents' household: the 1871 census records him as "Ernest Geary," age 1, in John and Lucy's home, and the 1881 census again shows "Ernest Geary," age 11, still with them. After 1881 he left the grandparents' household and moved away from Bedfordshire. He eventually settled in **Reading, Berkshire**, where he married Eva Maud Geary and died on 28 September 1936, aged 66. He does not appear in the Whitmore household censuses and remained separate from Ann's subsequent family. See [[francis-ernest-geary-1870]].

She married **William Whitmore** (born Bolnhurst, Bedfordshire, ~1852), a corn dealer's labourer (later a gardener), probably around 1876 in Bedford — the 1911 census records the marriage as 35 years' duration. They settled in Bedford's western wards and raised a family there. Known children include Phoebe (b.~1875), Florence Evelyn (b. Jun 1883), Sarah Jane (b. Dec 1886), Percy Edwin (b. Mar 1889), Edith May (b. Jan 1892), and Jeanie (b.~1887). Ann worked as a **charwoman** (1891 census).

Ann and William remained in Bedford through at least 1911. She was the sibling who stayed closest to the Bedford heartland rather than migrating south to Luton. Her children were christened at St Paul's, Bedford.
