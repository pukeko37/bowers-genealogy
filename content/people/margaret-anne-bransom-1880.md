Margaret Anne Bransom (1880–1964), fifth child of [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]]. She was born at Whipsnade and married [[james-hain-1877|James Hain]] (1877–1932) in December 1905 — the farmer at Dell Farm, Whipsnade.

The marriage kept her in the village where she had grown up, at Dell Farm just across the green from the Bransom family home By Whipsnade Green. Her younger sister [[minnie-rose-bransom-1884|Minnie Rose Bransom]] came to live with them as housekeeper, as recorded in the 1911 census.

She and James had two daughters: [[margaret-anne-hain-1907|Margaret Anne Hain]] (1907–1994) and [[dora-minnie-hain-1910|Dora Minnie Hain]] (1910–1996). On census day 1911 the baby Dora was visiting her Bransom grandparents — appearing in Edwin and Mary's household aged 0 as "Dora Minne Hain."

Margaret Anne outlived her husband by thirty-two years, dying in 1964.
