Sarah Bass was born in **July 1859** in Maulden, Bedfordshire — the eldest child of [[daniel-bass-1829|Daniel Bass]] (1829–1868) and [[mary-ward-tall-1827|Mary Ward Tall]] (1827–1907). She outlived virtually everyone in her generation: she died in **March 1951** in Luton, Bedfordshire, aged nearly 92.

## Childhood in Maulden

The 1861 census captures her as an infant of one, at Snow Hill, Maulden — her father Daniel (31, labourer) and mother Mary (33, stay maker) still together and the family newly formed. By 1871 her father had died, and the household had contracted: Mary (43, widowed, staymaker) was head, with Sarah (11), Florence (9), and Edward (7) at Duck End, Maulden. Sarah was listed as a scholar that year.

By 1881, at 21, she was a **dressmaker** — single, still living with her widowed mother in Maulden, alongside her younger brother Edward Ernest (17). Her mother had maintained the household as a stay maker for over a decade since Daniel's death.

## Marriage and Lavendon

In **April 1887** Sarah married **James Lay** (abt 1862/63–1931) at Ampthill, Bedfordshire — a stone mason from Lavendon, Buckinghamshire. The marriage moved her across the county border for the first time. They settled in Lavendon and remained there for the rest of their working lives. See [[lay-bass-1887]].

By 1891 she was running a **general shop** in Lavendon with James and their young son Hermann. The 1911 census records her as "assistant in shop" — the family business continuing two decades on, with James still working as a jobbing stone mason. They had been married 24 years by 1911 and had four children together.

## Her Mother's Last Years

In 1901 her widowed mother [[mary-ward-tall-1827|Mary Ward Tall]], now 73, moved to Lavendon to live with Sarah and James. She is listed as "mother-in-law" in the 1901 household — a natural arrangement for an aging widow, and a sign of the close bond between them. Mary died in Lavendon in April 1907.

## Children

Sarah and James Lay had four children, all born in Lavendon:
- Herman Vivian Lay (1888–1946)
- Beatrice Eva Lay (1890–date unknown)
- Gertrude Florence Lay (1892–1954)
- Oliver Cecil Lay (1894–1979)

## Long Life

Sarah outlived her husband James (died 1931) by twenty years, and died in **March 1951** in Luton, Bedfordshire — having witnessed the entirety of the Victorian, Edwardian, and both World War eras. She was the eldest child in the Bass-Tall family and the longest-lived of her generation: her mother died in 1907, her siblings earlier or at unknown dates.
