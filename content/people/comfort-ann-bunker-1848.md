Comfort Ann Bunker was born about November 1848 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire, the ninth and youngest child of [[james-bunker-1807|James Bunker]] and Elizabeth Watts. She was christened 19 November 1848 in Millbrook. Her death date is unknown; she was alive through the 1911 census. She was also known as Annie, Jane, and **Ann** — the last being the name given in the 1861 census, where she is listed simply as "Ann Bunker," aged 12, a scholar, at Dane Street, St Paul's parish, Bedford. The recorded birth year (~1849) is close to her actual birth of 1848.

She settled in Luton, Bedfordshire, where she worked as a **sewer of straw hats** — the characteristic trade of the town. She was consistently in Luton from at least 1881 through 1911. Together with her sister [[martha-sarah-bunker-1841|Martha Sarah]], she represents the Luton branch of the Bunker family that predates Frederick Bunker's settlement there.

Comfort was only four years old when her father died in 1852 and just three years younger than Caleb, her brother. She was his closest sibling in age and shared the Luton connection with him indirectly, through their sisters.
