Comfort Ann Bunker was born about November 1848 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire, the ninth and youngest child of [[james-bunker-1807|James Bunker]] and [[elizabeth-watts-1810|Elizabeth Watts]]. She was christened 19 November 1848 in Millbrook. Her death date is unknown; she was alive through the 1911 census.

**Names.** She was christened "Comfort Ann" but went by several names over her life:
- "Ann" in the 1861 census (aged 12, scholar, Dane Street Bedford)
- "Jane Bunker" in census records 1871–1891
- "Annie Bunker" when she married in 1892
- "Annie Ewington" in 1901 and "Jane Ewington" in 1911

**In the Sinfield household (1871–1891).** From 1871 Comfort Ann is recorded as "sister-in-law" in the household of **Joseph Sinfield** and his wife **Martha Sinfield** — confirmed as her sister [[martha-sarah-bunker-1841|Martha Sarah Bunker]], who married Sinfield on 25 May 1863 at St Paul's Bedford. When Joseph moved to Luton as a Police Constable by 1881, Comfort Ann moved with them, and she remained in the Sinfield household through 1891. She worked throughout as a **straw hat sewer** — the same trade as Martha throughout her life.

**Marriage.** On **28 March 1892**, aged 42, Comfort Ann married **Henry Ewington** at Luton, Bedfordshire, as "Annie Bunker" (single). Henry was a widower from Breachwood Green, Hertfordshire, born ~1852, occupation Bricklayer's Labourer. He had at least two children from his first marriage (Herbert W and Bertha Ewington). Together Comfort Ann and Henry remained in Luton at least through 1911.

Her story is one of long attachment to her sister's household before establishing her own late marriage. She waited four decades after leaving Bedford before she had a home of her own.

Comfort was only four years old when her father died in 1852, and just three years younger than Caleb — his closest sibling in age. She shares with [[martha-sarah-bunker-1841|Martha Sarah]] the status of Luton anchor for the Bunker family, predating [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick Bunker]]'s arrival there.

**Residence history:** 1848–1852 Millbrook · 1852–~1870 Bedford · 1871 Bedford (Sinfield household) · 1881–1911+ Luton
