Comfort Ann Bunker was born about November 1848 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire, the ninth and youngest child of [[james-bunker-1807|James Bunker]] and [[elizabeth-watts-1810|Elizabeth Watts]]. She was christened 19 November 1848 in Millbrook. She died in Luton in the **March quarter 1921**, aged 72, registered as **Comfort A. Ewington** (GRO vol 3b, page 434).

**Names.** She was christened "Comfort Ann" but went by several names over her life:
- "Ann" in the 1861 census (aged 12, scholar, Dane Street Bedford)
- "Jane Bunker" in census records 1871–1891
- "Annie Bunker" when she married in 1892
- "Annie Ewington" in 1901 and "Jane Ewington" in 1911

**In the Sinfield household (1871–1891).** From 1871 Comfort Ann is recorded as "sister-in-law" in the household of **Joseph Sinfield** and his wife **Martha Sinfield** — confirmed as her sister [[martha-sarah-bunker-1841|Martha Sarah Bunker]], who married Sinfield on 25 May 1863 at St Paul's Bedford. When Joseph moved to Luton as a Police Constable by 1881, Comfort Ann moved with them, and she remained in the Sinfield household through 1891. She worked throughout as a **straw hat sewer** — the same trade as Martha throughout her life.

**Marriage.** On **28 March 1892**, aged 42, Comfort Ann married **Henry Ewington** at Luton, Bedfordshire, as "Annie Bunker" (single). Henry was a widower from Breachwood Green, Hertfordshire, born ~1851, occupation Bricklayer's Labourer. He had at least two children from his first marriage (Herbert W and Bertha Ewington). Henry died in 1912 in Luton, aged 61 (GRO vol 3B, page 368), leaving Comfort Ann a widow. She survived him by nine years, dying in Luton in Q1 1921.

Her story is one of long attachment to her sister's household before establishing her own late marriage — and then outliving that husband too. She waited four decades after leaving Bedford to have a home of her own, and spent the last nine years of her life as a widow in the same town where her sister Martha had died in 1901.

Comfort was only four years old when her father died in 1852, and just three years younger than Caleb — his closest sibling in age. She shares with [[martha-sarah-bunker-1841|Martha Sarah]] the status of Luton anchor for the Bunker family, predating [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick Bunker]]'s arrival there.

**Residence history:** 1848–1852 Millbrook · 1852–~1870 Bedford · 1871 Bedford (Sinfield household) · 1881–1921 Luton
