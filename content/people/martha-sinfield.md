Martha Sinfield is known only through her marriage and her children's christening records. No birth or death record has been found for her.

She married **[[thomas-bunker-1738|Thomas Bunker]]** on 6 May 1761 at Millbrook, Bedfordshire. The marriage record renders her name as "Martha Sinfeild or Sinfild." In all children's christening records she appears only as "Martha."

Two children are documented:
1. Edward Bunker — christened 24 January 1762, Millbrook
2. [[james-thomas-bunker-1764|James Thomas Bunker]] — christened 7 April 1765, Millbrook

**Birth year estimated ~1737–41, Millbrook.** No birth or christening record has been found for Martha. The estimate derives from her 1761 marriage: a bride of typical marrying age (18–24) in 1761 would have been born roughly 1737–1743. Thomas Bunker, her husband, was christened June 1738 — consistent with Martha being born at about the same time.

**Parentage: probable Christopher Sinfield and Elizabeth of Millbrook, unconfirmed.** Two FamilySearch searches bearing on Martha's family of origin:

1. A search for Martha Sinfield records in Bedfordshire found a **Martha Sinfeild** christened at Millbrook on 18 May 1757, parents **John Sinfeild** and **Martha** (his wife). A death record for a Martha Sinfield in 1764 is consistent with this being the same person — she died young, aged about 7. She cannot be our bride.

2. A search for John Sinfield in Bedfordshire 1700–1800 found a **John Sinfield** christened **2 March 1722** at **Millbrook**, parents **Christopher Sinfield** and **Elizabeth**.

Taken together, the most plausible reading is: **Christopher Sinfield and Elizabeth** had children in Millbrook including John (1722) and, about fifteen years later, Martha (~1737–41). Our Martha is a younger sibling of John, not his daughter. John later named his own daughter Martha (1757) after his sister — a common naming pattern — but that child died in 1764. This hypothesis fits the ages, the geography, and the naming pattern, but remains unconfirmed: Martha's own baptism record has not been found, and no source directly names Christopher and Elizabeth as her parents.

A separate Marmaduke Sinfield family at Bedford had a daughter Martha christened 17 March 1757 (also in Nonconformist records), but she is similarly too young to be the 1761 bride and has no Millbrook connection.

Nothing is known of Martha's death date or burial.
