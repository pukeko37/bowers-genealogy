Martha Sinfield is known only through her marriage and her children's christening records. No birth or death record has been found for her.

She married **[[thomas-bunker-1738|Thomas Bunker]]** on 6 May 1761 at Millbrook, Bedfordshire. The marriage record renders her name as "Martha Sinfeild or Sinfild." In all children's christening records she appears only as "Martha."

Two children are documented:
1. Edward Bunker — christened 24 January 1762, Millbrook
2. [[james-thomas-bunker-1764|James Thomas Bunker]] — christened 7 April 1765, Millbrook

**Probable parentage: John Sinfield and Martha of Millbrook.** A FamilySearch search for Martha Sinfield records in Bedfordshire found a daughter named **Martha Sinfeild** christened at Millbrook on 18 May 1757, parents **John Sinfeild** and **Martha**. This 1757 baby is too young to be the 1761 bride — who must have been born roughly 1736–1741 to be of marriageable age. She was almost certainly a younger sister, or a child given the name of an earlier sibling who died. The John Sinfield and Martha household in Millbrook is the most plausible parental family for our bride: same village, same period, same surname. The bride's own birth record has not been found.

A separate Marmaduke Sinfield family was at Bedford with a daughter Martha christened 17 March 1757 (Nonconformist records also), but the Millbrook connection makes John Sinfield the stronger candidate.

**One generation further back.** A FamilySearch search for John Sinfield in Bedfordshire 1700–1800 (53 results) identified a **John Sinfield** christened **2 March 1722** at **Millbrook**, parents **Christopher Sinfield** and **Elizabeth**. This is the only Millbrook-born John Sinfield in the dataset and is consistent with being the same John Sinfield who had daughter Martha Sinfeild in Millbrook in 1757. If so, **Christopher Sinfield and Elizabeth** of Millbrook are Martha's probable grandparents. Christopher's own parentage and Elizabeth's maiden name are unresearched.

Nothing is known of Martha's death date or burial.
