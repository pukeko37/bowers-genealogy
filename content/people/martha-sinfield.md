Martha Sinfield is known only through her marriage and her children's christening records. No birth or death record has been found for her.

She married **[[thomas-bunker-1738|Thomas Bunker]]** on 6 May 1761 at Millbrook, Bedfordshire. The marriage record renders her name as "Martha Sinfeild or Sinfild" — variant spellings of what was likely "Sinfield." In all children's christening records she appears only as "Martha."

Two children are documented:
1. Edward Bunker — christened 24 January 1762, Millbrook (father Thomas Bunker, mother Martha)
2. [[james-thomas-bunker-1764|James Thomas Bunker]] — christened 7 April 1765, Millbrook (father Thomas Bunker, mother Martha)

Nothing is known of her origins, birth date, or fate. Whether further children existed is unknown — only two are attached to the FamilySearch record.
