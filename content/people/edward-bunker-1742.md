Edward Bunker was born on 1 October 1742 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire, the eighth child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker]] (1704–1784) and [[mary-reade-1702|Mary Reade]] (1702–1775). He spent his entire life in Millbrook, where his family had been settled since at least the 1730s.

He married **Mary Ashby** (1745–1813) on 16 November 1766 at Millbrook. The Ashby name re-enters the family through their ninth child, Mark Ashby Bunker, who was given his mother's maiden name. Edward and Mary had ten children between 1767 and 1792, all christened at Millbrook.

Mary died in 1813, a year before her husband. Edward died in February 1814, also in Millbrook, aged 71.

## Family

Edward was the 8th child of Thomas Bunker and Mary Reade — a brother of [[thomas-bunker-1738|Thomas Bunker (1738)]], the direct ancestor of Roland Bunker. For the full sibling context see [[bunker-reade-1728]].

His own family is documented at [[bunker-ashby-1766]]. Of his ten children, three are traced beyond birth:

- [[william-bunker-1767|William (1767)]] married Sarah Beal and died in Millbrook in 1847; in the 1841 census with a son Samuel.
- [[james-bunker-1777|James (1777)]] had three wives and died in Millbrook in 1864 aged ~87. Through his son John (1803) he is the progenitor of all the Victorian collateral Millbrook Bunkers, including the two cousins killed at Passchendaele in 1917.
- [[mark-ashby-bunker-1789|Mark Ashby Bunker (1789–1868)]] was convicted of sheep stealing in 1817 and transported to Australia, where he eventually settled and died in 1868.
