Elizabeth Cartwright (abt 1737–1785) married [[john-bunker-1746|John Bunker]] on 25 August 1768 at Millbrook. She was about nine years older than her husband. She died in 1785, leaving John a widower for eleven years until his own death in 1796. Her origins are not documented. See [[bunker-cartwright-1768]].
