Florence Eliza Birchmore was born about 1884 in Middlesex (baptised 8 February 1884), the youngest child of [[george-birchmore-plummer-jr-1850|George Birchmore Plummer Jr]] and [[elizabeth-sarah-morse-1852|Elizabeth Sarah Morse]]. She was in St Pancras in 1891, 1901, and still at St Jude, Gray's Inn Road in 1911 — the last census to place the family at their London address.

She married **Charles Henry Eyland** (born 1877). No marriage date or place is recorded in available sources. No children are listed.

Florence died in Devon in 1951, aged about 67. She was the last of her generation to die, outliving her father (1920), her sister Rosa (1934), her brother George William (1943), her mother Elizabeth (1928), and her sister Edith (1947). The Devon connection almost certainly came through [[george-william-birchmore-1880|George William]], who had settled in Sidmouth by 1911. Whether Charles Henry Eyland was also in Devon, or whether Florence was widowed before moving west, is not documented.
