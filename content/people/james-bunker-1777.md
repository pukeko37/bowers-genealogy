James Bunker was christened at Millbrook on 18 May 1777, the fifth child of [[edward-bunker-1742|Edward Bunker]] (1742–1814) and Mary Ashby (1745–1813). He spent his entire life in Millbrook and died there in 1864 aged approximately 87 — one of the longest-lived Bunkers of his generation, and the last of his line to remain in the village.

He married three times:
- **Sarah Pedder** — mother of at least Richard (~1800) and John (1803)
- **Mary Somerfield** — mother of at least Naomi (~1825) and possibly others
- **Catherine Blunt** — in the household by 1841, mother of at least Rachel (~1824)

The dates of these marriages and the deaths of the first two wives are not yet documented.

## In the Censuses

**1841:** James (~60) is at home with Catherine Blunt (~45) and a mixed household: Richard (~35), Naomi (~25), Rachel (~17), and a grandson Richard (~4).

**1861:** James (~84) is still in Millbrook with Catherine Blunt and a granddaughter Hannah (b.1854, Houghton Conquest). His son John (1803) has his own household nearby.

James died in 1864, aged approximately 87, one of the last of his generation in the village.

## Key Descendants

His son **[[john-bunker-1803|John Bunker (1803–1889)]]** — by Sarah Pedder — married Maria West (1806) in 1824 at Ampthill and became the anchor of the Millbrook collateral line through the Victorian era. John had nine known children. His son [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph (1840–1914)]] was the last Bunker to remain in Millbrook throughout his entire life, present at every census through 1911. All the later Millbrook Bunkers, including the two WWI casualties ([[millbrook|Albert Edward]] at Passchendaele and William Harold at Tyne Cot), descend from James through John.

## 1815 Fence Maintenance List

The list of properties adjoining Millbrook church with responsibility to maintain the fences (1815) includes a **Widow BUNKER** at the 8th pole (Kits Lane cottage). This is plausibly James's mother or another widow of the Edward (1742) line; it cannot be attributed to James himself as he was then ~38 and not widowed.

## Notes

The Millbrook parish register has not been searched for his marriage entries. His three wife surnames (Pedder, Somerfield, Blunt) are all attested in the census and local records but the underlying documents have not been located.
