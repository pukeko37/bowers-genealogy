Naomi Matthews was born about 1835 in Kensworth, Hertfordshire, to [[thomas-matthews-1797|Thomas Matthews]] (the younger, 1797–1862) and [[elizabeth-hopkins-abt-1801|Elizabeth Hopkins]] (abt 1801). Her birth predates her parents' formal marriage on 1 November 1840 by some five years — this may indicate an informal union before the recorded ceremony, or that Elizabeth had a prior relationship. Nothing in the available sources resolves the question.

She married **Charles Desborough** in Luton in **1864**. Nothing further is known of her — her death date, any children, and her subsequent life are unestablished.
