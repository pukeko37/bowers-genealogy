Thomas Edward Cook was born 9 April 1882 in Harpenden, Hertfordshire, the youngest of four known children of [[james-cook-1843|James Cook]] and Mary. His siblings were Joseph (1876), James Walter (1878), and Charles (1881).

He married [[alice-dumpleton-1883|Alice Mabel Dumpleton]] in 1906. Their son [[leslie-thomas-cook-1907|Leslie Thomas Cook]] was born in 1907. Alice died on 13 November 1940. Thomas died on 3 September 1955. Both are buried together at Kensworth — the same parish where his grandfather [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] and Elizabeth Frost had married in 1842.

The FamilySearch record for Thomas confirms his father as the James Cook (1843) of [[cook-frost-1842]], making Thomas a grandnephew of [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] and a first cousin once removed of [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] (who married William Thody and lived at Rose Cottage, Studham).
