John Brian Thody was born in 1936 in Luton, Bedfordshire — the youngest child of [[harry-thody-1894|Harry Thody]] and [[beulah-bunker-1896|Beulah Bunker]]. His birth is registered at vol. 3B, p. 458, indexed under 1937 — a likely late registration of a 1936 birth. He appears (redacted as living) in the 1939 Register at "Carnoy," 9 Oakley Road, Luton. He died in 1996. His fuller biography is not yet established.
