Richard William Mason was born on 5 March 1875 in Clerkenwell, Middlesex — the eldest of three sons of [[mason-james-1874|Richard Mason and Catherine James]], Welsh immigrants who ran a dairy at 27 Hosier Lane, Smithfield. His father died in 1890 and his mother in 1900, leaving all three brothers to manage on their own.

## Occupation

In 1881, aged six, Richard was in the family household in Bethnal Green with his father (cowkeeper), mother Kate, and infant brother John. By 1891 the family had settled at St Sepulchre Newgate — their father dead, sixteen-year-old Richard now working as a **milkman** under his widowed mother Kate, who ran the dairy.

By 1901 he was head of a dairy shop at St Sepulchre Without Newgate, the brothers' established commercial address, with John and Isaac both listed alongside him. By 1911 the household had moved to **Lambeth**, where Richard is again listed as head — now a **Grocer Shopkeeper** — with his brothers. The shift from dairy to grocer likely reflects the gradual evolution of the family trade as the brothers aged and the dairy gave way to a more general shop. Elizabeth Perry (a Suffolk woman in her late forties or fifties) appears in both the 1901 and 1911 households, presumably as a lodger or housekeeper.

Richard worked alongside his brothers throughout his working life and does not appear to have struck out independently. All three census entries where he is head of household show his brothers under the same roof.

## Later life

By 1939 Richard (then 64) was living at **150 Acre Lane, Brixton** — his brother John's newsagent and sub-post office — together with John, Mabel, and Nellie Norman. The 1939 National Register records his occupation as **Incapacitated**, suggesting he had been unable to work for some time. The probate record confirms 150 Acre Lane was his settled address at death, not a temporary arrangement.

Richard died on **28 November 1948** at **Olympic Hospital, London SE24** (Herne Hill / Tulse Hill area) — taken to hospital and died there. Administration was granted on 24 January 1949 in London to his brother [[john-james-mason-1878|John James Mason]], described in the probate record as "subpostmasters assistant." His estate was £221 8s. 3d. He was buried at **Abney Park Cemetery, Stoke Newington** — alongside his parents.

Richard was a lifelong bachelor. He never married and had no known children. This is confirmed consistently across all records: single in 1901, single in 1911, single in 1939, and at death his brother — not a widow or child — administered his estate.
