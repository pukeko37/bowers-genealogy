Elizabeth Ann Norman was born 7 May 1888, the fourth child of [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] and [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen Birchmore]]. She was 19 when her father died in 1907.

Elizabeth married Charles Stewart Main (1884–1963) in 1916, forming the [[main-norman-1916|Main-Norman family]]. They had one son, George Stewart Main (1919–?).

In 1921, Elizabeth and Charles Stewart were living at 143 De Beauvoir Road with her mother Ellen, sister Lillian, and brother Charles Jr and his wife. By 1939 they were still at 143 De Beauvoir Road — just 22 houses from her mother Ellen at number 121. Elizabeth was a housewife.

Their son George Stewart Main went on to work at the New Zealand High Commission in London, commuting from Birchington-on-Sea in Kent. He devoted 45 years to caring for three unmarried or widowed relatives in Kent — aunts Alice and Norah, and cousin Gwendoline.

Elizabeth died in 1953 in Lambeth, aged 65.
