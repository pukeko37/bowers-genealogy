Phoebe Geary was born 22 January 1854 in Haynes, Bedfordshire (christened the same day), the third surviving child of [[john-geary-1825|John Geary]] and [[lucy-morris-1826|Lucy Morris]]. Her name is spelled variously in records as Phobe, Phebe, and Phelbe. She died 28 January 1910 — just six days after her 56th birthday — and was buried at **Foster Hill Road Cemetery, Bedford**.

In 1871, aged 17, she is listed as a **straw platter** at the parental home in Haynes. In 1876 she married **Walter Southgate** (born Stowmarket, Suffolk, ~1841; occupation bricklayer's labourer / general labourer) at Bedford. They settled in Bedford's eastern wards and had a large family. Known children: Mary/Minnie (b.~1877), Samuel (b.~1879), Bertie (b.~1881), William (b.~1883), Charles (b.~1885), May (b.~1895), Alec (b.~1890), Amy Emma (b.~1898). Phoebe worked as a charwoman (1891 census).

Phoebe died at 56, survived by her husband Walter and several children. Her son **Bertie (Walter Berta Southgate)**, born 1881, took on the household after her death — in the 1911 census, Bertie is head of a Bedford household that includes his younger sisters May (16) and Amy (13), and his aunt [[lydia-rose-geary-1868|Lydia Rose Geary]] (with Lydia's son Jasper Fred), suggesting that Lydia moved in to help care for Phoebe's orphaned younger children after Phoebe's death in 1910.
