George Thody (1844–1935) was the third child of [[allen-thody-1813|Allen Thody]] and [[elizabeth-saunders-1811|Elizabeth Saunders]], and a brother of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]]. Born at Cotton End, Bedfordshire; died 1935 aged approximately 90–91 — a remarkable longevity. Appears in the 1851 census, age 6.
