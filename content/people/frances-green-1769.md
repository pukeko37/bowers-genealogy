Frances Green was born on 28 May 1769 in Swavesey, Cambridgeshire, and died in 1810 in Over — the village a few miles to the north where she had presumably moved on her marriage.

Her name is complicated. The baptismal record (Cambridgeshire Bishop's Transcripts) gives her as "Frances Marks," with her father named "Francis Marks." A second record (England, Births and Christenings) gives her as "Frances Mann," father "Francis Mann." By the time of her marriage in 1788, she is recorded as "Frances Green or Mann." She appears in genealogy records under the name Frances Green — her mother's maiden name. This practice of a child taking the mother's maiden name as their own surname was unusual but not unheard of in 18th-century England; it may reflect circumstances of the parents' relationship or simply a preference Frances herself expressed.

Her parents were Francis Mann (1746–1819) and Elizabeth Green (1745–1824).

Frances married [[robert-norman-1769|Robert Norman]] on 16 November 1788 at Swavesey — she was nineteen. Their first child Alice (b. ~1787) was born before the marriage, making Alice either pre-marital or her birth year slightly imprecise. Frances and Robert had seven children together: Alice, Mary, [[thomas-norman-1793|Thomas]], Elizabeth, James, Luke, and Richard.

Frances died in 1810, aged about 41. The cause and precise date are not recorded. Her youngest child Richard was about five at the time; her eldest children were grown. Robert lived on for another thirty-two years without remarrying.
