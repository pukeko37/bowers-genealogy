Elizabeth Thody (1847–1940) was the fourth child of [[allen-thody-1813|Allen Thody]] and [[elizabeth-saunders-1811|Elizabeth Saunders]], and the elder sister of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]]. Born at Cotton End, Bedfordshire; died 1940 aged approximately 92–93. She appears in the 1851 census, age 3, and again in the 1861 census at Eastcotts, Bedfordshire, age 13, sharing a household with her younger brother William (age 10).
