Annie May Bunker was born in 1888 in Millbrook, the youngest surviving child of [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker (1840–1914)]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]].

She appears at Millbrook in the 1891 census, but by 1901 — aged about twelve or thirteen — she was already in Luton, likely in domestic service. By 1911 she had moved to Marston Moretaine, Bedfordshire (about two miles from Millbrook).

**1908**: Annie May married **Albert White** (born 1884) in July 1908 at Ampthill. No children are documented.

Annie May died in 1929 at Ampthill, aged approximately forty or forty-one. Her husband Albert White survived her, dying in 1946.
