Mary Ann Walker was born around 1817 in the Aylesbury area of Buckinghamshire (the 1851 census records her birthplace as "Aubury, Buckinghamshire," almost certainly a transcription of Aylesbury). Little is known of her origins; her father may have been James Walker, who appears as a witness on the marriage register.

She married **[[able-matthews-1813|Able Matthews]]** on 22 November 1840 at Dunstable, Bedfordshire — a parish a few miles east of Whipsnade, suggesting Mary may have had connections to the Dunstable/Luton area. In the first year of marriage they were living with Able's sister [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] and her husband [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer Sr.]] at Houchen End, Whipsnade, with their infant daughter Sarah.

By 1851 the family had their own household in Whipsnade. Mary (34) and Able (38) had three children: Sarah (10), Elizabeth (4), and William (3).

She emigrated with Able and the children from Liverpool in 1855. Saints by Sea confirms she is listed as "Mary A. W. Matthews, Wife, 37" on the **Liverpool to Philadelphia** voyage, departing **27 February 1855** — she was alive at the time of sailing. The family stopped in St. Louis, Missouri, where **Mary died** — the date is unknown, but she was dead before Able reached Utah in 1861 (a span of six years in St. Louis). The cause of death is unrecorded.
