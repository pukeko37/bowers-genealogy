Norman Kenneth Bowers was born in 1925 in Portsmouth, the elder son of [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland Bunker]] and [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy Norman]]. He was born Norman Kenneth Bunker; in 1936, aged 11, his father changed the family name to Bowers. The headmaster announced the change to the whole school, and the boys were teased as "Bunker-Bowers" for years.

Norman followed his father into the Royal Navy but as an officer — unlike his father who had been enlisted. He studied engineering at university and served from WW2 through the early 1970s, roughly 25–28 years. After leaving the Navy he joined a practice of consultant engineers.

Norman stayed in the UK his entire life. When his parents emigrated to New Zealand in 1968, Norman remained behind. He died around 2008–2010.
