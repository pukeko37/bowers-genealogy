Ada Squires was born in 1864 in Fendrayton, Cambridgeshire — a small village about 6 miles west of Cambridge and 8 miles from Swavesey. She married [[robert-henry-norman-1868|Robert Henry Norman]] in 1899 at St Ives, Huntingdonshire. The couple lived in Swavesey, where Robert worked as an agricultural labourer. By 1911 they were at Middle Watch, Swavesey, with daughters Elsie (11) and Annie (9).

Ada died in 1923, leaving Robert a widower. He outlived her by 31 years, dying in 1954. Her maiden name Squires and Fendrayton origins are the only details recorded; her parentage has not been researched.
