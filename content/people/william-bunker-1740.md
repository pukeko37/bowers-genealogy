William Bunker (1740–1816) was the seventh child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and [[mary-reade-1702|Mary Reade]], christened 27 July 1740 at Millbrook. He married **[[ann-peet-1746|Ann Peet]]** (1746–1827) on 11 October 1768 at Steppingley — the village where he spent the rest of his life and where he died on 6 June 1816. Ann outlived him by eleven years, dying in 1827.

He appears in records as "William Banker," a common spelling variant. His sister [[mary-bunker-1737|Mary Bunker (1737)]] was buried at Steppingley in 1801 — two siblings in the same village.

See [[bunker-peet-1768]] for the full family narrative.
