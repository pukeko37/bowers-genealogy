Mary Saunders (born 28 December 1800, christened 26 May 1801, Cardington), eldest daughter of [[joseph-sanders-1774|Joseph Sanders]] and [[rebecca-arnold-1778|Rebecca Arnold]]. Left an orphan in 1812 when her mother Rebecca died; who raised her thereafter is unknown. Half-sister of [[elizabeth-saunders-1811|Elizabeth Saunders]], who was illegitimate and born after Joseph's death.
