William Matthews was christened 20 April 1806 at Kensworth, Hertfordshire, the third known child of [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews]] and [[mary-wilson-1779|Mary Wilson]].

He was baptised into the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints on **5 April 1848** at Bedfordshire, England — the same wave of conversions that swept through the family in the late 1840s.

In the **1851 census** he appears at Whipsnade listed as son of Thomas and Mary, aged 45 (born Kensworth, Hertfordshire), unmarried, agricultural labourer. He was still in the parental home with aged parents Thomas (84) and Mary (70) — the only resident child at that census.

**He emigrated in 1853, sailing with his brother Daniel on the same ship.** Saints by Sea confirms William Matthews (FamilySearch ID KWJH-VZZ, age 46, Whipsnade, Labourer) on the **Liverpool to New Orleans** voyage departing **17 January 1853** and arriving **6 March 1853** — the same vessel as [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis Matthews]] and his family. William is listed without related passengers, consistent with emigrating unmarried.

After landing at New Orleans, the party travelled up the Mississippi and Missouri rivers to Keokuk, Iowa. William then joined the **Cyrus H. Wheelock Company** for the overland trek, departing **13 June 1853** and arriving Utah **16 October 1853**.

His later fate in Utah — where he settled, whether he married, and when he died — has not yet been established from available sources.
