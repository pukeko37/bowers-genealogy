Eliza Bunker (1815–1885) was the third child of [[joel-bunker-1789|Joel Bunker]] and [[martha-taylor-1786|Martha Taylor]], born and christened at Eversholt, Bedfordshire in June 1815. Her birth at Eversholt confirms her father was living there from at least early 1815 — fifteen years earlier than the first newspaper reference to him at the Green Man Inn.

She married **[[stephen-danby-1812|Stephen Danby]]** (1812–1867), an excise officer, on 11 March 1838 at Eversholt. The Bucks Gazette announced the marriage on 24 March 1838, describing her as "eldest daughter of Mr. Joel Bunker, Green Man Inn, Eversholt." The newspaper called her the eldest daughter — though Job (1804) and Joseph (1810) were born before her, both were male, and there may have been no older daughters.

The excise service required frequent relocation. The family lived in Thame, Oxfordshire (1841), Staffordshire (1851), and then settled in Derbyshire from 1861 onwards. Stephen died in 1867; Eliza remained in Derby until her own death in 1885 aged 70. They had seven children.
