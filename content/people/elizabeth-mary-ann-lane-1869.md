Elizabeth Mary Ann Lane was born around **1869** in Bedford, Bedfordshire — the youngest daughter of [[samuel-plackett-lane-1841|Samuel Plackett Lane]] (abt 1841–1872) and [[lydia-bunker-1833|Lydia Bunker]] (1833–1922). Her father died when she was about three.

She appears in the **1871 census** (age 2) with her parents and sisters. She married **John White** on **24 April 1890** at Holy Trinity, Bedford (or Weymouth — the source record is ambiguous on the registration district). No further records have been traced after the marriage.

See [[bunker-lane-1863]] for the family narrative.
