Eliza Matthews was born in Whipsnade, Bedfordshire — christened 9 July 1843 (LDS records give a birth date of 9 February 1844). She was the eldest surviving child of [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis Matthews]] and [[sarah-garner-1822|Sarah Garner]], both of whom converted to the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in 1846.

Eliza is listed in the 1851 census in the Whipsnade household, aged 8. She emigrated with her family in 1853: the sea crossing from Liverpool to New Orleans departed **17 January 1853** (Saints by Sea passenger manifest records her as age 9); the family then joined the **Cyrus H. Wheelock Company** for the overland trek from Keokuk, Iowa, departing **13 June 1853**. The crossing was catastrophic — her sister Sarah Ann (aged 9) and sister Emma (aged 2) died on the trail in Nebraska in October 1853, as did her mother Sarah Garner. Eliza and her younger sister Mary were among the survivors who reached the Salt Lake Valley.

She subsequently married a man named Taylor and settled in **Tooele, Utah County**. She died there in **1930** — her obituary is the source that confirms her mother Sarah Garner also died on the trail. She was the longest-lived of the 1853 Matthews emigrants.
