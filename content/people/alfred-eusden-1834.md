Alfred Eusden was christened on Christmas Day, 25 December 1834, at Fenstanton, Huntingdonshire. His father was [[thomas-eusden-1799|Thomas Eusden]] (1799–1835); his mother was Mary Ann James, formerly a widow before her marriage to Thomas.

His father died on 2 September 1835 when Alfred was less than nine months old. His mother, widowed for a second time, married John William Skinner at Kimbolton in 1836. Alfred was raised in the Skinner household but kept his birth name: he is recorded as **Alfred Eusden** in all later records.

By 1859 Alfred had moved to London. He married **Mary Arthur** at St Leonard Shoreditch, Middlesex, on 30 August 1859, aged ~25. William Arthur also appears on the marriage record, likely Mary's father. No further records of Alfred are currently known.
