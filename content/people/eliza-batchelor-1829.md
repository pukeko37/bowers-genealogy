Eliza Batchelor was born 6 December 1829 in [[whipsnade|Whipsnade]], Bedfordshire — the second of four children of Thomas Batchelar (born c.1801, Dagnal, Buckinghamshire) and Susan Matthews (1800–1857), married 22 May 1826 in Hertfordshire. Her siblings were Emma (1827), Ann (1834), and Elizabeth (1842–1916). The print view confirms she lived in Whipsnade in the 1841, 1851, and 1861 censuses, and died there in 1867 — a lifelong Whipsnade resident.

Eliza married [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]] on 18 June 1859 at Old Church, Saint Pancras, London. [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen]] was born nine months after the wedding. Since both Eliza and George were lifelong Whipsnade residents, the London marriage was deliberate and unusual — why two local people would travel to St Pancras rather than marry at their home parish church is not documented. George had a prior connection to St Pancras (his son GBP Jr was born there in 1850), which may explain the choice of venue. Eliza's father **Thomas Batchelar** was present at the ceremony.

After the marriage, the family moved into Thomas Batchelar's household. The 1861 census places them all **By Whipsnade Green** in Thomas's house: Eliza listed as daughter, George as "son" (son-in-law), and both their children — [[george-birchmore-plummer-jr-1850|GBP Jr]] (age 11, George's son from his first marriage) and [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen]] (age 1). George's occupation in 1861 was **Agricultural Labourer**.

They had three children: Ellen (1860), Elizabeth Birchmore (1862–1954), and Thomas Birchmore (1865–1865, died in infancy).

The family held the license to The Chequers pub, Whipsnade — a connection that passed to George's third wife [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] after George's then Eliza's deaths.

Eliza died in 1867 in Whipsnade, aged 38, when Ellen was seven and Elizabeth was five. George married Ruth Cooke later that same year. See [[birchmore-plummer-batchelor-1859]] for the full family story.

### Notes

- Eliza's mother was Susan Matthews — the same surname as [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] (George's first wife, also from Whipsnade). Whether Susan was from the same Matthews family is unconfirmed but the geographic coincidence is notable.
