Lydia Bunker was born 24 September 1833 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire, the second child of [[james-bunker-1807|James Bunker]] and Elizabeth Watts. She was christened 28 March 1834 in Millbrook. She died in 1922 in Bedfordshire, aged 89.

Note: she is distinct from **Lydia Bunker (1804–1883)**, who was her father's sister (daughter of James Thomas Bunker) — two Lydia Bunkers in the same extended family.

Even before the family moved from Millbrook, Lydia had taken work as a domestic servant in Bedford. The **1851 census** places her in the household of James Sheppard (a nursery and seeds man) in **St Paul's parish, Bedford** — she was 17 and had already left home. Her father James died in 1852 and the family then relocated to Bedford; Lydia was already there.

By the **1861 census** she had returned to the family household at **Dane Street, St Paul's parish, Bedford**, aged 27, listed as a house servant within her mother Elizabeth's household. Her brother [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb]] (15, shoe maker) was present alongside sisters Martha, Phoebe, and Comfort. See [[caleb-bunker-1861-census]].

On **21 September 1863** Lydia married **[[samuel-plackett-lane-1841|Samuel Plackett Lane]]** at St Peter's Martin, Bedford. She was 30, Samuel was 22. Samuel was a boot maker in the same parish — he had been an apprentice at Hassett Street in 1861, in the same St Paul's district where Caleb was also in the shoe trade. The marriage connected the Lane and Bunker families directly. See [[bunker-lane-1863]].

They had three daughters: Edith Mercy (born ~1865), Mary A (born ~1868), and Elizabeth Mary Ann (born ~1869). **Samuel died in 1872**, aged 29, leaving Lydia a widow with daughters aged under eight.

As a widow, Lydia remained head of household in **Bedford Western Ward** through 1881 (daughters Edith, 18, and Mary A, 13, at home). By **1891** she was living with grandchildren Florence and Samuel Locke — Edith had married Frank Humphries Locke in 1884 and Lydia moved in to help. In **1901** she is mother-in-law in Edith and Frank Locke's household (Frank a stonemason, born Shoreditch), with further grandchildren Hilda and Gertrude Locke also present.

By **1911** Lydia was at the **Barton Almshouse, Turvey, Bedfordshire** — recorded age 77, widowed, with "BARTON ALMSHOUSE" given as her occupation field. She was supported by the almshouse charity in her final years. The census entry conflates her birthplace as "Born At Milbrock Turvey" — a transcription error combining her birthplace Millbrook with her current location Turvey.

She died in 1922 in Bedfordshire, registered as Lydia Lane, aged 89. She was one of the longest-lived of James and Elizabeth's nine children, and outlived her husband Samuel by half a century. She was Caleb's elder sister.
