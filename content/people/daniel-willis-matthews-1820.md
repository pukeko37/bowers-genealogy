Daniel Willis Matthews was born 24 June 1820 in Whipsnade, Bedfordshire, christened 17 December 1820. He was the eighth child of [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews]] and [[mary-wilson-1779|Mary Wilson]], two years younger than his sister [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] and three years older than [[charlotte-matthews-1823|Charlotte Matthews]].

## Early Life and Marriage

In 1841, Daniel (20) was still living in the Thomas and Mary Matthews household at Houchen End, Whipsnade. The following year he married **[[sarah-garner-1822|Sarah Garner]]**, on **9 October 1842** at Kensworth, Hertfordshire — Thomas Matthews's birthplace, just across the county border. Sarah was born 13 February 1822 in Caddington, Hertfordshire.

The marriage produced several children, all born in Whipsnade:
- **Eliza Matthews** (christened 9 July 1843) — survived, emigrated Utah, later married Taylor; died Tooele, Utah 1930
- **Sarah Ann Matthews** (born 11 May 1844) — died 1853 in Nebraska during the overland trail crossing; buried Pioneer Children's Memorial, Salt Lake City
- **Ann Matthews** (~1845)
- **Mary Matthews** (~1849)
- **Emma Matthews** (born 29 April 1851) — died 1853 on the trail; also buried Pioneer Children's Memorial, Salt Lake City

In the 1851 census, Daniel and Sarah appear as a family of five in Whipsnade: Daniel (30, Ag Lab), Sarah (29, born Caddington area), Eliza (8), Ann (6), Mary (2). Emma, born April 1851, may postdate the census or was simply not recorded.

## LDS Conversion

Both Daniel and Sarah were baptised into the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints on **12 September 1846**, at the Bedfordshire branch of the British Mission. This was the same date as his mother Mary Wilson's baptism, suggesting a branch-wide conversion event. The LDS records give a "marriage date" of 10 June 1847, which is likely the date of their temple/ecclesiastical sealing rather than the civil registration.

## Emigration — 1853

The emigration was in two legs. Daniel, Sarah, and their four children sailed from **Liverpool to New Orleans**, departing **17 January 1853** and arriving **6 March 1853**, on a voyage confirmed by Saints by Sea (BMR, p. 59). His brother [[william-matthews-1806|William Matthews]] sailed on the **same ship and same voyage** — the two brothers emigrated together. After landing at New Orleans the party would have travelled up the Mississippi and Missouri rivers to Keokuk, Iowa, where the overland company assembled.

The **Cyrus H. Wheelock Company** — approximately 400 people in 52 wagons — departed Keokuk overland on **13 June 1853** and arrived in the Salt Lake Valley **6–16 October 1853**. Daniel's family travelled in the division under Captain Kendall.

The crossing was catastrophic. **[[sarah-garner-1822|Sarah Garner]]** died circa October 1853 and never reached Salt Lake City — confirmed by her daughter Eliza's obituary. Daughters **Sarah Ann** (9) and **Emma** (1) also died on the trail in Nebraska and were buried at the Pioneer Children's Memorial, Salt Lake City. Of six family members who set out, three died. Only **Eliza** (9) and **Mary** (4) survived to Utah; the fate of **Ann** (8, confirmed on the passenger manifest) after the crossing is unknown.

**Note on sources:** The FamilySearch print view for Daniel attributes the voyage to "the Ellen Marie, 13 June 1853." The Saints by Sea record (which confirms Daniel's FamilySearch ID LTWY-3J3) places the sea crossing as Liverpool to New Orleans, 17 January 1853. The date "13 June 1853" was the Wheelock Company's overland departure, not the ship sailing. Whether "Ellen Marie" was the ship's name for this voyage is not confirmed from Saints by Sea; the date attribution in the FamilySearch print view appears garbled.

## Life in Southern Utah

Daniel settled in southern Utah, in the **Virgin City / Grafton** area of Washington County. He remarried — his second wife was **[[mary-lea-groves-1836|Mary Lea Groves]]**, born ~1836 in Missouri, previously married to a man named Lee (her sons Jacob Lee and John H. Lee appear as Daniel's stepsons in the 1900 census). Daniel and Mary Lea married around **1872**, the year their son Daniel Willis Matthews Jr. was born in Virgin, Washington County.

**1880 census**, Virgin City, Kane [Washington], Utah Territory: Daniel W. Mathews (65, Farmer, England) with Mary Lea (44, Missouri) and children: Maryam (24), Olive Lucy (22), Jacob (12), Daniel (8), Elaner (6), Charlott (4). The older children — Maryam (~1856) and Olive Lucy (~1858) — predate Daniel's marriage to Mary Lea and may be daughters of Sarah Garner who survived the trail.

**1900 census**, Grafton, Washington, Utah: Daniel W Mathews (80, born June 1820) with Mary L (64, Missouri, married 28 years), stepsons Jacob and John H Lee, son Daniel W. Matthews (28), daughter Charlot (23).

Daniel Willis Matthews died **15 April 1903** in Virgin, Washington County, Utah, and was buried in the Virgin Cemetery, aged 82.
