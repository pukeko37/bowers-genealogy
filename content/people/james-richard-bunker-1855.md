James Richard Bunker (christened 5 August 1855, Millbrook), eldest child of [[john-watts-bunker-1831|John Watts Bunker]] and Ann Woodland. His christening at Millbrook — two years after the family left the village — suggests a deliberate return to the ancestral parish. No further records traced.
