Kenneth Ernest Bunker was born in 1917, the elder son of [[ernest-caleb-bunker-1882|Ernest Caleb Bunker]] and [[helena-annie-melrose-1883|Helena Annie Melrose]], and died in 1985 aged ~68. He was born in the year his father was serving in WWI. [[alan-geoffrey-bowers-1928|Alan Bowers]] remembers visiting Kenneth and his brother Herbert Arthur in Bristol in the 1930s.
