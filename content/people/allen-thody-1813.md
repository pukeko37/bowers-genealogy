Allen Thody (born c. 1813) was a Bedfordshire farm labourer and the father of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]]. He married [[elizabeth-saunders-1811|Elizabeth Saunders]] on 19 October 1834 at St. Mary Cardington, Bedfordshire, and together they had five children.

The 1851 census records his birthplace as "Spain British Subject" — meaning he was born in Spain to British parents, not that his family was from Spain. His estimated birth year of 1813–1814 places his birth during the Peninsular War (1807–1814), suggesting his father was a British soldier serving in Spain at that time. The family was rooted in Cardington, Bedfordshire, where the Thody name is documented from at least 1744 — five generations of continuous local presence. Allen's birth in Spain was a one-time accident of his father's military posting; he returned to the family's Cardington community.

Allen is described as a Farm Labourer throughout his documented life. He could not write — he made his mark on his son William's birth certificate in 1850. He is named as William's father on the 1894 Whipsnade marriage certificate, still described as a Labourer.

Both Allen and his wife Elizabeth are listed in the FamilySearch tree as dying in 1898, in their mid-to-late eighties — a remarkable longevity shared by several of their children.
