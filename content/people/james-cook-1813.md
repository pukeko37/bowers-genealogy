James Cook was christened on 19 July 1813 at Kensworth, the second child of [[daniel-cook-1790|Daniel Cook]] and [[mary-birchmore-1785|Mary Birchmore]]. The 1841 census places him at age 25 in Daniel Cook's Whipsnade household — still living with his parents at the time of the first national census. No record of his marriage, employment, or death has been found. He disappears from the record after 1841.
