William Mathews was born 23 April 1837 at Whipsnade, Bedfordshire, the seventh child of [[peter-matthews-1802|Peter Matthews (1802)]] and Sarah Gates. He spent his entire life in Whipsnade, working as a farm labourer and later as a roadman. His address is recorded as **Houchen End** — the western hamlet of Whipsnade parish, also home to the [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews Sr.]] household (the LDS-connected branch) and to [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer Sr.]] The two Matthews lines living in the same small hamlet across successive generations is the principal evidence for a family connection between them, though it remains unproven.

He married **Elizabeth Bolton** (born ~1836) in the March quarter of 1862 in Bedfordshire, and they had nine children, all born at Whipsnade. He appears in every census from 1841 to 1901:

- **1841** — Whipsnade (child, age ~4)
- **1871** — Whipsnade
- **1881** — Whipsnade, Farm labor
- **1891** — Whipsnade
- **1901** — Whipsnade, Roadman

He died 29 April 1914; death registered at Luton/Dunstable.
