Mary Ann Norman was born 1 November 1861 in Swavesey, Cambridgeshire, the fifth child of [[thomas-norman-1827|Thomas Norman]] and [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann Tabram]] in the [[norman-tabram-1850|Norman-Tabram family]]. Her birth was registered at St Ives.

She married [[george-causer-1859|George Causer]] (born Staffordshire ~1859, occupation Engine Driver) in Huntingdonshire in 1883 and they settled in Swavesey. They had six children between 1885 and 1895. George died in 1898 leaving her a widow with young children. She was working as a laundress in 1901 and a charwoman in 1911. See [[norman-causer-1883]].

By 1939 she was at Middle Watch, Swavesey — the same hamlet where her youngest brother [[robert-henry-norman-1868|Robert Henry Norman]] also lived. She was 78, a widowed Old Age Pensioner, with her eldest daughter Elizabeth Causer and a lodger Richard Williams (19). She had not left Swavesey.

Mary Ann died in 1951 aged ~89 — two years before her niece [[elizabeth-ann-norman-1888|Elizabeth Ann Norman]] (Charles's daughter) died in 1953. She is the second longest-lived of Charles's siblings, after [[robert-henry-norman-1868|Robert Henry]] (died 1954).
