Elizabeth Hopkins was born about 1793, likely in Kensworth, Hertfordshire. She married [[thomas-matthews-1797|Thomas Matthews]] (the younger) on 1 November 1840 at Kensworth — his second marriage; he had been widowed. The 1841 census records her mother, also named Elizabeth Hopkins (aged 65), living in the household — suggesting the elder Elizabeth was widowed and had joined her daughter's home.

Elizabeth was approximately 47 at the time of marriage. The 1841 census suggested a birth year of ~1800–1801 (age recorded as 40, then rounded), but the more reliable 1861 census records her as born 1793, which is taken as the better estimate. Her one confirmed child with Thomas is [[naomi-matthews-1835|Naomi Matthews]] (b. ~1835, Kensworth), who married Charles Desborough in Luton in 1864. Naomi's birth year predates the 1840 marriage, which may mean she was born in an earlier relationship, or that the couple had a longer informal union before the formal ceremony.

Nothing is known of Elizabeth Hopkins beyond the 1841 and 1861 census records, in which she appears as Thomas Matthews's wife at North Side Kensworth Road. Her origins and death date remain unestablished. See [[matthews-hopkins-1840]] for the full family narrative.
