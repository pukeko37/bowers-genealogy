Mary Lea Groves was born around 1836 in Missouri (1880 census age 44; 1900 census age 64, giving a consistent birth year of ~1836). Her maiden name was **Groves**, confirmed by the Utah death certificate of her son Daniel Willis Matthews Jr. (1947), which lists his mother as "Mary L. Groves."

Before marrying [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis Matthews]], she had been married to a man named **Lee** — their sons Jacob Lee (born Utah ~1861) and John H. Lee (born Utah ~1868) appear as Daniel's stepsons in the 1900 census. This prior marriage likely took place in Utah, where she had evidently settled as a Mormon.

She married Daniel Willis Matthews around **1872** (the 1900 census records 28 years of marriage). Their children include:

- **Daniel Willis Matthews Jr.** (born 24 November 1872, Virgin, Washington County, Utah; died 21 May 1947, Cedar City, Iron County, Utah)
- **Charlotte/Charlot Matthews** (born ~1877, Utah; appears in 1900 census aged 23)

In the 1880 census at Virgin City, Kane, Utah, she is listed as Mary Lea Mathews (44, Missouri). In the 1900 census at Grafton, Washington, Utah, she is listed as Mary L. Mathews (64, Missouri, married 28 years).

Her death date and burial place are unrecorded in the sources so far ingested.
