Emma Geary was born in 1856 in Haynes, Bedfordshire (christened 20 January 1856), the fourth surviving child of [[john-geary-1825|John Geary]] and [[lucy-morris-1826|Lucy Morris]]. She died on 31 July 1874 at Bedford, aged 18, and was buried at **Foster Hill Road Cemetery, Bedford**.

In the 1861 census, aged 5, she appears as a Scholar at Church End, Haynes. The 1871 census offers an odd double record: one entry places her at the parental home as a **straw platter** (age 15), and a second entry records an "Emma Geary" as a Servant (age 15) in the household of Matilda Pratt at St Peter, Bedford. Given that straw plaiting was the dominant cottage industry of the area, the servant entry is likely a misidentification or a temporary placement — both records cannot be fully relied upon.

She died unmarried at 18. The cause of death is not recorded in available sources. She is the only sibling to die in early adulthood (the others who died young were infants: the first Eliza in 1856, Ellen in 1865, Frederick Louis in 1872).
