Sarah Gates was christened **27 November 1803** at **Aldbury, Hertfordshire** — a village near Tring, just across the county border from Bedfordshire. She was the eldest child of **Joseph Gates** (1781–Deceased) and **Elizabeth Inward** (1780–1843), who had married in Hertfordshire in June 1803, just five months before Sarah's birth. Her known siblings were Hannah (1812), Elizabeth (1814), Mary (1817), Charlotte (1822–1846), and Josiah (1826).

How Sarah came to be in the Whipsnade/Studham area as a young woman is not recorded. Her family's location was Aldbury, and the connection may have been through work, kin already settled in the Dunstable Downs area, or simply the movement of rural Hertfordshire families into adjacent Bedfordshire parishes. She married **[[peter-matthews-1802|Peter Matthews]]** on **17 January 1825** at **[[studham|Studham]]** — the nearest Bedfordshire parish to Aldbury, which suggests her family retained ties to that side of the border. The Studham marriage register recorded her name as "Sarah Yates or Gates," a common ambiguity in transcription; other records confirm Gates.

She and Peter settled in Whipsnade, where all eleven of their children were christened. The 1841 census placed the family at **Houchen End**, Whipsnade, with her younger sister **Charlotte Gates** (then 15) living with them — Charlotte died in 1846, aged about 24, having apparently spent at least part of her short life in her sister's household.

Sarah died in 1851, the year of the census. She was 47 or 48. The 1851 enumerator recorded her birthplace as "Arbury, Buckinghamshire" — almost certainly a mishearing or miswriting of Aldbury, Hertfordshire (the two villages are only a few miles apart and the names are easily confused). She is the last person recorded in her family before Peter was left a widower.
