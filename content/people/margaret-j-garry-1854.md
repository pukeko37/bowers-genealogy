Margaret J Garry (1854–July 1933) was born in Hartlepool, County Durham — a local woman when [[george-norman-1851|George Norman]] arrived from Swavesey, Cambridgeshire in 1875. She married George on **7 December 1875** at Christ Church, West Hartlepool, two months after his brother [[richard-norman-1853|Richard Norman]] had married [[ann-elizabeth-randall-1854|Ann Elizabeth Randall]] in the same town.

Her maiden name was Garry; her middle initial is recorded as "J" but the name behind it is not yet known. In the 1901 census she was recorded as "**May** Norman" — almost certainly a middle name or household nickname, since she appears as "Margaret" in all other census years.

Margaret was the daughter of a family not yet researched. She was a lifelong Hartlepool resident throughout her recorded life.

## Family life

Margaret and George had a large family, raising their children in **Stranton**, the inner township of West Hartlepool, through the 1880s and 1890s. By 1901 the household had moved to West Hartlepool proper. Their son William was christened at the **Lynne Street United Free Methodist Chapel** in 1879, placing the family in the Nonconformist tradition.

George died in September 1903, aged about 51, leaving Margaret a widow with several children still young. The 1911 census records her as widowed at West Hartlepool, heading a household of eight (herself and seven resident children). She survived George by thirty years, dying in July 1933.
