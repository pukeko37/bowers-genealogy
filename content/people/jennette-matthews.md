Jennette (also rendered Janet in some records) was born in Scotland about 1818. She married [[able-matthews-1813|Able Matthews]] as his second wife at some point after his arrival in Lehi, Utah in 1861 and before the 1870 census. Able's first wife [[mary-walker-1817|Mary Ann Walker]] had died in St. Louis, Missouri during the family's emigration journey.

Jennette appears alongside Able in both the 1870 and 1880 US censuses at Lehi, Utah County — recorded as age 52 in 1870 and 60 in 1880. No children appear in either census, consistent with the marriage having occurred when Jennette was already in her forties. Her maiden name, her route to Utah, and when and where she died remain unestablished.

See [[able-matthews-1813]] for the full narrative of her husband and the wider Matthews family emigration.
