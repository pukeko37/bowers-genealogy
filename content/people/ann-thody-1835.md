Ann Thody (1835–1913) was the eldest child of [[allen-thody-1813|Allen Thody]] and [[elizabeth-saunders-1811|Elizabeth Saunders]], and the eldest sister of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]]. Born at Cotton End, Bedfordshire; died 1913 aged approximately 77–78. She appears in the 1851 census, age 16, in the family household at Cotton End.
