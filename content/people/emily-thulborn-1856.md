Emily Thulborn was born on **21 August 1856** in Oakington, Cambridgeshire — a village about 5 miles north-west of Cambridge. She was the second child of [[newman-thulborn-1821|Newman Thulborn]] (abt 1821–1888) and [[hannah-how-1824|Hannah How]] (abt 1824–unknown), both Cambridgeshire people. See [[thulborn-how-oakington]] for her family of origin.

By 1871, at age 15, Emily was living at Willingham (about 6 miles from Oakington) — confirmed as a **servant** in the household of Robert Read, a farmer, with his wife and two sons. She married [[tom-norman-1856|Tom Norman]] on 30 October 1877 in Swavesey, Cambridgeshire. They were the same age — both born 1856 — and she was the closest match in age of any of the Norman siblings' spouses.

The couple had nine children between 1878 and 1893. The 1891 census at Alconbury Weston shows all seven children then born: Fred (12, Cambridgeshire), Charles (11, Cambridgeshire), Kate (9, Huntingdonshire), Alfred (7, Alconbury Weston), Florence (5), Albert (2), and Emily (1). Fred and Charles were born in Cambridgeshire before the move; Kate onward were born in Huntingdonshire, placing the family's migration from Swavesey to Alconbury Weston between 1879 and 1881. In 1901 Emily's occupation is recorded as "HOUSEKEEPER" — four of the nine children (Bertie/Albert, Emily, Thomas, Hannah) remained at home, along with Tom's elderly father Thomas Norman (74). By 1911 only Hannah (17) was still at home; the 1911 census gives "Years Married: 33" and records Tom's dual occupation. Emily's 1911 birthplace is given as "Swan Oakington" — a locality within Oakington parish. The 1911 original place was "Eaton Socon, Neots, Huntingdonshire" (the FamilySearch indexed place "Eaton, Nottinghamshire" is a transcription error; the census image reference is RG14/173/21, schedule 11).

Emily died in 1922 in Huntingdonshire (death registration Vol. 3B, p. 374), one year after the 1921 census, still at Eaton Socon. Tom survived her by three years, dying in 1925. Her son Albert Newman Norman — nicknamed "Bertie" in the 1901 census, and named to commemorate the maternal grandfather — kept the Crown Beerhouse from 1922, taking over from Tom in the final years of his life.

Note: a FamilySearch source attachment records an "Emily Thulborn" marrying James Ingle at St. Ives, Huntingdonshire in 1877 — this is a false attachment, a different person; our Emily married Tom Norman at Swavesey in the Chesterton registration district, not at St. Ives.
