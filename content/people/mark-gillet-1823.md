Mark Gillet (1823–1895) married [[elizabeth-west-bunker-1824|Elizabeth West Bunker]] on 14 April 1845 at Marston Moretaine, Bedfordshire. His origins, parentage, and death place are unresearched. The surname appears as both "Gillet" and "Gillett" in the records. The couple had ten children and settled at Maulden, Bedfordshire, where he was still resident in 1871.
