Jesse Cook was christened on 16 December 1821 at Kensworth, Hertfordshire, the sixth child of [[daniel-cook-1790|Daniel Cook]] and [[mary-birchmore-1785|Mary Birchmore]]. A further record dated 1 December 1823 from an Independent chapel in Bedford also carries the name Jesse Cooke with the same parents — this may be a delayed registration or a duplicate record at a different chapel. The Kensworth christening of 1821 is consistent with his age (15) in the 1841 census.

## 1841 Census

Jesse appeared in his parents' Whipsnade household at age 15 alongside his siblings [[james-cook-1813|James]] (25) and [[lois-cook-1827|Lois]] (14).

## Marriage and Family

Jesse married Phoebe Holland on 1 January 1843 at Kensworth. His father Daniel Cook was present; a witness was Ezekiel Holland (Phoebe's relation). Two different indexed records of the marriage give dates of 1 January and 6 January 1843 — likely the same event.

By 1851 the family was in Kensworth: Jesse (29, Ag Lab), wife Phoebe (34, Kensworth), and daughter Mary (8, Kensworth). Jesse's birthplace is given as Kensworth.

## Children

- Mary Cook (b. 1843, christened 4 Jun 1843, Whipsnade) — married Leonard Lambert, 5 Dec 1869, Paddington, Middlesex
- Henry Cook (b. abt 1843) — married Emily Ann Holland, 28 Sep 1865, Kensworth (age 22)
- Albert Cook (b. Aug 1848, christened 25 Nov 1860, Kensworth)

The late christening date for Albert (1860, twelve years after his birth) suggests delayed or informal registration, possibly in a Nonconformist context.

Note that Jesse's wife Phoebe Holland and his son Henry's wife Emily Ann Holland share a surname — the families may have been neighbours or relations.
