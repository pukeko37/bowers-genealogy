Nellie Norman was born in 1885, the second of nine children of [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] and [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen Birchmore]]. She was 22 when her father died in 1907.

Nellie never married. She lived with her sister [[mabel-annie-norman-1883|Mabel]] and brother-in-law John James Mason at 150 Acre Lane, Clapham, working as a stationer shop assistant in their newsagent business. When the shop was damaged by V-weapons in 1944, Nellie fled with Mabel and John to stay with [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy]] at Park Hill, Harpenden.

Nellie died in 1960 in Hackney, aged 75.
