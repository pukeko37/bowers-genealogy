David Batchelor is the father of [[thomas-batchelar-1803|Thomas Batchelar]] (1803–1872) and thus the paternal grandfather of [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]] (1829–1867). His dates of birth and death are not recorded in the available sources.

He married **Ann** (surname unknown) on 3 November 1801 at St Luke Old Street Church, Finsbury, London. Their known children — William (1802), Thomas (1803), Elizabeth (1805), and Susannah (1807) — were christened across three adjacent Chilterns parishes in Hertfordshire and Buckinghamshire: Little Gaddesden, Ivinghoe, and Edlesborough. The family's London marriage followed by Chilterns christenings may suggest a move from the metropolis to the countryside shortly after 1801, or a prior connection to both areas.

His paternity of Thomas is confirmed by two sources: the Ivinghoe christening record (1804), which names David Batchelor as the father, and the 1868 marriage record of Thomas (aged 64) at Whipsnade, which also names David Batchelor as his father.

David's own parents are not identified in any available source.
