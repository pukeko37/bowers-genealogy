George Allen Thody (c. 1874–1958) was the eldest child of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] and [[mathilde-parent-1853|Mathilde Parent]]. He was born in Canada — probably Sandwich, Ontario, where his mother's family was from — shortly after his parents married in Chicago in 1873.

In the 1881 census he appears with his father in Sharnbrook, Bedfordshire, aged 6, listed as "born Canada." His mother had returned to Canada with his younger brother Louis Albert, but George Allen stayed behind with his father. He used the name "Allen" in the 1891 census (Totternhoe, Bedfordshire, age 16, Printers Apprentice, born "Sandwick, Canada Ant.") — his middle name rather than George.

After the 1891 census he disappears from English records. He likely returned to Canada or emigrated elsewhere. He is shown as dying in 1958 in the FamilySearch tree, aged approximately 83–84. He was a half-brother of [[harry-thody-1894|Harry Thody]].
