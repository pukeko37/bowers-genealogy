Annie Maria Sinfield (born ~1864, Bedford), elder daughter of Joseph Sinfield and [[martha-sarah-bunker-1841|Martha Sarah Bunker]]. She married **Alfred John Hyde** (born ~1859, City of London), a blocker in the straw hat industry in Luton. Her widowed father Joseph Sinfield was living with the Hyde household in Luton in the 1911 census.
