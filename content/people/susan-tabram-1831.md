Susan Tabram was born around 1831 in Fenstanton, Huntingdonshire, the second child of [[richard-tabraham-1807|Richard Tabraham]] and [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary Eusden]] in the [[tabraham-eusden-1828|Tabraham-Eusden family]]. The FamilySearch christening record gives 25 December 1834 at Fenstanton — a three-year gap from the stated birth year — which may indicate delayed baptism or an estimated birth year. The 1841 census records her as age 10, consistent with birth around 1830–1831. Her mother died when Susan was about ten.

The 1841 census places Susan not at Fenstanton but in Elsworth, in the household of her paternal grandparents Richard Tabram (65) and Frances Tabram (60). Her father Richard (then about 34) does not appear in the record — he may have been working away. The 1851 census finds her back in Fenstanton, aged 19, unmarried, working as a housekeeper in her father's household.

Her eldest sister [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann]] married Thomas Norman in 1850; Susan remained in Fenstanton. She married **John Levitt** (1830–1909) at Fenstanton — the sources give either 2 October or 11 October 1859, with two records supporting the latter. A mistaken source record also attaches Susan's name to a "Ralph Cook" marriage at St. Ives in 1859; this appears to be a data error in FamilySearch. She had six children, all christened at Fenstanton:

- William Levitt (1860–1944) — christened 13 May 1860
- Mary Ann Levett (1863–1863) — christened 3 May 1863; died in infancy
- Lizzy Levitt (1865–Deceased) — christened 4 June 1865
- George Levitt (1867–Deceased)
- **Mary Eusden Levitt** (1869–Deceased) — christened 3 October 1869; named after her grandmother [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary Eusden]], who died before Susan turned eleven; the name is a deliberate act of remembrance, paralleling Ann's naming of [[alfred-eusden-norman-1865|Alfred Eusden Norman]] in 1865
- **Annie Tabrain Levitt** (1874–Deceased) — christened 31 May 1874; the middle name "Tabrain" (a phonetic rendering of Tabram) preserved the family surname in the next generation, completing the same pattern her sister Ann used with "Eusden"

The census trail shows John and Susan Levitt in Fen Stanton across every decade: 1861 at Fen End with infant William; 1871 at Fenstanton with William (11), George (3), and Mary Eusden (1), her father Richard also present in the record aged 64; 1881 with George (13), Mary (11), and Anne (6); 1891 with George (23) and Anne (16); and 1901 with John recorded as "gardener's labourer". John died in 1909. Susan died in 1911; her death was registered in the **Chesterton registration district, Cambridgeshire**. "Chesterton" in the death index is the district name, not a specific address — it covered Cambridge and surrounding villages. Her actual place of death within the district is unknown. She was 80 at death. Widowed in 1909, she may have moved to be near one of her children, but no census or address record survives to confirm where she was living in her final two years.
