Thomas Bunker was christened on 4 June 1738 at Millbrook, Bedfordshire, and buried on 14 December 1825 at Toddington, Bedfordshire, aged 87. He is Roland Bunker's great-great-grandfather, three generations back from Frederick Bunker.

He was the sixth child of **[[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker]]** (1704–1784) and **[[mary-reade-1702|Mary Reade]]** (1702–1775), both of Millbrook. Born into a large household of twelve children, he grew up in the village his family had recently settled, and he never left south Bedfordshire.

He married **[[martha-sinfield|Martha Sinfield]]** on 6 May 1761 at Millbrook. The marriage record spells her name "Martha Sinfeild or Sinfild" — the clerk recording both variant spellings. Two children are documented:

1. Edward Bunker — christened 24 January 1762, Millbrook
2. [[james-thomas-bunker-1764|James Thomas Bunker]] — born 17 April 1764; christened 7 April 1765, Millbrook

James Thomas is the direct ancestor of Roland Bunker. Martha's fate is unknown; no death record for her has been found.

Thomas outlived his father by 41 years, reaching age 87. He was buried at Toddington — the same town where his own parents had married in 1728, suggesting enduring family connections there. His grandson James Thomas Bunker had died the previous year, in 1824.
