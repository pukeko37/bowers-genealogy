Samuel Heels was born between 1797 and 1801 (the FamilySearch record gives both dates; his exact birth year is uncertain). He was associated with Whipsnade and the Totternhoe area of Bedfordshire. He died in 1845 (death registered in the Luton district) — just two years after the 1843 Chequers Inn pub auction in which he was listed as tenant.

## The Chequers Inn

Samuel Heels appears in the 1843 Bedfordshire Archives record of the Chequers Inn, Whipsnade as the sitting tenant, paying £16 per year rent to owner Edward Burr (Dunstable Brewery). His recorded residence was **On The Green** — Whipsnade Green, where the pub was situated. This is the earliest documentary evidence of the Heels family's connection to the Chequers.

## Marriages and Children

**First wife: Anne** (1803–1840). Married before 1824, near Totternhoe, Bedfordshire. Anne died in 1840. Their children:
- Mary Ann Heels (1825)
- Rebecka Heels (1826–1911)
- Elizabeth Heels (1834)
- Emma Heels (1838–1848)

**Second wife: [[hannah-maria-heels-1814|Hannah Maria Heels]]** (1814–1886). Married about 1842. Samuel and Hannah shared the Heels surname, suggesting they were distant relatives — she was likely from a separate branch of the same family (her origins were Lockinge, Berkshire). Their one child:
- Thomas Heels (1843–?)

Samuel died in 1845, only three years into his second marriage and two years after the pub auction. Hannah was left a widow at 31 with a two-year-old son. She subsequently married [[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham Batchelor]], who is likely to have taken over the Chequers licence through this connection.

## The Heeles → Batchelor Pub Transition

Samuel's death in 1845 is the key event explaining the licensee succession at the Chequers. His widow Hannah — already resident on Whipsnade Green with a family connection to the pub — brought the licence into her second marriage with Abraham Batchelor. The transition was a family affair, not an open-market change of tenant.
