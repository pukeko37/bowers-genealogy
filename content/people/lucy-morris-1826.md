Lucy Morris was born in 1826 in Haynes, Bedfordshire, christened 20 August 1826. She died in 1895 in Ampthill — the same year as her husband [[john-geary-1825|John Geary]]. She is Eliza Geary's mother and Roland Bunker's maternal grandmother.

She married John Geary in 1848 in Haynes and spent her life there through 1881, moving with John to Maulden by 1891. Named addresses include Silver Street and Church End.

Her parents were **Thomas Morris** (1802–1858) and **Mary Bennet** (1801–1834), married 1825 in Haynes. Mary Bennet died in 1834 when Lucy was about eight; Thomas died in 1858. Lucy was orphaned on both sides before her own children were grown. Her siblings were Susanna (1829), Joseph (1831), and James (1832).

Her name was carried forward in her granddaughter's full name: **Beulah Rebecca *Lucy* Bunker** (1896), Roland's elder sister. The "Lucy" in that otherwise opaque name almost certainly honours Lucy Morris, Eliza's mother.
