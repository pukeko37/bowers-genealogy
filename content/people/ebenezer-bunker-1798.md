Ebenezer Bunker (6 May 1798 – 9 March 1819) was the fifth child of [[james-thomas-bunker-1764|James Thomas Bunker]] and [[lydia-brown-1769|Lydia Brown]]. He died aged 20, unmarried and without children, five years after his brothers Caleb and Joseph had moved to Stevington. The circumstances and place of his death are not recorded in the FamilySearch sources.

The name Ebenezer recurs in both the Caleb (1790) line (Ebenezer Bunker b. 1820, son of Caleb) and the Joseph (1792) line (Ebenezer Bunker b. 1822, son of Joseph), suggesting this was a name the family wished to carry forward despite — or because of — this early death.
