William Spring was born about 1779 in Shillington, Bedfordshire, and christened 29 August 1779 at Shillington. His death is recorded as "Deceased" in the FamilySearch tree — the date is unknown. He is Rhoda Spring Bottoms's father and Eliza Geary's great-great-grandfather.

**Parents.** His father was [[william-spring-1753|William Spring]] (1753) of Shillington; his mother was [[sarah-kitchener-1752|Sarah Kitchener]] (1752), also of Shillington. They married 11 November 1773 at Shillington. See [[spring-kitchener-1773]].

**Two families.** William had children by two women:

1. **[[sarah-bottoms-1786|Sarah Bottoms]]** (1786–1829): Children [[rhoda-spring-bottoms-1806|Rhoda Spring Bottoms]] (born 1806, Wootton, Bedfordshire) and Phoebe Spring (born 1807). These children were born before his marriage to Mary (~1808). The christening record for Rhoda uses the double surname "Spring or Bottoms," suggesting some ambiguity about the parents' formal relationship. See [[spring-bottoms-1806]].

2. **Mary** (about 1782–1855, born Barton, Bedfordshire): Married about 1808. Eight children: William (1809), Mary (1811), Elizabeth (1812–1865), Sarah (1816), John (1818), Fanny (1819–1884), Thomas (1822), Charlotte (1822). The family lived in Barton-le-Clay, Bedfordshire through 1861.

**Residence.** William is recorded in Barton-le-Clay (1841), Barton (1851), and Barton (1861) — suggesting the family settled in that area, distinct from his birth parish of Shillington. Rhoda was born in Wootton, which is further north near Bedford; the family appear to have been mobile within the Bedfordshire area.
