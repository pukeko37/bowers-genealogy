Anne Bunker (1732–1800) was the fourth child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and [[mary-reade-1702|Mary Reade]], christened 25 June 1732 at Toddington. She married [[daniel-brightman-1718|Daniel Brightman]] (1718–1787) on 3 September 1753 at Millbrook, when she was about 21. The marriage took place in her father's village, suggesting the family was already established in Millbrook by 1753.

Together they had twelve children — a total matching her mother Mary Reade's — between 1754 and 1778. Daniel died in 1787, leaving Anne a widow for thirteen years. She died 20 April 1800 at Ampthill, predeceased by at least four of her children (infant Mary 1754, infant John 1762, Josias who died aged 7 in 1774, James who died aged 18 in 1777, and infant Jonas 1777).
