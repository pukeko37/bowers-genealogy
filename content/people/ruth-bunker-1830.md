Ruth Bunker was born in 1830 in Millbrook, the daughter of [[john-bunker-1803|John Bunker (1803)]] and Maria West. She was born in the same year as her brother John — they may have been twins, or the FamilySearch birth year for one of them is approximate.

She died in a fire in 1836, aged **five years old**. The death was reported in the *Northampton Mercury*, 14 May 1836, and generated a coroner's inquest. Her burial at Millbrook St Michael & All Angels is confirmed by two FMP records (National Burial Index and Bedfordshire Burials, both giving birth year 1830, death year 1836, transcript XAUTO/FHS/BEDFORDSHIRE/BUR/00320155). The age of five in the newspaper is consistent with a birth in 1830 and death in spring 1836.

This child was the "fire victim RB-H" previously treated as an unknown adult in the Ruth Bunker search. The identity is now confirmed: the Northampton Mercury fire inquest and the burial register entry #35 both refer to this five-year-old daughter of John (1803) and Maria West.
