Rebecca Bunker (1815–unknown) was the third child of [[james-bunker-1777|James Bunker (1777)]] and [[mary-somerfield-1793|Mary Somerfield]], born at Millbrook. She married **[[john-chapman|John Chapman]]** on **8 June 1835** and appears in the 1841 census as "Rebacah Chapman" (age 25) in her father's household at Village Sand End, Millbrook, with her husband and two children Thomas (5) and Bettsey (4).

See [[chapman-bunker-1835]] for the family.
