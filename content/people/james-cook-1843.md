James Cook (FamilySearch 9KCT-R5M) was born in 1843, listed as a son of [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] and Elizabeth Frost. He shares a birth year with his brother [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook]] — either the two were twins, or one birth year is approximate and a short interval separates them. Their mother Elizabeth Frost died in 1843, almost certainly from childbirth complications, leaving David a widower with at least two young children. David's father had married at Kensworth in 1842, and the family's roots straddle the Kensworth/Whipsnade area.

James makes his uncle [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] — whose illegitimate daughter [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] married William Thody — and his brother [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook (1843–1908)]] the Chequers Inn licensee.

He married a woman named Mary (maiden name not yet established) and settled in Harpenden, Hertfordshire. Their four known children were Joseph (1876), James Walter (1878), Charles (1881), and [[thomas-cook-1882|Thomas Edward Cook]] (9 Apr 1882). The FamilySearch record for Thomas Edward Cook links his father to David Cook (1816) and Elizabeth Frost, confirming the identification. His son Thomas Edward is buried at Kensworth — the same parish where his father David had married in 1842: a three-generation geographic continuity.
