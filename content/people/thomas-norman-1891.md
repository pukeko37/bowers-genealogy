Thomas Norman (1891–1981), eighth child of [[tom-norman-1856|Tom Norman]] and [[emily-thulborn-1856|Emily Thulborn]]; born Alconbury, Huntingdonshire. The longest-lived of the nine children, dying aged approximately 90. Still at home in Eaton Socon in the 1921 census with his parents; no further records traced.
