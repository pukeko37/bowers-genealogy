Leslie Thomas Cook (born 1907, Harpenden, Hertfordshire) was the only child of [[thomas-cook-1882|Thomas Edward Cook]] and [[alice-dumpleton-1883|Alice Mabel Dumpleton]]. His birth is documented via the Find a Grave records for Kensworth, where his parents are buried. His subsequent life is unresearched.
