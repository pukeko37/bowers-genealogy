Thomas Matthews the younger was christened **12 August 1798** at Kensworth, Hertfordshire (FamilySearch: G47L-98J), the son of [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews]] and [[mary-wilson-1779|Mary Wilson]]. He was, by christening date, the oldest child of the union — born before his parents formally married in June 1801, as was his sister [[susan-matthews-1800|Susan]].

Unlike his brothers [[able-matthews-1813|Able]] and [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis]] who emigrated to Utah, and his brother [[william-matthews-1806|William]] who followed them in 1853, Thomas spent his entire life in Kensworth, Hertfordshire — the village of his birth and his father's birth — working as an agricultural labourer.

## First marriage

On **3 December 1826** at Kensworth, Thomas married **Mary Squire**. Together they had at least four known children:

- **Mary Matthews** (christened 2 Sep 1827, Kensworth) — later married Daniel Evans, 27 Jan 1849, Kensworth.
- **Sarah Matthews** (christened 24 Jul 1831, Kensworth)
- **Charles Matthews** (b. ~1832, Kensworth) — appears in 1841 and 1851 censuses.
- **Naomi Matthews** (b. ~1835, Kensworth) — married Charles Desborough, 24 September 1864, Luton; 1864 marriage record names Thomas Matthews as her father.

Mary Squire died between 1835 (Naomi's birth) and 1840 (Thomas recorded as widowed at second marriage).

## Second marriage

On **1 November 1840** at Kensworth, Thomas (listed as "Widowed") married **Elizabeth Hopkins**. The 1841 census placed them at Kensworth with three children from the first marriage — Sarah (10), Charles (9), Naomi (5) — and Elizabeth Hopkins (65), likely Elizabeth's mother.

No children of Thomas and Elizabeth are known.

## Census record

**1841 (Kensworth):** Thomas Matthews (35, Hertfordshire), Elizabeth Matthews (40), Sarah (10), Charles (9), Naomi (5), Elizabeth Hopkins (65).

**1851 (Kensworth):** Thomas Matthews (51, b. ~1800, Kensworth), Married, Ag Lab; wife Elizabeth (51, Kensworth); son Charles (19); daughter Naomi (15); Thomas Hopkins (20).

**1861 (North Side Kensworth Road):** Thomas Matthews (63, b. ~1798, Kensworth), Married, Agricultural Labourer; wife Elizabeth (68, Kensworth); daughter Naomi (24); John Evans (11, Kensworth — likely grandson, son of daughter Mary who married Daniel Evans in 1849); David Matthews (8, Kensworth — relationship unclear).

## Death

Thomas Matthews died in **1862** in the Luton registration district (Vol. 3B, p. 283, Jan–Mar 1862) — the same year his father [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews Sr.]] died (4 January 1862). Father and son may have died within weeks of each other.

## Open questions

- When did Mary Squire die? Between 1835 (Naomi's birth) and 1840 (Thomas recorded as widowed at second marriage).
- Did Thomas convert to Mormonism as some of his siblings did?

**Resolved:** [[charles-matthews-1832|Charles Matthews]] (b. ~1832) married Mary (born Kensworth ~1838–41) and settled at Caddington, Bedfordshire, where he raised at least five children. He did not emigrate.

**Resolved:** "David Matthews" (8) in the 1861 household is [[david-matthews-1853|David Matthews]] (b. 1853, Kensworth), who remained in Kensworth through 1881 before moving to Luton. His parentage in the census is unrecorded; [[charles-matthews-1832|Charles]] is the most plausible father.
