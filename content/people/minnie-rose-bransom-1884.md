Minnie Rose Bransom (24 January 1884 – 1960), seventh and youngest child of [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]]. Christened 13 April 1884.

The 1911 census records her as "Rose Bransome," aged 27, Housekeeper, in the household of [[james-hain-1877|James Hain]] at Dell Farm, Whipsnade — her sister [[margaret-anne-bransom-1880|Margaret Anne Bransom]] had married James in 1905, and Rose had come to help manage the farmhouse. She is listed under her middle name rather than Minnie in that record.
