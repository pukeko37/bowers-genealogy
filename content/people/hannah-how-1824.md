Hannah How was born around 1824 in Kingston, Cambridgeshire — a village about 8 miles south-west of Oakington. She is recorded in the 1871 and 1881 censuses with her husband [[newman-thulborn-1821|Newman Thulborn]] at Oakington, giving her age consistently as 47 in 1871 and 52 in 1881 (suggesting birth ~1824 in 1871 and ~1829 in 1881 — a slight discrepancy, probably census rounding). Her birthplace "Kingston, Cambridgeshire" is consistent across both censuses.

FamilySearch records her as "Hannah How" with dates 1824–Deceased (no death date recorded). Her maiden name is given as "How" on the FamilySearch profile. She married Newman Thulborn probably around 1849–1850; their first child Lydia was born 1851.

Nothing is known of Hannah's own family background. She disappears from the record after the 1881 census (when she was in her mid-fifties). She is not recorded as living with any of her children in later censuses that have been checked. Her death date and place are not yet established.
