Sarah Knight was born 10 July 1755, christened 13 July 1755 at Cranfield, Bedfordshire, and died about 1839 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire. She married [[samuel-capel-1750|Samuel Capel]] on 15 November 1772 at Cranfield, a year before her birth year would suggest. She outlived Samuel by thirty years, dying in Millbrook — the same village where her daughter [[sarah-capel-1791|Sarah Capel]] was living in 1841 and 1851.

Her parents were William Knight (1729–?) and Mary Gurney (1733–?), married 21 September 1754 at Cranfield. Siblings: Hannah Knight (1758–?), William Knight (1761–1813), Rachel Knight (1764–1766), Thomas Knight (1770–1800).

Her son John Knight Keppel (1789) carries both her maiden name and her husband's surname — the same practice of embedding mother's maiden names found elsewhere in this research.

Sarah Knight is Caleb Bunker's great-great-grandmother on the maternal Watts line: Sarah → [[sarah-capel-1791|Sarah Capel]] → [[elizabeth-watts-1810|Elizabeth Watts]] → [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]].
