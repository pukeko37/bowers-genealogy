Sarah Matthews was christened 30 January 1803 at Kensworth, Hertfordshire, the second known child of [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews]] and [[mary-wilson-1779|Mary Wilson]]. She was born while the family was still living in Kensworth, Thomas's home parish, before they moved to Whipsnade sometime between 1808 and 1815.

Nothing further is known of Sarah. She does not appear in the 1841 or 1851 censuses with her parents. She may have married and left the area, or died young before those census years.

**Note:** Sarah is distinct from [[susan-matthews-1800|Susan Matthews]] (born Sep 1800), who is thought to be the eldest child — born before Thomas and Mary's formal June 1801 marriage — and who died unmarried in Whipsnade in 1857. Sarah and Susan are two different individuals.

See [[matthews-wilson-1801]] for the family context.
