Adeliza Fuller was born in 1862 in **Hemingford Abbots, Huntingdonshire** — a small parish immediately adjacent to Fenstanton, which explains the proximity to John Tabram's family. Her parents were **Thomas Fuller** and **Rachael Fuller** (née unknown), confirmed by her baptism record. Her siblings recorded in the 1871 census were **Albert R. Fuller** and **Cuthbert T. Fuller**.

She married [[john-tabram-1861|John Tabram (1861–1939)]] of Fenstanton in **December 1879** (St Ives registration district), probably at Fenstanton. Together they raised three children in Fenstanton:

- **Ellen Rachel Tabram** (1881–1882) — died in infancy
- **Ethel Fuller Tabram** (1883–) — middle name "Fuller" taken from Adeliza's birth/maiden name; married a Lomas
- **Mabel Sarah Tabram** (1885–)

The family remained in Fenstanton through the 1911 census, consistent with her husband's lifelong connection to the parish. At some point after 1911, they moved to **1 Southoe, Buckden**, a village about ten miles south-west of Fenstanton — likely to be near their daughter Ethel Lomas.

Adeliza died on **8 February 1938** at 1 Southoe, Buckden. Probate was granted in London on 4 April 1938 to **Ethel Lomas, married woman** (their daughter), with effects of **£609 15s. 8d.** Her husband John survived her by almost exactly one year, dying on 7 February 1939 at the same address.

### Note on her name

"Adeliza" is a medieval name (a variant of Adelaide) that enjoyed a modest Victorian revival. Her surname "Fuller" appears to have been her birth/maiden name, which she gave to her daughter Ethel as a middle name — a common Victorian practice of preserving the mother's family name in a child's given name.
