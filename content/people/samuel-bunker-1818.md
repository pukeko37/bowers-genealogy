Samuel Bunker (born about 1818, Steppingley — death not yet documented) was the son of [[thomas-bunker-1779|Thomas Bunker (1779–1849)]] and Mary Burton, and great-grandson of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and Mary Reade, through the James (1749) → Thomas (1779) line.

His birth in Steppingley (confirmed by three census returns) shows that his father Thomas (1779) had settled in Steppingley before ~1818, migrating from the James (1749) family's Millbrook origins.

He married **Hannah Sear** (born ~1820, Woburn/Water End area, Bedfordshire) in January 1840. Their first child [[ruth-bunker-1840|Ruth]] was born seven months later on 1 August 1840 at Westoning — a pre-marital conception, consistent with the pattern seen elsewhere in this family. See [[bunker-sear-1840]] for the full family.

Samuel was an agricultural labourer throughout his documented life. He appears in the Steppingley censuses from 1841 to 1871, then moves with part of his family to **Little Munden, Hertfordshire** by 1881, where he appears at age 62. His death date has not been established.

The census birth year estimates for Samuel vary slightly (1819–1821 across three censuses), all consistent with a true birth year of about 1818.
