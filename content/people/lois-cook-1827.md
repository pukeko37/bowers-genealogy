Lois Cook (christened 29 April 1827, Kensworth — died 1903) was the youngest child of [[daniel-cook-1790|Daniel Cook]] and [[mary-birchmore-1785|Mary Birchmore]], and the youngest sibling of [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] and [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]].

She must not be confused with [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois Brandsom]] (1843–1924, wife of [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook]]) — an entirely different person from the Bransom/Brandsom family of Edlesborough.

## Marriage

Lois married Henry Harrowell on 2 December 1849 at Whipsnade. Her father Daniel Cook was listed on the marriage record; a witness was John Harrowell. Henry Harrowell (born abt 1827, Whipsnade) was an agricultural labourer, and the couple settled at By Whipsnade Green — the same hamlet as her brother David's family.

## Life

In the 1851 census, Lois is recorded as a **School Mistress** — a notable occupation for a working-class woman in this period, suggesting education or literacy above the agricultural norm of her family. Her husband Henry was listed as an Agricultural Labourer. Margaret Harrowell (65, not known where born) also appears in the 1851 household — possibly Henry's mother.

By 1861 the family at By Whipsnade Green included four children: Rhoda (8), Mary (6), Emma (3), and Dorcas (1).

## Children

- Rhoda Harrowell (born 27 Sep 1852, christened 18 Mar 1853, Wesleyan Methodist, Dunstable)
- Mary Harrowell (born abt 1855, Whipsnade)
- Emma Harrowell (born abt 1858, Whipsnade)
- Dorcas Harrowell (born 8 Jan 1860, christened 26 Mar 1860, Wesleyan Methodist, Dunstable)
- Further children may appear in census records from 1871 onward.

The Wesleyan Methodist christenings at Dunstable suggest the Harrowell family worshipped at a Dunstable chapel rather than the Whipsnade parish church — consistent with a Nonconformist tradition similar to the Brandsom family (who also registered children at Wesleyan Methodist Dunstable).

## Later Life

Census references exist for 1871, 1881, 1891, and 1901 but were not fully extracted from the clipping. Lois died in 1903.
