Harriet Dean was born in 1844 and died in 1931, aged approximately 87. She married [[samuel-bunker-1842|Samuel Bunker (1842–1913)]] on **25 December 1865** at Millbrook — Christmas Day. FamilySearch ID: MM1H-PVN.

Her origins — parents, birthplace, siblings — are unknown. The maiden name Dean is preserved in her daughter's name: Minnie **Maria Dean** Bunker (1870).

Samuel died in 1913. Harriet survived him by 18 years, dying in 1931. In 1919 she was approximately 75 years old.

Harriet is **Mrs Bunker of the 1919 Huett effigy story** — confirmed by Samuel's obituary (*Bedfordshire Times*, 20 June 1913), which establishes him as the Millbrook church clerk and sexton for approximately 20 years. It was Samuel who encountered the buried effigies while digging in the churchyard; after his death, Harriet kept a stone head he had found there. In 1919 she led excavators to the spot, and the two Tudor effigies of William and Mary Huett were recovered. Her location in the 1921 census has not yet been checked.
