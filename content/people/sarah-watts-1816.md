Sarah Watts (born 1816, Edlesborough, Buckinghamshire) married [[jeremiah-bransom-1808|Jeremiah Bransom]] on 7 November 1833 at Edlesborough. Together they had at least six children.

By 1881 Sarah (listed as "Sarah Brandsom, 68, from Edlesborough") was living in the household of her daughter [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois]] and son-in-law [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook]] at Whipsnade, suggesting she was widowed or that Jeremiah had left the area.

Her children: Frederic (1836), Betsy (1838), Elizabeth (1838), Alfred (1840), [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois]] (1843–1924), and [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin]] (1850–1928).
