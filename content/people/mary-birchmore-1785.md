Mary Birchmore was born in 1785 and christened 17 April 1785 in Whipsnade, Bedfordshire. Her parents were **William Birchmore** (1751–?) and **Mary Simonds** (1750–?), who married in Whipsnade on 9 August 1776. Her siblings were William Jr (1777), Thomas (1779), Elizabeth (1781), and Hannah (1792).

She married [[daniel-cook-1790|Daniel Cook]] on 12 October 1810 and had seven children, the most significant to this wiki being [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] and [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]]. She appears in census records in Whipsnade in 1841 and 1851. Her death date is unknown.

## The Birchmore Family of Whipsnade

Mary's Birchmore family had been in Whipsnade for at least two generations before her. Her father William Birchmore (1751) is the earliest recorded Birchmore in these clippings. George Birchmore Plummer Sr's father was named George Birchmore — a Whipsnade Birchmore of a generation that could have been a sibling of Mary herself (born roughly 1780–1795 would fit). If GBP Sr's father was among William Birchmore's children not captured in the Mary Birchmore record, then GBP Sr and Ruth Cooke would have been second cousins before their 1867 marriage. This is a plausible hypothesis given the tight community, but is not confirmed by available clippings.
