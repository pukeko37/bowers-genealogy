Elizabeth Sarah Morse was born in 1852 in Wiltshire (birth registration: Swindon). By 1881 she had moved to London and was living in St Pancras, where she remained through the 1911 census at St Jude, Gray's Inn Road. Also recorded as Elizabeth Sarah Morris.

She had a long-term relationship with [[george-birchmore-plummer-jr-1850|George Birchmore Plummer Jr]] — no formal marriage record has been found — and together they had four children: [[rosa-birchmore-1874|Rosa]], [[edith-eleanor-birchmore-1876|Edith Eleanor]], [[george-william-birchmore-1880|George William]], and [[florence-eliza-birchmore-1884|Florence Eliza]]. All used the Birchmore surname. See [[birchmore-morse-1874]].

Elizabeth died on 16 July 1928 at Belmont, Withycombe Raleigh, Devon — a parish near Exmouth, East Devon. Probate was granted in London on 5 October 1928 to her son [[george-william-birchmore-1880|George William Birchmore]], who was recorded as "of no occupation." Her estate was valued at **£2177 18s. 10d.**, a substantial sum suggesting accumulated savings or an inheritance. She is described in the probate record as a widow, confirming that [[george-birchmore-plummer-jr-1850|George Birchmore Plummer Jr]] (d. 1920) predeceased her.

Her son George William had moved to Sidmouth by 1911; Elizabeth followed him to Devon in later life, though she settled at Withycombe Raleigh (Exmouth area) rather than Sidmouth itself. See [[elizabeth-sarah-birchmore-probate-1928]].
