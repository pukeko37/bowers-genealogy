George Bunker was born in 1868 in Millbrook, the fourth child of [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker (1840–1914)]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]]. FamilySearch records his christening as 19 October 1873 — the same date attributed to his siblings John (born 1871) and James (born 1873). This is almost certainly a data error for George and John; the date may reflect a batch parish christening of younger children on that day.

George was at home in Millbrook in 1871 and 1881. He followed his brothers north to the Nottinghamshire coalfields, and is recorded there in the 1911 census.

**1896**: George married his first wife, **Catherine Ward** (1875–1902), on 25 January 1896 at Mansfield, Nottinghamshire. They had three children: Annie (1896), Thomas (1899), and Dorothy (1902). Catherine died in 1902 — possibly in or around childbirth with Dorothy.

**1908**: George married his second wife, **Annie Maria Elston** (born 1886), in Nottinghamshire. They had one child: Frank (1909–1993).

George died on 7 June 1916 at Grimsby, Lincolnshire — away from his Nottinghamshire home. He was buried at South Normanton, Derbyshire (see [[south-normanton]]). Whether his death was connected to wartime work or service is not established.
