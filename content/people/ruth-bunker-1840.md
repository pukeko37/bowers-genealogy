Ruth Bunker (born 1 August 1840, Westoning, Bedfordshire — death not yet documented) was the eldest child of [[samuel-bunker-1818|Samuel Bunker]] (~1818) and Hannah Sear, and great-great-granddaughter of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] through the James (1749) → Thomas (1779) → Samuel (1818) line. FamilySearch ID LKBG-11S.

She is the confirmed identity of **RB-K** in [[ruth-bunker-findmypast-register]].

## Birth and christening

Ruth was born in Westoning — her mother Hannah was from the Woburn/Water End area of Bedfordshire, and Westoning lies between that area and Steppingley where the family lived. She was born seven months after her parents' January 1840 marriage, indicating a pre-marital conception. She was not christened until **5 May 1844** at Steppingley, St Lawrence — aged 3 years 9 months. This delayed christening matches the Nonconformist late-registration practice documented throughout the Bunker family.

## Life in Steppingley

Ruth spent her entire documented life in Steppingley, appearing there in four consecutive censuses (1841, 1851, 1861, 1871) as a daughter in her parents' household. She never left home during this period — still living with her parents at age 30 in 1871.

Her occupation was in the Bedfordshire straw trade:
- **1861**: Straw Plaiter (age 20)
- **1871**: Straw Plaiter (age 30)
- **1881**: Hat Sewer (Straw) — an evolution from raw plaiting to finished hats

## Move to Little Munden

By the **1881 census** the family had moved to **Little Munden, Hertfordshire**, about 12 miles south of Steppingley. Ruth (age ~40) was still living with her father Samuel (62) and a much younger brother Edward (14). This move is why she disappears from FindMyPast's Bedfordshire search results after 1871 — she was in Hertfordshire, outside the search scope. See [[ruth-bunker-findmypast-register]].

## The FMP birth year discrepancy

All four census appearances in FindMyPast show birth year "1841." Her actual birth date is 1 August 1840. The discrepancy arises because all censuses were taken in March–June, before her August birthday: at each enumeration she was 0, 10, 20, and 30 years old respectively, giving a consistently derived year of 1841. There is no genuine discrepancy.
