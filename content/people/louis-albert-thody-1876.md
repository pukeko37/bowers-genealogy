Louis Albert Thody (8 December 1876 – 1975) was the second child of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] and [[mathilde-parent-1853|Mathilde Parent]], and the longest-lived of William's children — he died at approximately 98 or 99 years of age.

Born at Cotton End, Bedfordshire, he was christened on 13 February 1877 at the Wesleyan Methodist chapel, Toddington, Bedfordshire. When his mother returned to Canada c. 1879–1881 to care for her father, Louis Albert went with her. He appears in the 1891 Totternhoe census as "Albert Thody," age 14, Printers Apprentice, born Cardington.

By 1904 he had emigrated to Utah, where he was baptised into the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints on 16 November 1904 (confirmed by Stewart Eccles). Multiple LDS membership records (under the names Louis Albert Thody, Lewis Albert Thody, and Louis Abert Thody) attach him to his parents William Allen Thody and Matilda E. Parent.

In 1938 he married Lottie Olvia Burch in Los Angeles, California, at the age of 61. He was a half-brother of [[harry-thody-1894|Harry Thody]].
