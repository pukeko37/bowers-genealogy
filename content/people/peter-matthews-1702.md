Peter Matthews was born about 1702 at Dunstable, Bedfordshire, the eldest son of [[peter-matthews-1665|Peter Matthews (1665)]] and [[mary-jenkins-1665|Mary Jenkins]]. He was buried at Dunstable on 26 June 1748. He married **[[mary-busby-1709|Mary Busby]]** on 21 May 1727 and had nine children, all christened at Dunstable. Among them were [[peter-matthews-1734|Peter Matthews (1734)]], who continued the line through to Kensworth and eventually Whipsnade, and **Thomas Matthews (1741)**, whose adult life has not been researched but who is a candidate for the father of [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews (1767)]] — see [[peter-matthews-1702-familysearch]] for the reasoning.

See [[matthews-busby-1727]] for the family.
