Peter Matthews was christened 24 October 1802 at [[whipsnade|Whipsnade]], Bedfordshire, the third child of [[peter-matthews-1770|Peter Matthews (1770)]] and [[mary-walker-1766|Mary Walker]]. He married **Sarah Gates** (1803–1851) on an unknown date in 1825 at Studham, Bedfordshire — the neighbouring parish to Whipsnade, where his mother Mary Walker would later live in her old age (1851, in daughter Sarah's household).

Peter and Sarah raised their family in Whipsnade, where all eleven children were christened. Sarah died in 1851, leaving Peter a widower at about 49. He survived for another forty years, dying in 1892 aged approximately 90 — a longevity shared by his mother Mary Walker, who lived to roughly 89–93, and his father's line more broadly.

His son [[william-mathews-1837|William Mathews (1837)]] lived at **Houchen End**, Whipsnade — the same hamlet long associated with the [[matthews-wilson-1801|Thomas Matthews Sr.]] household and with [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer Sr.]] This address coincidence, combined with the shared Kensworth origin of Peter (1770) and Thomas (1767), is the strongest available argument that the two Matthews lines were related, though no documentary evidence has yet established the link.

See [[matthews-gates-1825]] for the family narrative.
