Susan Matthews was born 17 September 1800 (christened 26 October 1800) in [[whipsnade|Whipsnade]], Bedfordshire. She was the second child of [[peter-matthews-1770|Peter Matthews]] (1770–?) and [[mary-walker-1766|Mary Walker]] (1766–1859), who married in Whipsnade in June 1798. Susan was thus a native of the village where she would spend her entire life.

**Correction (2026-05-20):** An earlier version of this page incorrectly attributed Susan to the [[matthews-wilson-1801|Thomas Matthews + Mary Wilson]] family on the basis of geographic proximity and surname. The FamilySearch record (KZB3-93C), which is now supported by multiple independent sources confirming Peter Matthews as her father, establishes her correct family of origin. She is not a child of Thomas Matthews (1767) and Mary Wilson.

She married **[[thomas-batchelar-1803|Thomas Batchelar]]** on 22 May 1826 at Shenley, Hertfordshire. Their four children were all christened in Whipsnade: Emma (1827), [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza]] (1829), Ann (1834), and Elizabeth (1842).

The 1851 census records her occupation as **Grocer** — an unusual independent role for a married woman of the time, and a sign of some household standing beyond the agricultural labourer's family her husband headed.

Susan died on 20 November 1857 in Whipsnade, aged 57. After her death, Thomas remained in Whipsnade and married **Susan Durent** (née Maynard) in January 1868.

### The Matthews surname connection

Susan's daughter [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza]] married [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]] in 1859. George's first wife was [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] (1815–1858), also from Whipsnade. Whether Anne was from Peter Matthews's family — perhaps a grandchild or cousin — has not been confirmed, but it is probable that both women drew from the same Matthews community rooted in Whipsnade across this period.
