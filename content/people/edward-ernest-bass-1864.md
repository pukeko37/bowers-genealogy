Edward Ernest Bass was born in **1864** in Maulden, Bedfordshire — the youngest child of [[daniel-bass-1829|Daniel Bass]] (1829–1868) and [[mary-ward-tall-1827|Mary Ward Tall]] (1827–1907). His father died in 1868 when Edward was about four, leaving his mother to raise three children alone. He died on **14 August 1936** in Flitwick, Bedfordshire, aged 72 — having lived his entire life within a few miles of his birthplace.

## Childhood in Maulden

The 1871 census shows him at seven as a scholar, at Duck End, Maulden — the youngest in the household with his widowed mother Mary (staymaker, head) and sisters Sarah (11) and Florence (9). By 1881, at seventeen, he was working as a **butcher**, still at home in Maulden with his mother and sister Sarah.

## Marriage and Career

In **1885** (per the marriage registration index; the FamilySearch tree records 1886) Edward married **Mary Ann Dennis** in the Ampthill district. The 1891 census finds the couple in Ampthill, where Edward had retrained as a **carpenter** — also present were Eliza Dennis (34) and Sophia Dennis (59), likely Mary Ann's sister and mother. By 1901 the couple had moved north to **Kettering, Northamptonshire**, where Edward worked as a **carpenter and joiner**. By 1911 they had returned to Bedfordshire, settling in **Flitwick** — the village immediately south of Maulden — where Edward was working as a **carpenter on a small holder** (a smallholding, i.e., a small farm).

He remained in Flitwick for the rest of his life, at **16 Maulden-road** — on the road that leads back to his birth village.

The Kettering move is explained by family: his first cousin [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick Bunker]] was living in Kettering from 1891, and his children [[beulah-bunker-1896|Beulah]] (1896) and [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland]] (1898) were born there. Edward and Frederick were first cousins — their mothers, [[mary-ward-tall-1827|Mary Ward Tall]] and [[rebecca-tall-1844|Rebecca Tall]], were sisters. Edward was in Kettering in 1901 (and evidently earlier, during Beulah's and Roland's births); Frederick had moved on to Ilford, Essex by that census. The cousins shared the same Kettering years before parting ways.

## Second Marriage

Mary Ann Bass (née Dennis) disappears from the record after 1911. The probate entry on Edward's death in 1936 names **Lucy Washington Bass, widow** as the administrator — meaning Lucy was his wife at death. She had presumably married him as Lucy Washington some time after Mary Ann's death (date unknown). No children of either marriage appear in the sources.

## Death and Probate

Edward Ernest Bass died on **14 August 1936** at Flitwick, Bedfordshire, aged 72. Probate was granted in London on 29 September 1936 to Lucy Washington Bass. His estate was valued at **£156 4s. 7d.**

## Family Connections

Edward was the youngest of three children in the [[bass-tall-1855|Bass-Tall family]]. His elder sister [[sarah-bass-1859|Sarah Bass]] married James Lay and settled in Lavendon, Buckinghamshire, living to 1951. Florence Bass (1862), the middle sibling, disappears from the record after the 1871 census. Through his mother's family, Edward was nephew by marriage to [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]] and first cousin once removed to [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick Bunker]].
