William Bunker was christened at Millbrook on 6 September 1767, the first child of [[edward-bunker-1742|Edward Bunker]] (1742–1814) and Mary Ashby (1745–1813). He spent his entire life in Millbrook and married twice — a long life in the village, with at least eight children across both marriages.

## Marriages and Children

### First marriage: Ann Denton (1766–1807)

William married **Ann Denton** on 15 August 1792 at Millbrook. Ann was born in 1766, a year before William. They had at least four children:

|-------|-----------|------|-------|
| Rachel Bunker | 12 Mar 1794, Millbrook | — | |
| Elizabeth Bunker | 28 Mar 1798, Millbrook | 1798 | Died in infancy |
| William Bunker | 28 Jun 1800, Millbrook | 1800 | Died in infancy |
| Sarah Bunker | 28 Jun 1807, Millbrook | — | See note below |

Ann Denton died in 1807. **Sarah Bunker (chr. 28 Jun 1807)** is attributed in FamilySearch to the Sarah Beal marriage, but since that marriage did not take place until 1808, she was almost certainly Ann's last child — possibly born at the cost of her mother's life.

### Second marriage: Sarah Beal (1776)

William married **Sarah Beal** on 12 September 1808 at Millbrook, a year after Ann's death. Sarah was born in 1776 in Lidlington, Bedfordshire — a village about 3 miles from Millbrook — the daughter of William Beal (1747–1828) and Martha Mitchel (1753–1787). Her mother had died when she was about eleven. Known children by Sarah:

The FamilySearch sources record further events at Millbrook in June 1813, December 1815, and October 1821 not yet identified — these may be additional children who died in infancy or whose records are not yet in the tree.

**Samuel Bunker** (~1820): A source dated 27 October 1819 at Millbrook is not linked to any named child in FamilySearch, but the 1841 census shows William (aged ~70) and Sarah with a son Samuel (~20) — born approximately 1820–21. This source is the most likely record of his christening.

## In the Censuses

**1841:** William (~70, actual ~74 — census rounded down to nearest 5), with wife Sarah Beal (~65) and son Samuel (~20). The household was in the Village, Millbrook.

William died 1847 and Sarah 1848. No trace of Samuel after the 1841 census in parish or census records — but a newspaper entry from October 1892 (Bedfordshire Mercury, Labourers' Friend Society) lists "Samuel Bunker, 73, years with Mr G Allen, Ampthill" winning first prize for longest service with the same employer. If 73 is his age, he was born ~1819, which matches this Samuel exactly. This would place him in Ampthill at age ~73, still working for a single employer. See [[joseph-samuel-bunker-newspapers-findmypast]] for the full context.

## 1815 Fence Maintenance List

The list of Millbrook properties responsible for maintaining the church fences (1815) includes a **William BUNKER** at the 4th pole — a cottage near the parsonage, landlord Lord Ossory. William would have been about 48 at the time, which fits as his own tenancy.

## Notes

The Beal family was from Lidlington, not Millbrook. Solomon Beale (1825–1894), the Millbrook Methodist lay preacher, may be connected to the same Beal family but the relationship has not been traced.

Rachel Bunker (1794), William's eldest surviving child, and William Bunker (1811) and Emily Bunker (1817) by Sarah Beal have not been traced beyond their christening records.
