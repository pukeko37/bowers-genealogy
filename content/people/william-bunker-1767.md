William Bunker was christened at Millbrook on 6 September 1767, the first child of [[edward-bunker-1742|Edward Bunker]] (1742–1814) and Mary Ashby (1745–1813). He spent his entire life in Millbrook, where he appears in the 1841 census and died in 1847.

He married **Sarah Beal**, who survived him by about a year, dying in 1848. They had at least one son, **Samuel (~1820–21)**, who was still living with them in 1841.

## In the Censuses

**1841:** William (~70, actual age ~74) with wife Sarah Beal (~60) and son Samuel (~20). The 1841 census rounded adult ages down to the nearest 5, so his recorded age of 70 is consistent with an actual birth year of 1767 (age 74 → recorded as 70).

He is the last of his immediate household in Millbrook; he died 1847, Sarah 1848, and no further trace of Samuel has been found.

## 1815 Fence Maintenance List

The list of properties adjoining Millbrook church (1815) includes a **William BUNKER** at the 4th pole — a cottage near the parsonage, landlord Lord Ossory. William would have been approximately 48 at the time, which is consistent with this being his own tenancy. This is the most plausible identification for this entry.

## Notes

The Beal/Beale family was well established in Millbrook (Solomon Beale the Methodist lay preacher was a Millbrook Beal). The marriage of William to Sarah Beal has not been located in the registers.
