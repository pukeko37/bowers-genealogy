Mary Reade was born on 28 October 1702 and died on 2 July 1775, buried at Steppingley or Millbrook, Bedfordshire. (Steppingley and Millbrook shared parish register records, and the two parishes are adjacent.) She is Roland Bunker's great-great-great-grandmother.

She married **[[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker]]** on 23 January 1728 at Toddington, Bedfordshire. Together they had twelve children, all documented in FamilySearch with 19 attached sources — the most thoroughly evidenced ancestor at this level of the tree. Her christenings of all twelve children are documented, spanning from 1728 to 1754.

Mary predeceased her husband by nine years. She was 72 at the time of her death. Her maiden name Reade is known only from the marriage record; in all christening records she appears simply as "Mary."
