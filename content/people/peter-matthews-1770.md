Peter Matthews was born in 1770 and christened 7 January 1770 at Kensworth, Hertfordshire. He was the son of [[peter-matthews-1734|Peter Matthews (1734–1812)]] and **Susannah Durrant** (1739–1821), who had married on 12 October 1761 at Flamstead, Hertfordshire. His known siblings were Susannah Matthews (1763) and Mary Matthews (1767). See [[matthews-durrant-1761]] for the family.

His father Peter (1734) was the son of [[peter-matthews-1702|Peter Matthews (1702)]] and [[mary-busby-1709|Mary Busby]], of Dunstable — meaning Peter (1770) was the fourth consecutive Peter Matthews in the direct line. The family had been rooted at Dunstable for at least two generations before Peter (1734) settled at Kensworth.

He married [[mary-walker-1766|Mary Walker]] on 28 June 1798 at [[whipsnade|Whipsnade]], Bedfordshire — moving from his native Kensworth across the county border. They settled in Whipsnade, where all their children were christened.

**Children** (all at Whipsnade unless noted):
1. Mary Matthews — christened 12 May 1799
2. [[susan-matthews-1800|Susan Matthews]] — christened 26 October 1800; married Thomas Batchelar 1826; mother of [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]]
3. Peter Matthews — christened 24 October 1802
4. Thomas Matthews — christened 8 April 1804
5. James Matthews — christened 25 March 1806 (died 1889)
6. Sarah Matthews — christened 19 June 1808 (died 1869); married John Grace; their daughter's household hosted Mary Walker in 1851
7. Richard Matthews — christened 21 April 1810, **Studham**, Bedfordshire (died 1883)
8. John Matthews — christened 1813 (died 1890)

In the 1841 census Peter appears on Whipsnade Green ("On The Green"), age 70, with his wife Mary age 70 and two young girls (likely grandchildren). No death record has been found; he was at least 71 in 1841.

### Ancestry

Peter's parents — Peter Matthews (1734–1812) and Susannah Durrant (1739–1821) — were married at Flamstead, Hertfordshire in 1761. Flamstead is the same Hertfordshire village associated with the Birchmore and Batchelor families in this cluster, though no specific connection between these families and the Matthews family at Flamstead has been established.

### Relationship to the Matthews-Wilson family

A separate Matthews family also active in Whipsnade — [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews]] (1767) and [[mary-wilson-1779|Mary Wilson]] — produced [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] (1815), who became [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr's]] first wife. Whether Peter Matthews (1770) and Thomas Matthews (1767) were related is an open question — but a strong candidate now exists.

Peter (1734) had a brother **Thomas Matthews (christened 23 September 1741, Dunstable)** — see [[matthews-busby-1727]]. If this Thomas (1741) had a son Thomas in 1767, that son would have been born in Kensworth, where the family had settled, and Peter (1770) and Thomas (1767) would be **first cousins**. The circumstantial evidence is substantial: same village of origin, same Whipsnade destination, same Houchen End hamlet. Establishing what became of Thomas (1741) after 1741 would resolve this.
