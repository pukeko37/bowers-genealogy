Mary Hall (1730–1782) married [[richard-norman-1723|Richard Norman]] on 1 October 1749 at Over, Cambridgeshire, and is the mother of [[robert-norman-1769|Robert Norman]] (1769–1842).

Her parentage, birthplace, and background are unknown from current sources. She is documented only by her marriage record and her presence in Robert Norman's FamilySearch profile.

Robert was born in 1769 — when Mary was about 39 and Richard about 46. He was their only documented child. Mary died in 1782, a year before Richard. Robert was thirteen at his mother's death, and fourteen at his father's.
