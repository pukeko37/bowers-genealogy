William Bunker (1731–1790) was the third child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and [[mary-reade-1702|Mary Reade]], christened 26 March 1731 at Toddington, Bedfordshire. He died in 1790 at Westoning — the ancestral home of the Bunker family before their move to Millbrook, where his father Thomas had been christened in 1704.

No spouse or children are documented in FamilySearch. He is a dead end in the current record set.

His return to or remaining in Westoning rather than staying in Millbrook sets him apart from most of his siblings, who stayed in the Millbrook–Ampthill–Steppingley orbit.
