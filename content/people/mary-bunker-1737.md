Mary Bunker (abt 1737–1801) was the fifth child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and [[mary-reade-1702|Mary Reade]]. Her FamilySearch record carries a date anomaly: indexed birth year 1737, but the attached christening record reads 22 July 1735 at Toddington. The 1735 record may belong to a different child or be a misattachment; the 1737 date is consistent with her position in the family sequence.

She married **[[william-roffe|William Roffe]]** on 4 May 1764 at Millbrook and had five children. She was buried at Steppingley on 24 April 1801. Her brother [[william-bunker-1740|William Bunker (1740)]] also settled in Steppingley — two siblings in the same village.

See [[roffe-bunker-1764]] for the full family narrative.
