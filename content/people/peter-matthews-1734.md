Peter Matthews was born 28 January 1734 at Kensworth, Bedfordshire, and christened 28 June 1734 at Dunstable — a five-month gap that likely reflects the family's movement between parishes. He was the son of [[peter-matthews-1702|Peter Matthews (1702)]] and [[mary-busby-1709|Mary Busby]].

He married **Susannah Durrant** (1739–1821) on 12 October 1761 at **Flamstead, Hertfordshire** — the same village where the Birchmore and Batchelor families were rooted. Their three known children were all christened at Kensworth: Susannah (1763), Mary (1767), and [[peter-matthews-1770|Peter Matthews (1770)]]. He died at Kensworth on 6 January 1812, aged about 78.

His brother **Thomas Matthews (1741)** — son of the same Peter (1702) and Mary Busby — is a candidate for the father of [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews (1767)]], patriarch of the parallel LDS/Wilson line at Whipsnade. If so, Thomas (1767) and Peter (1770) were first cousins, explaining their shared Kensworth origin, parallel migration to Whipsnade, and documented convergence at Houchen End.
