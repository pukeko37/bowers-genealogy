Rhoda Spring Bottoms was born in November 1806 in Wootton, Bedfordshire, and christened 17 May 1807 at Wootton by Bedford. She died on 4 January 1884 at Haynes, Bedfordshire, and was buried at St Mary's Churchyard, Haynes. She was the wife of [[joseph-geary-1804|Joseph Geary]], mother of [[john-geary-1825|John Geary]], and paternal grandmother of [[eliza-geary-1859|Eliza Geary]].

**Origins.** Her father was [[william-spring-1779|William Spring]] (1779–Deceased) of Shillington, Bedfordshire; her mother was [[sarah-bottoms-1786|Sarah Bottoms]] (1786–1829). See [[spring-bottoms-1806]]. The christening record gives her name as "Rhoda or Phoebe Spring or Bottoms" — the dual surname reflecting the ambiguous status of her parents' relationship (Rhoda was born before William Spring's later marriage to Mary, about 1808). She was born in Wootton, a different parish from the Spring family's Shillington base.

**One known sibling** from Sarah Bottoms: Phoebe Spring (1807), born just one year after Rhoda.

**Marriage.** Rhoda married [[joseph-geary-1804|Joseph Geary]] on 20 April 1824 at Clophill, Bedfordshire. She was 17; Joseph was about 20. Clophill lies immediately adjacent to both Haynes and Maulden — the cluster where both families were rooted. See [[geary-spring-1824]].

**Family life.** The couple settled in Haynes and raised ten children there over nearly three decades (1825–1853). Rhoda is in the 1841 census at Plum's Lane, Haynes, and appears in subsequent census returns at the same address. She lived in Haynes her entire married life and died there in 1884, predating her husband Joseph by six years and her son John by eleven years.

**Significance.** Through Rhoda, the Geary family connects into the Spring line — a Shillington/Bedfordshire agricultural family stretching back at least to [[william-spring-1753|William Spring]] (1753) and [[sarah-kitchener-1752|Sarah Kitchener]] (1752), and through the Kitchener family to **William Kitchener** (1721) and Elizabeth Gudgeon (1727–1764). This is the deepest ancestral line currently established in the Geary branch.
