Lena Florence Thody (c. 1889–1927) was the youngest child of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] and [[mathilde-parent-1853|Mathilde Parent]]. She was born in Totternhoe, Bedfordshire, about a year before her mother died (1892). She appears in the 1891 census at Totternhoe as "Florence Thody," age 1. By 1901 she was 11, living at Studham with her father and step-mother [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]].

She died in 1927 at approximately 37–38, circumstances unknown. She was a half-sister of [[harry-thody-1894|Harry Thody]].

### Notes

- The FamilySearch print view gives her name as "Lena Florence Thody" but she appears as "Florence Lena Thody" in the 1901 census and as "Florence Thody" in the 1891 census.
