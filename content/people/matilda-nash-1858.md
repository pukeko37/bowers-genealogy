Matilda Nash (1858–1936) is listed in the FamilySearch tree as having "lived together" with [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] and having an illegitimate daughter with him: [[mary-ann-nash-1880|Mary Ann Nash]] (1880–1963).

The relationship apparently occurred c. 1879–1880, while William's wife [[mathilde-parent-1853|Mathilde Parent]] was in Canada caring for her father. Mary Ann was born in Kentish Town, London — a long way from William's Police Constable posting in Bedfordshire. Nothing further is known of Matilda Nash or the circumstances of the relationship.

By 1891 Mary Ann (as "Minnie Thody") was living in the Thody household at Totternhoe, presumably taken in by William.
