Mary Eusden was born in 1771 and died in 1825, buried at Fenstanton, Huntingdonshire. She is identified as the wife of [[thomas-euseden-1764|Thomas Euseden]] (1764–1842) on the basis that she is the only unaccounted-for Eusden burial in Fenstanton across the entire period 1797–1850 — the span covering the whole family. She took the Eusden name on marriage; her maiden name is unknown.

She and Thomas married no later than ~1796, given that their eldest child [[william-eusden|William]] was born in 1797. Their five children were:
1. [[william-eusden|William Eusden]] (1797–1843)
2. [[thomas-eusden-1799|Thomas Eusden]] (1799–1835)
3. [[sarah-eusden-1804|Sarah Eusden]] (1804–1833)
4. [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary Eusden]] (1807–1841)
5. [[henry-eusden|Henry Eusden]] (1809–1839)

She died in 1825 aged ~54, when her youngest child Henry was ~16 and her eldest William ~28. Her eldest son [[william-eusden|William]] married Sarah Cox at Fenstanton in 1825 — possibly the same year as his mother's death. The other siblings' marriages followed over the next eight years.

Thomas outlived her by seventeen years, dying in November 1842. He did not remarry; the 1833 Fenstanton marriage "Thos Eusden × Mary Ann James" was their son Thomas, not the father.

Her Cambridgeshire origins remain possible: the christening of her daughter Mary at Ely in January 1808 suggests the family may have been temporarily in the Ely area around 1807–1808, which could point to her having roots there.
