Florence Sarah Fensome (born 1884, Pilsley, Derbyshire), youngest child of [[phoebe-alice-bunker-1843|Phoebe Alice Bunker]] and [[samuel-fensome-1842|Samuel Fensome]]. Married **Leonard Holland** by 1911; the couple were living at Hucknall, Nottinghamshire, and Phoebe and Samuel came to live with them in their later years.
