Frederick Charles Tall was born in 1846 in Ampthill, Bedfordshire — the son of [[edward-tall-1806|Edward Tall]] (carpenter) and [[sarah-ward-1806|Sarah Ward]]. He was a younger sibling of [[rebecca-tall-1844|Rebecca Tall]] (born 1844).

Frederick died on 21 May 1859, aged 12–13, at **Grey Friars Walk, St Paul's, Bedford**. He was buried on 25 May 1859 at Foster Hill Road Cemetery, Bedford (plot D9-76). His death was announced in the Bedfordshire Times and Independent on 28 May 1859. The family attended Howard Chapel, a Nonconformist congregation in Bedford; Howard Chapel's burial ground had closed in 1855, so actual interments went to Foster Hill Road Cemetery while the chapel maintained its death register.

Frederick died eleven years before his sister Rebecca — who also died young, aged 26, in July 1870. The family experienced significant child mortality. His early death in Bedford suggests the Tall family was resident there by the late 1850s, at least part of the year, while Sarah Ward's census records show her remaining in Maulden through 1891.

Frederick's page also provides the documentary confirmation that Sarah's maiden name was Ward (the record reads "Edward Tall, a carpenter, and Sarah (nee Ward)") — resolving an open question in the wiki.
