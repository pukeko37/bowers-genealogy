Charles Cook was born in 1847 in Whipsnade, Bedfordshire — the eldest of three illegitimate children of [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] (1820–1904). His father is unknown. His siblings were [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]] (1848) and [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] (1856).

Charles appears in Whipsnade in the 1861 census, listed at By Whipsnade Green alongside Ruth (head, Dressmaker), his sister Mary (13), Lucy (4), and a lodger George Gutteridge. He does not appear in any subsequent census record; he may have left Whipsnade after 1861. His further history is unknown.

Note: Charles Cook (1847) is entirely distinct from [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook (1843–1908)]], a Whipsnade man of the same name who was the son of [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] and Elizabeth Frost and who married [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois Brandsom]]. The two have been confused in earlier records. Also distinct from [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] (1816), Ruth's brother, who married Charlotte Matthews.
