Edward Tall was born on 9 November 1806 in Ampthill, Bedfordshire, christened/baptised the same day. He was a **carpenter**. Census records place him in Maulden, Bedfordshire through at least 1861; by 1870 he was living at **Albert Street, St Peter's, Bedford**. He died on 16 July 1870 in Bedford, aged 63, and was buried on 19 July 1870 at **Foster Hill Road Cemetery, Bedford**. Howard Chapel's burial ground had closed in 1855; Edward's death was recorded in the Howard Chapel register, but the actual burial was at Foster Hill Road. Howard Chapel was the Tall family's congregation — Edward, his son [[william-tall-1830|William]], and his son [[frederick-charles-tall-1846|Frederick Charles]] all appear in its register.

He married [[sarah-ward-1806|Sarah Ward]] on **4 May 1827 in Maulden** and they had six children together: [[mary-ward-tall-1827|Mary Ward]] (1827), [[william-tall-1830|William]] (1830), James (1832–1833), Susan (1837–1848), [[rebecca-tall-1844|Rebecca]] (1844), and [[frederick-charles-tall-1846|Frederick Charles]] (1847). Four of the six died before him — only Mary Ward and Rebecca survived to adulthood, and Rebecca died in July 1870 just nine days before Edward himself.

The timing of Edward's death is striking: Rebecca died 7 July 1870; Edward died 16 July 1870, nine days later. Whether his death was coincidence, grief-related, or from the same illness that took Rebecca is unknown.

Edward's death in 1870 left [[sarah-ward-1806|Sarah Ward]] alone to raise their grandson [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick]], then just one year old. She did so in Maulden until Frederick joined his father Caleb in Derbyshire sometime before 1881.
