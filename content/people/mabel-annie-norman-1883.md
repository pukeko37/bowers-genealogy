Mabel Annie Norman was born 26 October 1883, the eldest of nine children of [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] and [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen Birchmore]] in the [[norman-birchmore-1883|Norman-Birchmore family]]. She was 24 when her father died in 1907.

Mabel married John James Mason (Jack, 1878–1962) in 1912 in Hackney, forming the [[mason-norman-1912|Mason-Norman family]]. John was one of the orphaned Mason brothers from Hosier Lane. They ran a newsagent and stationer's shop at 150 Acre Lane, Clapham. Mabel's sister Nellie lived with them and worked in the shop.

Their children were John Isaac Mason (1913–1984) and Catherine Ellen Mason (1916–1970).

In 1939, the household at 150 Acre Lane included Mabel, John, Nellie, and John's brother Richard William Mason with his wife Martha. In 1944–45, the shop was damaged by V-weapons. Mabel, John, and Nellie fled to 32 Park Hill, Harpenden to stay with Ivy and Roland. After the war, they rebuilt and reopened the shop.

Mabel died in 1969 in Camberwell, aged 86.
