Elizabeth Matthews (born ~1847, Whipsnade), second child of [[able-matthews-1813|Able Matthews]] and [[mary-walker-1817|Mary Ann Walker]]. She sailed with her family from Liverpool in February 1855, aged about 8. Her fate after St. Louis is untraced; she does not appear in known Utah records.
