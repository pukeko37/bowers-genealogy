Albert Geary was born 26 July 1866 in Haynes, Bedfordshire (christened 16 September 1866), the ninth child of [[john-geary-1825|John Geary]] and [[lucy-morris-1826|Lucy Morris]]. He died in 1942, aged 75, and was buried at **Luton General Cemetery, Rothesay Road**, on 19 March 1942.

Albert's life traces an arc from the rural Bedfordshire his parents knew to a settled working life in Luton, passing through the Maulden interlude that connected him to his parents' final years.

In 1881, aged 14, he is still at home with his parents at The Warren, Haynes. By 1891 he had married **Charlotte** (born ~1860, Maulden, Bedfordshire) and was working as a **Woodman** — his father's trade — in **Maulden**, the same village his parents had recently moved to. The Maulden connection deepened: Albert married a Maulden woman and the couple's children (Ernest and Elizabeth) were born in Maulden. By 1901 the family had moved to **Luton**, where Albert worked as a **Railway Platelayer**. By 1911 he had switched to **Gardener**, the occupation he retained to old age.

Charlotte died at some point after 1911. In 1928 Albert married **Caroline Stanbridge** in Luton. The 1939 National Register finds him at 76 Windsor Street, Luton, with Caroline (63) and members of the Stanbridge family — Caroline's son Percy (28) and a daughter — sharing the household.

Albert was the only son to survive to adulthood: his brother John (born ~1862) disappears from census records, and Frederick Louis (born 1871) died in infancy. He outlived all his sisters except Elizabeth, who died in 1936.
