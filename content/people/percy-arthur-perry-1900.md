Percy Arthur Perry (1900, Dunstable) was the eldest child of [[arthur-perry-1866|Arthur Perry]] and [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]]. He appears in the 1901 and 1911 censuses in his father's Dunstable household. He had military service in 1918. He married Dorothy Annie Purser in December 1927 at Luton.
