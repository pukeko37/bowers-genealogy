Thomas Eusden was born in 1799, the eldest known child of [[thomas-euseden-1764|Thomas Euseden]] (1764–1842). He is distinguished from his father by date throughout the records.

He married **Mary Ann James** (a widow) on 21 February 1833 at Fenstanton — this was his first marriage; he is recorded as "Single" in the marriage register. Their only known child, [[alfred-eusden-1834|Alfred Eusden]], was christened on Christmas Day, 25 December 1834, at Fenstanton.

Thomas was buried at Fenstanton on **2 September 1835**, aged 36, leaving Mary Ann widowed again with Alfred not yet a year old. His father Thomas Euseden senior (then ~71) was still alive and living in the Fenstanton area.

Mary Ann remarried the following year: the 1836 Huntingdonshire marriages index records "Mary Ann (widow) Eusden × Jn Wm Skinner, Kimbolton" — the same woman, now remarrying as Eusden widow, taking infant Alfred into the Skinner household. Alfred later kept the Eusden name: he appears in London records as Alfred Eusden when he married Mary Arthur at Shoreditch St Leonard on 30 August 1859.

Thomas's siblings — [[william-eusden|William]], [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary]], [[sarah-eusden-1804|Sarah]], and [[henry-eusden|Henry]] Eusden — all married at Fenstanton in the same decade.
