William Matthews was christened 9 October 1864 at Whipsnade, Bedfordshire, the eldest child of [[william-mathews-1837|William Mathews (1837)]] and Elizabeth Bolton. FamilySearch gives his birth year as 1865, consistent with the 1871 census (age 6), though the christening record places the event in October 1864 — he was likely born in late 1864.

He worked as a farm labourer and later a gamekeeper. He married **Rebecca Grizzell** (born ~1865), and the household was at Studham in 1901 — which explains why she appeared in the [[familysearch-kensworth-matthews-search]] results as "Rebecca Matthews (born 1866, Kensworth) at Studham 1901." Her maiden name was Grizzell; she was not born a Matthews. By 1911 the family had returned to Whipsnade.

**Census trail:** 1871 Whipsnade (scholar), 1881 Whipsnade, 1891 Whipsnade, 1901 Studham (gamekeeper), 1911 Whipsnade.

**Children with Rebecca Grizzell:**

1. Alice Matthews (1892–1976)
2. Anne Matthews (1894–1982)
3. Lillian Matthews (1896–1967)
4. William Walter Matthews (1898–1965)
5. Winifred Matthews (1904–1964)
6. Albert Edward Matthews (1908–1965)

All six children survived to adulthood.
