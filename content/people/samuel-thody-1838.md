Samuel Thody (1838–1921) was the second child of [[allen-thody-1813|Allen Thody]] and [[elizabeth-saunders-1811|Elizabeth Saunders]], and a brother of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]]. Born at Cotton End, Bedfordshire; died 1921 aged approximately 82–83. Appears in the 1851 census, age 13. He lived to an advanced age.
