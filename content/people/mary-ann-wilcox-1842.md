Mary Ann Wilcox was born in 1842 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire. She married [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker]] on 2 October 1859 at Millbrook — she was seventeen, he was nineteen. FamilySearch ID: MM14-3ZC.

Her origins — parents, siblings — are not yet traced. She was a Millbrook woman who married a Millbrook man and spent her entire life in the village.

Joseph died in 1914. Mary Ann survived him by at least seven years. In 1921 she was approximately 79 years old — an aged widow.

## Note: not the Mrs Bunker of the 1919 story

Mary Ann was initially a candidate for the "Mrs Bunker" of the 1919 Huett effigy story, based on her husband Joseph being a lifelong Millbrook resident who died before 1919. However, Samuel Bunker's obituary (*Bedfordshire Times*, 20 June 1913) confirms that it was **Samuel**, not Joseph, who served as clerk and sexton at Millbrook parish church. The Mrs Bunker of 1919 is therefore Samuel's widow, [[harriet-dean-1844|Harriet Dean]].
