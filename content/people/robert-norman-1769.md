Robert Norman was born in February 1769 in Over, Cambridgeshire, and died there on 17 June 1842, buried two days later in the same parish. His entire documented life was spent in the Over/Swavesey area of the South Cambridgeshire fens.

He married [[frances-green-1769|Frances Green]] on 16 November 1788 at Swavesey — the parish just a few miles south of Over. Frances (1769–1810) was born the same year as Robert, in Swavesey itself. Together they had seven children, all born in the Over/Swavesey area: Alice (~1787, born before the marriage), Mary (1790), [[thomas-norman-1793|Thomas]] (1793–1855), Elizabeth (1795–1877), James (1801–1872), Luke (1803–1878), and Richard (1805–1890).

Frances died in 1810, leaving Robert a widower with children ranging in age from about five to about twenty-three. No second marriage is recorded. The 1841 census finds Robert at Church End, Over, aged 70, sharing the household with Alice Norman (F, 50) — almost certainly his eldest daughter Alice, then in her mid-fifties (the 1841 census rounded adult ages).

Robert died at 73, having outlived his wife by thirty-two years. His son [[thomas-norman-1793|Thomas]] survived him by thirteen years, eventually dying in Swavesey in 1855. The family's roots in Over and Swavesey stretch back at least to Robert's generation; his own parents are not yet identified.
