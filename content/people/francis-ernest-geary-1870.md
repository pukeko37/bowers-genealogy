Francis Ernest Geary was born 8 February 1870 in Haynes, Bedfordshire, the illegitimate son of [[ann-geary-1851|Ann Geary]] (1851–1922). No father is named in the christening record. He died 28 September 1936 at Reading, Berkshire, aged 66.

He was christened on 11 May 1873 at Haynes — a late christening, more than three years after his birth, on the same day as his aunt [[lydia-rose-geary-1868|Lydia Rose Geary]] (born 1868, also receiving a delayed christening). Both were brought to the font together in a single family occasion.

Francis Ernest was raised not by his mother Ann but by his **grandparents** [[john-geary-1825|John Geary]] and [[lucy-morris-1826|Lucy Morris]] in the Haynes household. The censuses record him under the name "Ernest Geary" — he went by Ernest, not Francis. He appears in the 1871 census as "Ernest Geary," age 1, with John and Lucy; and again in 1881 as "Ernest Geary," age 11, at The Warren, Haynes, alongside his uncle Albert (14) and aunt Lydia (12). His mother Ann had by this point moved to Bedford and married William Whitmore (c.1876), establishing a separate family. Francis Ernest does not appear in the Whitmore household.

After 1881 he disappears from the Haynes records. He ended up in **Reading, Berkshire** — a move of some 40 miles south-west, outside the Bedfordshire orbit entirely. He lived at **17 Cressington Road, Reading**. He married **Eva Maud Geary** (née unknown). He died on 28 September 1936 aged 66. Probate was granted on 20 October 1936 at the **Oxford** probate registry (which had jurisdiction over Berkshire), with Eva Maud as widow and co-administrator, along with Jessie Willis — identified as the wife of William John Willis. Whether the Willises were Eva's relatives, solicitors, or co-executors is not known. Effects were valued at **£1,204 18s. 8d.** — a modest estate consistent with a lifetime of working-class employment.

In the probate calendar he is recorded as **"Frank Ernest Geary"** — the name he apparently used in official and legal contexts, distinct from the "Francis" of his birth registration and the "Ernest" of his childhood censuses. See [[francis-ernest-geary-1870-probate-1936]] for the primary source.

Francis Ernest was the only known child of Ann Geary before her marriage. He is a member of the [[geary-morris-1848]] household by upbringing, not by birth.
