George Cook was born on 18 January 1815 in Luton, Bedfordshire (Baptist register), the third child of [[daniel-cook-1790|Daniel Cook]] and [[mary-birchmore-1785|Mary Birchmore]]. His siblings born nearby include [[james-cook-1813|James]] (1813) and [[david-cook-1816|David]] (1816), all christened at Kensworth; George's Luton Baptist registration suggests the family may have attended a Luton chapel as well.

Only two birth records are documented in FamilySearch. No marriage, death, or census record appears for him. He should not be confused with [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook (1843–1908)]], the Chequers Inn licensee, who is George's nephew — the son of George's brother [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]]. Earlier research incorrectly attributed Charles to George; the David Cook Family Tree 1 print view corrects this.
