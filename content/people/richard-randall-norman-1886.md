Richard Randall Norman (1886–1969), sixth child of [[richard-norman-1853|Richard Norman]] and [[ann-elizabeth-randall-1854|Ann Elizabeth Randall]]; born West Hartlepool. His name combines his father's first name and his mother's maiden name — a deliberate family memorial. He lived to 83 and is the most traceable descendant of this branch from living memory.
