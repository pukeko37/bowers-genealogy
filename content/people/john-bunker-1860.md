John Bunker (born ~1860, Bedford), third child of [[john-watts-bunker-1831|John Watts Bunker]] and Ann Woodland. Present at a mass christening at St Mary's Bedford on 1 September 1867 along with siblings Harry and Emma. No further records traced. Not to be confused with [[john-bunker-1871|John Bunker (1871–1900)]], son of Joseph Bunker (1840).
