Daniel Brightman (abt 1718–1787) married [[anne-bunker-1732|Anne Bunker]] on 3 September 1753 at Millbrook. He was about fourteen years older than his wife. His origins are not documented. He died in 1787, predeceasing Anne by thirteen years. See [[brightman-bunker-1753]].
