Helena Annie Melrose was born in 1883 and died in 1960. She married [[ernest-caleb-bunker-1882|Ernest Caleb Bunker]] on 1 September 1913 at the Wesleyan Chapel, Gloucester Road, Horfield, Bristol. Their two sons were [[kenneth-ernest-bunker-1917|Kenneth Ernest]] (1917–1985) and [[herbert-arthur-bunker-1922|Herbert Arthur]] (1922–2007). She died in 1960, seven years before Ernest.
