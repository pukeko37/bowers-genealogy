Florence Bass was born in **1862** in Maulden, Bedfordshire — the middle child of [[daniel-bass-1829|Daniel Bass]] (1829–1868) and [[mary-ward-tall-1827|Mary Ward Tall]] (1827–1907). Her father died when she was about six. She appears in the 1871 census as a nine-year-old scholar at Duck End, Maulden — then vanishes from Maulden entirely, only to resurface in 1881 in London, and to marry and settle in Ampthill. Her death date is not recorded in current sources.

## Childhood and Service

By 1881, at seventeen, Florence was working as a **domestic servant** in Islington, London — in the household of Thomas Brown, a commercial clerk. She was the only one of her siblings to leave Bedfordshire during this period; sister [[sarah-bass-1859|Sarah]] remained in Maulden until marriage in 1887, and brother [[edward-ernest-bass-1864|Edward]] stayed until at least 1881. Florence's London service was the first step on a path that would bring her back to the Ampthill area.

## Marriage and Ampthill

In **1889** Florence married **Sidney David Shotbolt** (abt 1865–Deceased) at Ampthill, Bedfordshire. Sidney was born in **Maulden** — Florence had married a man from her own home village, and the couple settled not in London or Maulden but in nearby **Ampthill**, where they remained for the rest of their documented lives.

The 1891 census records a striking moment: Florence Shotbolt (29) is listed as married with son Frank — aged **0 days**. Frank was born on or immediately before census day, April 1891.

## Family Life in Ampthill

Sidney worked as a shoemaker (1891), boot and shoe maker (1901), and bootmaker dealer (1911) — a trade he built over three decades in Ampthill. Florence assisted in keeping the household. The 1911 census records them as having been married 21 years (a slight discrepancy with the 1889 registration, likely rounding), with a family of five — Frank (20), Eva (18), and Reginald (17) all still at home.

Notably, in 1891 Florence was in Ampthill at the same time as her brother [[edward-ernest-bass-1864|Edward Ernest Bass]] — he had settled there after his 1885 marriage to Mary Ann Dennis. The two Bass siblings were neighbours in the same Bedfordshire town in that census year.

## Children

Three children, all born in Ampthill:
1. Frank Shotbolt (1891–date unknown) — born on census day
2. Eva Shotbolt (1893–date unknown)
3. Reginald Shotbolt (1894–date unknown)
