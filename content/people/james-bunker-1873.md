James Bunker was born on 1 May 1873 in Millbrook, the sixth child of [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker (1840–1914)]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]]. He was christened 19 October 1873 at Millbrook — a plausible five-month gap for a child born in May.

James appears in the Millbrook household in the 1881 census, but by 1891 — aged only about seventeen or eighteen — he had already left for Nottinghamshire, the earliest documented of the brothers' migration north. By January 1893 he was settled enough in South Normanton, Derbyshire to marry there, and remained in that area for the rest of his life.

His recorded occupation in 1895 was **Collier** — the only sibling explicitly documented as a coal miner, though his brothers who went to the same area likely worked in the same industry.

**1893**: James married **Julia Carter** (born 1871) on 17 January 1893 at South Normanton. They had six children between 1894 and 1904.

James is recorded at South Normanton in 1901, Nottinghamshire in 1911, and Blackwell, Derbyshire in the 1939 National Register. He was buried in Derbyshire on 15 March 1945, aged approximately 71.

One son, Albert Edward Bunker (1897–1916), was killed in the First World War.
