Albert Bunker (born ~1875, New Tupton, Derbyshire; died 11 October 1924, Derby), fourth child of [[george-bunker-1836|George Bunker]] and [[ann-pedder-1838|Ann Pedder]]. His New Tupton birthplace confirms the family's arrival in Derbyshire around 1875. He remained in Derby for the rest of his life, still in his parents' household in 1901, and died there aged about 49. Not to be confused with [[albert-bunker-1863|Albert Bunker (1863)]], son of David Bunker and Marian Bentley.
