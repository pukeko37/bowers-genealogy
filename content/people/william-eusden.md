William Eusden was born in 1797, the eldest child of [[thomas-euseden-1764|Thomas Euseden]] (1764–1842). He married **Sarah Cox** at Fenstanton in 1825 — the first of the siblings to marry, at age ~28. Birth and death dates are from the Fenstanton burial register.

He died in 1843 at Fenstanton, aged 46 — the year after his father. The final years of the Eusden family at Fenstanton were marked by a cluster of deaths: [[henry-eusden|Henry]] in 1839, [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary]] around 1841, Thomas senior in November 1842, and William in 1843.

His siblings were [[thomas-eusden-1799|Thomas]] (1799–1835), [[sarah-eusden-1804|Sarah]] (1804–1833), [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary]] (1807–1841), and [[henry-eusden|Henry]] (1809–1839).
