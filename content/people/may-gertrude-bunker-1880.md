May Gertrude Bunker was born in 1880, the fifth child of [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]] and [[margaret-barnsley-1850|Margaret Barnsley]], and died in 1960 aged ~80. She was the longer-lived of the two surviving daughters, outliving her mother Margaret by nearly 40 years. No further details are recorded beyond her dates.
