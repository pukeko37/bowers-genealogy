Elizabeth West Bunker (25 December 1824 – March 1878) was the eldest child of [[john-bunker-1803|John Bunker (1803–1889)]] and Maria West (1806). Born on Christmas Day, she carried her mother's maiden name as a middle name — a naming gesture that quietly preserved the West line. She was also known as Betsy.

She was christened at Millbrook on 17 October 1825, some ten months after her birth — consistent with the Nonconformist background of the broader Bunker family.

**Marriage and settlement.** Elizabeth married **[[mark-gillet-1823|Mark Gillet]]** (1823–1895) on 14 April 1845 at Marston Moretaine, Bedfordshire. She was 20; he was 21. She left Millbrook for the Marston Moretaine area, where the couple appear in the 1851 census. By 1861 they had moved to **Maulden** — just three miles from Millbrook — and were still there in 1871. She never went far from home.

**Children.** Ten children with Mark Gillet: John (1846), James (1847), Sarah (1848), George (1851), Joseph (1853, died in infancy), Ann (1856), Jane Ann (1859), William (1861), Elizabeth Maria (1864), Ada Marianne (1870). The family spelling varies between "Gillett" and "Gillet."

Elizabeth died in March 1878 in Bedfordshire, aged 53. Mark Gillet survived her by seventeen years (died 1895).

**In context.** Elizabeth is the eldest of ten siblings. While her brothers Joseph (1840) and Samuel (1842) spent their lives in Millbrook itself, and her brother George (1845) went as far as London and Tottenham, Elizabeth found a middle course — leaving but staying within easy reach of her father John (1803), who lived until 1889. Her settlement at Maulden meant she died within a short walk of where she was born.
