George Birchmore was born in 1786, christened 20 January 1786 at Flamstead, Hertfordshire — the son of [[george-birchmore-1762|George Birchmore]] (1762–1843) and Susannah Redding. He died 31 January 1838 at Roe End Farm, Flamstead; his burial at **St Leonard Churchyard, Flamstead** is confirmed by the Find a Grave memorial listing (which records him as "George Burchmore, d. 31 Jan 1838" — exact date match). He farmed at Roe End Farm his entire life.

### Two families

Before his marriage, George had a son by **Elizabeth Plummer** (1790–1866): [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]] (1813–1884), known as GBP Sr. No marriage between George and Elizabeth is recorded; GBP Sr took his mother's surname Plummer. Elizabeth raised GBP Sr alone. She outlived George by 28 years, dying in 1866. See [[birchmore-plummer-flamstead]].

George married **Ann Norton** (1797–1866) on 6 April 1815, Flamstead — two years after GBP Sr's birth. With Ann he had four children: George Burchmore (1817–1866), William Burchmore (1819–1916), John Norton Burchmore (1821–1873), and Anne Burchmore (1832–?). These are GBP Sr's half-siblings. See [[birchmore-norton-1815]].

### Significance

George Birchmore (1786) is the direct paternal link between the Flamstead farming Birchmores and [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]], who grew up in Bedfordshire and spent his life at Whipsnade. The illegitimacy of GBP Sr's birth explains both his "Plummer" surname (taken from his mother) and his complex name history throughout his life. George died when GBP Sr was 25 — they may or may not have had a relationship; no contact is documented.
