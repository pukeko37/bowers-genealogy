Lucy Cook was born 24 October 1856, the youngest of three illegitimate children of [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] (1820–1904). Her father is unknown. Her siblings were [[charles-cook-1847|Charles Cook]] (1847) and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]] (1848).

When Lucy was about 11, her mother Ruth married [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]] in December 1867. This made Lucy the step-sister of [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen Birchmore]] — but Lucy was only four years older than Ellen, and they were raised together more like sisters. This relationship became the crucial link connecting the Norman and Bunker families two generations later.

Lucy married William Allen Thody (1850–1911) on 6 February 1894 at Whipsnade. Their only child was [[harry-thody-1894|Harry Thody]] (1894–1986). William died in 1911, and Lucy moved to Rose Cottage, Studham.

Lucy became like a second mother to [[ivy-helen-norman-1899|Ivy Norman]]. Ivy and her sister Norah often spent time at Rose Cottage visiting Lucy. It was through Lucy's son Harry that Ivy met her future husband [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland Bunker]]. Lucy died in early 1930, aged 74. The FamilySearch sources give 25 February 1930; the gravestone inscription recorded at Find a Grave reads **25 January 1930**. The inscribed date is likely the more reliable of the two for the exact day and month — the GRO registration quarter (Q1) is consistent with either date. The February date is retained in the frontmatter pending further verification.
