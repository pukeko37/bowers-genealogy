Arthur Perry (born c.1866, Harlington, Bedfordshire) was a Railway Porter who worked for the Great Northern Railway Company. He married [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]] in 1898 in Luton, Bedfordshire.

## Life

The 1901 census finds Arthur as head of household in Dunstable, age 35, born Harlington, Bedfordshire, occupation Railway Porter. His wife Florence (née Bransom, age 27, born Whipsnade) and infant son Percy (age 1, born Dunstable) are with him.

By 1911 Arthur was still a Railway Porter in Dunstable (employed by the GN Railway Co.), living with his son [[percy-arthur-perry-1900|Percy]] (age 11) and daughter [[elsie-perry-1907|Elsie]] (age 4). His sister-in-law [[clara-lucy-bransom-1877|Clara Bransom]] (Florence's younger sister, age 33) was also in the household. His wife Florence was not present — she appears in the 1911 census at her parents' home in Whipsnade, with their middle child [[florence-hilda-perry-1903|Florence Hilda]] (age 8). The reasons for this separation of household across two census nights are not known from available sources.

Arthur's parents and earlier life have not been researched.
