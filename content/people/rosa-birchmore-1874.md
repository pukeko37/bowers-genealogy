Rosa Birchmore was born about 1874 in Middlesex, the eldest child of [[george-birchmore-plummer-jr-1850|George Birchmore Plummer Jr]] and [[elizabeth-sarah-morse-1852|Elizabeth Sarah Morse]]. She grew up in St Pancras — present in the 1881 and 1891 censuses there — then disappears from the Birchmore household records after 1891. The reason was marriage: she married **George Henry Lomax** in September 1898 at Pancras, London (GRO vol 1b, page 45).

What became of Rosa and George Henry Lomax after 1898 is not yet established. Her fate — including where she lived, whether she had children, and when and where she died — requires further research.
