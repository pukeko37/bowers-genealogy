Joseph Batchelor was born in 1755 in Flamstead, Hertfordshire (christened 11 January 1755). He married Sarah Bingham (1758–?). He is the father of [[thomas-bachelor-1781|Thomas Bachelor]] (1781), who migrated to Whipsnade and whose son [[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham Batchelor]] later held the Chequers Inn licence.

## Parents and Deep Roots

Joseph's father was James Batchelor (1725), who married Mary Field on 31 May 1748 in Leighton Buzzard, Bedfordshire — placing the Batchelor family at the Hertfordshire/Bedfordshire border across multiple generations, a full generation before Joseph. The Flamstead connection thus extends to at least 1725.

Joseph's siblings (all children of James Batchelor and Mary Field) included:
- Mary Bachelor (1749), Thomas Batchelor (1750), William Bachelor (1751), Ann Bachelor (1753)
- Daniel Batchelor (1756), Meria Bachelor (1757), Samuel Batchelor (1758), George Batchelor (1759)

## Children with Sarah Bingham

Elizabeth (1774), Hannah (1776), Lydia (1777), James (1780), [[thomas-bachelor-1781|Thomas]] (1781), Mary (1781), Fanny (1783), Ann (1785), William (1787), Samuel (1789–1872)

## The Dagnall Question

Joseph's son Thomas Bachelor (1781) settled in Whipsnade. A separate Thomas Batchelar was born in 1803 in **Dagnall, Buckinghamshire** — approximately 5 miles from Flamstead, across the next county boundary. This Thomas Batchelar (1803) is the probable father of [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]], GBP Sr's second wife. Whether Thomas Batchelar (1803, Dagnall) was a son or nephew of Joseph Batchelor remains unconfirmed, but the geography and surname strongly suggest a shared family origin. If confirmed, it would establish [[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham Batchelor]] and [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]] as cousins.

Note: Joseph's son Samuel Batchelor (1789–1872) shares a name and approximate generation with Samuel Heels (1797–1845), the Chequers tenant — they are different people.
