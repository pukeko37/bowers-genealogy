Albert Bunker (born ~1863, Bedford), third child of [[david-bunker-1837|David Bunker]] and Marian Bentley; born in the Bedford area before the family's northeast migration. Not to be confused with [[albert-bunker-1875|Albert Bunker (1875)]], son of George Bunker. No records traced beyond early censuses.
