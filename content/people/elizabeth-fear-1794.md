Elizabeth Fear was born in 1794 — no specific date or birthplace is recorded. She died on 29 July 1832 in Swavesey, Cambridgeshire, aged about 37, and was buried the same day.

Her parents were Richard Fear (1755–1840) and Sarah Woodcock (1762–1843), who married at Swavesey on 24 October 1784. Elizabeth was the eldest surviving child; her siblings were William (1797–1799, died aged two), Mary (1802–1866), and Sarah (died before 1789). The Fear family was rooted in Swavesey.

She married [[thomas-norman-1793|Thomas Norman]] at Swavesey on 20 August 1815 (a second record gives 13 August as the date — likely the banns versus the ceremony). They appear to have initially settled in the Willingham area: their first surviving child Thomas (born October 1815) was christened at Willingham, Cambridgeshire, just two months after the wedding. This baby Thomas died in infancy or childhood and does not appear in later records. By 1821, when their son Robert was born, the family had moved or returned to Swavesey.

Elizabeth and Thomas had at least five children: Thomas (1815, died young), Robert (1821), Sarah (1822), [[thomas-norman-1827|Thomas]] (1827–1905), and Ann (1832–1843). Elizabeth died on 29 July 1832 — the same year Ann was born. The timing strongly suggests she died from complications of Ann's birth. She left Thomas a widower with four surviving children, the youngest less than a year old.

[[thomas-norman-1827|Thomas Norman (1827)]] later spoke of his mother's early death in the context of his own family history: he was about five when she died, close in age to his own son's age when he married Ann Tabram.
