John Tabram was born in 1861 in Fenstanton, Huntingdonshire, the third child of [[john-tabram-1836|John Tabram (1836)]] and Elizabeth Lewis (1837–1903). He was baptised on 7 April 1861 in a bulk baptism together with his older siblings Fanny and George Richard. His aunt was [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann Tabram]] (his father's sister), who had married [[thomas-norman-1827|Thomas Norman]] of Swavesey in 1850 — making [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]] and his siblings John's first cousins.

He married **[[adeliza-fuller-1862|Adeliza Fuller]]** (1862–1938) in December 1879 (St Ives registration district; probably Fenstanton), and they remained in Fenstanton through the 1911 census — five decades of unbroken residence in his birth parish, following the same pattern as his father. They had three children:

- **Ellen Rachel Tabram** (1881–1882) — died in infancy
- **Ethel Fuller Tabram** (1883–) — her middle name "Fuller" was her mother Adeliza's maiden/birth name; married a Lomas
- **Mabel Sarah Tabram** (1885–)

[[adeliza-fuller-1862|Adeliza]] died on **8 February 1938** at 1 Southoe, Buckden — probate granted 4 April 1938 to Ethel Lomas, effects £609 15s. 8d. The couple had moved from Fenstanton to Buckden at some point after 1911, likely in old age to be near Ethel. John survived Adeliza by almost exactly one year, dying on **7 February 1939** at the same address.

Probate was granted in London on 4 March 1939 to **Ethel Fuller Lomas, married woman** — Ethel having married a Mr Lomas and carrying her mother's name as her middle name. Effects were £506 12s. 4d.

His brother **James Lewis Tabram** (1868–1941) outlived him by two years.
