Ruth Bunker (17 August 1794 – 7 April 1806) was the third child of [[james-thomas-bunker-1764|James Thomas Bunker]] and [[lydia-brown-1769|Lydia Brown]]. FamilySearch records both her christening and her death as 7 April 1806 — the same day as the mass christening at Millbrook parish church in which six of her siblings were also presented. She died aged 11, on the day she was christened, likely already ill when brought to the church.

She never married and had no children.

The name Ruth recurs in the next generation: Caleb (1790) named a daughter Ruth (b. 1822).
