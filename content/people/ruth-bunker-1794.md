Ruth Bunker (born 17 August 1794, Millbrook) was the third child of [[james-thomas-bunker-1764|James Thomas Bunker]] and [[lydia-brown-1769|Lydia Brown]]. She was christened at Millbrook on 7 April 1806 in the family's mass baptism ceremony.

## Death date — confirmed data error; Ruth survived 1806

FamilySearch (MM14-4B7) records her death date as **7 April 1806** — identical to her christening date. The Millbrook parish burial register (FindMyPast, [[bunker-millbrook-burials-fmp]]) has now been searched: **no Ruth Bunker burial appears at any point before 1836**. The only Ruth Bunker ever buried at Millbrook is the 1836 fire victim (RB-H in [[ruth-bunker-findmypast-register]]). The 1806 death date is definitively a data error — the christening date copied into the death field in a collaborative-tree entry.

The 7 April 1806 mass christening was a deliberate act: James Thomas and Lydia Brown had seven children baptised together at Millbrook parish church on that day, the eldest (Caleb) aged 15 and the youngest (Lydia) aged 2. Ruth was 11 at the time. The ceremony was a family registering years of Nonconformist-raised children with the Church of England in one act — not the emergency baptism of a dying child. The burial register now closes this question entirely.

**Ruth (1794) survived 1806.** Her fate after that year is untraced under the Bunker name.

## Probable fate: married William Cook, 1816

Ruth (1794) is the strongest candidate for the Millbrook marriage of 31 December 1816 between **Ruth Bunker** and **William Cook (Cooke)** (FamilySearch M98P-NR5; [[ruth-bunker-findmypast-register]] event #6). The FamilySearch record assigns the bride a birth year of ~1795 — one year from Ruth's actual 1794 birth, consistent with census rounding. Her only rival for the marriage is [[bunker-garratt-1777|Ruth Martha Bunker (1790)]], daughter of Joseph (1754) — but the five-year gap between the 1790 birth and the ~1795 FamilySearch estimate makes that the weaker fit.

Their daughter **Harriet Cooke** was christened 15 May 1825 at Tilsworth, All Saints, showing the family had moved ~15 miles south to the Leighton Buzzard area by that date. As Ruth Cook she would not appear in further FindMyPast searches for "Ruth Bunker."

This identification remains provisional. The source underlying the ~1795 birth year in FamilySearch M98P-NR5 should be checked — a census entry would be more reliable than a tree inference.

The name Ruth recurs in the next generation: Caleb (1790) named a daughter Ruth (b. 1822). See [[bunker-brown-1788]].
