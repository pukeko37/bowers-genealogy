Henry Eusden was born in 1809, the youngest child of [[thomas-euseden-1764|Thomas Euseden]] (1764–1842). He married **Mary Papworth** at Fenstanton in 1833, the same year as his brother [[thomas-eusden-1799|Thomas]] also married there. Birth and death dates are from the Fenstanton burial register.

He died in 1839 at Fenstanton, aged ~30 — the first of the cluster of Eusden deaths in the early 1840s that would effectively end the family's presence at Fenstanton. His sister [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary]] died around 1841, his father Thomas senior in November 1842, and his brother [[william-eusden|William]] in 1843.

His siblings were [[william-eusden|William]] (1797–1843), [[thomas-eusden-1799|Thomas]] (1799–1835), [[sarah-eusden-1804|Sarah]] (1804–1833), and [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary]] (1807–1841).
