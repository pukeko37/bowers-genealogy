William Woodcraft (1777–1835), a Whipsnade man whose origins are undocumented. He married [[susan-maynard-1802|Susan Maynard]] on 20 May 1827 at Whipsnade; he was about 50 at the time, suggesting a possible prior family. Five children were christened at Whipsnade between 1828 and 1836. If the 1835 death year is correct, his youngest child Charles was born posthumously. Alice Woodcroft (1791), who married Thomas Bachelor in the same parish in 1816, may have been a sibling or relation, but the connection is not established.
