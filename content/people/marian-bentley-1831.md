Marian Bentley was born about 1831 in Biggleswade, Bedfordshire, some six years before her future husband [[david-bunker-1837|David Bunker]]. She married David on 11 October 1859 at St Paul's Church, Bedford. They had four children, all born in Bedford before the family's move to Peterborough. Marian died in 1914 in Peterborough after more than 50 years of marriage; David survived her by six years. Her parentage has not been established. See [[bunker-bentley-1859]].
