Albert Bunker was born in 1865 in Millbrook, the third child of [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker (1840–1914)]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]]. He was christened 4 June 1865 at Millbrook and registered at Ampthill.

Albert grew up in Millbrook, appearing in the household in the 1871 and 1881 censuses. By 1891 he had moved to Nottinghamshire alongside his brothers Thomas and George, and by 1901 was at South Normanton, Derbyshire. He was still in Nottinghamshire in 1911 (see [[south-normanton]]).

**1887**: Albert married **Emma Roberts** on 11 April 1887 at Moggerhanger, Bedfordshire — the same parish where his brother Thomas had married Charlotte Roberts in 1885. The Roberts women were likely sisters.

Albert and Emma had eleven children between 1888 and 1905, but suffered severe infant mortality: of the eleven, at least five died as infants or young children, and one son, Arthur James (1895–1916), was killed in the First World War.

Albert's mother [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]], by then widowed, was living with Albert and Emma in the 1921 census, aged approximately 79.

His date of death is not recorded.
