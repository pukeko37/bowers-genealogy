Mary Ann Cook (FamilySearch G4LP-4D7) was born in 1839, listed in the David Cook FamilySearch Family Tree 1 (LRWN-XV2) as a daughter of [[david-cook-1816|David Cook]] and Elizabeth Frost. Her birth year is three years before her parents' 1842 marriage at Kensworth — either she was born before the formal union, or the marriage date in the FamilySearch record carries an error.

Her mother Elizabeth Frost died in 1843, the same year as the birth of Mary Ann's brother [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook]]. No further records of Mary Ann Cook have been identified. See [[cook-frost-1842]] for the family context.
