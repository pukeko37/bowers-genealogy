Elizabeth (maiden name unknown; formerly wife of James Meyers) is the mother of [[allen-thody-1813|Allen Thody]] (1813–1898). She married [[william-thody-1774|William Thody]] at **Badajoz, Spain, around 1809**, during the Peninsular War campaign of the 40th Regiment of Foot.

She was already a widow when she married William. Her first husband, **James Meyers**, had fathered a son — **William Myers** — born at **Elvas, Portugal**. The Portuguese birthplace suggests her first husband was also a soldier on campaign in the Peninsula.

The only direct document for Elizabeth is a legal examination dated **5 December 1814**, in which she petitioned Cardington parish for support for her son William Myers. In it she states she married William Thody "about five years ago at Badajoz in Spain" and that William is "now a Private Soldier in the 40th Regiment of Foot, serving in America." At this point Allen Thody would have been about fourteen months old.

Nothing is known about Elizabeth after December 1814. By 1841 William Thody was living alone in Chelsea, suggesting she had died at some point in the intervening years. No death record has been found.
