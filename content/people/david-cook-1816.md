David Cook was born in 1816 in Kensworth, Bedfordshire (christened 22 December 1816 in Kensworth, Hertfordshire — the county border sits through this area). He was the brother of [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] (1820–1904), both children of [[daniel-cook-1790|Daniel Cook]] (1790–1857) and [[mary-birchmore-1785|Mary Birchmore]] (1785–?). See [[cook-birchmore-1810]].

## First Marriage: Elizabeth Frost

David first married Elizabeth Frost (1818–1843) on 8 May 1842 in Kensworth. Elizabeth died in 1843, the same year their son [[charles-cook-1843|Charles]] was born — she may not have survived childbirth. The three children of this marriage were:

1. Mary Ann Cook (1839) — born before the marriage; the birth year discrepancy with the 1842 wedding date suggests she may have been born out of wedlock, or the marriage date has an error
2. [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook]] (1843–1908) — later held the Chequers Inn licence 1904–1908
3. James Cook (1843) — possibly a twin with Charles, or the birth years of two children have been conflated

See [[cook-frost-1842]] for this family.

## Second Marriage: Charlotte Matthews

After Elizabeth's death, David married [[charlotte-matthews-1823|Charlotte Matthews]] (1823–1910) in 1856 at Whipsnade. Charlotte was the sister of [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] (first wife of [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]]). The marriage deepened the link between the Cook and Matthews families, a connection reinforced when GBP Sr married Ruth Cooke — David's sister — in 1867. Their six children were:

1. Anne Cooke (1858)
2. Sarah Cooke (1860)
3. Betsy Cook (1862–1888)
4. Jesse Cooke (1863)
5. Daniel Cooke (1864)
6. Amy Cooke (1867)

David and Charlotte lived at By Whipsnade Green throughout their lives. Every census from 1851 to 1891 records him in Whipsnade. He died in 1900 (death registered in the Luton district); Charlotte outlived him by a decade, her death also registered in the Luton district in 1910. See [[cook-matthews-whipsnade]].

**Family note:** The Cook/Cooke family of Kensworth also connects to the wider wiki through Ruth Cooke's mother, Mary Birchmore — whose maiden name may link to the Birchmore family from which GBP Sr derived his name. This parallel Birchmore connection (GBP Sr's name; Ruth's mother's name) in the same small community is noted but not yet documented.
