Mary A Lane was born around **1868** in Bedford, Bedfordshire — the second daughter of [[samuel-plackett-lane-1841|Samuel Plackett Lane]] (abt 1841–1872) and [[lydia-bunker-1833|Lydia Bunker]] (1833–1922). Her father died when she was about four.

She is recorded in the **1881 census** (age 13) living with her mother in Bedford. No records have been traced after 1881 — no marriage, death, or later census appearance has been identified. Her middle initial "A" has not been expanded.

See [[bunker-lane-1863]] for the family narrative.
