Herbert Thomas Barnsley Bunker was born in 1875 in New Tupton, Derbyshire (registered Chesterfield 1874), the second child of [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]] and [[margaret-barnsley-1850|Margaret Barnsley]]. He died in about March 1917 in Alcester, Warwickshire, aged ~42, and was buried at Coughton Court, Warwickshire on 16 March 1917.

His middle name **Barnsley** carries his mother's maiden name forward, in the same tradition as his half-brother [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick William **Tall** Bunker]]. His first name **Herbert** recurs across the family: [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland **Herbert** Bunker]] (Frederick's son, born 1898) was given it as a middle name while Herbert was still alive, and Ernest later named his son [[herbert-arthur-bunker-1922|Herbert Arthur]] in 1922. Whether this reflects a wider family attachment to the name or direct naming after Herbert himself in each case is unclear. Caleb consistently honoured his wives' surnames in his children's names.

**Railway career.** Herbert worked in railroad employment from at least 1906, consistent with family oral history that he was a station master whose family lived in railway houses next to his stations. His career took him progressively south and west from his Derbyshire roots: Sheffield (1901), Raunds, Northamptonshire (1911), and finally Sambourne, Warwickshire — a village near Alcester where he died.

**Holiday host to Roland.** [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland Bunker]] used to stay with Herbert and his family during school holidays as a boy — roughly 1905–1913, before Roland joined the Royal Navy in April 1914. By 1911 Herbert was at Raunds, Northamptonshire, likely one of the stations Roland visited. These stays are the documented point of contact between the two Bunker branches.

**Marriage and children.** Herbert married **[[martha-emma-smith-1877|Martha Emma Smith]]** (1877–1940) in 1901 at Kings Norton, Worcestershire. They had three children, all born during his railway years: [[sybil-may-bunker-1901|Sybil May]] (1901–1973), [[herbert-lawrence-bunker-1905|Herbert Lawrence]] (1905–?), and [[grace-bunker-1909|Grace]] (1909–1974). The 1911 census noted "poor name spelling" for the children's entries. See [[bunker-smith-1901]].

**Death.** Herbert died around March 1917 in Alcester, Warwickshire. His obituary was published in Derby on 13 March 1917 and he was buried on 16 March 1917 at Coughton Court — an estate near Alcester that served as a Red Cross convalescent hospital during WWI, suggesting his death may have been connected to the war. The FamilySearch Vitals record "July 1917" as his death month, but this conflicts with the obituary and burial dates and is almost certainly a transcription error. Probate was granted 27 April 1917 in Warwickshire.

*Source for holiday visits: family oral history, from Alan Bowers (b. 1928), Roland's son.*
