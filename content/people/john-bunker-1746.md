John Bunker (1746–1796) was the tenth child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and [[mary-reade-1702|Mary Reade]], christened 5 October 1746 at Millbrook. He married **[[elizabeth-cartwright-1737|Elizabeth Cartwright]]** (1737–1785) on 25 August 1768 at Millbrook. Elizabeth was nine years his senior and died in 1785, leaving John a widower for eleven years. Only one child — Mary (1771) — is recorded in FamilySearch, which may be incomplete.

John died on 20 July 1796, aged ~50, buried at Ampthill rather than Millbrook. Records give his name alternately as "John Bunker or Buncker."

See [[bunker-cartwright-1768]] for the full family narrative.
