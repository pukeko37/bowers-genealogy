James Purton Turvey (1867–1910), co-tenant of [[dell-farm|Dell Farm]], Whipsnade alongside [[james-hain-1877|James Hain]] until his death in 1910. His daughter **Hilda Kate Turvey** (1894–1987) married [[albert-ralph-cook-1886|Albert Ralph Cook]] in 1915 at Whipsnade. James died before the marriage, so Cook joined a family already established in the same Whipsnade farming community. Origins and parentage not yet researched.
