Sophia Panter (1829–1903) married [[james-bunker-1824|James Bunker]] on Christmas Day 1850 at Stevington, Bedfordshire. The 1861 census gives her birthplace as "Warrington, Bedfordshire," which appears to be a transcription error for an unidentified Bedfordshire village; her surname is also recorded as "Painter" in some sources. They had four daughters — Jane (1852), Ruth (1855), Hannah (1864), and Mary Ann (1866) — and spent their entire married lives at Silver Street, Stevington. Sophia died in 1903, a year before James.
