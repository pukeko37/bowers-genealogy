John Geary was born in 1825 in Haynes (Hawnes), Bedfordshire, christened 4 December 1825. He died on 6 February 1895 in Ampthill and was buried at Saint Mary The Virgin, Maulden — confirming the family's Maulden connection in their final years. He is Eliza Geary's father and Roland Bunker's maternal grandfather.

His occupation was **Woodman** (recorded 1871) — a rural trade fitting for the Haynes area, a scattered woodland and farming parish.

He married [[lucy-morris-1826|Lucy Morris]] in 1848 in Haynes. Together they had eleven children, four of whom died in childhood. See [[geary-morris-1848]].

John spent the great majority of his life in Haynes — every census from 1841 to 1881 places him there, at addresses including Plum's Lane and Church End. By 1891 he and Lucy had moved to Maulden; he died there four years later and was buried at the parish church.

His own parents were [[joseph-geary-1804|Joseph Geary]] (1804–1890) and [[rhoda-spring-bottoms-1806|Rhoda Spring Bottoms]] (1806–1884), married 20 April 1824 at Clophill, Bedfordshire. Clophill lies immediately adjacent to Maulden — the Geary family's roots were always within this tight cluster of villages. See [[geary-spring-1824]].

John was the eldest of ten children. His siblings (all born Haynes): Pamela (1828–1887), Sarah (1830–1918), Martha (1833), Mary (1835), Joseph (1838), George (1840–1890), Walter (1843–1923), Caleb (1847), Henry (1852–1853, died in infancy). The 1841 census places the whole family at Plum's Lane, Haynes. Joseph Geary lived to 86; Rhoda died 4 January 1884 and was buried at St Mary's Churchyard, Haynes.

Through Rhoda, John's ancestry connects into the **Spring** family of Shillington, Bedfordshire — her father was [[william-spring-1779|William Spring]] (1779), whose own parents were [[william-spring-1753|William Spring]] (1753) and [[sarah-kitchener-1752|Sarah Kitchener]] (1752), married Shillington 1773. The Kitchener line extends one further generation to William Kitchener (1721) and Elizabeth Gudgeon (1727–1764).
