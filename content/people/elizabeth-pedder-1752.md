Elizabeth Pedder (abt 1752–1830) married [[james-bunker-1749|James Bunker]] on 2 March 1772 at Millbrook. She outlived her husband by twelve years, dying in 1830. Her origins are not documented.

The Pedder surname appears elsewhere in the Millbrook Bunker network: James Bunker (1777), son of Edward Bunker (1742), had a first wife named Sarah Pedder. Whether Elizabeth and Sarah were related is unestablished. See [[bunker-pedder-1772]].
