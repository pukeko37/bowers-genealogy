Sarah Bottoms (1786–1829) was the mother of [[rhoda-spring-bottoms-1806|Rhoda Spring Bottoms]] (1806–1884) and Phoebe Spring (1807), by [[william-spring-1779|William Spring]] (1779). She is Eliza Geary's great-great-grandmother.

Her dates (1786–1829) come from the FamilySearch tree. Her birthplace and maiden name background are unknown. She died in 1829, when her daughter Rhoda was 22 years old and had already been married five years to Joseph Geary.

The nature of her relationship with William Spring is unclear — Rhoda's christening record gives the name "Rhoda or Phoebe Spring or Bottoms," suggesting the children may not have been born within a formal marriage, or that the marriage was not recorded in the usual way. William Spring subsequently married a woman named Mary (about 1808) — if Sarah Bottoms was a prior wife, she and William had separated or she had died before his later marriage. She died in 1829 while William was still alive.
