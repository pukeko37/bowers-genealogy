George Birchmore Plummer Jr was born in approximately 1850, the only child of [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]] and [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] (1815–1858). His christening is recorded at Whipsnade in 1850. His parents were based in [[whipsnade|Whipsnade]], Bedfordshire — where they had married in 1835 — and he is listed in Whipsnade in the 1851 census (age 1). See [[birchmore-plummer-matthews-1835]] for his parents' story.

**Birth and christening note:** The updated FamilySearch record gives birth as 1850, Whipsnade — consistent with all census evidence (1851, 1861, 1871 all cite Whipsnade; 1901, 1911 give "Whipsnade, Bedfordshire"; 1881 and 1891 give Bedford/Bedfordshire). However, the record also shows his christening at St Pancras Old Church on 9 June 1850, and a "Birth Registration" custom event citing St Pancras — suggesting GBP Sr had the child christened in London, possibly while visiting. This is internally inconsistent since Whipsnade fell in the Luton registration district, not St Pancras. An earlier version of this FamilySearch entry gave birth as St Pancras Old Church, which was an error — the census evidence firmly points to Whipsnade. The christening at St Pancras remains unexplained.

His mother Anne died in 1858 when George Jr was about eight. His father remarried twice more — to Eliza Batchelor (1859) and Ruth Cooke (1867). George Jr's relationship with his half-siblings from these later marriages is not documented.

### Occupation

George Jr's occupation evolved across his working life. By 1881 he was a **Dairy Farmer** — not merely a delivery man but the operator of a small dairy — in St Pancras. By 1891 he is recorded as a **Milkman**, and by 1911 as a **Milk Carrier**, a more labouring role suggesting a decline in economic status in his later years. A child's marriage certificate names him "George Birchmore, a milkman." He operated in the St Pancras / Gray's Inn Road area throughout.

This occupation places him in the same London dairy trade network as [[mason-james-1874|Richard and Catherine Mason]], who ran a milk shop at 27 [[hosier-lane-city-of-london|Hosier Lane]], Smithfield — about one mile south. See [[birchmore-morse-1874]] for a discussion of the possible connection.

### Name

Unlike his father, who eventually settled on Birchmore Plummer, George Jr **used the Birchmore name** throughout. His children all used the surname Birchmore.

### Family

George Jr married [[elizabeth-sarah-morse-1852|Elizabeth Sarah Morse]] (1852–1928) on **25 July 1871 at Kentish Town, Middlesex** — before Rosa was born in 1874. They had four children. See [[birchmore-morse-1874]].

1. [[rosa-birchmore-1874|Rosa Birchmore]] (abt 1874–?)
2. [[edith-eleanor-birchmore-1876|Edith Eleanor Birchmore]] (1876–1947)
3. [[george-william-birchmore-1880|George William Birchmore]] (1880–1943) — moved to Sidmouth, Devon
4. [[florence-eliza-birchmore-1884|Florence Eliza Birchmore]] (abt 1884–1951)

George Jr died in **1920 at St Thomas, Devon** — a district of Exeter. His son [[george-william-birchmore-1880|George William]] had moved to Sidmouth, Devon by 1911, and his partner [[elizabeth-sarah-morse-1852|Elizabeth Morse]] died in Devon in 1928. George Jr had left London and followed his family to the south-west in his last years. He was about 70 at his death.
