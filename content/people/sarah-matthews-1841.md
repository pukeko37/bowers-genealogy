Sarah Matthews (born ~1841, Whipsnade), eldest child of [[able-matthews-1813|Able Matthews]] and [[mary-walker-1817|Mary Ann Walker]]. She sailed with her family from Liverpool to Philadelphia in February 1855 — aged about 14 — en route to Utah. Her fate after the family reached St. Louis is untraced; the family may have split during their time there before Able finally reached Utah in 1861.
