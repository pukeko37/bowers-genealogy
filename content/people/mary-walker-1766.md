Mary Walker was born around 1766, the daughter of **Richard Walker** and **Mary** (surname unknown) of Kensworth, Hertfordshire. She married [[peter-matthews-1770|Peter Matthews]] on 28 June 1798 at [[whipsnade|Whipsnade]], Bedfordshire, and they settled there permanently.

She had eight children at Whipsnade between 1799 and 1813, the second of whom — [[susan-matthews-1800|Susan Matthews]] (1800–1857) — would become the mother of [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]].

Mary outlived her husband and at least some of her children. By the 1851 census she was living in **Studham**, Bedfordshire, in the household of her son-in-law John Grace (who had married her daughter Sarah), listed as a 84-year-old "Mother-in-Law" with occupation "Pauper Formerly on Ag Lab Wife" and birthplace "Kensworth, Hertfordshire." She died on 20 November 1859 — the same date of death as her daughter Susan two years earlier, which is a striking coincidence in the records (both listed as 20 November, different years).

### Birth year conflict

Two sources give different dates:
- The LDS Record of Members gives birth date **1 February 1766**, birthplace Whipsnade (possibly Kensworth)
- The Kensworth christening register records a **Mary Walker christened 18 February 1770** at Kensworth

The census ages (70 in both 1841 and 1851 per different records) are consistent with birth c.1766–1771. The LDS record date (1766) is adopted in the wiki as the most specific, but the four-year gap from the christening record is unexplained. It is possible the 1770 christening belongs to a sibling or cousin rather than to this Mary Walker.

### Parents

Father: **Richard Walker** (Kensworth area). Mother: **Mary** (surname unknown). No further details on the Walker family are available from current sources.
