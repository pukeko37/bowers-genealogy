Herbert Arthur Bunker was born in 1922, the younger son of [[ernest-caleb-bunker-1882|Ernest Caleb Bunker]] and [[helena-annie-melrose-1883|Helena Annie Melrose]], and died in 2007 aged ~85. His given name Herbert connects to a recurring family name: his uncle [[herbert-thomas-barnsley-bunker-1875|Herbert Thomas Barnsley Bunker]] (died 1917) and his half-cousin once removed [[roland-herbert-bunker-1898|Roland **Herbert** Bunker]] (born 1898, 24 years before Herbert Arthur) both carried it. Roland's middle name shows "Herbert" was already valued in the family well before the 1917 death, so it may be a wider Bunker family name rather than solely a memorial to the uncle — though the timing of the 1917 death and the 1922 birth makes a memorial reading natural. [[alan-geoffrey-bowers-1928|Alan Bowers]] remembers visiting Herbert Arthur and his brother Kenneth in Bristol in the 1930s.
