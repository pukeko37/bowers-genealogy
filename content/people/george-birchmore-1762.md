George Birchmore was born 16 December 1762, christened 25 January 1763 at Flamstead, Hertfordshire. He died 1 August 1843 at Flamstead, aged 80. His whole life was spent in Flamstead; his burial at **St Leonard Churchyard, Flamstead** is confirmed by the Find a Grave memorial listing. He farmed at **Row End Farm** (also recorded as Roe End Farm), Flamstead.

He married **Susannah Redding** (born 1763) on 28 October 1784 at Flamstead. Two children are documented in FamilySearch: Sarah Burchmore (1782, born before the marriage) and [[george-birchmore-1786|George Birchmore]] (1786–1838). The St Leonard listing also shows **William Burchmore (1791–17 Jan 1856)** buried at Flamstead — his birth year falls within the Redding marriage and strongly suggests he was a third child of George and Susannah, a sibling of George (1786) not previously recorded. See [[birchmore-redding-1784]].

His parents were Thomas Birchmore (1729–1806) and Sarah Andrew (1735–1816), married 1758 at Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire. Thomas Birchmore's burial at St Leonard (d. 25 Jul 1806) is confirmed by the same listing. The Flamstead Birchmores were an established Hertfordshire farming family.

George Birchmore (1762) is GBP Sr's paternal grandfather — two generations of illegitimacy separating him from Ellen Birchmore. The Flamstead Birchmore family is distinct from the Whipsnade Birchmore family (William Birchmore 1751) — both named Birchmore but no documented connection between the two lines.
