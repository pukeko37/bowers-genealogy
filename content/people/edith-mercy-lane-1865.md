Edith Mercy Lane was born around **1865** in Bedford, Bedfordshire — the eldest daughter of [[samuel-plackett-lane-1841|Samuel Plackett Lane]] (abt 1841–1872) and [[lydia-bunker-1833|Lydia Bunker]] (1833–1922). Her father died when she was about seven, leaving her mother Lydia to raise three daughters alone in Bedford.

She appears in the **1871 census** (age 6) and the **1881 census** (age 18) with her mother in Bedford. In **1884** she married **Frank Humphries Locke**, a stonemason born in Shoreditch. They had at least four children: Florence Locke (~1887), Samuel Locke (~1889), Hilda Locke (~1894), and Gertrude Locke (~1897).

By **1901** her mother Lydia (then 67, widowed) was living with Edith and Frank as "mother-in-law" in the household — Lydia's decades of widowhood had been anchored by Edith's family.

No death date has been located.
