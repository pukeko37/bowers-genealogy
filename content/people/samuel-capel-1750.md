Samuel Capel was born in 1750 in Cranfield, Bedfordshire, and died in 1808 in Cranfield. He married [[sarah-knight-1755|Sarah Knight]] on 15 November 1772 at Cranfield, forming the [[capel-knight-1772|Capel-Knight family]]. They had ten children, all born in the Cranfield area.

Samuel is Caleb Bunker's great-great-grandfather on the maternal Watts line: Samuel → [[sarah-capel-1791|Sarah Capel]] → [[elizabeth-watts-1810|Elizabeth Watts]] → [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]].

The spelling of the family name varies considerably across records: Capel, Cappuel, Cappoul, Kepple, Keppel — reflecting inconsistent parish registration. Several of his children also appear under different name spellings in different records.
