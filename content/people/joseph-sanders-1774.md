Joseph Sanders (baptised 30 October 1774 – buried 22 June 1810, Cardington) was a Cardington shoemaker and the husband of [[rebecca-arnold-1778|Rebecca Arnold]].

He was baptised at St Mary's Cardington on 30 October 1774, the son of **John Sanders** and his wife **Elizabeth**. He married Rebecca Arnold at Cardington on 14 May 1799, signing his name while she signed with a mark. They had three children before his death: Mary (born 1800), Anne (christened 1804), and Sarah (born c. 1808).

Joseph was buried at Cardington on 22 June 1810, his occupation recorded as Shoemaker. He was 35 years old. His widow Rebecca survived him by just over two years, dying in August 1812.

He is not the father of [[elizabeth-saunders-1811|Elizabeth Saunders]], who was born thirteen months after his death.
