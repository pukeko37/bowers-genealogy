John Isaac Mason (1913–1984) was the son of [[john-james-mason-1878|John James Mason]] (Jack) and [[mabel-annie-norman-1883|Mabel Annie Norman]]. He is named in his father's probate record (1962) and is documented in the FamilySearch sources for his father. He emigrated to **Brazil in 1961**, a year before his father's death. He died in 1984. No further details are established.
