Mary Busby was born 2 February 1709 at Dunstable, Bedfordshire, and christened 6 February 1709 at Millbrook, Bedfordshire — the Millbrook christening may indicate her family's parish connection rather than their place of residence. She is also recorded as "Mary Bushbee" in some sources. Her parents were **Thomas Busby** (1674–1724) and **Elizabeth** (1660–Deceased).

She married **[[peter-matthews-1702|Peter Matthews (1702)]]** on 21 May 1727 and had nine children, all christened at Dunstable. She outlived Peter by twelve years, dying in 1760.
