Charles Woodcraft (christened 14 February 1836, Whipsnade; died 1876), youngest child of [[william-woodcraft-1777|William Woodcraft]] and [[susan-maynard-1802|Susan Maynard]]. Born posthumously if William's death year of 1835 is correct. Appears in his mother's 1841 household aged 5; no further detail beyond the death year.
