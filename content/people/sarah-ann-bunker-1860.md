Sarah Ann Bunker (19 October 1860, Bedford), eldest child of [[george-bunker-1836|George Bunker]] and [[ann-pedder-1838|Ann Pedder]]. Christened 9 December 1860 at a Wesleyan Methodist chapel, Bedford Circuit — confirming the family's Nonconformist practice. Present in the 1871 and 1881 censuses; left home before 1891; no further records traced.
