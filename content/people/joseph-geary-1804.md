Joseph Geary was born in 1804 in Haynes (Hawnes), Bedfordshire, and died in 1890 aged 86. He was the father of [[john-geary-1825|John Geary]] (1825–1895) and ten other children, and the husband of [[rhoda-spring-bottoms-1806|Rhoda Spring Bottoms]] (1806–1884). He is Eliza Geary's paternal grandfather and Roland Bunker's great-grandfather.

**Marriage.** He married Rhoda Spring Bottoms on 20 April 1824 at Clophill, Bedfordshire — the parish adjacent to Haynes and Maulden. She was the daughter of [[william-spring-1779|William Spring]] (1779) of Shillington. Joseph was 19 or 20 at the time of the marriage; Rhoda was 17. See [[geary-spring-1824]].

**Family.** The couple had ten children, all born in Haynes: John (1825), Pamela (1828), Sarah (1830), Martha (1833), Mary (1835), Joseph (1838), George (1840), Walter (1843), Caleb (1847), Henry (1852–1853). The family is confirmed at Plum's Lane, Haynes in the 1841 census, where Joseph (35) and Rhoda (35) appear as head and wife with seven children.

**Occupation.** Not specified in the current sources, but a Haynes rural background (farming or woodland trades) is probable for the period.

**Longevity.** Joseph lived to at least 1890 — one year after his son John's confirmed census appearances at Haynes (1881) and Maulden (1891). Rhoda died in January 1884. Joseph survived his wife by six years.

His own parents are not established in the current sources.
