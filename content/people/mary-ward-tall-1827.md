Mary Ward Tall was born on **12 September 1827** — the eldest child of [[edward-tall-1806|Edward Tall]] (1806–1870) and [[sarah-ward-1806|Sarah Ward]] (1806–1894). She was christened on 2 December 1827 at **Ampthill** (Edward's home town) rather than Maulden. Her birth came just four months after her parents' marriage on 4 May 1827; she was conceived before the wedding. Her middle name **Ward** preserves her mother's maiden name — a naming practice found repeatedly across the families in this research.

She was the eldest of six children and the longest-lived, outlasting all her siblings: [[william-tall-1830|William]] (died 1862), James (died 1833), Susan (died 1848), [[rebecca-tall-1844|Rebecca]] (died 1870), and [[frederick-charles-tall-1846|Frederick Charles]] (died 1859).

**Before marriage.** The 1851 census finds Mary at 23 as **head of her own household** in Maulden, working as a **stay maker** — a trade she would continue for the rest of her working life. Living with her at the time was her youngest sister Rebecca Tall (6). She was already an independent working woman four years before her marriage.

**In Maulden.** Census records place Mary in Maulden in every decade from 1851 through 1891 — forty years of residence. She married **[[daniel-bass-1829|Daniel Bass]]** in October 1855 in Maulden, forming the [[bass-tall-1855|Bass-Tall family]]. They had three children: [[sarah-bass-1859|Sarah]] (1859–1951), [[florence-bass-1862|Florence]] (1862), and [[edward-ernest-bass-1864|Edward Ernest]] (1864–1936). Daniel died in 1868 at ~39, leaving Mary a widow with young children. She maintained the household as stay maker through the 1860s, 1870s, and 1880s. By 1891 she had adapted further — the census records her as a **corset maker** (the modern term for her trade), living in Maulden as daughter in the household of her elderly mother [[sarah-ward-1806|Sarah Ward]] (recorded as "Sarah Sall," 84, a likely transcription error for "Tall"). Her mother Sarah died in January 1894; Mary had shared the village with her for over twenty years.

**Later life.** By 1901 Mary had moved to **[[lavendon|Lavendon, Buckinghamshire]]** — a village near Olney, across the county border — to live with her eldest daughter [[sarah-bass-1859|Sarah]] and son-in-law James Lay. The 1901 census records her as "mother-in-law" in the Lay household, aged 73, still listed as a corset maker. She died there in April 1907, aged 79. Her daughter Sarah was still alive — she would outlive Mary by 44 years, dying in March 1951 at nearly 92.

Mary Ward Tall is the link between the Tall family of Maulden and any descendants through the Bass line. She is [[rebecca-tall-1844|Rebecca Tall]]'s elder sister — meaning she was Caleb Bunker's sister-in-law, and [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick Bunker]]'s aunt by marriage.
