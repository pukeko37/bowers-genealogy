Robert Henry Norman was born in 1868 in Swavesey, Cambridgeshire, the youngest child of [[thomas-norman-1827|Thomas Norman]] and [[ann-tabram-1829|Ann Tabram]] in the [[norman-tabram-1850|Norman-Tabram family]]. His birth was registered at St Ives.

He married [[ada-squires-1864|Ada Squires]] (born Fendrayton, Cambridgeshire 1864) in 1899 at St Ives, and they had two daughters: Elsie (1900–1987) and Annie (1902–1985). The family lived at Middle Watch, Swavesey — a hamlet within the parish — where Robert worked as an agricultural labourer throughout his life. See [[norman-squires-1899]].

Ada died in 1923. Robert, the youngest of his generation, outlived her by 31 years, finally dying in 1954 aged about 86 — the longest-lived of all [[charles-norman-1859|Charles Norman]]'s siblings, and one of the last links to the Swavesey branch of the family.

He outlived [[alice-norman-1890|Alice Norman]] Mason's residence in Balham and may have been alive when the Norman family moved to Kent. He and his nephew [[george-stewart-main-1919|George Main]] (born 1919) were alive at the same time for 35 years.
