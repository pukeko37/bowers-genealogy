Elizabeth Saunders (baptised 25 August 1811 – 11 October 1898) was born in Cardington, Bedfordshire, and married [[allen-thody-1813|Allen Thody]] on 19 October 1834 at St. Mary Cardington.

## Origins

Elizabeth was the illegitimate daughter of **[[rebecca-arnold-1778|Rebecca Arnold]]** (born about 1778 – buried August 1812, Cardington). Rebecca was herself the daughter of **Samuel Arnold**, a farmer at Cotton End, Cardington — Elizabeth's maternal grandfather. Samuel Arnold's will of 1790 left £150 specifically to Rebecca, a significant bequest from a farming household.

Rebecca had married **[[joseph-sanders-1774|Joseph Sanders]]**, a Cardington shoemaker, in 1799. They had three daughters together: Mary (born 1800), Anne (christened 1804), and Sarah (born c. 1808). Joseph died on 22 June 1810, leaving Rebecca a widow. Thirteen months later, Rebecca had Elizabeth — born about 24 June 1811, baptised 25 August 1811 as "Elizabeth, illegitimate Child of Rebecca Saunders, Widow, aged nine weeks." **Elizabeth's father is unknown.** Joseph Sanders was already a year dead; he had no part in Elizabeth's conception.

On 25 August 1811, Rebecca had two children baptised at once: the long-delayed Sarah (born c. 1808, finally christened at age three) and the newborn Elizabeth. Rebecca was buried at Cardington in August 1812, when Elizabeth was approximately fourteen months old. Elizabeth was orphaned before she could form any memory of her mother.

Elizabeth's half-siblings through Rebecca — Mary, Anne, and Sarah Saunders — are Elizabeth's closest blood relations on her maternal side. Whether Elizabeth maintained any relationship with them as an adult is unknown.

Her maternal line connects her to the **Arnold farming family of Cotton End** — the very hamlet where she would live her entire married life with Allen Thody. Her grandfather Samuel Arnold's brothers Robert and John Arnold were named in the 1790 will as co-executors. The witnesses at Elizabeth and Allen's 1834 marriage were **John Arnold** and **Elizabeth Arnold** (both signing with marks), almost certainly from the same Arnold family.

## Life

Elizabeth is recorded as "Elizabeth Thody formerly Saunders" on her son William's birth certificate (1850). The 1851 census records her at Cotton End, Eastcotts, age 39, occupation Lace Maker — a common cottage industry for Bedfordshire village women. She appears in every census from 1841 through 1891, consistently in the Cardington / Eastcotts area with her husband Allen.

By 1891, two of her daughters were living with her and Allen: Ann Thody (56) and Elizabeth Rogers (43), with granddaughter May Rogers (3).

She died on 11 October 1898, six months after her husband Allen (who died 22 March 1898). They had been married 64 years.
