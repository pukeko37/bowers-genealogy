Elizabeth Saunders (1811–1898) was born in Cardington, Bedfordshire, and married [[allen-thody-1813|Allen Thody]] on 19 October 1834 at St. Mary Cardington. She is recorded as "Elizabeth Thody formerly Saunders" on her son William's birth certificate (1850). The 1851 census records her at Cotton End, Bedfordshire, age 39, with her husband and five children.

She died in 1898, the same year as her husband Allen. Their youngest child [[william-allen-thody-1850|William]] was 47 at the time.
