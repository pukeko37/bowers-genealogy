Amy Bunker (born ~1878, Derby, Derbyshire), youngest child of [[george-bunker-1836|George Bunker]] and [[ann-pedder-1838|Ann Pedder]]. The only child born in Derby proper after the family moved on from New Tupton. Unmarried through the 1911 census; married **Lewis Allsop** on 1 July 1911 in Derby — shortly after the census.
