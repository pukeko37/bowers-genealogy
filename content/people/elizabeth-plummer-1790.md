Elizabeth Plummer was born around 1790 and died in 1866. She is known only through the FamilySearch record of [[george-birchmore-1786|George Birchmore]] (1786–1838), which lists her as the mother of [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]] (GBP Sr, 1813–1884). No marriage between Elizabeth and George Birchmore is recorded.

GBP Sr was born in 1813, two years before George married Ann Norton in 1815. Elizabeth raised GBP Sr as an unmarried mother. GBP Sr took her surname "Plummer" — a name he would carry alongside "Birchmore" throughout his life, eventually settling on "George Birchmore Plummer." Elizabeth outlived George Birchmore by 28 years, dying in 1866. No other children of Elizabeth are recorded.

Her origins are unknown — she may have been from Studham or the surrounding Hertfordshire/Bedfordshire border area, which is consistent with GBP Sr's 1861 census birthplace of Studham.
