Sarah Ward was born on 27 March 1806 in Flitwick, Bedfordshire, christened 12 April 1807. She lived her entire life in and around Maulden, Bedfordshire — census records place her there in 1851, 1861, 1871, 1881, and 1891. She died in Maulden on 6 January 1894, aged 87.

She was the wife of [[edward-tall-1806|Edward Tall]] (1806–1870), a carpenter. **Her maiden name was Ward** — confirmed by the Find a Grave record for their son [[frederick-charles-tall-1846|Frederick Charles Tall]] (1846–1859), which explicitly names her as "Sarah (nee Ward)." The earlier uncertainty in the wiki about whether she was "née Tall" or "née Ward" is now resolved: she was born Ward and took the Tall surname on marriage.

She married Edward on **4 May 1827 in Maulden**. Their six children were: [[mary-ward-tall-1827|Mary Ward Tall]] (1827–1907), [[william-tall-1830|William Toll]] (1830–1862), James (1832–1833), Susan (1837–1848), [[rebecca-tall-1844|Rebecca Tall]] (1844–1870), and [[frederick-charles-tall-1846|Frederick Charles Tall]] (1847–1859). The firstborn, Mary Ward, carried Sarah's maiden name as her middle name. Four of the six died before Sarah. Sarah outlived all but Mary Ward. Census records consistently place Sarah in Maulden; Edward's death records and their children's Bedford addresses suggest Edward was increasingly based in Bedford from the late 1850s, while Sarah remained in the family home at Maulden.

In the summer of 1870 Sarah lost both her daughter Rebecca (7 July) and her husband Edward (16 July). She then raised her grandson [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick]] in Maulden. Frederick's 1871 census entry places him in Maulden, consistent with Sarah's care. By 1881, Frederick had moved to Matlock, Derbyshire to be with his father [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb]].

Sarah outlived both her daughter and her grandson's early childhood, dying in Maulden on **6 January 1894** at 87. Her death was announced in the Bedford Mercury on 13 January 1894 — a long life, most of it spent in the same village.
