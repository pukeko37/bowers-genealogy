Job Bunker was christened on 14 May 1780 at Millbrook, the second son of [[joseph-bunker-1754|Joseph Bunker (1754–1808)]] and [[martha-garratt-1756|Martha Garratt]]. He was the second of twelve children.

He married **[[rebecca-atterbury-1785|Rebecca Atterbury]]** (born 1785) and had eight children, most born in Millbrook between 1807 and 1821. His survival to adulthood and fatherhood resolves a long-standing open question in [[bunker-garratt-1777]] — Job was not simply untraced but left a family in Millbrook spanning the early nineteenth century.

His death date and place are not yet established. Given that his youngest children were born around 1821, he likely lived at least into the 1820s.

## Children

See [[bunker-atterbury]] for the full family. Eight children: Samuel (1807), Joel (1808–1875), Patty (1810), Sarah (1812), Rebecca (1816), John (1818), Anne (1819, died in infancy), Anne (1821).

## Notes

The naming of his daughter Patty (1810) likely echoes his sister [[patty-bunker-1797|Patty Bunker (~1797)]], one of his siblings who remains untraced. The two daughters both named Anne — the first dying in infancy in 1819, the second surviving — follows the replacement-naming pattern common in this family.

Not to be confused with [[job-bunker-1804|Job Bunker (son of Joel)]], who is a different person from the Eversholt branch.
