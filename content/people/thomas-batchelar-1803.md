Thomas Batchelar was born 9 November 1803 in Dagnall, Buckinghamshire (christened 22 June 1804 in Ivinghoe, the neighbouring parish), and died 22 December 1872 in Whipsnade, Bedfordshire. The probate record describes his occupation as **Shepherd** — a skilled pastoral role on the Whipsnade chalk downland, more specific than the "agricultural labourer" label of the censuses. He left a will; probate was granted 5 March 1873 at the Principal Registry, with **Henry Perkins of Whipsnade, Farmer**, as sole executor. His estate was under £200. Perkins was the tenant of [[dell-farm|Dell Farm]] from 1851 to 1871 — a near-neighbour on the village green. His wife Anne (1830–1867) and three children who died young (Henry Holloway 1856–1864, Mary Florence 1861–1862, Henry 1859–1874) are buried at St Mary Magdalene; Henry Perkins himself is not in the churchyard list, suggesting he was buried elsewhere or his stone is lost.

**Father confirmed (2026-05-20):** The Ivinghoe christening record names his father as **David Batchelor** and his mother as **Ann**. This is corroborated by the 1868 marriage record of Thomas himself (aged 64), which again lists David Batchelor as his father. See [[batchelor-ann-1801]].

He is confirmed as the father of [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]] (1829–1867): he attended the 1859 marriage of Eliza to [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]] at St Pancras, was named in Eliza's christening record, and the 1861 census places the Birchmore family in Thomas's household at Whipsnade Green.

Thomas married **[[susan-matthews-1800|Susan Matthews]]** on 22 May 1826 at Shenley, Hertfordshire. Susan was the daughter of [[peter-matthews-1770|Peter Matthews]] and [[mary-walker-1766|Mary Walker]] of Whipsnade. Their four children — Emma (1827), Eliza (1829), Ann (1834), Elizabeth (1842) — were all christened in Whipsnade. See [[batchelar-matthews-1826]].

Susan died 20 November 1857. Thomas remarried on 11 January 1868 at Whipsnade, wedding **[[susan-maynard-1802|Susan Maynard]]** (aged 67). The marriage record names David Batchelor (Thomas's father) and **George Maynard** — confirming George as Susan's father and her maiden name as Maynard. The "Durent" surname she carried into this marriage came from her second husband, Edmund Durrant — this was in fact Thomas's third marriage: Susan had first married William Woodcraft at Whipsnade in 1827, was widowed by 1835, then married Edmund Durrant at Whipsnade in 1849. Susan was a **School Mistress** in Whipsnade in 1851 (1851 census occupation), a notable independent role for a woman in the village. She was born in Luton, Bedfordshire (confirmed by the 1871 census). The 1871 census records Thomas (69) and Susan (71, birthplace Luton, Bedfordshire) at Whipsnade. The 1871 Susan is this second wife, not Susan Matthews.

**The Abraham Batchelor connection:** [[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham Batchelor]] held the Chequers Inn licence before [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]] and [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] took it in 1867. Abraham's line traces to Thomas Bachelor (1781, Flamstead) and beyond. Whether Thomas Batchelar (1803, Dagnall) and that Flamstead Bachelor family share a common ancestor through [[david-batchelor-unknown|David Batchelor]] is the outstanding open question.
