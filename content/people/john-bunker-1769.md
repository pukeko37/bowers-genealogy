John Bunker was born in 1768 and christened on 20 August 1769 at Millbrook — the second child of [[edward-bunker-1742|Edward Bunker]] (1742–1814) and Mary Ashby (1745–1813). He was buried at Millbrook on 16 December 1795, aged 27. The burial register names his father as "Edwd Bunker."

No marriage or children are recorded. He died the year after his elder brother [[william-bunker-1767|William]] married Ann Denton, and nearly twenty years before their father Edward. The cause of death is unknown.

Only two sources exist for John: his christening record and his burial entry. He left no documented trace in between.
