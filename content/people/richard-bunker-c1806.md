Richard Bunker (born c.1804–1806, Bedfordshire) was the son of [[james-bunker-1777|James Bunker (1777)]] by his first wife **Sarah Pedder**. He appears in the 1841 census aged 35 at Village Sand End, Millbrook, in his father's household. The Millbrook page previously estimated his birth as "c.1800" — the census age of 35 revises this to c.1804–1806.

He is James's older surviving son; his brother **John Bunker (1803)** had his own household nearby by 1841. No marriage, death, or further census record has been found for Richard.
