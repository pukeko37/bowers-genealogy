John Bunker was born in 1871 in Millbrook, the fifth child of [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker (1840–1914)]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]]. FamilySearch records his christening as 19 October 1873 — the same anomalous date given for siblings George (born 1868) and James (born 1873). This may reflect a batch christening of the younger children on that day, or a data error.

Unlike his elder brothers, John did not migrate to the coalfields. He is recorded in Millbrook in the censuses of 1871, 1881, and 1891 — the only brother to remain in Millbrook through 1891.

**1893**: John married **Ellen Mary Roffe** on 16 October 1893 at Millbrook. Their daughter Bertha Florence Bunker was born in 1893 — possibly before the marriage or very shortly after. A second child, Albert Edward Bunker, was born in 1894. Albert Edward was killed in the First World War in 1917.

John died in 1900 at Ampthill, aged approximately 28–29, leaving a widow and two young children.
