Edith Eleanor Birchmore was born in 1876 in St Pancras, London, the second child of [[george-birchmore-plummer-jr-1850|George Birchmore Plummer Jr]] and [[elizabeth-sarah-morse-1852|Elizabeth Sarah Morse]]. She was in St Pancras in 1881 and 1891.

In **1898 she married Alfred Browne** at St Pancras — the same year her elder sister Rosa married George Henry Lomax, also at Pancras. The two sisters married in the same year, both in the St Pancras neighbourhood where they had grown up. No children are recorded for Edith and Alfred.

Edith died on 10 June 1947; probate was granted in London on 13 August 1947. No death place is specified in the record but the London probate strongly indicates she remained in the capital throughout her life. This sets her apart from her siblings: her brother George William and sister Florence both ended up in Devon, her sister Rosa in Bournemouth — but Edith stayed in London.
