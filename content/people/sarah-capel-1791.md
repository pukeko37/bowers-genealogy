Sarah Capel was born in 1791, christened 13 March 1791 at Cranfield, Bedfordshire — the daughter of [[samuel-capel-1750|Samuel Capel]] (1750–1808) and [[sarah-knight-1755|Sarah Knight]] (1755–1839). She died after 1851 (alive in the Millbrook census that year); her FamilySearch death date is "after 1851."

Sarah had three marriages across thirty years, twice widowed before she was 50:

1. **[[john-watts-1790|John Watts]]** (1790–1814) — married 27 July 1809, Cranfield. Four children: John (1809), [[elizabeth-watts-1810|Elizabeth]] (1810), Joseph (1811), James (1813). Widowed 1814, aged ~23.
2. **Edward Roffe** (1769–1838) — married 19 April 1824, Cranfield (ten years after John's death). Four children: William (1825), Edward (1827), Susanna (1829), Ruth (1833). Widowed 1838, aged ~47.
3. **John Thompson** (1804–?) — married 15 April 1839, Millbrook. No children recorded.

Her many known names reflect these marriages: Sarah Watts, Sarah Roffe, Sarah Thompson, Sarah Keppel. She is recorded at Village Hollow, Millbrook in 1841 and 1851.

Sarah is Caleb Bunker's maternal grandmother — a resilient woman who raised four young Watts children alone from 1814, remarried a widower, outlived him too, and married a third time in her late forties. Her daughter [[elizabeth-watts-1810|Elizabeth Watts]] (1810–1868) married [[james-bunker-1807|James Bunker]] in 1830.
