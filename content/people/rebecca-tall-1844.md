Rebecca Tall was born in 1844 in Bedfordshire, the daughter of [[edward-tall-1806|Edward Tall]] and [[sarah-ward-1806|Sarah Ward]] of Maulden. She appears in the 1851 census in Maulden aged about seven. She married [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]] on Christmas Day 1868 at Howard Chapel, Mill Street, Bedford — a Nonconformist chapel, suggesting the family's religious affiliation.

Their son [[frederick-bunker-1869|Frederick William Tall Bunker]] was born in October 1869, named "Tall" in the middle to honour his mother's family. Rebecca died on 7 July 1870, aged about 26, and was buried in Ampthill. Frederick was approximately one year old.

Her father [[edward-tall-1806|Edward Tall]] died just nine days later, on 16 July 1870. Two deaths in the same family within nine days left Frederick entirely without his maternal line's generation — only his grandmother [[sarah-ward-1806|Sarah Ward]] remained, and she raised him.

Rebecca's life is almost entirely undocumented beyond the marriage and burial records. She was too young and died too soon to leave much trace.
