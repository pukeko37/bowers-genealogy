Walter Cook (Walter Cooke in christening records, christened 11 October 1868) was the eldest son of [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook (1843–1908)]] and [[lois-brandsom-1843|Lois Brandsom]]. He was born and raised in Whipsnade.

He is recorded in the 1871 census (age 2), 1881 census (age 12), and 1891 census (age 22). He stood as a witness at the marriage of [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] and [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] at Whipsnade Parish Church on 6 February 1894. The other witness was [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]] — the daughter of his mother's brother [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]] (Lucy's sister). Walter and Florence were first cousins.

His subsequent history beyond 1894 has not been located.
