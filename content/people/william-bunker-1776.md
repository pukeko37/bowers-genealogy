William Bunker (1776–1855) was the second surviving son of [[william-bunker-1740|William Bunker (1740–1816)]] and [[ann-peet-1746|Ann Peet]], and younger brother of [[thomas-bunker-1767|Thomas Bunker (1767)]]. Born and raised in Steppingley, he spent most of his life there before moving to Ampthill (Dunstable Street) in his final years. FamilySearch ID L2TY-LPT. Agricultural labourer.

He had two marriages. His first wife **Elisabeth Burridge** (1777–1807) died after nine years of marriage, having had two children who both died in infancy. His second wife **Hannah Bitchener** (1782–?) produced at least five further children over a long marriage. See [[bunker-burridge-1798]] and [[bunker-bitchener-1808]].

The census trail is consistent: Steppingley in 1841 and 1851, then Ampthill (Dunstable Street) in 1861, where he died in 1855 per GRO. His children from the second marriage were all still living in Steppingley in his later years; the move to Ampthill was presumably in old age, possibly to live with a child.

No Ruth appears among his documented children.
