Sarah Eusden was born in 1804, the eldest known child of Thomas Euseden (1764–1842). She married **John Asplan** at Fenstanton in 1829, one year after her sister [[mary-eusden-1807|Mary]] married Richard Tabraham at the same church. She died in 1833, four years after her marriage, at around age 29.

No children of Sarah and John Asplan are yet known.
