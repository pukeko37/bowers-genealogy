Joel Bunker (1789–1861) was the sixth child of [[joseph-bunker-1754|Joseph Bunker (1754)]] and [[martha-garratt-1756|Martha Garratt]], baptised 26 April 1789 at Millbrook. He married **[[martha-taylor-1786|Martha Taylor]]** (1786–1836) on 30 January 1809 at Millbrook — just two months after his father's death on 27 November 1808. He died in 1861 at Ampthill, aged ~72.

He is the first of his parents' nine surviving children to be traced into the next generation.

## Family

Joel and Martha had at least four children. His son **Joseph Bunker (1810)** is a significant figure: if Joseph (1810) had a son of his own born around 1837, that would identify the "Joseph Bunker (~1837)" in Millbrook's later census records as a grandson of Joel and great-grandson of the cordwainer Joseph (1754) — confirming that this line persisted in the village. The connection is not yet documented but is the most direct research lead from this line.

The child "Job Bunker" is listed in FamilySearch with a birth year of 1804 — five years before the marriage. This is almost certainly a dating error; 1814 is more plausible. The name echoes Joel's brother [[joseph-bunker-1754|Job Bunker (1780)]].

Martha Taylor died in 1836, leaving Joel a widower. He died in 1861 at Ampthill — just a few miles from Millbrook. His presence in Ampthill at death means he should appear in the 1841 and 1851 censuses, and would have been alive when the 1861 census was taken.
