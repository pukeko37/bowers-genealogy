Alice Mabel Dumpleton was born in 1883, daughter of [[alfred-dumpleton|Alfred Dumpleton]] and Emma Hucklesby. She married [[thomas-cook-1882|Thomas Edward Cook]] in 1906. Their son Leslie Thomas Cook was born in 1907. Alice died 13 November 1940 at Kensworth and is buried there with Thomas.

Alfred and Emma had five children: four sons and Alice as their only daughter. Arthur Thomas Dumpleton (1879–1951), buried at Kensworth, is likely one of her brothers. Lizzie Dumpleton (1876) at Kensworth is from a different branch — Alfred and Emma's children were four boys and Alice only. See [[dumpleton-hucklesby]].
