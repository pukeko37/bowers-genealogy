Martha Sarah Bunker was born in 1841 in Millbrook, Bedfordshire, the sixth child of [[james-bunker-1807|James Bunker]] and Elizabeth Watts. She died on 7 July 1901 in Luton, Bedfordshire, and was buried there on 10 July. An obituary was published in Luton on 12 July 1901.

In the 1861 census she is recorded at Dane Street, St Paul's parish, Bedford, aged 20, as a **bonnet sewer** — a common trade in the region alongside straw plaiting and lace making. Martha followed the Bunker family pattern of relocating after her father's death in 1852, eventually leaving Bedford for Luton where she remained through 1901. Her presence in Luton pre-dates Caleb's son Frederick's settlement there, and her sister [[comfort-ann-bunker-1848|Comfort Ann]] also lived in Luton.
