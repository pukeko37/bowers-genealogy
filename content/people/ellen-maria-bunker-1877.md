Ellen Maria Bunker was born in 1877 in Millbrook, the eighth child of [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker (1840–1914)]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]]. She was christened 29 April 1877 at Millbrook.

She remained in Millbrook through the 1881 and 1891 censuses. By 1911 she had moved to Luton, where she was living with her husband.

**1904**: Ellen Maria married **Walter Davies** (born 1877) on 2 August 1904 at Millbrook — in her home village, aged about 27. They had one child: Beatrice Maud Davies, born in 1906.

Ellen Maria died in 1915 in Luton, aged approximately 37–38, leaving Beatrice aged about eight or nine. Beatrice Maud Davies lived to 2003.
