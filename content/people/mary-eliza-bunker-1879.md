Mary Eliza Bunker was born on 8 May 1879 in Millbrook, the ninth child of [[joseph-bunker-1840|Joseph Bunker (1840–1914)]] and [[mary-ann-wilcox-1842|Mary Ann Wilcox]].

She was at home in Millbrook in both the 1881 and 1891 censuses. By 1901 she had moved to Luton, and she remained there through at least the 1939 National Register — making her the most consistently Luton-based of the daughters.

**1899**: Mary Eliza married **Edward Albert Freeman Sr.** (born 1877) at Ampthill in 1899. Their first child, Dorothy Mary, was born the same year. Three more children followed: John Henry (1900), Nellie (1903), and Edward Albert Freeman Jr. (1909–1909, who died in infancy).

Her date of death is not recorded, but she was alive and in Luton in 1939, aged approximately sixty.
