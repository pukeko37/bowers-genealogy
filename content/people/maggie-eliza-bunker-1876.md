Maggie Eliza Bunker was born in 1876, the third child of [[caleb-bunker-1845|Caleb Bunker]] and [[margaret-barnsley-1850|Margaret Barnsley]], and died in 1915 aged ~39. No further details are recorded beyond her dates.

Her middle name Eliza may echo her grandmother Elizabeth (Watts) Bunker, or may simply have been a family choice. She died two years before her brother Herbert and three years before her father Caleb — three of the six children of this marriage predeceased their father.
