George Causer was born in Staffordshire around 1859. He married [[mary-ann-norman-1861|Mary Ann Norman]] in Huntingdonshire in 1883 and the couple settled in Swavesey, Cambridgeshire. By 1891 he was working as an **Engine Driver** — a skilled industrial occupation uncommon in an agricultural village, suggesting he may have worked on the railways or agricultural machinery. The couple had six children born between 1885 and 1895.

George died in 1898, leaving Mary Ann a widow with children aged from infancy to 13. The 1901 census records her as widowed and working as a laundress to support the family.

Nothing more is known of his origins, family, or the circumstances of his death. He is the only non-local spouse among the Swavesey Norman siblings — everyone else married within Cambridgeshire or Huntingdonshire.
