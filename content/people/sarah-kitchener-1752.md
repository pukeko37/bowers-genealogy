Sarah Kitchener was born and christened on 3 December 1752 in Shillington, Bedfordshire. She married [[william-spring-1753|William Spring]] on 11 November 1773 at Shillington — both parties from the same parish, just one year apart in age. Her death date is unknown. She is Eliza Geary's great-great-great-grandmother.

**Parents.** Her father was **William Kitchener** (1721–Deceased) and her mother **Elizabeth Gudgeon** (1727–1764), who married in 1746 at Shillington. Elizabeth Gudgeon died in 1764, when Sarah was about twelve years old. The Kitchener family was rooted in Shillington. Sarah's known siblings: William Kitchener (1748) and James Kitchener (1750).

**Family.** With William Spring, Sarah had eight children. See [[spring-kitchener-1773]].

The Kitchener ancestry (William Kitchener 1721 + Elizabeth Gudgeon 1727) is the current outer limit of established ancestry on this line. William Kitchener (1721) and Elizabeth Gudgeon (1727) have not been traced further in available sources.
