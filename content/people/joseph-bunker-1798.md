Joseph Bunker (born about 1797, Steppingley — died January 1868, Ampthill) was the eldest son of [[thomas-bunker-1767|Thomas Bunker (1767–1843)]] and Susannah Foulkes. FamilySearch ID M98G-525. Not to be confused with [[joseph-bunker-1792|Joseph Bunker (1792)]] of Stevington, who is from a different branch entirely (the James Thomas 1764 line).

His christening at Steppingley on 27 August 1797 is confirmed by the parish record, giving his parents as "Thos Bunker" and "Susannah." FamilySearch lists his birth year as 1798, which is likely an error or estimate — 1797 is the better-evidenced date.

He married **Mary Brown** (born ~1800, Westoning, Bedfordshire) on 11 May 1821 at Steppingley and spent his entire life there, appearing in the 1841, 1851, and 1861 censuses at Church Street, Steppingley. He died in January 1868 at Ampthill — the registration district town for Steppingley — aged approximately 70.

His eight children and their family are documented in [[bunker-brown-1821]]. No daughter named Ruth appears among them.
