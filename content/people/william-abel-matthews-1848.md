William Abel Matthews Sr. was born **18 March 1848** in Whipsnade, Bedfordshire, the third and youngest confirmed child of [[able-matthews-1813|Able Matthews]] and [[mary-walker-1817|Mary Walker]]. He appears in the 1851 census in Whipsnade aged 3, alongside sisters Sarah (10) and Elizabeth (4).

He emigrated with his father from Liverpool in 1855, stopping in St. Louis, Missouri, where his mother **Mary Ann Walker** died. His father Able reached Utah in 1861 with the David H. Cannon Company; William would have crossed at the same time, aged approximately 13.

The family settled in **Lehi, Utah County**, where William's first son, William Abel Matthews Jr., was born in 1871. He subsequently moved south to **Virgin, Washington County, Utah** — the same town where his uncle [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis Matthews]] had settled — and spent the rest of his life there.

He married **Rebecca Vince** and had three children:
- William Abel Matthews Jr. (born 1871, Lehi, Utah)
- Betsy Edith Matthews (born 1884)
- Ralph Vince Matthews (born 1893)

He died **16 August 1917** in Virgin, Washington County, Utah, aged 69, and was buried there.
