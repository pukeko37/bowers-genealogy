James Bunker (1749–1818) was the eleventh child of [[thomas-bunker-1704|Thomas Bunker (1704)]] and [[mary-reade-1702|Mary Reade]], christened 21 February 1749 at Millbrook. He married **[[elizabeth-pedder-1752|Elizabeth Pedder]]** (1752–1830) on 2 March 1772 at Millbrook and had five children. He died 10 July 1818 in Bedfordshire; Elizabeth outlived him by twelve years, dying in 1830.

The Pedder surname also appears in the [[bunker-ashby-1766|Edward Bunker (1742)]] line — Edward's son James Bunker (1777) married a Sarah Pedder. Whether the two Pedder families are connected is not established.

See [[bunker-pedder-1772]] for the full family narrative.
