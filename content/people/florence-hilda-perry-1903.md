Florence Hilda Perry (1903, Whipsnade) was the second child of [[arthur-perry-1866|Arthur Perry]] and [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]]. Unlike her siblings she was born at Whipsnade rather than Dunstable. In the 1911 census she was living at Whipsnade with her mother and maternal grandparents [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]], age 8. Further life history not yet established.
