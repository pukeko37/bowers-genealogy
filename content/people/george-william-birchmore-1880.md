George William Birchmore was born 21 September 1880 in St Pancras, London, the third child of [[george-birchmore-plummer-jr-1850|George Birchmore Plummer Jr]] and [[elizabeth-sarah-morse-1852|Elizabeth Sarah Morse]]. He grew up in St Pancras, and is recorded there in 1881, 1891, and 1901.

By 1911 he had moved to Sidmouth, Devon — a significant departure from the family's London base — and remained there through 1939. His occupation is recorded as Grocer Servant.

His mother [[elizabeth-sarah-morse-1852|Elizabeth]] died in Devon in 1928, and his sister [[florence-eliza-birchmore-1884|Florence]] also died in Devon in 1951, suggesting he drew other family members to follow him west.

When his mother [[elizabeth-sarah-morse-1852|Elizabeth]] died at Belmont, Withycombe Raleigh (Exmouth area) on 16 July 1928, George William administered her probate. He is described in that record as "of no occupation," suggesting he was not in paid work at age 47. Her estate was £2177 18s. 10d. He was living nearby — she at Withycombe Raleigh, he recorded at Sidmouth — both in East Devon.

George William died in Exeter, Devon in 1943, probate granted December that year.
