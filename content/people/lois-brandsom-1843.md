Lois Brandsom (born 1843, Whipsnade – died 1924) was the fifth child of [[jeremiah-bransom-1808|Jeremiah Bransom]] and [[sarah-watts-1816|Sarah Watts]]. She was christened on 20 September 1846 at Whipsnade. Her brother was [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] (1850–1928).

She married [[charles-cook-1843|Charles Cook]] on 21 March 1868 at Whipsnade, with her father Jeremiah Brandsom present. Together they had eight children:

1. [[walter-cook-1869|Walter Cooke]] (1869–Deceased) — christened 11 October 1868
2. Henry Cooke (1870–Deceased)
3. William Cooke (1872–Deceased)
4. James Cook (1873–Deceased)
5. Charles Cooke (1876–Deceased)
6. George Cook (1877–Deceased)
7. Edward Cooke (1880–Deceased)
8. [[albert-ralph-cook-1886|Albert Ralph Cook]] (1886–1932)

She appears in Whipsnade in every census from 1851 through 1911. The 1881 census shows her mother Sarah Brandsom (aged 68, from Edlesborough) living with the family — suggesting Sarah was by then widowed or dependent. Lois died in 1924, her death registered in the Luton district.

The Bransom-Cook web: her brother Edwin married [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]], whose sister was [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]]. Lois's son [[walter-cook-1869|Walter]] stood as a witness at Lucy's 1894 wedding alongside Edwin and Mary's daughter [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]] — first cousins on both sides of the Bransom family.

## The Chequers Inn

On Charles's death in 1908, Lois briefly held the licence for the Chequers Inn, Whipsnade — the village's only public house. She appears in the FamilySearch licensee register as "Louis Cook," which is a transcription error for Lois. She was the last of the extended Cook/Batchelar/Birchmore family network to hold the licence; the next licensee, James Vicars (1909), had no family connection.
