Susannah Durrant (1739–1821) married [[peter-matthews-1734|Peter Matthews (1734–1812)]] on 12 October 1761 at Flamstead, Hertfordshire. She is the mother of [[peter-matthews-1770|Peter Matthews (1770)]], through whom the Whipsnade Matthews line descends.

Flamstead was the Matthews family's marriage parish and is likely to have been Susannah's home parish as well — but this has not been confirmed. Her parentage and origins are unresearched. She outlived her husband (who died 1812) by nine years, dying in 1821.

Their three children were christened at Kensworth: Susannah Matthews (1763), Mary Matthews (1767), and [[peter-matthews-1770|Peter Matthews (1770)]].
