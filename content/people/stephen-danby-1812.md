Stephen Danby (abt 1812–1867) was an excise officer who married [[eliza-bunker-1815|Eliza Bunker]] on 11 March 1838 at Eversholt, Bedfordshire. He is described as being from Hockliffe in the Bucks Gazette marriage notice. His career as an excise officer took the family to Thame, Staffordshire, and Derbyshire. He died in 1867 leaving Eliza a widow. See [[danby-bunker-1838]].
