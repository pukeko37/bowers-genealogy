Helen Fensome (born ~1864, Bedford), eldest child of [[phoebe-alice-bunker-1843|Phoebe Alice Bunker]] and [[samuel-fensome-1842|Samuel Fensome]]. Confirmed by the 1871 census and her marriage record. She married **John George Mason** on 28 December 1891 at Pilsley, Derbyshire; no further records traced after marriage.
