Mary Saunders Cook was born in June 1848 in Market Street, Hertfordshire — the second of three illegitimate children of [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] (1820–1904). Her father is unknown. Her siblings were [[charles-cook-1847|Charles Cook]] (1847) and [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]] (1856). Her christening in Whipsnade was delayed until 18 August 1850, over two years after her birth, suggesting Ruth was temporarily living in Market Street when Mary was born before returning to Whipsnade.

When Mary was about 19, her mother Ruth married [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer]] in December 1867, and the household settled at the Chequers Inn, Whipsnade. Mary's half-step-sister (in effect) was [[ellen-birchmore-1860|Ellen Birchmore]], GBP Sr's daughter by his second wife.

Mary married [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] in 1871 and together they raised seven children in Whipsnade:

1. [[albert-bransom-1871|Albert Bransom]] (1871–1940)
2. [[florence-bransom-1873|Florence Bransom]] (1873–1963)
3. [[ebenezer-bransom-1875|Ebenezer Bransom]] (1875–1965)
4. [[clara-lucy-bransom-1877|Clara Lucy Bransom]] (1877–1957) — married Cyril Baldock 1912
5. [[margaret-anne-bransom-1880|Margaret Anne Bransom]] (1880–1964)
6. [[walter-william-bransom-1881|Walter William Bransom]] (1881–1963)
7. [[minnie-rose-bransom-1884|Minnie Rose Bransom]] (1884–1960)

Mary spent her entire recorded life in Whipsnade — "By Whipsnade Green" is her listed address, and she appears in every census from 1851 through 1911. She died in 1937 aged approximately 89, her death registered in the Luton district.

Florence Bransom stood as a witness at her aunt [[lucy-cook-1856|Lucy Cook]]'s wedding to [[william-allen-thody-1850|William Allen Thody]] at Whipsnade on 6 February 1894.
