Able Matthews (also spelled **Abel** in LDS and official records) was born 19 April 1813 in Whipsnade, Bedfordshire — the fifth child of [[thomas-matthews-1767|Thomas Matthews]] and [[mary-wilson-1779|Mary Wilson]]. He spent his early adult life in the Whipsnade and Dunstable area before becoming one of the family's Mormon emigrants to Utah.

## Marriage to Mary Walker — 1840

Able married **[[mary-walker-1817|Mary Walker]]** on 22 November 1840 at Dunstable, Bedfordshire, with James Walker (likely her father) as a witness. The 1841 census found Able, Mary, and an infant daughter Sarah living in the household of his sister [[anne-matthews-1815|Anne Matthews]] and her husband [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|George Birchmore Plummer Sr.]] at Houchen End, Whipsnade — the two families sharing a roof in the early years of both marriages.

By 1851 the couple had their own household in Whipsnade. Abel Mathews, aged 38, is recorded as head of household and agricultural labourer, with wife Mary (34, born Aylesbury area, Buckinghamshire) and three children: Sarah (10), Elizabeth (4), and William (3). All children born in Whipsnade. A fourth child, **Anna**, was born 23 February 1853 — after the 1851 census and before the 1855 emigration.

## LDS Conversion and Emigration

The family converted to the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in the 1840s as part of the wider Matthews family conversion. Able was baptised LDS; his mother [[mary-wilson-1779|Mary Wilson]] was baptised in January 1851.

Able emigrated in 1855. Saints by Sea confirms the family sailed **Liverpool to Philadelphia**, departing **27 February 1855** and arriving **20 April 1855**. The FamilySearch sources attribute the voyage to the ship *Siddons*; Saints by Sea confirms the route and dates but does not name the ship in the passenger record. **Mary Ann Walker was alive at departure** — she is listed in the Saints by Sea record as "Mary A. W. Matthews, Wife, 37." The family stopped in St. Louis, Missouri, where **Mary died** between April 1855 and June 1861. He eventually joined the overland crossing in 1861 with the **David H. Cannon Company**, departing 1 June 1861 and arriving in Deseret on **16 August 1861**.

He was recorded as a member of the LDS church in **Lehi, Utah County** in 1861, and was naturalised as a US citizen in Utah Territory in 1866 and again in 1878.

## Life in Lehi, Utah

Able settled in **Lehi, Utah County**, where he worked as a farmer. He remarried — his second wife, **[[jennette-matthews|Jennette]]** (born Scotland, ~1818), appears with him in both the 1870 and 1880 censuses at Lehi. In 1870 she was 52; in 1880 she was 60. No children appear in those censuses, suggesting she was past child-bearing age when they married.

Able died on **24 March 1891** and was buried at Lehi City Cemetery, Utah. His son William (born Whipsnade, ~1848) appears to have also settled in Utah, dying in Virgin, Washington County in August 1917.
