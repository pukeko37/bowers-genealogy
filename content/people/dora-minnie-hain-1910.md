Dora Minnie Hain (1910–1996), younger daughter of [[james-hain-1877|James Hain]] and [[margaret-anne-bransom-1880|Margaret Anne Bransom]], born at Dell Farm, Whipsnade. On census day 1911 she appears (aged 0, recorded as "Dora Minne Hain") in the household of her maternal grandparents [[edwin-bransom-1850|Edwin Bransom]] and [[mary-saunders-cook-1848|Mary Saunders Cook]] — a newborn visiting the Bransom family home. A memorial for "Dora Hain" with no dates appears in the St Mary Magdalene churchyard list (Find a Grave); this may be her, though no death date is recorded on the stone.
