Thomas Bunker (born about 1767 – died 1843, Steppingley) was the eldest son of [[william-bunker-1740|William Bunker (1740–1816)]] and [[ann-peet-1746|Ann Peet]], born before his parents' October 1768 marriage. He spent his entire documented life in the Steppingley / Houghton Conquest area. FamilySearch ID L2TT-F6F.

His christening on 10 January 1773 at Steppingley was delayed — he was about five or six years old at the time, consistent with the Nonconformist late-registration practice seen across the broader Bunker family.

He married **Susannah Foulkes** (1765–1843) at Steppingley on 21 November 1796. Unusually, both Thomas and Susannah died in the same year, 1843. The 1841 census places him not at Steppingley itself but at **How End, Houghton Conquest** — the next parish west. His five documented children are in [[bunker-foulkes-1796]].

The most significant unresearched lead from his family is his eldest son **Joseph Bunker (1798–1868)**. Joseph survived to 1868 — the right age to have children in the 1830s–1840s — and is the prime candidate for the parent of the Ruth Bunker born ~1841 who appears in Steppingley across all four censuses 1841–1871 (RB-K in [[ruth-bunker-findmypast-register]]). Joseph's family is entirely undocumented.
