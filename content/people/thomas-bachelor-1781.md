Thomas Bachelor was born in 1781 in Flamstead, Hertfordshire (christening recorded 6 February 1780 — the year discrepancy suggests he was born in late 1780 and the 1781 birth year is approximate). His parents were Joseph Batchelor (1755) and Sarah Bingham (1758). He married Alice Woodcroft (1791–?) on 2 August 1816 in Whipsnade, settling there permanently. His address in Whipsnade was "Frog Corner." He appears in the 1841 and 1851 census in Whipsnade.

Their children included George (1811, before the marriage), David (1817), Sarah (1819), [[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham]] (1825), and Rebecca (1829). See [[bachelor-woodcroft-1816]].

Thomas is the patriarch of the Whipsnade Bachelor family. His son [[abraham-batchelor-1825|Abraham]] held the Chequers Inn licence before [[george-birchmore-plummer-1813|GBP Sr]] and [[ruth-cooke-1820|Ruth Cooke]] took it over in 1867.

**Relationship to Thomas Batchelar (1803, Dagnall):** Thomas Bachelor (1781, Flamstead) and Thomas Batchelar (1803, Dagnall) are from neighbouring Chilterns villages — Flamstead and Dagnall are 4 miles apart. Thomas Batchelar is the probable father of [[eliza-batchelor-1829|Eliza Batchelor]], GBP Sr's second wife. The family connection between the two Thomas Batchelar/Bachelor lines has not yet been confirmed by clippings, but is a priority research question: if they are cousins or uncle-nephew, it would establish Abraham and Eliza as cousins.
