Charles Matthews was born around 1832 at Kensworth, Hertfordshire, the son of [[thomas-matthews-1797|Thomas Matthews the younger]] and his first wife Mary Squire. He appears in the 1841 census at Kensworth (age 9) and the 1851 census (age 19, with father Thomas and step-mother Elizabeth Hopkins).

Unlike his uncles [[able-matthews-1813|Able]] and [[daniel-willis-matthews-1820|Daniel Willis]], Charles did not emigrate with the LDS group. He married **Mary** (born ~1838–1841, also from Kensworth) and by the 1871 census had settled at **Caddington**, Bedfordshire, a village two miles east of Kensworth. Known children from the 1871 and 1881 censuses:

- Henry Matthews (~1864)
- Elizabeth Matthews (~1865)
- Herbert Matthews (~1870)
- Mary Matthews (~1870)
- William Matthews (date unknown)

The family continued at Caddington through at least 1881. No death record has been located. Charles's wife Mary (by then a widow, listed without a spouse) appears in the 1901 census at Caddington with her adult children Elizabeth and William.

**Note:** A "David Matthews" born 1853 at Kensworth appears in Thomas the younger's 1861 household. If David was Charles's illegitimate or early son (Charles would have been ~21 in 1853), that could explain his presence — but David's parentage in the census is unrecorded. See [[david-matthews-1853]].
