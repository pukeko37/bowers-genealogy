Albert Newman Norman was born in **1888** in Alconbury Weston, Huntingdonshire — the sixth child of [[tom-norman-1856|Tom Norman]] (1856–1925) and [[emily-thulborn-1856|Emily Thulborn]] (1856–1922). His middle name **Newman** commemorates his maternal grandfather, [[newman-thulborn-1821|Newman Thulborn]] (abt 1821–1888) of Oakington — a Victorian naming practice that preserved the Thulborn family connection in the Norman line.

He is recorded as "Bertie" in the 1901 census, aged 12, still at home in Alconbury Weston with his parents and younger siblings Thomas and Hannah. By 1911 the family had moved to **Eaton Socon** on the Huntingdonshire/Bedfordshire border, where his father Tom was recorded as both farm labourer and beerhouse keeper.

Albert succeeded his father at the **Crown Beerhouse, Great North Road, Eaton Socon** — the same premises Tom ran in 1911. He is documented as keeper there from at least **1922 to 1931**, with the owner recorded as Mrs Archbald. **Kelly's 1924 Bedfordshire Directory** lists him independently in the commercial section as "Norman Albert Newman, Crown P.H." (the directory using P.H. generically for the licensed premises). Tom died in 1925 with Albert already three years into his tenure; the handover was a going-concern succession, not an inheritance following a death. The timing suggests Tom formally handed over to Albert in the early 1920s as he aged, while Emily was still alive. Albert held the licence through 1931 at minimum; his eventual departure date and destination are not yet known.

Albert died in **1970**, aged about 82. No marriage or children have been identified.
